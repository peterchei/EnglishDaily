# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-12

## 1. **Landfall** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlænd.fɔːl/
**Definition:** The moment when a storm or hurricane reaches the land from the sea.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 登陸（指颱風等）

**Example 1:**
The typhoon made landfall in Zhejiang province early this morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
颱風今早於浙江省登陸。

**Example 2:**
Evacuations were completed hours before the storm made landfall.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在風暴登陸前數小時，疏散行動已經完成。

## 2. **Subpoena** (Noun/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /səˈpiː.nə/
**Definition:** A legal document ordering someone to appear in court or provide evidence.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 傳票（法律）

**Example 1:**
The journalists were issued a subpoena to reveal their sources.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
記者們收到傳票，要求披露消息來源。

**Example 2:**
The company was subpoenaed to produce documents regarding its recent sales.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司收到傳票，需提交有關近期銷售的文件。

## 3. **Fossil** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɒs.əl/
**Definition:** The remains or traces of a prehistoric plant or animal preserved in rock.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 化石

**Example 1:**
The museum purchased a rare T. rex fossil for a record-breaking price.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
博物館以破紀錄的價格買入了一具罕見的暴龍化石。

**Example 2:**
Scientists study the fossil to understand how the species evolved.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
科學家研究這具化石，以了解該物種如何演化。

## 4. **Firearm** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfaɪə.rɑːm/
**Definition:** A weapon capable of firing a projectile, such as a pistol or rifle.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 火器、槍械

**Example 1:**
Police recovered two firearms from the scene of the shooting.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方在槍擊現場尋回了兩支槍械。

**Example 2:**
Strict laws govern the ownership of firearms in this country.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個國家對槍械的擁有權有嚴格的法律規管。

## 5. **Remake** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈriː.meɪk/
**Definition:** A new version of an older film, game, or piece of music.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重製版

**Example 1:**
Fans have waited over a decade for this game remake.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
粉絲們已經等待了超過十年才等到這個遊戲重製版。

**Example 2:**
The film is a modern remake of a classic 1950s story.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這部電影是五十年代經典故事的現代重製版。

## 6. **Life expectancy** (Noun phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlaɪf ɪkˌspek.tən.si/
**Definition:** The number of years a person is expected to live on average.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 平均預期壽命

**Example 1:**
The report highlights a concerning drop in healthy life expectancy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該報告強調了健康預期壽命令人擔憂的下降趨勢。

**Example 2:**
Better healthcare has significantly increased life expectancy globally.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
更好的醫療保健顯著提高了全球的平均預期壽命。
