# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-16

## 1. **Crumble** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkrʌm.bəl/
**Definition:** To break or fall apart into small fragments; to lose strength or fail.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 崩塌、粉碎、瓦解

**Example 1:**
The old stone bridge began to crumble after years of neglect.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰座古老石橋經過多年失修後開始崩塌。

**Example 2:**
England's defensive formation started to crumble under pressure from the Argentinian strikers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺阿根廷前鋒嘅壓力下，英格蘭嘅防線開始瓦解。

## 2. **Shatter** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃæt.ər/
**Definition:** To break suddenly or violently into small pieces; to destroy or damage something (like hope or dreams) completely.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 粉碎、破滅

**Example 1:**
The late goal served to shatter the home team's hopes of winning the trophy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰個臨完場前嘅入波粉碎咗主隊贏取獎盃嘅希望。

**Example 2:**
Be careful not to shatter the glass vase while moving the furniture.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
搬傢俬嗰陣小心唔好打碎個玻璃花樽。

## 3. **Disciplinary** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdɪs.ə.plɪn.er.i/
**Definition:** Relating to the punishment or enforcement of rules and standards of behaviour.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 紀律嘅、懲戒嘅

**Example 1:**
The football association is considering disciplinary action against the players involved.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
足球總會正考慮對涉事球員採取紀律處分。

**Example 2:**
Poor timekeeping may result in a formal disciplinary meeting with your manager.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
遲到可能會導致你需要同經理進行正式嘅紀律會議。

## 4. **Appoint** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈpɔɪnt/
**Definition:** To choose someone for a job or position of responsibility.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 任命、委派

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister will appoint a new chancellor later this week.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相將會喺本週稍後任命一位新嘅財政大臣。

**Example 2:**
They decided to appoint an independent committee to investigate the matter.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢哋決定任命一個獨立委員會去調查呢件事。

## 5. **Screening** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskriː.nɪŋ/
**Definition:** The process of testing or examining people to detect a disease, condition, or suitability for a role.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 篩查、篩選、檢測

**Example 1:**
Newborn babies undergo screening to identify potential health conditions early.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
初生嬰兒會接受篩查，以便及早發現潛在嘅健康狀況。

**Example 2:**
The company conducts rigorous security screening for all new employees.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司會為所有新員工進行嚴格嘅安全審查。

## 6. **Curfew** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɜː.fjuː/
**Definition:** A regulation requiring people to remain indoors between specified hours, typically at night.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 宵禁、禁足令

**Example 1:**
The government is debating whether to introduce a social media curfew for teenagers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正討論係咪應該對青少年實施社交媒體宵禁。

**Example 2:**
Soldiers patrolled the streets to ensure everyone obeyed the night-time curfew.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
士兵喺街道巡邏，確保每個人都遵守夜間宵禁。

## 7. **Deficiency** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfɪʃ.ən.si/
**Definition:** A lack or shortage of something that is required or necessary.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 缺乏、不足、缺陷

**Example 1:**
The blood test revealed a vitamin deficiency in his diet.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
驗血報告顯示佢飲食中缺乏維生素。

**Example 2:**
The military is monitoring testosterone deficiency among the troops to maintain performance.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
軍方正監察部隊中嘅睾酮缺乏情況，以保持作戰能力。
