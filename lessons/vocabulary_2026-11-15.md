# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-15

## 1. **Faint-hearted** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfeɪntˌhɑː.tɪd/
**Definition:** Lacking the courage or determination to do something difficult or dangerous.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 膽小的，沒勇氣的

**Example 1:**
The new welfare policy is not for the faint-hearted as it requires significant reforms.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢項新嘅福利政策唔係俾膽小嘅人做，因為佢需要重大改革。

**Example 2:**
This high-speed mountain path is definitely not for the faint-hearted.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢條高速登山徑絕對唔係俾膽小嘅人行。

## 2. **Appetite** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈæp.ə.taɪt/
**Definition:** A strong desire or interest in something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 渴求，強烈興趣

**Example 1:**
The local government has little appetite for further tax increases this year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
地方政府今年對進一步加稅冇乜意欲。

**Example 2:**
There is a growing appetite among young people for more sustainable public transport.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
年輕人對於更可持續嘅公共交通工具嘅需求越嚟越大。

## 3. **Staggered** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈstæɡ.əd/
**Definition:** Arranged in a way that things happen at different times rather than all at once.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 交錯的，分階段的

**Example 1:**
The company introduced a staggered start time for employees to reduce traffic congestion.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司實施咗員工分階段返工時間，以減少交通擠塞。

**Example 2:**
We are implementing a staggered approach to returning to the office after the holidays.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋正採取分階段方式喺假期後返辦公室。

## 4. **Mechanical** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /məˈkæn.ɪ.kəl/
**Definition:** Relating to machines or operated by a machine.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 機械的，自動的

**Example 1:**
The rocket was caught by giant mechanical arms during the landing process.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
火箭喺降落過程中被巨型機械臂捉住。

**Example 2:**
He carried out the task in a purely mechanical way without thinking.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢用一種純粹機械式嘅方法完成任務，完全冇諗過。

## 5. **Lawsuit** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɔː.suːt/
**Definition:** A problem or complaint that a person or organization brings to a court of law to be settled.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 訴訟，控告

**Example 1:**
The port authority filed a lawsuit to recover the costs of repairing the damaged dock.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
港口管理局提出訴訟，以彌補維修受損碼頭嘅費用。

**Example 2:**
Several companies are facing a class-action lawsuit regarding data privacy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
有幾間公司正面臨關於數據隱私嘅集體訴訟。

## 6. **Incompetence** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈkɒm.pɪ.təns/
**Definition:** Lack of skill or ability to do your job or a task as it should be done.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 無能，不稱職

**Example 1:**
The minister resigned due to claims of incompetence regarding the transport failure.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
部長因為涉及交通系統故障嘅無能指控而辭職。

**Example 2:**
The investigation revealed that the delay was caused by sheer incompetence.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
調查顯示，延誤係由純粹嘅無能所導致。

## 7. **Outage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈaʊt.ɪdʒ/
**Definition:** A period when a service, such as electricity or an internet connection, is not working.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中斷，停運

**Example 1:**
The global tech company is working to fix the cause of the major system outage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢間全球科技公司正致力修復導致嚴重系統中斷嘅原因。

**Example 2:**
We experienced a two-hour power outage during the storm last night.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
昨晚風暴期間，我哋經歷咗兩個鐘嘅停電。
