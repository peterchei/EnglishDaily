# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-26

## 1. **Underpin** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌndəˈpɪn/
**Definition:** To support or form the basis of an argument, a claim, or a structure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 作為……的基礎；鞏固；支撐

**Example 1:**
The new research findings underpin the government's decision to change the policy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新的研究結果為政府改變政策的決定提供了基礎。

**Example 2:**
Strong economic growth serves to underpin the stability of the nation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
強勁的經濟增長有助於鞏固國家的穩定。

## 2. **Oversight** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈəʊvəsaɪt/
**Definition:** The failure to notice something, or the act of supervising a process or system.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 疏忽；監管

**Example 1:**
The mistake was made due to a simple administrative oversight.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這個錯誤是由於一個簡單的行政疏忽造成的。

**Example 2:**
The committee is responsible for the oversight of public spending.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該委員會負責公共開支的監管。

## 3. **Run-up** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrʌn.ʌp/
**Definition:** The period of time immediately before an important event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （重要事件前的）準備期；前奏

**Example 1:**
Politicians often make many promises in the run-up to a general election.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政客在國會大選前的準備期常會許下許多承諾。

**Example 2:**
We were extremely busy in the run-up to the product launch.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在產品發布會前的準備期，我們忙得不可開交。

## 4. **Stood down** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /stʊd daʊn/
**Definition:** To stop a formal state of emergency or to withdraw from a position or duty.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 解除（緊急狀態等）；退位；退出

**Example 1:**
The emergency services stood down after the risk of flooding decreased.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在洪水風險降低後，緊急救援服務已解除戒備。

**Example 2:**
The candidate decided to stand down after the scandal broke.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醜聞爆出後，該候選人決定退出競選。

## 5. **Publicise** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌpʌblɪˈsaɪz/
**Definition:** To make information about something available to the public to get attention.
**Pronunciation Note:** (British English spelling)
**Cantonese Meaning:** 宣傳；公之於眾

**Example 1:**
The charity is using social media to publicise their latest campaign.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該慈善機構正利用社交媒體宣傳他們最新的活動。

**Example 2:**
The event was heavily publicised in local newspapers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該活動在當地報紙上被大肆宣傳。

## 6. **Prominent** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprɒmɪnənt/
**Definition:** Important, well-known, or easily noticeable.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 傑出的；顯眼的；重要的

**Example 1:**
Several prominent members of the industry attended the exhibition.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
幾位業界的傑出人士參加了這次展覽。

**Example 2:**
The building occupies a prominent position in the city centre.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這座建築在市中心佔據著一個顯眼的位置。
