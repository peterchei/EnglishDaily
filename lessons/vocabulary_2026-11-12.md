# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-12

## 1. **Overhaul** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈəʊvəhɔːl/
**Definition:** A thorough examination and extensive repair or restructuring of a system or machine.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 全面檢查；徹底改革

**Example 1:**
The government plans a radical overhaul of the welfare system to support younger workers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府計劃對福利制度進行徹底改革，以支援較年輕的勞工。

**Example 2:**
Our department needs an urgent overhaul of its filing process to improve productivity.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們部門需要緊急徹底整頓檔案處理流程，以提高生產力。

## 2. **Remedy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈremədi/
**Definition:** A medicine or treatment for a disease or injury; a way of solving a problem.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 治療藥物；補救方法

**Example 1:**
They are questioning if expensive cold remedies are truly more effective than basic pills.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他們正在質疑昂貴的感冒藥是否真的比基本藥丸更有效。

**Example 2:**
The company is looking for a legal remedy to address the copyright infringement.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司正在尋求法律補救措施來處理侵權問題。

## 3. **Whistleblower** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɪsəlbləʊə/
**Definition:** A person who informs on a person or organization regarded as engaging in unlawful or immoral activity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 舉報人；揭弊者

**Example 1:**
New safeguards are being introduced to ensure the safety of whistleblowers in the media industry.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正引入新的保障措施，以確保傳媒行業內舉報人的安全。

**Example 2:**
The whistleblower provided crucial evidence that exposed the company’s internal corruption.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該舉報人提供了關鍵證據，揭露了公司的內部腐敗。

## 4. **Administration** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ədˌmɪnɪˈstreɪʃn/
**Definition:** The process or activity of running a business or organization; in UK law, a state of insolvency where a company is placed under the control of an insolvency practitioner.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 管理；（公司）破產管理狀態

**Example 1:**
The discount retailer is edging towards administration as it struggles to find a buyer.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家折扣零售商因難以尋找買家，正處於破產管理的邊緣。

**Example 2:**
The administration of the local hospital has been criticised for long waiting lists.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當地醫院的管理層因長長的輪候名單而受到批評。

## 5. **Toxic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɒksɪk/
**Definition:** Poisonous; harmful or unpleasant in a pervasive way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 有毒的；有害的

**Example 1:**
There has been a significant drop in levels of toxic chemicals found in our rivers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我們河流中發現的有毒化學物質含量已顯著下降。

**Example 2:**
We need to change the toxic culture in the office to make it a better place to work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們需要改變辦公室內有害的文化，使其成為一個更好的工作環境。

## 6. **Clash** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /klæʃ/
**Definition:** A violent confrontation or disagreement; a loud, harsh noise.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 衝突；撞擊聲

**Example 1:**
Clashes erupted in the city centre as protesters gathered to demand lower housing costs.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
示威者聚集要求降低住房成本，市中心爆發了衝突。

**Example 2:**
There was a clash of opinions during the meeting regarding the new project deadline.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
會議期間，大家對新項目的截止日期意見不合。

## 7. **Audit** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɔːdɪt/
**Definition:** An official inspection of an individual's or organization's accounts or records.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 審計；查賬

**Example 1:**
The government has ordered an internal audit of all library collections to ensure quality.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府已下令對所有圖書館藏書進行內部審計，以確保質量。

**Example 2:**
We are conducting an annual audit of our finances to ensure transparency for investors.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們正在對財務狀況進行年度審計，以確保投資者的透明度。
