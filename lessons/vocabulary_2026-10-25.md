# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-25

## 1. **Gusts** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɡʌsts/
**Definition:** Sudden, strong rushes of wind.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 一陣陣強風

**Example 1:**
The weather report warned that gusts could reach 80mph during the storm.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
天氣預報警告指，風暴期間陣風時速可達80英里。

**Example 2:**
Strong gusts blew the umbrella out of my hand while I was walking to the station.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我步行去車站時，強風把我手中的雨傘吹走了。

## 2. **Citing** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsaɪtɪŋ/
**Definition:** Mentioning something as a reason or an example to support an argument or claim.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 引用（作理由或證據）

**Example 1:**
The letter was sent citing concerns about the family's privacy and safety.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這封信發出時，引用了對家庭隱私和安全的擔憂。

**Example 2:**
She resigned from her position, citing a desire to focus on her health.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她辭去了職位，理由是希望專注於自己的健康。

## 3. **Factual** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfæktʃuəl/
**Definition:** Concerned with or based on facts; true and accurate.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 事實的，基於事實的

**Example 1:**
The journalist promised to provide a factual account of the events.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
記者承諾提供一份基於事實的事件報導。

**Example 2:**
It is important to have a factual discussion rather than relying on rumors.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
進行基於事實的討論很重要，而不是依賴傳聞。

## 4. **Plug the gap** (Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /plʌɡ ðə ɡæp/
**Definition:** To provide something that is missing or to solve a problem caused by a shortage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 填補空缺，補救不足

**Example 1:**
The government is looking for new investors to plug the gap in funding.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在尋找新的投資者來填補資金缺口。

**Example 2:**
We need to find a temporary staff member to plug the gap while Sarah is on leave.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在莎拉休假期間，我們需要找一位臨時員工來補位。

## 5. **Demolish** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈmɒlɪʃ/
**Definition:** To pull or knock down a building or structure completely.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 拆毀，拆除

**Example 1:**
The council plans to demolish the old shopping centre to build new flats.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
市議會計劃拆除舊購物中心，以興建新公寓。

**Example 2:**
The house was demolished because it was considered unsafe for residents.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這所房子因被認為對住戶不安全而被拆毀。

## 6. **Anguish** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈæŋɡwɪʃ/
**Definition:** Severe mental or physical pain or suffering.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 劇痛，極度痛苦

**Example 1:**
He expressed his deep anguish over the loss of his friends in the accident.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他對在事故中失去朋友表達了深深的痛苦。

**Example 2:**
She watched in anguish as the fire destroyed her family home.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當大火燒毀她家時，她痛苦地看著這一切。

## 7. **Unveil** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnˈveɪl/
**Definition:** To show or announce something new or secret for the first time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公布，揭幕

**Example 1:**
The company will unveil its latest smartphone at the event next week.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司將在下週的活動中公布其最新的智能手機。

**Example 2:**
The Chancellor is expected to unveil new tax policies in the upcoming budget.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
預計財政大臣將在即將到來的預算案中公布新的稅務政策。
