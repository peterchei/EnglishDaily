# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-09

## 1. **Hypocrisy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /hɪˈpɒkrəsi/
**Definition:** The practice of claiming to have moral standards or beliefs to which one's own behaviour does not conform.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 虛偽

**Example 1:**
The opposition leader accused the government of hypocrisy for promising lower taxes while increasing spending.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
反對派領袖指責政府虛偽，一邊承諾減稅，一邊卻增加開支。

**Example 2:**
It is pure hypocrisy to lecture others on punctuality when you are always late yourself.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你自己遲大到，卻去說教別人要守時，這簡直是虛偽。

## 2. **Concerted** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈsɜːtɪd/
**Definition:** Jointly arranged, planned, or carried out; done with great effort and determination.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 齊心協力的；協調一致的

**Example 1:**
The company made a concerted effort to reduce its carbon footprint this year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家公司今年齊心協力，致力於減少碳足跡。

**Example 2:**
There was a concerted campaign by local residents to save the historic library from demolition.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當地居民發起了一場協調一致的運動，以拯救那座歷史悠久的圖書館免遭拆除。

## 3. **Undermine** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌndəˈmaɪn/
**Definition:** To lessen the effectiveness, power, or ability of someone or something, especially gradually or insidiously.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 暗中破壞；削弱

**Example 1:**
Constant criticism from colleagues can undermine your confidence at work.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
同事不斷的批評會削弱你在工作上的自信心。

**Example 2:**
The scandal threatened to undermine the authority of the organisation's president.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這宗醜聞威脅到要削弱該組織主席的權威。

## 4. **Resonates** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrezəneɪts/
**Definition:** To evoke images, memories, or emotions; to strike a chord with someone.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 引起共鳴

**Example 1:**
The message of the speech resonates with young people who are worried about their future.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這次演講的內容引起了那些為未來擔憂的年輕人的共鳴。

**Example 2:**
Her story resonates deeply because many people have experienced similar challenges.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她的故事引起了深刻的共鳴，因為許多人都經歷過類似的挑戰。

## 5. **Expropriations** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌeksproupriˈeɪʃnz/
**Definition:** The action by the state or an authority of taking property from its owner for public use or benefit.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 徵收（土地或財產）

**Example 1:**
The government announced plans for the expropriation of private land to build a new railway.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府宣佈了徵收私人土地以興建新鐵路的計劃。

**Example 2:**
Local farmers are protesting against the mass expropriation of their ancestral fields.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當地農民正在抗議政府大規模徵收他們的祖傳農田。

## 6. **Cements** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /sɪˈments/
**Definition:** To settle or establish something firmly; to strengthen a relationship or status.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 鞏固；加強

**Example 1:**
This victory cements his position as the most popular candidate in the race.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這次勝利鞏固了他作為競選中最受歡迎候選人的地位。

**Example 2:**
The two companies signed a deal to cement their long-term partnership.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
兩家公司簽署了一項協議，以鞏固他們的長期合作關係。

## 7. **Attribute** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈtrɪbjuːt/
**Definition:** To regard something as being caused by someone or something; to assign credit or blame.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 把……歸因於；認為……是……所引起

**Example 1:**
The police attribute the cause of the fire to faulty electrical wiring.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方將火災的原因歸咎於電線故障。

**Example 2:**
She attributes her success to hard work and the support of her family.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她將自己的成功歸功於勤奮努力以及家人的支持。
