# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-09

## 1. **Derailed** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdiːˈreɪld/
**Definition:** (Of a train) moved off the tracks, or metaphorically, a plan or process that has failed or gone off course.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 出軌；(計劃) 失敗或偏離軌道

**Example 1:**
The freight train derailed after hitting an obstacle on the tracks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這列貨運火車在撞到軌道上的障礙物後出軌了。

**Example 2:**
The company’s expansion plans were derailed by the sudden economic downturn.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司的擴張計劃因突如其來的經濟衰退而被迫中止。

## 2. **Commute** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈmjuːt/
**Definition:** The journey that a person makes regularly to get to work or school and back home.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 通勤；上下班的往返行程

**Example 1:**
Dense fog is expected to make the Monday morning commute very difficult.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
預計濃霧會令週一早上的通勤變得非常困難。

**Example 2:**
I spend over an hour on my daily commute by train.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我每天搭火車通勤要花一個多小時。

## 3. **Phase out** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌfeɪz ˈaʊt/
**Definition:** To gradually stop using or producing something over a period of time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 逐步淘汰；分階段停止

**Example 1:**
The government plans to phase out petrol cars by 2030 to reduce emissions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府計劃在 2030 年前逐步淘汰汽油車，以減少排放。

**Example 2:**
We are going to phase out this old software and replace it with a new system.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們打算逐步淘汰這款舊軟件，並用新系統取而代之。

## 4. **Random** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrændəm/
**Definition:** Done, chosen, or happening without method or conscious decision; unplanned.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 隨機的；胡亂的；沒有規律的

**Example 1:**
The gunman began firing at random in the town square.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那名槍手在廣場上隨機開火。

**Example 2:**
They chose a random selection of people to interview for the survey.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們隨機選取了一些人進行調查訪問。

## 5. **Verdict** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvɜːdɪkt/
**Definition:** A decision or judgment made after considering the facts, especially by a jury, or an official opinion.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 裁決；決定；判斷結果

**Example 1:**
The technology critic gave his verdict on the new gaming remake.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那位科技評論家對這款新遊戲重製版給出了他的評價。

**Example 2:**
The jury reached a unanimous verdict after two days of deliberation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
陪審團在商議兩天後達成了一致的裁決。

## 6. **Tread water** (Idiom)
**Pronunciation:** /tred ˈwɔːtər/
**Definition:** To keep one's head above water by moving the feet and hands, or metaphorically, to make no progress.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 踩水（保持頭部浮出水面）；原地踏步（無進展）

**Example 1:**
The pianist managed to tread water for nine hours before being rescued at sea.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那位鋼琴家在海上踩水九個小時，最終獲救。

**Example 2:**
Without more funding, the project is just treading water.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果沒有更多資金，這個項目就只是在原地踏步。
