# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-07

## 1. **Firebrand** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfaɪə.brænd/
**Definition:** A person who is passionate about a particular cause and often encourages others to take radical or aggressive action.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 激進分子；煽動者

**Example 1:**
The new chief of staff is a known political firebrand who thrives on confrontation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位新任幕僚長是一位眾所周知的政治激進分子，喜歡對抗。

**Example 2:**
He was a firebrand in his youth, leading protests against the government.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他年輕時是一位煽動者，曾領導反政府示威。

## 2. **Barrister** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbær.ɪ.stər/
**Definition:** A type of lawyer in the UK who represents clients in the higher courts.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大律師（英國法律體系）

**Example 1:**
The barrister argued that there was insufficient evidence to convict his client.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
大律師辯稱沒有足夠證據將其當事人定罪。

**Example 2:**
She has spent twenty years working as a barrister specializing in criminal law.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她從事刑事法律大律師工作已有二十年。

## 3. **Regulator** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈreɡ.jə.leɪ.tər/
**Definition:** An official or organization that checks that a business or industry is obeying the law and acting correctly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 監管機構；監管者

**Example 1:**
The broadcasting regulator is investigating complaints about the presenter's behavior.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
廣播監管機構正在調查有關該主持人行為的投訴。

**Example 2:**
Financial regulators have tightened the rules to prevent another banking crisis.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
金融監管機構收緊了規則，以防止再次發生銀行危機。

## 4. **Takeaway** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈteɪk.ə.weɪ/
**Definition:** A main point or piece of information that you learn or understand from an event or meeting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 主要收穫；要點

**Example 1:**
The key takeaway from the election results is that voters want significant change.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
選舉結果的主要收穫是選民們想要重大的改變。

**Example 2:**
What was your biggest takeaway from the training session today?
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你從今天的培訓課程中得到最大的收穫是什麼？

## 5. **Oust** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /aʊst/
**Definition:** To force someone out of a position of power, especially a political one.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 罷免；趕下台

**Example 1:**
The president was ousted from office following a massive public protest.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統在大規模公眾抗議後被罷免下台。

**Example 2:**
There is a move within the party to oust the current leader before the next election.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
黨內有一股勢力試圖在下次選舉前將現任領導人趕下台。

## 6. **Autonomous** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɔːˈtɒn.ə.məs/
**Definition:** Having the ability to operate or act independently, often referring to technology or vehicles.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 自動的；自主的

**Example 1:**
The company is investing heavily in developing autonomous vehicles for city transport.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司正投入大量資金開發用於城市交通的自動駕駛車輛。

**Example 2:**
These robots are autonomous and can perform complex tasks without human intervention.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這些機器人是自動的，可以在沒有人類干預的情況下執行複雜任務。
