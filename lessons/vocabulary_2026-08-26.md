# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-26

## 1. **Empowerment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪmˈpaʊəmənt/
**Definition:** The process of becoming stronger and more confident, especially in controlling one's life and claiming one's rights.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 賦權，授權，增強自信

**Example 1:**
Dolly Parton’s music often focuses on themes of female empowerment.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
桃莉·巴頓的音樂經常聚焦於女性賦權的主題。

**Example 2:**
The workshop aims to provide empowerment to young people seeking career advice.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個研討會旨在為尋求職業建議的年輕人增強自信。

## 2. **Mistreatment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌmɪsˈtriːtmənt/
**Definition:** The act of treating someone or something badly or in a cruel way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 虐待，處理不當

**Example 1:**
The report highlights years of mistreatment faced by victims within the system.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該報告強調了受害者在體制內多年來遭受的虐待。

**Example 2:**
He complained about the mistreatment of the animals at the local shelter.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他投訴了當地收容所對動物的虐待行為。

## 3. **Regulator** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈreɡjuleɪtə(r)/
**Definition:** An official body that controls a public business activity or industry to ensure it operates correctly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 監管機構，調節器

**Example 1:**
The energy regulator announced an increase in the price cap for the winter months.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
能源監管機構宣佈調高冬季月份的價格上限。

**Example 2:**
The banking regulator is investigating the firm's recent financial records.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
銀行業監管機構正在調查該公司近期的財務記錄。

## 4. **Retaliatory** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈtæliətəri/
**Definition:** Done in order to hurt someone because they have done something harmful to you.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 報復性的

**Example 1:**
Canada has imposed retaliatory tariffs on several American goods.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
加拿大已對多項美國商品徵收報復性關稅。

**Example 2:**
The government warned that any attack would lead to a retaliatory strike.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府警告說，任何攻擊都將導致報復性打擊。

## 5. **Degenerative** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈdʒenərətɪv/
**Definition:** (Of a disease) getting progressively worse over time, causing the function or structure of the affected tissues to deteriorate.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 退化性的

**Example 1:**
Research suggests that many former players suffer from a degenerative brain disease.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
研究顯示許多前球員患有退化性腦部疾病。

**Example 2:**
Early diagnosis is crucial when dealing with degenerative conditions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在處理退化性疾病時，早期診斷至關重要。

## 6. **Back up** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæk ʌp/
**Definition:** Extra equipment, supplies, or power that you can use if the main ones fail or are lost.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 後備，備用，支援

**Example 1:**
Spanish firms are investing in battery back ups to avoid future power failures.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
西班牙公司正在投資電池後備電源，以避免未來的停電事故。

**Example 2:**
Always keep a digital back up of your important documents.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請務必保留重要文件的數位備份。
