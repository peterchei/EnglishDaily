# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-16

## 1. **Underpinning** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌndəˈpɪnɪŋ/
**Definition:** A foundation, basis, or set of ideas that supports something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 基礎、支柱

**Example 1:**
The report examines the economic underpinning of the government’s new employment strategy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢份報告審視咗政府新就業策略嘅經濟基礎。

**Example 2:**
Strong historical ties provide the underpinning for our current diplomatic relations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
深厚嘅歷史聯繫為我哋目前嘅外交關係提供咗基礎。

## 2. **Alignment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈlaɪnmənt/
**Definition:** The state of being arranged in a line or in proper relative positions; agreement or cooperation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 一致、對齊、結盟

**Example 1:**
The minister spoke about the alignment of British food standards with those of the EU.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
部長談及英國食品標準與歐盟標準嘅一致性。

**Example 2:**
We need to ensure our goals are in alignment with the company’s long-term vision.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋需要確保我哋嘅目標同公司嘅長遠願景保持一致。

## 3. **Electorate** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈlektərət/
**Definition:** All the people who are allowed to vote in an election.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 選民（總稱）

**Example 1:**
The MP returned to work to serve the needs of her local electorate.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位國會議員重返崗位，去服務佢選區選民嘅需求。

**Example 2:**
Political parties must listen to the concerns of the wider electorate.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政黨必須聆聽廣大選民嘅憂慮。

## 4. **Resilience** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzɪliəns/
**Definition:** The capacity to recover quickly from difficulties or toughness.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 韌性、適應力、復原力

**Example 1:**
The crew showed great resilience during their record-breaking patrol at sea.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
船員喺佢哋破紀錄嘅海上巡邏期間展現出極強嘅韌性。

**Example 2:**
Building resilience in supply chains is essential for modern businesses.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺現代商業中，建立供應鏈嘅韌性係至關重要嘅。

## 5. **Inflating** (Verb - Present Participle)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈfleɪtɪŋ/
**Definition:** Increasing the perceived value, price, or size of something, often dishonestly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誇大、抬高（價格或價值）

**Example 1:**
The lawsuit alleges that the company was inflating its share price by hiding sales data.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
訴訟指控該公司透過隱瞞銷售數據嚟抬高股價。

**Example 2:**
He was accused of inflating his resume to get a better job offer.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢被指控誇大自己嘅履歷以獲取更好嘅工作錄取。

## 6. **Incentive** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈsentɪv/
**Definition:** A thing that motivates or encourages one to do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誘因、激勵

**Example 1:**
The report suggests a tax incentive to encourage companies to hire more young people.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
報告建議提供稅務誘因，以鼓勵企業聘請更多年輕人。

**Example 2:**
There is little financial incentive for them to change their current business model.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢哋冇咩財政誘因去改變目前嘅商業模式。

## 7. **Engagement** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənt/
**Definition:** A formal agreement to meet, or involvement in an activity or dialogue.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 會面、參與、約定

**Example 1:**
The royal couple attended several public engagements during their visit to India.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
皇室夫婦喺訪問印度期間出席咗幾個公開會面活動。

**Example 2:**
Higher levels of student engagement lead to better learning outcomes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
較高水平嘅學生參與度會帶來更好嘅學習成果。
