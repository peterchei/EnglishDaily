# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-17

Hello everyone! Let's look at some important vocabulary from today's headlines.

## 1. **Buffer** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbʌfə/
**Definition:** Something that reduces the impact of a shock or protects against harm or difficulty.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 緩衝物；保護層

**Example 1:**
The Amazon rainforest acts as a vital buffer against the effects of global climate change.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
亞馬遜雨林作為抵禦全球氣候變遷影響的重要緩衝。

**Example 2:**
We need a financial buffer in our savings account to handle unexpected emergencies.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們需要在儲蓄戶口中預留一筆緩衝資金，以應對突發的緊急情況。

## 2. **Tenure** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtenjə/
**Definition:** The period during which someone holds an important job or position.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 任期

**Example 1:**
Her tenure as press secretary was surprisingly short, lasting only a few days.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
她作為新聞秘書的任期短得令人驚訝，僅僅持續了幾天。

**Example 2:**
The professor is celebrating ten years of tenure at the university.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
那位教授正在慶祝他在這所大學任職滿十年。

## 3. **Deforestation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdiːˌfɒrɪˈsteɪʃn/
**Definition:** The action or process of clearing or destroying a wide area of trees.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 森林砍伐；毀林

**Example 1:**
Brazil has reported a significant reduction in deforestation across the Amazon region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
巴西報告稱亞馬遜地區的森林砍伐量有顯著下降。

**Example 2:**
Global laws are being tightened to prevent illegal deforestation by agricultural companies.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
全球法律正在收緊，以阻止農業公司進行非法森林砍伐。

## 4. **Algorithm** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈælɡərɪðəm/
**Definition:** A process or set of rules followed by a computer to solve problems or make decisions, especially on social media.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 演算法

**Example 1:**
Many people worry that social media algorithms can promote toxic or harmful content.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多人擔心社交媒體的演算法會助長有害內容。

**Example 2:**
The app uses a complex algorithm to suggest movies based on your viewing history.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該應用程式使用複雜的演算法，根據你的觀看記錄來推薦電影。

## 5. **Offensive** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈfensɪv/
**Definition:** An organized military attack or a concerted effort to achieve a specific goal.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 進攻；攻勢

**Example 1:**
The Ukrainian army launched a new offensive to reclaim territory in the east.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
烏克蘭軍隊發動了新一輪攻勢，以收復東部領土。

**Example 2:**
The company is launching a marketing offensive to win back its customers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這家公司正發動一場行銷攻勢，以贏回客戶。

## 6. **Dampen** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdæmpən/
**Definition:** To make something feel less strong, intense, or positive; to discourage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 減弱；使沮喪；潑冷水

**Example 1:**
Poor weather failed to dampen the spirits of the crowd at the festival.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
惡劣的天氣並沒能澆熄節日人群的興致。

**Example 2:**
Recent diplomatic tensions have dampened hopes for a quick peace agreement.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
近期外交緊張局勢減弱了人們對達成快速和平協議的期望。
