# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-04

## 1. **Debris** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdeb.riː/
**Definition:** Scattered pieces of rubbish or remains after something has been destroyed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 瓦礫、碎片

**Example 1:**
Rescuers are clearing the debris to find people trapped under the collapsed structure.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援人員正在清理瓦礫，以尋找被困在倒塌建築物下的人。

**Example 2:**
After the flood, the streets were covered in mud and heavy debris.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
洪水過後，街道上佈滿了泥漿和沉重的碎片。

## 2. **Holdout** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhəʊld.aʊt/
**Definition:** A person who refuses to agree to an action or decision that everyone else is accepting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 堅持己見者、拒絕妥協的人

**Example 1:**
The judge refused to remove the lone holdout on the jury despite the long trial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管審訊時間很長，法官仍拒絕更換陪審團中那名拒絕妥協的成員。

**Example 2:**
She is the final holdout in the office who still prefers using paper files instead of digital ones.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她是辦公室裡最後一個堅持用紙質檔案而非電子檔案的人。

## 3. **Shrapnel** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃræp.nəl/
**Definition:** Small metal pieces that fly out from an exploding bomb or shell.
**Cantonese Example:** 彈片

**Example 1:**
The medical team removed sharp pieces of shrapnel from the patient's leg.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
醫療團隊從病人的腿部取出了鋒利的彈片。

**Example 2:**
Windows nearby were shattered by the shrapnel from the blast.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
附近的窗戶被爆炸產生的彈片震碎了。

## 4. **Barb** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /bɑːb/
**Definition:** A sharp, hook-like point on a fishhook, arrow, or animal part, pointing backwards.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 倒鉤、刺

**Example 1:**
The stingray’s barb can cause serious injury if it pierces the skin.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
如果刺穿皮膚，魔鬼魚的倒鉤會造成嚴重傷害。

**Example 2:**
Be careful removing the hook; it has a barb that might tear the fabric.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
移除魚鉤時要小心，它有一個倒鉤可能會扯破布料。

## 5. **Humanoid** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhjuː.mə.nɔɪd/
**Definition:** Having an appearance or characteristics that resemble a human being.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 人形的、類人的

**Example 1:**
The theme park features humanoid robots that can dance and interact with visitors.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該主題公園設有能跳舞並與遊客互動的人形機器人。

**Example 2:**
Scientists have developed a humanoid machine to perform tasks in dangerous environments.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
科學家研發了一種人形機器，用於在危險環境中執行任務。

## 6. **Advocate** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈæd.və.kət/
**Definition:** A person who publicly supports or suggests a particular cause or policy.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 倡導者、支持者

**Example 1:**
As an advocate for clean energy, she frequently writes articles about solar power.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
作為清潔能源的倡導者，她經常撰寫有關太陽能的文章。

**Example 2:**
He is a strong advocate for improving mental health services in the community.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他是改善社區心理健康服務的堅定支持者。
