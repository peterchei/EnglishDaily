# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-06

## 1. **Scrutiny** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskruːtəni/
**Definition:** Critical observation or examination.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 詳細審查；仔細檢查

**Example 1:**
The government's new housing policy is under intense scrutiny by the opposition party.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府的新房屋政策正受到反對黨的嚴密審查。

**Example 2:**
Investors are demanding more scrutiny of the tech industry’s data centre expansion.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
投資者要求對科技行業的數據中心擴張進行更嚴格的審查。

## 2. **Impeachment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪmˈpiːtʃmənt/
**Definition:** A charge of misconduct made against the holder of a public office.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 彈劾

**Example 1:**
The politician faces a possible impeachment vote following the recent election results.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於近期的選舉結果，這位政治人物面臨可能的彈劾投票。

**Example 2:**
The constitutional process for impeachment requires a majority vote in the assembly.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
彈劾的憲法程序需要在議會中獲得多數票通過。

## 3. **Sophistication** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /səˌfɪstɪˈkeɪʃn/
**Definition:** The quality of being complex, advanced, or highly developed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 複雜性；老練；精密

**Example 1:**
Criminals today are using a level of sophistication that challenges our current policing methods.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
現代罪犯所使用的手段之精密，對我們現行的警務方法構成了挑戰。

**Example 2:**
The software’s sophistication allows it to detect fraud in milliseconds.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該軟件的精密程度使其能夠在幾毫秒內檢測出欺詐行為。

## 4. **Discrimination** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˌskrɪmɪˈneɪʃn/
**Definition:** The unjust or prejudicial treatment of different categories of people.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 歧視

**Example 1:**
New laws aim to reduce pay discrimination by requiring firms to publish salary ranges.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新法律旨在通過要求公司公佈薪資範圍來減少薪酬歧視。

**Example 2:**
The company has a strict policy against discrimination in the workplace.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司在工作場所設有嚴格的反歧視政策。

## 5. **Decor** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdeɪkɔː(r)/
**Definition:** The style and furnishings of a room or building.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 裝飾風格；室內裝潢

**Example 1:**
The President made several controversial changes to the decor of the Oval Office.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統對橢圓形辦公室的裝潢風格進行了幾項具爭議的改動。

**Example 2:**
We decided to update the office decor to create a more modern working environment.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們決定更新辦公室的裝潢，以創造更現代化的工作環境。

## 6. **Turbine** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɜːbaɪn/
**Definition:** A machine for producing continuous power in which a wheel or rotor is made to revolve by a fast-moving flow of water, steam, gas, or air.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 渦輪；渦輪機

**Example 1:**
Engineers are struggling to manufacture the complex blades used in jet turbines.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
工程師們正致力於製造噴氣式渦輪機中使用的複雜葉片。

**Example 2:**
The wind turbine provides renewable energy for thousands of homes in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這台風力渦輪機為該地區的數千個家庭提供可再生能源。
