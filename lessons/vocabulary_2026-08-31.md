# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-31

Hi there! Here is your English vocabulary lesson based on today's news headlines.

## 1. **Capsize** (verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkæp.saɪz/
**Definition:** (of a boat) to turn over in the water.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （船隻）翻沉

**Example 1:**
The small ferry began to capsize after hitting a large rock near the port.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰架小型渡輪喺碼頭附近撞到大石之後開始翻沉。

**Example 2:**
Rescue teams are worried that more people could be trapped inside if the vessel continues to capsize further.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援隊擔心如果船隻繼續翻沉，會有更多人被困入面。

## 2. **Reshuffle** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːˈʃʌf.əl/
**Definition:** A change in the positions of people within a group or organization, especially a government.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 改組（尤指政府或內閣）

**Example 1:**
The opposition leader announced a major front-bench reshuffle this morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
反對派領袖今朝宣佈咗一項重要嘅前座議員改組。

**Example 2:**
Political analysts suggest that this reshuffle aims to strengthen the party's image.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政治分析員認為今次改組係為咗加強政黨嘅形象。

## 3. **Accession** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əkˈseʃ.ən/
**Definition:** The process of officially joining an international organization or agreement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加入（國際組織或協議）

**Example 1:**
Voters in Iceland have rejected the proposal to resume accession talks with the European Union.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
冰島選民否決咗重啟加入歐盟談判嘅提議。

**Example 2:**
The country needs to meet specific economic criteria before their formal accession can be approved.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢個國家需要達到特定嘅經濟標準，佢哋嘅正式加入先至會獲得批准。

## 4. **Airlift** (verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈeə.lɪft/
**Definition:** To transport people or supplies by aircraft, especially in an emergency.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 空運（尤指緊急情況下）

**Example 1:**
Several hikers were airlifted to safety after the flash flood caught them by surprise.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
喺山洪爆發令行山人士措手不及後，有幾個人被空運到安全地方。

**Example 2:**
The charity is working to airlift medical supplies to the disaster zone immediately.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰間慈善機構正努力將醫療物資即時空運到災區。

## 5. **Cloning** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkləʊ.nɪŋ/
**Definition:** The process of creating an exact copy of biological material or, in digital contexts, simulating a person's voice or likeness.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 複製；（聲音或外貌的）仿製

**Example 1:**
Actors are demanding new laws to protect themselves against unauthorized AI voice cloning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
演員正要求制定新法律，保護佢哋免受未經授權嘅人工智能聲音複製。

**Example 2:**
The technology behind AI voice cloning has advanced rapidly in the last few years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
人工智能聲音複製背後嘅技術喺過去幾年發展得好快。

## 6. **Manhunt** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmæn.hʌnt/
**Definition:** An organized search for a person who is wanted for a crime.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 搜捕（通緝犯）

**Example 1:**
Police have launched a massive manhunt for the suspect involved in the shooting at the rave.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方已經針對參與銳舞派對槍擊案嘅疑犯展開大規模搜捕。

**Example 2:**
The manhunt continues as officers search the woods near the Swiss border.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
隨住警員喺瑞士邊境附近嘅樹林搜索，搜捕行動仍然持續。
