# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-24

## 1. **Reassess** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriː.əˈses/
**Definition:** To think about something again, especially because your opinions or the facts have changed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重新評估

**Example 1:**
The government will no longer require patients with severe disabilities to reassess their condition for benefits.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府將不再要求嚴重殘疾人士重新評估他們的狀況以獲取福利。

**Example 2:**
After the market crash, the company had to reassess its long-term investment plans.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
股市崩盤後，該公司不得不重新評估其長期投資計劃。

## 2. **Stakes** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /steɪks/
**Definition:** The amount of money or the level of risk involved in a situation or gamble.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 風險；賭注

**Example 1:**
The sinking of the ship in Libya has raised the stakes of the ongoing conflict.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
利比亞境內船隻沉沒事件提高了這場持續衝突的風險。

**Example 2:**
In high-pressure jobs, the stakes are often very high for the employees involved.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在高壓工作中，相關員工往往面臨極高的風險。

## 3. **Funnel** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfʌn.əl/
**Definition:** To move or send something through a restricted space or a specific channel.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 輸送；通過管道傳送

**Example 1:**
The firm was accused of funneling advanced AI chips to markets under strict trade restrictions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司被指控將先進的人工智能晶片輸送到受嚴格貿易限制的市場。

**Example 2:**
The charity aims to funnel donations directly to the families most in need.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該慈善機構旨在將捐款直接輸送到最需要的家庭。

## 4. **Rally** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈræl.i/
**Definition:** To improve or recover from a weak state, often used in the context of stock prices or health.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 回升；反彈

**Example 1:**
Tech stocks began to rally following the news of the CEO's return to the company.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在行政總裁重返公司的消息傳出後，科技股開始回升。

**Example 2:**
Despite a difficult morning, the team managed to rally in the final minutes of the match.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
儘管上午表現困難，球隊還是設法在比賽最後幾分鐘內反彈。

## 5. **Flagged** (Verb - Past Participle used as Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /flæɡd/
**Definition:** Marked or identified as needing attention, often used for security or administrative alerts.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被標記（通常指需要特別留意或審查）

**Example 1:**
The traveler was stopped at the airport because his passport had been flagged by immigration.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該旅客在機場被攔下，因為他的護照已被移民局標記。

**Example 2:**
I have flagged this email for your review before we send it to the client.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在我們將此郵件發送給客戶之前，我已將其標記供你審閱。

## 6. **Warhead** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɔː.hed/
**Definition:** The front part of a missile or torpedo that contains the explosive material.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 彈頭

**Example 1:**
Reports suggest that the satellite may be equipped with a nuclear warhead.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
有報告指出該衛星可能裝有核彈頭。

**Example 2:**
The military experts were concerned about the potential range of the new warhead design.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
軍事專家對這種新型彈頭設計的潛在射程感到擔憂。

## 7. **Dyslexic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈlek.sɪk/
**Definition:** Experiencing difficulty in learning to read or interpret words, letters, and other symbols.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 讀寫障礙的

**Example 1:**
Many dyslexic employees feel hesitant to disclose their condition to potential employers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多有讀寫障礙的員工對是否向潛在雇主透露自己的情況感到猶豫。

**Example 2:**
Schools are providing better support for dyslexic students to help them succeed in class.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學校正在為有讀寫障礙的學生提供更好的支援，以幫助他們在課堂上取得成功。
