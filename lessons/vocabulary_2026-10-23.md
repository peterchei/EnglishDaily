# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-23

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary appearing in recent news reports.

## 1. **Vulnerability** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌvʌlnərəˈbɪləti/
**Definition:** A weakness or flaw that can be exploited by an attacker or a problem.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 弱點；漏洞

**Example 1:**
The software developer quickly patched the security vulnerability to protect user data.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軟件開發人員迅速修復了安全漏洞以保護用戶數據。

**Example 2:**
The island's vulnerability to extreme weather makes it difficult to maintain long-term infrastructure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該島嶼對極端天氣的脆弱性，使得長期維護基礎設施變得困難。

## 2. **Contingency** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈtɪndʒənsi/
**Definition:** A future event or circumstance that is possible but cannot be predicted with certainty; a backup plan.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 應急方案；不測事件

**Example 1:**
The government is creating a contingency plan in case the supply chain breaks down.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在制定應急方案，以防供應鏈中斷。

**Example 2:**
We should always have a contingency for when things do not go as expected.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當事情不如預期發展時，我們應該總是有一個應急計劃。

## 3. **Disruption** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈrʌpʃn/
**Definition:** The act of preventing something from continuing as expected.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中斷；擾亂

**Example 1:**
The train strike caused major disruption for commuters traveling into London this morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
火車罷工導致今天早上前往倫敦的通勤者面臨嚴重交通中斷。

**Example 2:**
Climate change is causing significant disruption to global food production.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
氣候變化正在對全球糧食生產造成嚴重的擾亂。

## 4. **Sworn** (Adjective/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /swɔːn/
**Definition:** Having taken a formal oath to tell the truth in a legal setting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 發過誓的；宣誓過的

**Example 1:**
The witness gave a sworn statement regarding what she saw on the night of the incident.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
證人就她在事發當晚所見的情況提交了一份宣誓證詞。

**Example 2:**
He is now required to answer all questions under sworn testimony in the court.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他現在被要求在法庭上以宣誓作證的形式回答所有問題。

## 5. **Oversight** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈəʊvəsaɪt/
**Definition:** The act of watching or supervising something; or an unintentional failure to notice something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 監管；疏忽

**Example 1:**
The regulator provides independent oversight of the banking industry to ensure fairness.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
監管機構對銀行業進行獨立監管，以確保公平。

**Example 2:**
Due to a clerical oversight, the meeting was scheduled for the wrong date.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於行政上的疏忽，會議日期被排錯了。

## 6. **Phenomenon** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /fəˈnɒmɪnən/
**Definition:** A fact or situation that is observed to happen or exist, especially one whose cause is in question.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 現象

**Example 1:**
The rise of social media influencers is a fascinating modern phenomenon.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
社交媒體網紅的崛起是一個迷人的現代現象。

**Example 2:**
Scientists are studying the strange weather phenomenon occurring in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
科學家正在研究該地區出現的奇特天氣現象。

## 7. **Foot the bill** (Idiom)
**Pronunciation:** /fʊt ðə bɪl/
**Definition:** To pay for something, especially when it is expensive or an unexpected cost.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 付錢；買單

**Example 1:**
If the company fails to cover the expenses, the government will have to foot the bill.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
如果公司無法支付這些費用，政府將不得不買單。

**Example 2:**
We shouldn't expect the taxpayers to foot the bill for private corporate losses.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們不應該期望納稅人為私人企業的損失買單。
