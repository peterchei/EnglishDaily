# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-11

Hi there! Today we are looking at some essential vocabulary derived from current news headlines, focusing on terms that will help you understand British reporting and formal communication.

## 1. **Attrition** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈtrɪʃ.ən/
**Definition:** The process of gradually reducing the strength or effectiveness of someone or something through sustained attack or pressure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 消耗；磨損

**Example 1:**
The army is currently engaged in a long war of attrition against the militia.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軍隊目前正與民兵進行一場漫長的消耗戰。

**Example 2:**
High staff attrition rates are a major concern for the company this year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
高員工流失率是該公司今年的主要擔憂。

## 2. **Enclosure** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈkləʊ.ʒər/
**Definition:** An area that is sealed off with an artificial or natural barrier.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 圍欄；圍場

**Example 1:**
Visitors are strictly forbidden from entering the tiger enclosure at the zoo.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
動物園嚴禁遊客進入老虎圍欄。

**Example 2:**
The new building project includes an enclosure for the garden's waste bins.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個新建築項目包括一個為花園垃圾桶設置的圍欄。

## 3. **Flag** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /flæɡ/
**Definition:** To draw attention to or mark something that requires further consideration or action.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 標記；標示

**Example 1:**
Please flag any suspicious emails you receive from unknown senders.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
請標記你收到的任何來自未知寄件人的可疑郵件。

**Example 2:**
The software automatically flagged the transaction as a potential security risk.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該軟件自動將這筆交易標記為潛在的安全風險。

## 4. **Junta** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhʊn.tə/
**Definition:** A military or political group that rules a country after taking power by force.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 軍政府；執政集團

**Example 1:**
The country has been ruled by a military junta for the past three years.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
過去三年，該國一直由軍政府統治。

**Example 2:**
International sanctions have been imposed on the junta to encourage democratic reform.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
國際社會已對軍政府實施制裁，以鼓勵民主改革。

## 5. **Malicious** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /məˈlɪʃ.əs/
**Definition:** Intended to do harm or cause damage, often used in the context of cyber threats.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 惡意的

**Example 1:**
The security team is working to block malicious software from entering our network.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
安全團隊正致力於阻止惡意軟件進入我們的網絡。

**Example 2:**
He was accused of spreading malicious gossip about his colleagues.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他被指控散佈關於同事的惡意流言。

## 6. **Snatched** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /snætʃt/
**Definition:** To take something or someone away suddenly and forcefully.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 攫取；強行擄走

**Example 1:**
The thief snatched her handbag and ran into the crowded station.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
小偷搶走了她的手提包，然後跑進了擁擠的車站。

**Example 2:**
There are reports of young people being snatched off the streets by armed groups.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
有報導稱年輕人被武裝組織從街上強行擄走。

## 7. **Lethal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈliː.θəl/
**Definition:** Sufficient to cause death; deadly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 致命的

**Example 1:**
The prisoner survived the administration of the lethal injection.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那名囚犯在被注射致命藥物後倖存了下來。

**Example 2:**
Mixing these two chemicals can create a lethal gas.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
將這兩種化學物質混合會產生致命氣體。
