# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-03

## 1. **Forecourt** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɔːkɔːt/
**Definition:** An open area in front of a building, often used specifically for a petrol station area where pumps are located.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加油站前院；前庭

**Example 1:**
Fuel prices are clearly displayed on the signs at the petrol forecourt.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
油價清晰地展示在加油站前院的招牌上。

**Example 2:**
The company is installing new security cameras across every forecourt to prevent theft.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司正在所有加油站前院安裝新的保安攝錄機以防止盜竊。

## 2. **Relentless** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈlentləs/
**Definition:** Continuing in a severe or extreme way; never stopping or becoming less intense.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 持續不斷的；無情的

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister promised a relentless effort to improve safety on the streets.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相承諾會不懈努力，改善街道安全。

**Example 2:**
She has faced relentless criticism from the media since announcing her retirement.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
自從宣佈退休以來，她一直面臨來自媒體的持續批評。

## 3. **Visibility** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌvɪzəˈbɪləti/
**Definition:** The state of being able to be seen; the degree to which something can be clearly observed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 可見度；曝光率

**Example 1:**
The singer decided to step back from public visibility to protect her mental health.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
為了保護心理健康，這位歌手決定減少在大眾面前曝光。

**Example 2:**
Poor visibility due to the thick fog made it difficult for the helicopters to land safely.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
大霧導致能見度極低，令直升機難以安全著陸。

## 4. **Proliferation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˌlɪfəˈreɪʃn/
**Definition:** The rapid increase in the number or amount of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 激增；擴散

**Example 1:**
Social media companies are struggling to control the proliferation of fake AI-generated images.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
社交媒體公司正努力控制虛假人工智能生成圖像的激增。

**Example 2:**
The government is concerned about the proliferation of unregulated small businesses in the area.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府對該地區缺乏監管的小型企業激增感到擔憂。

## 5. **Collide** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈlaɪd/
**Definition:** To hit something violently when moving; to crash into another object or person.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 碰撞；衝突

**Example 1:**
Two firefighting helicopters collided mid-air while working in poor weather conditions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩架消防直升機在惡劣天氣下工作時於半空中相撞。

**Example 2:**
Their opinions often collide during meetings, as they have very different working styles.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於工作風格大相徑庭，他們的意見在會議中經常產生衝突。

## 6. **Curb** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kɜːb/
**Definition:** To control or limit something that is considered undesirable.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 控制；限制

**Example 1:**
The city council is introducing new measures to curb illegal small boat crossings.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
市議會正推出新措施以限制非法的小船渡海行為。

**Example 2:**
You need to curb your spending if you want to save enough for a house deposit.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果你想存夠錢支付房屋首期，就需要控制你的開支。
