# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-04

## 1. **Prohibit** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˈhɪb.ɪt/
**Definition:** To officially forbid or stop something from being done by law or rule.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 禁止

**Example 1:**
The new government policy will prohibit the sale of these products to anyone under eighteen.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府的新政策將禁止向任何十八歲以下的人士出售這些產品。

**Example 2:**
Safety regulations prohibit smoking inside the airport terminal.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
安全條例禁止在機場航站樓內吸煙。

## 2. **Neglect** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /nɪˈɡlekt/
**Definition:** To fail to give proper attention or care to someone or something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 忽視；疏忽

**Example 1:**
The study warns that many medical centres neglect to screen patients for early warning signs.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該研究警告稱，許多醫療中心忽視了對患者進行早期徵兆篩查。

**Example 2:**
You should not neglect your health just because you are busy with work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你不應該因為工作忙碌而忽視自己的健康。

## 3. **Impose** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪmˈpəʊz/
**Definition:** To force a rule, tax, or punishment to be accepted or obeyed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強加；徵收（稅款等）

**Example 1:**
The US government decided to impose heavy duties on imported goods from various countries.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
美國政府決定對來自多國的進口商品徵收重稅。

**Example 2:**
We should not impose our personal beliefs on others during the discussion.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們在討論時不應將個人信仰強加於人。

## 4. **Resumption** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzʌmp.ʃən/
**Definition:** The act of starting something again after it has stopped.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 恢復；重新開始

**Example 1:**
Diplomats are hopeful about the resumption of peace talks between the two nations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
外交官對兩國恢復和平談判感到樂觀。

**Example 2:**
The resumption of normal train services will be announced later this evening.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
火車服務恢復正常的通知將於今晚稍後發布。

## 5. **Munitions** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /mjuːˈnɪʃ.ənz/
**Definition:** Military weapons, ammunition, equipment, and stores.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 軍火；彈藥

**Example 1:**
Construction workers were shocked to discover old munitions buried in the village soil.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
建築工人在村莊的土壤中發現埋藏的舊軍火時感到震驚。

**Example 2:**
The site was cleared of all dangerous munitions before the redevelopment project began.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在重建項目開始前，該地點的所有危險彈藥已被清理乾淨。

## 6. **Fluctuation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌflʌk.tʃuˈeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** A rise and fall in the number or amount of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 波動；起伏

**Example 1:**
The company is struggling to manage costs due to the constant fluctuation in chip prices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於晶片價格不斷波動，該公司難以控制成本。

**Example 2:**
Investors are concerned about the sharp fluctuation of the stock market this week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
投資者對本週股市的劇烈波動感到擔憂。
