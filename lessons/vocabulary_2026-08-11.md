# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-11

## 1. **Eliminate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈlɪm.ɪ.neɪt/
**Definition:** To remove or get rid of something completely.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 消除；根除

**Example 1:**
The government is working on a plan to eliminate poverty in the region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正致力於一項旨在消除該地區貧困的計劃。

**Example 2:**
We need to eliminate all unnecessary expenses to save money for the project.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我們需要消除所有不必要的開支，為這個項目省錢。

## 2. **Hollowed out** (Adjective/Verb phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhɒl.əʊd aʊt/
**Definition:** Having had the inside or central part removed, or (of a town/industry) reduced to a state of decline and emptiness.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被掏空；變得空洞（指市中心衰落）

**Example 1:**
The local high street has been hollowed out by the rise of online shopping.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
網上購物的興起使當地的商業街變得十分蕭條。

**Example 2:**
Years of neglect have hollowed out the sense of community in the old neighbourhood.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
多年的忽視已經掏空了這個舊社區的鄰里感。

## 3. **Infrastructure** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪn.frəˌstrʌk.tʃər/
**Definition:** The basic physical systems of a business, region, or nation, such as transport, communication, and power supplies.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 基礎設施

**Example 1:**
The company is investing billions into AI infrastructure to improve data processing.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司正在投入數十億資金用於人工智能基礎設施，以提升數據處理能力。

**Example 2:**
Improving public transport infrastructure is a priority for the city council.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
改善公共交通基礎設施是市議會的首要任務。

## 4. **Rhetoric** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈret.ər.ɪk/
**Definition:** Language that is intended to influence people and may not be completely honest or reasonable.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 修辭；誇誇其談（常用於政治口吻）

**Example 1:**
The politician's speech was full of empty rhetoric but lacked real solutions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位政客的演講充滿了空洞的修辭，卻缺乏切實的解決方案。

**Example 2:**
Don't be fooled by the election rhetoric; look at their actual track record.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
不要被選舉時的誇誇其談所蒙蔽，要看看他們實際的政績。

## 5. **Modelling** (Verb/Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɒd.əl.ɪŋ/
**Definition:** Creating a representation or simulation to show how something works or what it will look like.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 建模；模擬

**Example 1:**
The tech firm is modelling how their software will affect employee productivity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家科技公司正在模擬他們的軟件將如何影響員工的生產力。

**Example 2:**
Computer modelling helps scientists predict the path of the heatwave.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
電腦模擬有助於科學家預測熱浪的路徑。

## 6. **Discipline** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdɪs.ə.plɪn/
**Definition:** The practice of training people to obey rules or a code of behaviour.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 紀律；管教

**Example 1:**
There is a heated debate about how to maintain discipline in schools.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
關於如何維持學校紀律，存在著激烈的爭論。

**Example 2:**
The airline crew had to enforce strict discipline when the passenger refused to follow safety rules.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當乘客拒絕遵守安全規定時，機組人員必須執行嚴格的紀律。
