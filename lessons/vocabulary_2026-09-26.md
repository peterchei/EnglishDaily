# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-26

## 1. **Remuneration** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˌmjuːnəˈreɪʃn/
**Definition:** Money paid for work or a service.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 報酬、酬金

**Example 1:**
The lawyers are seeking fair remuneration for the hours spent on the complex legal case.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
律師們正為處理這宗複雜法律案件所花費的時間尋求合理的報酬。

**Example 2:**
The company’s executive remuneration package has been criticised for being excessive.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這家公司的高層薪酬方案因被指過高而受到批評。

## 2. **Meddle** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmedl/
**Definition:** To interfere in something that is not one's concern.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 干預、管閒事

**Example 1:**
It is alleged that the software bots were programmed to meddle with sensitive government databases.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
據稱這些軟件機器人被編程為干預敏感的政府數據庫。

**Example 2:**
I don't like it when outsiders try to meddle in our internal office affairs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我不喜歡外人試圖干預我們辦公室的內部事務。

## 3. **Confiscate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒnfɪskeɪt/
**Definition:** To take or seize someone's property with authority.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 沒收

**Example 1:**
Police will confiscate any e-bikes that do not meet the legal safety requirements.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方將沒收任何不符合法定安全要求的電動自行車。

**Example 2:**
The teacher had to confiscate the student's phone for using it during the exam.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學生在考試期間使用手機，老師不得不將其沒收。

## 4. **Legitimacy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /lɪˈdʒɪtɪməsi/
**Definition:** The quality of being fair, reasonable, or legally valid.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 合法性、正當性

**Example 1:**
The ambassador questioned the legitimacy of the claims made by the foreign politician.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
大使質疑那位外國政客所發表言論的正當性。

**Example 2:**
The election results were challenged, casting doubt on the government's legitimacy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
選舉結果受到挑戰，令政府的合法性受到質疑。

## 5. **Makeover** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmeɪkəʊvə(r)/
**Definition:** A thorough renovation or improvement of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 改頭換面、翻新

**Example 1:**
The classic video game received a Scottish makeover to appeal to a wider audience.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這款經典電子遊戲進行了蘇格蘭風格的翻新，以吸引更多受眾。

**Example 2:**
Our office is getting a complete makeover this weekend with new furniture and paint.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們的辦公室這個週末將進行全面翻新，換上新的家具和油漆。

## 6. **Disrupt** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈrʌpt/
**Definition:** To interrupt an event, activity, or process by causing a disturbance.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中斷、擾亂

**Example 1:**
Cyberattacks aim to disrupt the daily life of citizens by targeting essential data centres.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
網絡攻擊旨在通過針對關鍵數據中心來擾亂市民的日常生活。

**Example 2:**
Heavy rain is expected to disrupt train services across the country tomorrow.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
預計大雨會擾亂明天全國的火車服務。
