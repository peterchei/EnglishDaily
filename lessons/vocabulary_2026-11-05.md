# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-05

## 1. **Tightrope** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtaɪt.rəʊp/
**Definition:** A difficult situation in which you have to be very careful because a small mistake could have serious results.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 鋼索（比喻極度危險或需要小心處理的處境）

**Example 1:**
The politician is walking a tightrope between satisfying his voters and keeping his party united.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那位政客正在走鋼索，要在滿足選民與維持黨內團結之間取得平衡。

**Example 2:**
Managing a small business during an economic downturn is a constant tightrope walk.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在經濟下滑期間經營小企業，簡直是時刻都在走鋼索。

## 2. **Scathing** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskeɪ.ðɪŋ/
**Definition:** Criticizing someone or something in a very severe, harsh, and angry way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 嚴厲的；尖酸刻薄的；痛斥的

**Example 1:**
The journalist wrote a scathing review of the government's handling of the crisis.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
記者對政府處理危機的方式寫了一篇嚴厲的批評文章。

**Example 2:**
She received a scathing email from her boss after missing the deadline.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她在錯過截止日期後，收到了一封來自上司的尖酸斥責電郵。

## 3. **Reassurance** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriː.əˈʃɔː.rəns/
**Definition:** Words or actions that help to remove someone's doubts or fears.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 安慰；保證；消除疑慮

**Example 1:**
The manager gave the staff reassurance that their jobs were safe despite the merger.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
經理向員工保證，儘管進行了合併，但他們的工作仍然安全。

**Example 2:**
I needed some reassurance that I was on the right track with my project.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我需要一些保證，好讓我知道我的項目正走在正確的軌道上。

## 4. **Culprit** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkʌl.prɪt/
**Definition:** A person or thing that is responsible for a crime or a problem.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 罪魁禍首；肇事者

**Example 1:**
Police are still looking for the culprit who hacked the hospital’s computer system.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方仍在尋找入侵醫院電腦系統的肇事者。

**Example 2:**
Bad diet is often the main culprit behind low energy levels throughout the day.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
不良飲食往往是一整天精神不振的罪魁禍首。

## 5. **Batter** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæt.ər/
**Definition:** To hit something with great force repeatedly, often causing damage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 連續猛擊；重創；（強風暴雨）侵襲

**Example 1:**
The coastal town was battered by severe storms throughout the winter months.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那個沿海城鎮在整個冬季都遭到強烈風暴的重創。

**Example 2:**
Violent waves continued to batter the sides of the small fishing boat.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
猛烈的波浪持續拍打著那艘小漁船的船身。

## 6. **Fierce** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /fɪəs/
**Definition:** Showing strong, intense, or aggressive feeling or action.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 激烈的；強烈的；兇猛的

**Example 1:**
There was fierce competition between the two companies to win the government contract.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩家公司為了贏得政府合約，展開了激烈的競爭。

**Example 2:**
The islanders had to endure fierce winds during the typhoon.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
島上的居民在颱風期間必須忍受強烈的狂風。
