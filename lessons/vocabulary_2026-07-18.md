# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-18

## 1. **Degrade** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈɡreɪd/
**Definition:** To lower the character, quality, or condition of something; in military terms, to reduce the effectiveness or power of a force.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 降低；削弱（軍事力量）

**Example 1:**
The military operation aims to degrade the enemy's ability to launch further attacks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軍事行動旨在削弱敵人發動進一步攻擊的能力。

**Example 2:**
Constant exposure to sunlight can degrade the quality of the plastic over time.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
長時間暴露在陽光下會隨時間推移令塑膠質素下降。

## 2. **Blitz** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /blɪts/
**Definition:** A sudden, energetic, and concentrated effort or campaign to achieve something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 閃電戰；密集式的行動或攻勢

**Example 1:**
The government is planning a policy blitz to address the housing crisis within their first hundred days.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正計劃一場政策閃電戰，以在執政首百日內解決房屋危機。

**Example 2:**
After the holidays, we will start a marketing blitz to boost our sales figures.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
假期過後，我們將展開一場密集式的營銷攻勢以提升銷售額。

## 3. **Reboot** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːˈbuːt/
**Definition:** An act or instance of restarting or restoring something to a refreshed state.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重啟；重新振作

**Example 1:**
The party is hoping for a total reboot to regain the trust of the voters.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該政黨希望進行全面重啟，以重拾選民的信任。

**Example 2:**
It is time for a reboot of our project strategy to stay competitive in the market.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
是時候重啟我們的項目策略，以保持市場競爭力。

## 4. **Dissent** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈsent/
**Definition:** The expression or holding of opinions at variance with those previously, commonly, or officially held.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 異議；反對意見

**Example 1:**
The state has started to clamp down on any form of public dissent.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國家已開始打壓任何形式的公眾異議。

**Example 2:**
There is growing dissent among the workforce regarding the new office attendance policy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
員工對於新的辦公室出勤政策越來越多異議。

## 5. **Remanded** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈmɑːndɪd/
**Definition:** To be sent back into custody by a court of law while awaiting trial.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 還押；扣留（候審）

**Example 1:**
The suspect was remanded in custody until the trial begins next month.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
疑犯被還押候審，直至下個月審訊開始。

**Example 2:**
He was remanded to prison after failing to meet the bail conditions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他因未能滿足保釋條件而被還押監獄。

## 6. **Moonshot** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmuːn.ʃɒt/
**Definition:** An ambitious, exploratory, and ground-breaking project undertaken without any expectation of near-term profitability or success.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 登月計劃；高難度且具創新性的計劃

**Example 1:**
The company’s latest moonshot involves developing AI that can rival human intelligence.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司最新的「登月計劃」涉及開發能夠媲美人類智能的人工智能。

**Example 2:**
Success in a moonshot project requires both patience and a willingness to embrace failure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
要在「登月計劃」中取得成功，既需要耐心，也需要勇於面對失敗。

## 7. **Row** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /raʊ/
**Definition:** A noisy argument or quarrel.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 爭吵；口角

**Example 1:**
A diplomatic row has broken out between the two countries over the recent incident.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國因最近的事件爆發了外交爭吵。

**Example 2:**
The couple had a public row about which restaurant to visit.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
那對情侶為了去哪家餐廳吃飯而在公眾場合爭吵。
