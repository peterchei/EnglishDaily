# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-24

## 1. **Complacent** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /kəmˈpleɪ.sənt/
**Definition:** Showing smug or uncritical satisfaction with oneself or one's achievements, often leading to a lack of awareness of potential dangers.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 自滿的，自鳴得意的

**Example 1:**
The government cannot afford to be complacent about national security threats.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府不能對國家安全威脅掉以輕心。

**Example 2:**
He grew complacent after his initial success and stopped studying hard.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他在初次成功後變得自滿，不再努力學習。

## 2. **Contractor** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈtræk.tər/
**Definition:** A person or company that undertakes a contract to provide materials or labor to perform a service or job.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 承辦商，承包商

**Example 1:**
The defence contractor is working closely with the government to improve technology.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這間國防承辦商正與政府緊密合作以提升技術。

**Example 2:**
We hired a local contractor to renovate our kitchen.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們聘請了一位本地承辦商來裝修我們的廚房。

## 3. **Emerges** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈmɜːdʒɪz/
**Definition:** To come out into view or become known; to rise from a hidden or obscure state.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 出現，浮現，顯露

**Example 1:**
New details about the project emerges as the investigation continues.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
隨著調查持續，有關該項目的新細節浮現出來。

**Example 2:**
The sun finally emerges from behind the thick clouds.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
太陽終於從濃厚的雲層後顯露出來。

## 4. **Crude** (Noun/Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /kruːd/
**Definition:** In a natural or raw state; not yet refined (often used in the context of petroleum).
**Cantonese Meaning:** 原油（名詞）；粗糙的，未經加工的（形容詞）

**Example 1:**
The price of Brent crude has risen significantly due to global instability.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於全球局勢不穩，布蘭特原油價格大幅上漲。

**Example 2:**
The report was based on a very crude estimation of the costs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這份報告是基於對成本非常粗略的估算。

## 5. **Trafficked** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtræf.ɪkt/
**Definition:** To deal or trade in something illegally; often used regarding the illegal movement of people or goods.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 非法販運

**Example 1:**
International agencies are working to stop babies from being trafficked across borders.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際機構正致力於阻止嬰兒被跨境販運。

**Example 2:**
The criminal gang was caught having trafficked illegal goods for years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個犯罪集團被抓獲，他們多年來一直從事非法貨物販運。

## 6. **Landmark** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlænd.mɑːk/
**Definition:** An event, discovery, or change that is very important or significant.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 里程碑式的，具重大意義的

**Example 1:**
The two countries signed a landmark agreement to cooperate on energy policy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國簽署了一項關於能源政策合作的里程碑式協議。

**Example 2:**
This court ruling is considered a landmark decision for human rights.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這項法院裁決被視為人權領域具重大意義的決定。

## 7. **Rogue** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rəʊɡ/
**Definition:** Behaving in an unpredictable or dangerous way, often departing from expected standards or legal control.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 行為異常的，流氓的，不受控制的

**Example 1:**
Experts worry about the potential risks posed by rogue AI models.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
專家擔心行為異常的 AI 模型可能帶來的潛在風險。

**Example 2:**
The company was accused of operating like a rogue state within the tech industry.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司被指控在科技行業內表現得像個流氓政權。
