# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-11

## 1. **Relapse** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈriː.læps/
**Definition:** A worsening of an illness after a period of improvement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 復發、病情惡化

**Example 1:**
The study shows that this medication is highly effective in preventing a relapse.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
研究顯示呢隻藥喺預防復發方面非常有效。

**Example 2:**
He suffered a relapse after working too hard and not getting enough rest.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢因為工作太過勞累加上休息不足，導致病情復發。

## 2. **Anonymous** (adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈnɒn.ɪ.məs/
**Definition:** With a name that is not known or not made public.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 匿名、唔公開姓名

**Example 1:**
The witness chose to remain anonymous during the court proceedings.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位證人選擇喺法律程序中保持匿名。

**Example 2:**
She received an anonymous letter containing sensitive information about the company.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢收到一封匿名信，入面包含咗關於公司嘅機密資訊。

## 3. **Affair** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈfeər/
**Definition:** A secret sexual relationship, or an event/matter of public interest.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 婚外情、風流韻事、事件

**Example 1:**
The board is investigating the CEO regarding an alleged affair with a staff member.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
董事會正調查行政總裁同員工之間據稱嘅婚外情。

**Example 2:**
The government is handling the international affair with great caution.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府正非常謹慎地處理呢單國際事務。

## 4. **Detention** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈten.ʃən/
**Definition:** The state of being kept in official custody, often in a prison or secure facility.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 拘留、扣留

**Example 1:**
The suspect is currently in federal detention awaiting trial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰名疑犯目前正被聯邦拘留，等候審訊。

**Example 2:**
After the protest, several individuals were held in police detention for questioning.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
示威之後，有幾個人被警方拘留以進行盤問。

## 5. **Outperform** (verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌaʊt.pəˈfɔːm/
**Definition:** To perform better than someone or something else.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 表現優於、勝過

**Example 1:**
Their new product has consistently outperformed competitors in the market.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢哋嘅新產品喺市場上一直表現優於競爭對手。

**Example 2:**
The student worked hard to ensure her grades would outperform the class average.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰位學生好努力，確保佢嘅成績可以高過班級平均水平。

## 6. **Possession** (noun)
**Pronunciation:** /pəˈzeʃ.ən/
**Definition:** The state of having or owning something, often used in legal contexts regarding illegal items.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 擁有、持有（非法物品）

**Example 1:**
He was charged with the possession of illegal materials found in his home.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢因為喺屋企被發現持有非法物品而被控告。

**Example 2:**
Personal possession of small amounts of cannabis is handled differently in this region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺呢個地區，個人少量持有大麻係有唔同嘅處理方式。
