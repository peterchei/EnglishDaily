# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-18

Hello everyone! Let’s explore some useful vocabulary from today’s headlines to help you sound more like a native speaker of British English.

## 1. **Looting** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈluːtɪŋ/
**Definition:** The act of stealing goods from shops or buildings, typically during a war, riot, or period of disorder.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 搶掠 / 劫掠

**Example 1:**
The police were deployed to prevent looting after the protests turned violent.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
抗議活動變得暴力後，警方被部署以防止搶掠。

**Example 2:**
Several shops in the city centre reported looting during the civil unrest.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
市中心的幾家商店報告稱在內亂期間遭到搶掠。

## 2. **Ablaze** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈbleɪz/
**Definition:** Burning very strongly; on fire.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 著火 / 烈火熊熊

**Example 1:**
The historic building was left completely ablaze after the fire broke out.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
火災發生後，這座歷史建築被燒得烈火熊熊。

**Example 2:**
Emergency services struggled to reach the town hall while it was still ablaze.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
緊急服務部門在市政廳依然著火時難以進入。

## 3. **Spire** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈspaɪə(r)/
**Definition:** A tall, pointed structure on the top of a building, especially a church or town hall.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 塔尖 / 尖塔

**Example 1:**
The spire of the medieval church collapsed during the heavy storm.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那座中世紀教堂的塔尖在暴風雨中倒塌了。

**Example 2:**
You can see the town hall spire from almost anywhere in the city.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你幾乎可以在城裡的任何地方看到市政廳的尖塔。

## 4. **Casualties** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkæʒjuəltiz/
**Definition:** People killed or injured in a war, accident, or disaster.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 傷亡人員

**Example 1:**
The military spokesperson confirmed there were casualties following the explosion at the base.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軍方發言人證實基地發生爆炸後出現了傷亡。

**Example 2:**
High numbers of casualties were reported after the building collapsed.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
建築物倒塌後，據報有大量人員傷亡。

## 5. **Smear** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /smɪə(r)/
**Definition:** An attempt to damage someone's reputation by making false or damaging statements.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誹謗 / 抹黑

**Example 1:**
The politician claimed the allegations against him were just a smear campaign.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該政客聲稱針對他的指控只是一場抹黑運動。

**Example 2:**
They used social media to spread lies as part of a deliberate smear.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們利用社交媒體散佈謊言，作為蓄意抹黑的一部分。

## 6. **Delicate** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdelɪkət/
**Definition:** Requiring careful handling or thought because it is easily damaged or likely to cause offence.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 微妙的 / 棘手的

**Example 1:**
The prime minister is in a delicate position regarding the new trade agreement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相在新貿易協定問題上處於一個微妙的境地。

**Example 2:**
Negotiating peace in such a volatile region is a very delicate task.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在如此動盪的地區談判和平是一項非常棘手的任務。
