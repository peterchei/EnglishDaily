# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-08

## 1. **Dwindling** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdwɪndlɪŋ/
**Definition:** Gradually diminishing in size, amount, or strength.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 逐漸減少的；縮減中的

**Example 1:**
The army is struggling to defend the city with its dwindling supplies of ammunition.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軍隊正因為彈藥供應逐漸減少而難以防守這座城市。

**Example 2:**
Public interest in the long-running scandal is finally dwindling.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公眾對這宗長期醜聞的興趣終於逐漸減退。

## 2. **Severance** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsevərəns/
**Definition:** The action of ending a connection or employment; specifically, a payment made to an employee when their employment is terminated.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 終止（關係或合約）；遣散費

**Example 1:**
The executive was offered a generous severance package after his sudden departure.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那位行政人員在突然離職後，獲得了一筆優厚的遣散費。

**Example 2:**
The severance of diplomatic ties between the two nations caused market instability.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
兩國之間外交關係的斷絕導致了市場不穩定。

## 3. **Stakes** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /steɪks/
**Definition:** A share or financial interest in a business or project.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 股份；權益

**Example 1:**
The investment firm decided to sell its private stakes in the technology company.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家投資公司決定出售其在該科技公司的私人股份。

**Example 2:**
He holds a significant stake in the success of the new environmental initiative.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他在這項環保倡議的成功中擁有重大利益。

## 4. **Medics** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmedɪks/
**Definition:** Medical professionals, especially those working in emergency services or the military.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 醫務人員；軍醫

**Example 1:**
The medics were the first to arrive at the site of the missile strike.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
醫務人員是第一批抵達導彈襲擊現場的人。

**Example 2:**
Field medics are working under extreme pressure to save the injured soldiers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
戰地醫務人員正承受極大壓力去拯救受傷士兵。

## 5. **Governing body** (Noun Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡʌvənɪŋ ˌbɒdi/
**Definition:** An organization or group that has the authority to oversee and control the activities of a specific sector or sport.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 管理機構；監管組織

**Example 1:**
FIFA is the global governing body responsible for international football.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際足協是負責國際足球運動的全球管理機構。

**Example 2:**
The school's governing body is meeting tonight to discuss the new safety regulations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學校的管理委員會今晚開會，討論新的安全規定。

## 6. **Exclave** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈekskleɪv/
**Definition:** A portion of a state or territory that is geographically separated from the main part by surrounding alien territory.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 外飛地（被別國領土包圍的本國領土）

**Example 1:**
Ceuta is a Spanish exclave located on the north coast of Africa.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
休達是位於非洲北岸的一塊西班牙外飛地。

**Example 2:**
The citizens living in the exclave rely heavily on imports from the mainland.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
居住在該飛地的公民非常依賴從本土進口的物資。
