# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-10

## 1. **Coercive** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /kəʊˈɜː.sɪv/
**Definition:** Using force or threats to persuade someone to do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強制性的、高壓的

**Example 1:**
The judge highlighted his history of coercive behavior towards his partners.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法官強調了他對伴侶過往的強制性行為。

**Example 2:**
Schools are implementing new policies to prevent coercive bullying among pupils.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學校正在實施新政策，以防止學生之間出現高壓霸凌。

## 2. **Tumble** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtʌm.bəl/
**Definition:** To fall or drop suddenly and quickly in value or level.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 暴跌、翻滾

**Example 1:**
Share prices began to tumble after the investigation was announced.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
調查宣佈後，股價開始暴跌。

**Example 2:**
The toddler had a small tumble while learning to run in the park.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
那幼童在公園學跑時跌倒了一下。

## 3. **Status quo** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌsteɪ.təs ˈkwəʊ/
**Definition:** The present situation or the way things are now.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 現狀

**Example 1:**
The government prefers to maintain the status quo in diplomatic relations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府傾向於維持外交關係的現狀。

**Example 2:**
They were afraid that any change would upset the status quo.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們擔心任何變動都會打破現狀。

## 4. **Trilateral** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /traɪˈlæt.ər.əl/
**Definition:** Involving three groups, countries, or sides.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 三邊的、三方的

**Example 1:**
The trilateral talks aim to find a peaceful resolution to the conflict.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
此次三方會談旨在為衝突尋求和平解決方案。

**Example 2:**
A trilateral agreement was signed between the UK, France, and Germany.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
英、法、德三國簽署了一項三邊協議。

## 5. **Rogue** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rəʊɡ/
**Definition:** Behaving in a dangerous, uncontrolled, or dishonest way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 流氓的、失控的、不可預測的

**Example 1:**
The cybersecurity expert identified a rogue agent within the system.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
網絡安全專家發現系統內出現了一個失控的代理程式。

**Example 2:**
Rogue traders caused the bank to lose millions of pounds.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
失控的交易員導致銀行損失了數百萬英鎊。

## 6. **Flexibility** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌflek.səˈbɪl.ə.ti/
**Definition:** The ability to change easily according to the situation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 靈活性、彈性

**Example 1:**
Both sides showed flexibility during the negotiation process.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
雙方在談判過程中都表現出了靈活性。

**Example 2:**
My office offers the flexibility to work from home two days a week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我的辦公室提供每週在家工作兩天的彈性。

## 7. **Locked and loaded** (Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌlɒkt ənd ˈləʊ.dɪd/
**Definition:** Completely prepared and ready for action.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 準備就緒、嚴陣以待

**Example 1:**
The emergency response team is locked and loaded for the storm tonight.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
緊急救援小隊已為今晚的風暴做好嚴陣以待的準備。

**Example 2:**
He is locked and loaded with all the data needed for the presentation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他已經準備好簡報所需的所有數據。
