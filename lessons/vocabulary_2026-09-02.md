# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-02

## 1. **Capital** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkæp.ɪ.təl/
**Definition:** Relating to a crime that is punishable by death.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 可判處死刑的（罪行）

**Example 1:**
Murder is a capital offence in this jurisdiction.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
喺呢個司法管轄區，謀殺係一項可判處死刑嘅罪行。

**Example 2:**
The judge reminded the jury that they were dealing with a capital case.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
法官提醒陪審團，佢哋處理緊一單可判死刑嘅案件。

## 2. **DUI** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdiː.juːˈaɪ/
**Definition:** An abbreviation for "driving under the influence" of alcohol or drugs.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 酒駕或藥駕

**Example 1:**
The golfer was arrested for a suspected DUI after the crash.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位高爾夫球手喺車禍後因為涉嫌酒駕被捕。

**Example 2:**
A DUI conviction can lead to the permanent loss of your driving licence.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
酒駕定罪會導致你永久吊銷駕駛執照。

## 3. **Autonomous** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɔːˈtɒn.ə.məs/
**Definition:** Having the ability to operate independently without human control.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 自主嘅、自動化嘅

**Example 1:**
The company is testing autonomous aircraft to spray crops more efficiently.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢間公司正喺度測試無人駕駛飛機，以更有效率咁噴灑農作物。

**Example 2:**
We are entering an era of fully autonomous vehicles on our roads.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋正進入一個道路上充滿全自動駕駛車輛嘅時代。

## 4. **Raucous** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrɔː.kəs/
**Definition:** Making or constituting a disturbingly harsh and loud noise, often associated with wild parties.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 喧嘩嘅、吵鬧嘅

**Example 1:**
The area is well-known for its raucous nightlife and crowded bars.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢個地方以其喧嘩嘅夜生活同埋擠擁嘅酒吧而聞名。

**Example 2:**
The raucous laughter from the room next door kept me awake all night.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
隔籬房傳嚟嘅嘈吵笑聲令我成晚都瞓唔著。

## 5. **Nail-biting** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈneɪlˌbaɪ.tɪŋ/
**Definition:** Used to describe a situation that causes great anxiety or tension because the outcome is uncertain.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 令人極度緊張嘅

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister faced a nail-biting session of questions in Parliament today.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相今日喺國會面臨一場令人極度緊張嘅答問環節。

**Example 2:**
It was a nail-biting finish to the football match, with both teams tied until the last minute.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰場足球賽事嘅結局極度緊張，兩隊直至最後一分鐘都係平手。

## 6. **Self-harm** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌselfˈhɑːm/
**Definition:** The act of deliberately hurting oneself, often as a response to emotional distress.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 自殘

**Example 1:**
The NHS has reported a sharp increase in young people seeking help for self-harm.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國民保健署（NHS）報告話，尋求自殘相關支援嘅年輕人數量急劇增加。

**Example 2:**
Providing better mental health support is crucial to preventing self-harm among teenagers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
提供更好嘅心理健康支援，對預防青少年自殘至關重要。
