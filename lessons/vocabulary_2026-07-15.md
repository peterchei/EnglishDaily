# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-15

## 1. **Piecemeal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpiːs.miːl/
**Definition:** Done in small, separate stages rather than all at once or in a complete way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 零碎地；斷斷續續地

**Example 1:**
The government's piecemeal approach to the new social media laws has caused frustration among parents.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府對新社交媒體法例所採取嘅零碎處理手法，令家長感到好沮喪。

**Example 2:**
Repairs to the old building were carried out in a piecemeal fashion over several years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰座舊建築嘅維修工程喺過去幾年係斷斷續續咁進行嘅。

## 2. **Flattered** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈflæt.əd/
**Definition:** To make someone or something appear better or more attractive than they really are, or to have beaten someone comprehensively in a sporting context.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 令（某人或某事物）顯得更好；（喺比賽中）大勝或壓倒性擊敗

**Example 1:**
The team’s recent success flattered their poor performance throughout the season.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
球隊最近嘅成功掩蓋咗佢哋成個賽季嘅差劣表現。

**Example 2:**
Spain completely flattened their opponents in the final match, winning by four goals.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
西班牙喺決賽中以壓倒性姿態擊敗對手，贏咗四球。

## 3. **Warrant** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɒr.ənt/
**Definition:** A legal document issued by a judge or official that allows the police to do something, such as arrest someone or search a place.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 手令；搜查令；逮捕令

**Example 1:**
The police obtained a warrant to search the suspect's house for further evidence.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方攞到一張搜查令，去搜查疑犯屋企搵更多證據。

**Example 2:**
A judge refused to sign the arrest warrant because there was insufficient evidence.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於證據不足，法官拒絕簽署嗰張逮捕令。

## 4. **About-face** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈbaʊt.feɪs/
**Definition:** A complete change of opinion, policy, or plan.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 徹底改變（立場、政策或計劃）；一百八十度大轉變

**Example 1:**
The company performed a sudden about-face regarding their decision to cut staff benefits.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司針對削減員工福利嘅決定，突然作出咗一百八十度大轉變。

**Example 2:**
His about-face on the environmental policy shocked many of his political supporters.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢喺環境政策上嘅立場大轉變，令佢好多支持者感到震驚。

## 5. **Contention** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈten.ʃən/
**Definition:** A point of disagreement or an area where people are struggling for control.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 爭論；爭議；爭奪

**Example 1:**
Access to the narrow waterway has been a point of contention for many years.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
進入呢條狹窄水道嘅權利，多年嚟一直係爭議嘅焦點。

**Example 2:**
The new tax law is a subject of much contention among the political parties.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
新稅法係各政黨之間爭論好大嘅議題。

## 6. **Backlash** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæk.læʃ/
**Definition:** A strong and negative reaction by a large number of people to a change or a recent event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強烈反對；反彈（指公眾對某事嘅強烈負面反應）

**Example 1:**
The company faced a massive public backlash after they released the faulty AI feature.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司喺發布嗰個有問題嘅人工智能功能後，面臨強烈嘅公眾反彈。

**Example 2:**
The politician ignored the backlash from voters and continued with the controversial plan.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰位政客無視選民嘅強烈反對，繼續推行嗰項爭議性計劃。
