# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-16

## 1. **Incentive** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈsentɪv/
**Definition:** A thing that motivates or encourages someone to do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誘因、激勵

**Example 1:**
The company offers a financial incentive for employees who finish their projects ahead of the deadline.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司為提前完成項目嘅員工提供經濟誘因。

**Example 2:**
There are strong incentives for tech firms to prioritise safety in their new AI models.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
科技公司有強大嘅誘因去喺佢哋嘅新 AI 模型入面優先考慮安全。

## 2. **Line-up** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlaɪn.ʌp/
**Definition:** A group of people or things that are organised to take part in an event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 陣容、節目安排

**Example 1:**
The festival announced an impressive line-up of international musicians for this summer.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
音樂節宣佈咗今個夏天有一個令人印象深刻嘅國際音樂人陣容。

**Example 2:**
He was surprised to see his name removed from the official tour line-up.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢好驚訝見到自己個名從正式巡迴演出陣容入面被剔除。

## 3. **Collision** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈlɪʒ.ən/
**Definition:** An instance of one moving object or person striking violently against another.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 碰撞、衝突

**Example 1:**
Traffic was delayed for hours following a multi-vehicle collision on the motorway.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
高速公路發生多車碰撞事故後，交通延誤咗幾個鐘。

**Example 2:**
The news helicopter was reporting on the scene of a fatal bus collision.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
新聞直升機當時正喺現場報導一宗致命巴士碰撞事故。

## 4. **Flares** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /fleəz/
**Definition:** Bright lights used as a signal, a means of illumination, or a defensive measure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 照明彈、信號彈

**Example 1:**
The navy ship fired warning flares when the small boat entered restricted waters.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當嗰隻小船進入禁航區時，海軍軍艦發射咗警告照明彈。

**Example 2:**
Rescue teams used hand-held flares to signal their location to the search helicopter.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
救援隊利用手持信號彈向搜救直升機發出佢哋嘅位置信號。

## 5. **Objectification** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əbˌdʒek.tɪ.fɪˈkeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The action of degrading someone to the status of a mere object.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 物化（將人當作物件對待）

**Example 1:**
The watchdog criticised the advertisement for the objectification of women.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
監管機構批評嗰個廣告物化女性。

**Example 2:**
Society is becoming more aware of the dangers of the objectification of individuals in media.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
社會對於媒體中物化個人所帶來嘅危險正變得越嚟越有意識。

## 6. **Tanker** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtæŋ.kər/
**Definition:** A ship, road vehicle, or aircraft for carrying liquids, especially petroleum, in bulk.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 油輪、罐車

**Example 1:**
The oil tanker was attacked while passing through the narrow strait.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰架油輪喺穿過嗰個狹窄海峽時受到攻擊。

**Example 2:**
Safety standards for the tanker fleet have been tightened to prevent environmental disasters.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
油輪船隊嘅安全標準已經收緊，以防止環境災難。
