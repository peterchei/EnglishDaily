# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-27

## 1. **Ambition** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /æmˈbɪʃ.ən/
**Definition:** A strong desire to achieve or do something, typically requiring determination and hard work.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 野心；抱負

**Example 1:**
The government’s new housing target is an ambitious plan to help first-time buyers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府新的房屋目標是一項雄心勃勃的計劃，旨在幫助首次置業者。

**Example 2:**
She has the ambition to become a lead architect at the airport construction firm.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她有志向成為機場建築公司的首席建築師。

## 2. **Longevity** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /lɒnˈdʒev.ə.ti/
**Definition:** Long life or the length of time that something lasts.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 長壽；持久；持續時間

**Example 1:**
The chancellor is concerned about the longevity of the current economic pressures.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
財政大臣對當前經濟壓力的持續時間感到擔憂。

**Example 2:**
We need to consider the longevity of these building materials before starting renovations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在開始翻新工程之前，我們需要考慮這些建築材料的耐用性。

## 3. **Modest** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɒd.ɪst/
**Definition:** Relatively moderate, limited, or small in amount, rate, or level.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 適度的；謙虛的；不大的

**Example 1:**
There is a sense of modest optimism regarding the upcoming budget report.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
對於即將發布的預算報告，人們抱有一種謹慎而適度的樂觀。

**Example 2:**
The company reported a modest increase in profits this quarter.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司報告稱本季度利潤有適度增長。

## 4. **Definitive** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfɪn.ə.tɪv/
**Definition:** Final and providing a settled answer or decision; not to be questioned.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 決定性的；最終的；明確的

**Example 1:**
Tehran is still waiting for a definitive response from the international mediators.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
德黑蘭仍在等待國際調解人給出明確的答覆。

**Example 2:**
This book is considered the definitive guide to British history.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這本書被認為是關於英國歷史的權威指南。

## 5. **Meddle** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmed.əl/
**Definition:** To interfere in something that is not one's concern.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 干預；插手；管閒事

**Example 1:**
The agency warned that the bots should not meddle with public websites.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該機構警告稱，這些機器人不應干預公共網站。

**Example 2:**
Please stop meddling in my private affairs at work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請停止干預我在工作中的私事。

## 6. **Iconic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /aɪˈkɒn.ɪk/
**Definition:** Very famous or popular, especially being considered typical of a particular period or society.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 標誌性的；具代表性的

**Example 1:**
Silent Hill is an iconic horror game that has a huge fanbase in the UK.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
《沉默之丘》是一款標誌性的恐怖遊戲，在英國擁有龐大的粉絲群。

**Example 2:**
The building is an iconic landmark in the heart of Paris.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這座建築是巴黎市中心的一個標誌性地標。

## 7. **Suspected** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /səˈspek.tɪd/
**Definition:** Believed to be the cause of something or to have committed a crime, though not yet proven.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 疑似的；被懷疑的

**Example 1:**
Emergency services were called to the scene of a suspected gas explosion.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
緊急服務部門被召喚到一起疑似瓦斯爆炸的現場。

**Example 2:**
The police are investigating the suspected theft of the memorial plaques.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
警方正在調查疑似盜竊紀念牌匾的案件。
