# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-09

## 1. **Bereaved** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /bɪˈriːvd/
**Definition:** Having lost a relative or close friend through their death.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 喪失親友的；哀傷的

**Example 1:**
The ceremony was held to offer support to the bereaved families of the victims.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
舉行這場儀式是為了向受害者的喪親家庭提供支持。

**Example 2:**
She felt a deep empathy for the bereaved community after the tragedy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
悲劇發生後，她對那群痛失親友的社區感同身受。

## 2. **Mastermind** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɑːstəmaɪnd/
**Definition:** A person with outstanding intellect who plans and directs a complex or difficult project or activity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 幕後策劃者；主謀

**Example 1:**
Police have finally captured the alleged mastermind behind the high-profile museum heist.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方終於逮捕了那宗矚目博物館劫案的涉嫌主謀。

**Example 2:**
He is considered the mastermind of the company's successful digital transformation strategy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他被視為該公司成功進行數碼轉型策略的幕後策劃者。

## 3. **Glitch** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɡlɪtʃ/
**Definition:** A sudden, usually temporary, malfunction or fault of equipment or a system.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 小故障；技術問題

**Example 1:**
A software glitch caused the cancellation of over a hundred flights this morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
今早一個軟件小故障導致了一百多個航班被取消。

**Example 2:**
We experienced a minor glitch during the presentation, but it was quickly resolved.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們在簡報期間遇到了一點小技術問題，但很快就解決了。

## 4. **Tit-for-tat** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌtɪt.fəˈtæt/
**Definition:** Retaliatory; involving the equivalent of what one has received, typically an injury or insult.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 以牙還牙的；報復性的

**Example 1:**
The two countries are engaged in a dangerous tit-for-tat exchange of military strikes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國正陷入一場危險的報復性軍事打擊行動。

**Example 2:**
Their argument quickly descended into a petty tit-for-tat battle of words.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們的爭論很快就演變成幼稚的唇槍舌劍。

## 5. **Heist** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /heɪst/
**Definition:** A robbery, especially one involving a large sum of money or valuable items from a store or institution.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 搶劫；劫案

**Example 1:**
Security has been tightened at the gallery following the daring art heist last night.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在昨晚那宗大膽的藝術品劫案後，畫廊已加強了保安。

**Example 2:**
The police investigation into the bank heist is still ongoing.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
針對該銀行劫案的警方調查仍在進行中。

## 6. **Progression** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˈɡreʃ.ən/
**Definition:** The process of developing or moving gradually towards a more advanced state.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 進展；晉升；演進

**Example 1:**
The company is updating its career progression policy to include proficiency in new technologies.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司正在更新其職業晉升政策，將新技術的熟練程度納入考量。

**Example 2:**
We are monitoring the progression of the project to ensure we meet the deadline.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們正在監測項目的進度，以確保能趕上截止日期。
