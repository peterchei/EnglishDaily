# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-13

## 1. **Ringleader** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrɪŋˌliː.dər/
**Definition:** The person who leads a group of people who are doing something dishonest or illegal.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 首腦；主謀

**Example 1:**
The police have finally caught the ringleader of the grooming gang.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方終於捉到嗰個拐賣誘姦集團嘅首腦。

**Example 2:**
He acted as the ringleader in the protest, organising all the other participants.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢喺示威中充當咗首腦，組織埋所有其他參與者。

## 2. **Patron** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpeɪ.trən/
**Definition:** A person who uses a particular shop, restaurant, or service, especially regularly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 常客；光顧者

**Example 1:**
The restaurant staff ensured all patrons reached the emergency exit safely.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
餐廳職員確保所有光顧者都安全到達緊急出口。

**Example 2:**
The pub is popular with local patrons who come here every Friday night.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢間酒吧深受每逢星期五晚嚟光顧嘅常客歡迎。

## 3. **Heatwave** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhiːt.weɪv/
**Definition:** A period of time such as a few days or weeks when the weather is much hotter than usual.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 熱浪

**Example 1:**
The UK is experiencing an exceptionally long heatwave this summer.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英國今年夏天正經歷一場異常漫長嘅熱浪。

**Example 2:**
Public health officials are warning the elderly to stay hydrated during the heatwave.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公共衛生官員呼籲長者喺熱浪期間要保持水分充足。

## 4. **Correspondent** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌkɒr.ɪˈspɒn.dənt/
**Definition:** A person employed by a newspaper, television station, etc. to report on a particular subject or send reports from a foreign country.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 通訊員；特派記者

**Example 1:**
Our political correspondent is reporting live from outside Downing Street.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我哋嘅政治特派記者正喺唐寧街街外進行現場報導。

**Example 2:**
The foreign correspondent sent a detailed report about the situation in the Middle East.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢位外國特派記者發送咗一份關於中東局勢嘅詳細報告。

## 5. **Survivor** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /səˈvaɪ.vər/
**Definition:** A person who continues to function or prosper in spite of opposition, hardship, or trauma.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 倖存者；生還者

**Example 1:**
The politician was described as a true survivor of the volatile political era.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢位政治人物被形容為動盪政壇中真正嘅生存者。

**Example 2:**
Rescuers are still searching for survivors in the aftermath of the fire.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
救援人員仍然喺火災過後搜尋倖存者。

## 6. **Blowback** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbləʊ.bæk/
**Definition:** Negative repercussions or an adverse reaction, especially to a political or social action.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 反噬；負面迴響

**Example 1:**
The company faced significant blowback after releasing their new AI feature.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰間公司發佈咗新嘅人工智能功能之後，面對住重大嘅負面迴響。

**Example 2:**
The government expected some blowback from the public regarding the new tax policy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府預計新稅務政策會引起公眾嘅反噬。
