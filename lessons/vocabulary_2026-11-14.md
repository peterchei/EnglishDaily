# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-14

## 1. **Overload** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌəʊvəˈləʊd/
**Definition:** To put too much electricity, weight, or information onto a system or object, causing it to fail.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 超載；負荷過重

**Example 1:**
The old electrical system began to fail because it was overloaded by too many appliances.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
舊嘅電力系統因為負荷過多電器而開始失靈。

**Example 2:**
Try not to overload your brain with too much new information at once.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
試吓唔好一次過塞太多新資訊入個腦度，令自己負荷過重。

## 2. **Unaccounted** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnəˈkaʊntɪd/
**Definition:** Not having been explained or found; missing.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 下落不明；無法解釋的

**Example 1:**
After the cruise ship collision, rescue teams were relieved that no passengers were left unaccounted for.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
遊輪相撞之後，救援隊見到冇乘客下落不明，都鬆一口氣。

**Example 2:**
There is still a large sum of money unaccounted for in the company's annual report.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司年度報告入面仲有一大筆錢係去向不明。

## 3. **Non-critical** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌnɒnˈkrɪtɪkəl/
**Definition:** Not serious or life-threatening in the context of injuries or situations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 非緊急的；無生命危險的

**Example 1:**
Most of the people injured in the accident suffered only non-critical wounds.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
喺嗰單意外入面受傷嘅人，大部分都只係受咗輕傷，冇生命危險。

**Example 2:**
The hospital prioritises urgent cases over non-critical ones.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醫院會優先處理緊急個案，而非緊急嘅就會排後。

## 4. **Recovery** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈkʌvəri/
**Definition:** The process of returning to a normal state after a period of difficulty or failure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 恢復；復甦

**Example 1:**
Microsoft announced a broad recovery of its services after the global IT outage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
微軟宣佈喺全球 IT 故障之後，服務已經全面恢復。

**Example 2:**
The economy is showing signs of slow recovery after the recent recession.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經濟喺最近嘅衰退之後，開始出現緩慢復甦嘅跡象。

## 5. **Aborted** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈbɔːtɪd/
**Definition:** Stopped an activity or process before it is finished, usually because of a problem.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中止；半途而廢

**Example 1:**
The pilot aborted the landing when he saw an obstruction on the runway.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
機師見到跑道上有障礙物，於是中止咗降落。

**Example 2:**
SpaceX decided to abort the test flight due to unexpected technical issues.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
SpaceX 因為出現預料之外嘅技術問題，決定中止嗰次飛行測試。

## 6. **Illicit** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈlɪsɪt/
**Definition:** Illegal or forbidden by law or social custom.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 非法的；違禁的

**Example 1:**
The authorities are cracking down on the illicit trade of computer chips.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當局正喺度嚴厲打擊電腦晶片嘅非法貿易。

**Example 2:**
He was arrested for his involvement in an illicit gambling ring.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢因為參與非法賭博集團而被捕。

## 7. **Stark** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /stɑːk/
**Definition:** Clear, obvious, or severe; often used to describe a unpleasant reality.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 明顯的；嚴峻的；赤裸裸的

**Example 1:**
The global outage served as a stark reminder of our dependence on big technology companies.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰次全球故障為我哋帶來一個嚴峻嘅提醒，話畀我哋知我哋有幾咁依賴大型科技公司。

**Example 2:**
There is a stark contrast between the wealth of the city and the poverty in the suburbs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢個城市嘅財富同郊區嘅貧窮形成咗一個好強烈嘅對比。
