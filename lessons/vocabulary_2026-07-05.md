# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-05

## 1. **Infrastructure** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪn.frəˌstrʌk.tʃər/
**Definition:** The basic physical and organizational structures and facilities (e.g., buildings, roads, power supplies) needed for the operation of a society or enterprise.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 基礎設施

**Example 1:**
The government plans to invest heavily in transport infrastructure to improve connectivity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府計劃投放大量資金於交通基礎設施，以改善連接性。

**Example 2:**
Attacking energy infrastructure is a common tactic in modern conflicts.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
攻擊能源基礎設施是現代衝突中常見的策略。

## 2. **Revenue** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrev.ən.juː/
**Definition:** Income, especially when of a company or organization and of a substantial nature.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 收益、收入

**Example 1:**
The company’s annual revenue has increased significantly since the new product launch.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
自新產品發布以來，該公司的年度收入顯著增加。

**Example 2:**
Exporting oil remains a primary source of national revenue for the country.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
石油出口仍然是該國國家收入的主要來源。

## 3. **Bridge-building** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbrɪdʒˌbɪl.dɪŋ/
**Definition:** The process of establishing understanding or cooperation between different groups or people who have previously been at odds.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 建立溝通橋樑、修補關係

**Example 1:**
The diplomat is focused on bridge-building between the two warring factions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位外交官專注於在兩個交戰派系之間建立溝通橋樑。

**Example 2:**
After years of silence, their meeting was an important step in bridge-building.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經過多年的冷戰，他們的會面是修補關係的重要一步。

## 4. **Enduring** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈdjʊə.rɪŋ/
**Definition:** Continuing or long-lasting; able to withstand hardship or adversity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 持久的、忍耐的

**Example 1:**
The city is famous for its enduring charm and historic architecture.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這座城市以其持久的魅力和歷史建築而聞名。

**Example 2:**
They have an enduring friendship that has lasted over thirty years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們擁有一段持續了三十多年的深厚友誼。

## 5. **Publicly** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpʌb.lɪk.li/
**Definition:** In a way that is visible to or known by the public; openly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公開地

**Example 1:**
The celebrity publicly apologized for her remarks on social media.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那位名人就在社交媒體上的言論公開道歉。

**Example 2:**
It is rare to see the manager criticize his team publicly.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經理公開批評他的團隊是很少見的。

## 6. **Humidity** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /hjuːˈmɪd.ə.ti/
**Definition:** A measurement of the amount of water vapour in the air.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 濕度

**Example 1:**
High humidity can make hot weather feel much more oppressive.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
高濕度會讓炎熱的天氣感覺更加悶熱。

**Example 2:**
The humidity levels in the greenhouse are kept constant to help the plants grow.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
溫室內的濕度水平保持恆定，以幫助植物生長。

## 7. **Flexible** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈflek.sə.bəl/
**Definition:** Able to change or be changed easily according to the situation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 靈活的、具彈性的

**Example 1:**
We need a more flexible approach to handle unexpected problems.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我們需要一個更靈活的方法來處理突發問題。

**Example 2:**
Many employees prefer flexible working hours to achieve a better work-life balance.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多員工偏好靈活的工作時間，以實現更好的工作與生活平衡。
