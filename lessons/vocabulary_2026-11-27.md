# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-27

Hi there! Today we will look at some key vocabulary derived from current events in the news, helping you understand British media and global affairs more effectively.

## 1. **Friction** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfrɪk.ʃən/
**Definition:** Disagreement or lack of harmony between people or groups.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 摩擦、爭執

**Example 1:**
There is a lot of friction between the two nations regarding the new trade agreement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國之間喺新貿易協定上面有好多摩擦。

**Example 2:**
Diplomatic friction often arises when countries have different priorities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當國家之間嘅優先事項唔同，外交摩擦往往就會產生。

## 2. **Shortfall** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃɔːt.fɔːl/
**Definition:** An amount that is less than the level that was expected or needed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 不足額、缺口

**Example 1:**
The government is struggling to address the budget shortfall before the end of the year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正努力喺年底前解決預算不足嘅問題。

**Example 2:**
We need to find more staff to cover the shortfall in our delivery team.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋需要搵多啲人手嚟填補送貨團隊嘅人手缺口。

## 3. **Volatility** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌvɒl.əˈtɪl.ə.ti/
**Definition:** The quality of being likely to change suddenly and unexpectedly, especially for the worse.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 波動性、不穩定性

**Example 1:**
Investors are worried about the high volatility in the stock market this week.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
投資者好擔心今個星期股市嘅高波動性。

**Example 2:**
The political volatility in the region has made it difficult to plan long-term projects.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該地區嘅政治不穩定性令到長期項目好難規劃。

## 4. **Convene** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈviːn/
**Definition:** To come together for a meeting or activity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 召集、集合

**Example 1:**
The committee will convene on Monday to discuss the latest findings.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
委員會將會喺星期一召開會議，討論最新嘅發現。

**Example 2:**
Global leaders are expected to convene in London for the climate summit.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
預計全球領袖將會喺倫敦集合，參加氣候峰會。

## 5. **Disdain** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈdeɪn/
**Definition:** The feeling that someone or something is unworthy of one's consideration or respect.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 鄙視、輕蔑

**Example 1:**
He looked at the suggestion with complete disdain.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢以完全鄙視嘅態度睇嗰個建議。

**Example 2:**
Her disdain for the new policy was obvious during the meeting.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺會議期間，佢對新政策嘅輕蔑係顯而易見嘅。

## 6. **Gusts** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɡʌsts/
**Definition:** Sudden, strong rushes of wind.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 一陣陣強風

**Example 1:**
The severe weather brought heavy rain and strong gusts to the coast.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
惡劣天氣為沿海地區帶嚟大雨同強風。

**Example 2:**
We had to stay indoors because the gusts were strong enough to blow over garden furniture.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋被迫留喺屋企，因為陣風強到可以吹跌花園傢俬。
