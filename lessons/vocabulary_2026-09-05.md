# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-05

## 1. **Limbo** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɪmbəʊ/
**Definition:** A state of uncertainty or waiting where no progress can be made.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 處於懸而未決的狀態；半天吊

**Example 1:**
The murder case is in legal limbo until the court decides on a retrial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在法院決定重審之前，這宗謀殺案一直處於法律上的懸而未決狀態。

**Example 2:**
Many residents are in limbo, not knowing if they will be relocated after the disaster.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
災難發生後，許多居民處於半天吊的狀態，不知道自己是否會被安置。

## 2. **Distrust** (Noun/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈtrʌst/
**Definition:** The feeling that someone or something cannot be relied upon; lack of trust.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 不信任；懷疑

**Example 1:**
There is deep-seated distrust between the local community and the police force.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當地社區與警隊之間存在深層的不信任。

**Example 2:**
He distrusts the information provided by the anonymous source.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他不信任匿名消息來源所提供的資訊。

## 3. **Unanimous** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /juˈnænɪməs/
**Definition:** Fully in agreement; when all members of a group vote or decide the same way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 一致的；全體同意的

**Example 1:**
The jury failed to reach a unanimous verdict in the high-profile trial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
陪審團在這次備受矚目的審判中未能達成一致的裁決。

**Example 2:**
The board reached a unanimous decision to invest in new technology.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
董事會一致決定投資新技術。

## 4. **Sabotage** (Noun/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsæbətɑːʒ/
**Definition:** Deliberately destroy, damage, or obstruct something, especially for political or military advantage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 蓄意破壞；暗中破壞

**Example 1:**
Authorities are investigating whether the airport incident was an act of sabotage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當局正在調查機場事故是否屬於蓄意破壞行為。

**Example 2:**
Rival groups attempted to sabotage the peace talks by leaking confidential documents.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
敵對團體試圖透過洩露機密文件來破壞和談。

## 5. **Liability** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌlaɪəˈbɪləti/
**Definition:** The state of being legally responsible for something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 法律責任；債務

**Example 1:**
The company is trying to limit its liability for the environmental damage caused.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司正試圖限制其對造成的環境損害所負的法律責任。

**Example 2:**
They are assessing whether the suspect should be held criminally liable for the deaths.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們正在評估疑犯是否應對這些死亡事件承擔刑事責任。

## 6. **Stalled** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /stɔːld/
**Definition:** To stop making progress or to delay; to come to a standstill.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 停滯；擱置；拋錨

**Example 1:**
Peace negotiations have stalled due to disagreements over the border regions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於在邊境地區問題上存在分歧，和平談判已經停滯。

**Example 2:**
The project stalled when the main sponsor pulled out of the deal.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當主要贊助商退出協議時，該項目陷入了停滯。
