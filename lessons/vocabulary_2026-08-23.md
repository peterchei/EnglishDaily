# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-23

Hello everyone! Here are 6 useful words and phrases for today's lesson, curated from current British news headlines.

## 1. **Overrule** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌəʊvəˈruːl/
**Definition:** To use one's official authority to reject or cancel a decision made by someone else.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 否決、推翻（決定）

**Example 1:**
The housing minister wants to give mayors the power to overrule local councils on planning decisions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
房屋大臣希望賦予市長權力，在規劃決策上推翻地方議會的決定。

**Example 2:**
The judge decided to overrule the previous objection raised by the lawyer.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
法官決定推翻律師早前提出的反對。

## 2. **Wreckage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrekɪdʒ/
**Definition:** The remains of something that has been badly damaged or destroyed, typically a vehicle or building.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 殘骸

**Example 1:**
Rescuers are still digging through the wreckage of the shopping mall to find survivors.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援人員仍在商場殘骸中挖掘，希望能找到倖存者。

**Example 2:**
Debris and wreckage from the crash were scattered across the motorway.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
車禍後的碎片和殘骸散落在高速公路上。

## 3. **Probation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˈbeɪʃn/
**Definition:** A period of time during which an offender is monitored and must follow specific rules after being released from prison.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 感化期、緩刑期

**Example 1:**
Offenders leaving prison on probation will face strict restrictions on where they can go.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
刑滿獲釋並處於感化期的罪犯，其活動範圍將受到嚴格限制。

**Example 2:**
He is currently on probation for a minor offence committed last year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他因去年犯下的一項輕微罪行，目前正處於感化期。

## 4. **Retaliatory** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈtæliətəri/
**Definition:** Involving an action taken in return for an injury or wrong, especially in trade or war.
**Cant_onese Meaning:** 報復性的

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister announced retaliatory tariffs after trade talks with the US collapsed.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
與美國的貿易談判破裂後，總理宣佈了報復性關稅。

**Example 2:**
The company warned that any further attacks would lead to retaliatory legal action.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司警告稱，任何進一步的攻擊都將導致報復性的法律行動。

## 5. **Miscalculation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌmɪskælkjuˈleɪʃn/
**Definition:** A wrong judgment of a situation or the outcome of an action.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誤算、判斷錯誤

**Example 1:**
The Canadian leader described the new trade tariffs as a serious miscalculation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
加拿大領導人形容新的貿易關稅是一個嚴重的誤算。

**Example 2:**
It was a huge miscalculation to leave the child alone on the mountain.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
把孩子獨自留在山上是一個巨大的判斷錯誤。

## 6. **Spotlight** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈspɒtlaɪt/
**Definition:** To be the focus of public attention or interest.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 焦點、聚光燈

**Example 1:**
The robot horse stole the spotlight at the technology conference this week.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那匹機械馬在本週的科技會議上成了焦點。

**Example 2:**
The investigation has put the company’s safety policies back in the spotlight.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這項調查再次將該公司的安全政策置於公眾關注的焦點之下。
