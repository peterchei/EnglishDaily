# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-14

## 1. **Cross-party** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkrɒs.pɑː.ti/
**Definition:** Involving or representing different political parties working together.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 跨黨派的

**Example 1:**
The cross-party committee was formed to ensure a balanced view on AI regulation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這個跨黨派委員會成立的目的是為了確保對人工智能監管有平衡的看法。

**Example 2:**
There is a cross-party consensus that new laws are needed to protect human rights.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在需要制定新法律以保障人權這一點上，各黨派達成了共識。

## 2. **Stationary** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsteɪ.ʃən.ər.i/
**Definition:** Not moving; still.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 靜止的；不動的

**Example 1:**
The train was stationary when it was struck by the missile.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
火車在被導彈擊中時是靜止不動的。

**Example 2:**
It is dangerous to walk in front of a stationary vehicle if the driver cannot see you.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果司機看不到你，走在靜止的車輛前面是很危險的。

## 3. **Locomotive** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌləʊ.kəˈməʊ.tɪv/
**Definition:** A railway engine that pulls a train.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 火車頭；機車

**Example 1:**
The locomotive was separated from the carriages after the impact.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
撞擊後，火車頭與車廂分離了。

**Example 2:**
Engineers are working to repair the damaged locomotive as quickly as possible.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
工程師們正努力盡快修理受損的火車頭。

## 4. **Warped** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /wɔːpt/
**Definition:** Unreasonable, strange, or distorted in a way that is morally wrong.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 扭曲的；不正常的；乖張的

**Example 1:**
The criminal had a warped sense of justice, thinking he was helping people by stealing.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這個罪犯有一種扭曲的正義感，認為他通過偷竊是在幫助別人。

**Example 2:**
It is hard to understand the warped logic behind such a violent decision.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
很難理解這種暴力決定背後那扭曲的邏輯。

## 5. **Profitable** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprɒf.ɪ.tə.bəl/
**Definition:** Making or likely to make a financial gain.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 有利可圖的；賺錢的

**Example 1:**
The company is looking for new and profitable ventures in the tech sector.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司正在科技領域尋找新的、有利可圖的投資項目。

**Example 2:**
North Korea will only participate if they find the arrangement to be profitable.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果北韓認為這種安排有利可圖，他們才會參與。

## 6. **Pacing** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpeɪ.sɪŋ/
**Definition:** The speed at which something happens or develops.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 節奏；速度控制

**Example 1:**
The pacing of the AI development needs to be carefully monitored by experts.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
人工智能發展的節奏需要由專家謹慎地監控。

**Example 2:**
The pacing of the movie was perfect, keeping the audience engaged until the end.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這部電影的節奏掌握得很好，讓觀眾全程都保持專注。

## 7. **Preliminary** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /prɪˈlɪm.ɪ.nər.i/
**Definition:** Coming before a more important action or event; introductory.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 初步的；預備的

**Example 1:**
The preliminary results show that the opposition bloc is slightly in the lead.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
初步結果顯示反對派聯盟稍微領先。

**Example 2:**
We need to conduct a preliminary study before we spend any money on the project.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在我們為該項目投入資金之前，需要進行一項初步研究。
