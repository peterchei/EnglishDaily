# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-12

## 1. **Derailed** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdiːˈreɪld/
**Definition:** To cause a train to leave its tracks; also used figuratively to mean causing a plan or process to fail.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 出軌；（計劃）被打亂、脫軌

**Example 1:**
Emergency services are working to assist passengers after the passenger train derailed in Normandy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在諾曼第客運火車出軌後，緊急部門正致力於協助乘客。

**Example 2:**
The sudden political scandal completely derailed the government’s plans for the new budget.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
突如其來的政治醜聞徹底打亂了政府關於新預算的計劃。

## 2. **Scrutinise** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskruːtɪnaɪz/
**Definition:** To examine something very closely and thoroughly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 細閱；仔細檢查

**Example 1:**
The public continues to scrutinise the record-breaking donation given to the Reform party.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公眾繼續仔細檢視給予改革黨的那筆破紀錄捐款。

**Example 2:**
Please scrutinise the contract documents before you sign them to ensure everything is correct.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請在簽署合同文件前仔細檢查，以確保一切無誤。

## 3. **Coup** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kuː/
**Definition:** A sudden, violent, or illegal seizure of power, or a highly successful or impressive achievement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 政變；（令人印象深刻的）成功之舉

**Example 1:**
Capturing the strategic coastline was considered a major military coup for the rebel forces.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
奪取這片戰略海岸線被視為叛軍的一大軍事成就。

**Example 2:**
The company’s decision to hire the world-famous director was a marketing coup.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司聘請那位世界著名導演的決定，是一次非常成功的行銷手段。

## 4. **Lingering** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɪŋɡərɪŋ/
**Definition:** Staying in a place or state for a long time; slow to disappear.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 持久的；揮之不去的；逗留的

**Example 1:**
The rescue team struggled with the lingering heat inside the burnt-out ferry.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援隊在燒毀的渡輪內，努力對抗那揮之不去的熱氣。

**Example 2:**
There is still a lingering doubt in her mind about whether she made the right decision.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她心裡對於自己是否做了正確決定，仍存有揮之不去的疑慮。

## 5. **Notorious** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /nəʊˈtɔːriəs/
**Definition:** Famous or well-known, typically for some bad quality or deed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 聲名狼藉的；惡名昭彰的

**Example 1:**
She testified against her father during the trial of the notorious mafia clan.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在那場惡名昭彰的黑手黨家族審判中，她指證了自己的父親。

**Example 2:**
That area of the city is notorious for its high crime rates and lack of safety.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該市的那一區因高犯罪率和治安欠佳而惡名昭彰。

## 6. **Tighten** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtaɪtən/
**Definition:** To make something firmer, or to exercise more control or stricter rules.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 收緊；加強控制

**Example 1:**
The Houthis are attempting to tighten their grip on the vital Red Sea shipping lanes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
胡塞武裝正試圖收緊他們對重要的紅海航道的控制。

**Example 2:**
The government decided to tighten security measures at all major airports following the incident.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
事發後，政府決定收緊所有主要機場的安全措施。

## 7. **Anticipated** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ænˈtɪsɪpeɪtɪd/
**Definition:** Expected or looked forward to, often with excitement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 預期的；備受期待的

**Example 1:**
Gamers are excited to play the hotly anticipated new superhero game from Insomniac.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
玩家們對於能玩到這款由 Insomniac 製作、備受期待的超級英雄遊戲感到興奮。

**Example 2:**
The highly anticipated announcement regarding the new policy is expected tomorrow.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
關於新政策的公告備受期待，預計將於明天發布。
