# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-19

## 1. **Annexation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌan.ekˈseɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The action of taking control of a territory or country, usually by force.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 兼併；吞併

**Example 1:**
The international community condemned the illegal annexation of the border region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際社會譴責咗嗰個邊境地區嘅非法兼併。

**Example 2:**
The threat of annexation caused significant alarm among the local population.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
吞併嘅威脅令到當地居民感到非常恐慌。

## 2. **Ballistic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /bəˈlɪs.tɪk/
**Definition:** To become extremely angry or irrational (often used in the phrase 'go ballistic').
**Cantonese Meaning:** 勃然大怒；發狂

**Example 1:**
My boss went ballistic when he found out about the error in the report.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當我上司發現份報告有錯嗰陣，佢發晒火。

**Example 2:**
He went ballistic after the team lost the match in the final minute.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰支球隊喺最後一分鐘輸咗波之後，佢簡直發晒狂。

## 3. **Credentials** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /krɪˈden.ʃəlz/
**Definition:** Documents or digital data, such as usernames and passwords, that prove a person's or system's identity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 憑證；登入資料；資歷證明

**Example 1:**
The hacker managed to steal the company's login credentials to access the server.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
黑客成功偷取咗公司嘅登入資料，從而入侵咗伺服器。

**Example 2:**
You need to provide your professional credentials before you can apply for the position.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你要提供專業資歷證明，先可以申請呢個職位。

## 4. **Defect** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdiː.fekt/
**Definition:** A physical or mechanical fault, imperfection, or flaw.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 缺陷；瑕疵

**Example 1:**
A software defect was responsible for the widespread disruption of the flight network.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
一個軟件缺陷係導致航班網絡廣泛癱瘓嘅原因。

**Example 2:**
The manufacturer recalled the cars due to a serious safety defect in the brakes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
廠商因為煞車系統有嚴重嘅安全缺陷，而回收嗰批車。

## 5. **Escalated** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈes.kə.leɪ.tɪd/
**Definition:** To increase in intensity, magnitude, or scope.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 升級；惡化

**Example 1:**
The situation escalated quickly after the news of the explosion broke.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
爆炸消息傳出後，局勢好快就升級咗。

**Example 2:**
They escalated their complaints to the management after receiving no response from the support team.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺收唔到客戶支援團隊嘅回覆後，佢哋將投訴升級咗俾管理層處理。

## 6. **Fiery** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfaɪə.ri/
**Definition:** Having a strong, intense, or easily angered character; or involving intense emotion.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 火爆嘅；激烈嘅

**Example 1:**
The president is known for his fiery temperament during press conferences.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統以佢喺記者會上火爆嘅脾氣而聞名。

**Example 2:**
The two politicians had a fiery debate about the new economic policy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
兩位政治人物就新經濟政策進行咗一場激烈嘅辯論。

## 7. **Millisecond** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɪl.iˌsek.ənd/
**Definition:** A unit of time equal to one thousandth of a second.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 毫秒

**Example 1:**
The computer system failed within a millisecond, causing total chaos.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
電腦系統喺一毫秒內故障，導致咗全面嘅混亂。

**Example 2:**
In professional racing, the winner is often decided by a few milliseconds.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺專業賽車入面，勝負往往取決於嗰幾毫秒。
