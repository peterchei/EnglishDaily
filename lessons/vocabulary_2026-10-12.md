# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-12

## 1. **Interfere** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪntəˈfɪə/
**Definition:** To get involved in and try to influence a situation that should not concern you, in a way that is annoying or unwanted.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 干預、介入

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister claimed that foreign countries tried to interfere with the integrity of the Brexit vote.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相聲稱外國試圖干預英國脫歐公投的公正性。

**Example 2:**
Please do not interfere in my personal affairs while I am trying to finish my work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當我正試圖完成工作時，請不要干預我的私事。

## 2. **Encroach** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈkrəʊtʃ/
**Definition:** To gradually take away someone else’s rights, time, or territory in a way that is annoying or harmful.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 侵佔、蠶食

**Example 1:**
The man was arrested after he decided to encroach on the tiger enclosure at the wildlife park.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那名男子在決定侵入野生動物園的老虎圍欄後被捕。

**Example 2:**
Development projects should not encroach upon protected green spaces in the city.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
開發項目不應侵佔城市裡的受保護綠地。

## 3. **Fiscal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɪskl/
**Definition:** Relating to government money, especially taxes and public spending.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 財政的

**Example 1:**
The think tank warned that the ongoing war could create severe fiscal challenges for the government's upcoming budget.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
智庫警告稱，持續的戰爭可能會給政府即將到來的預算帶來嚴峻的財政挑戰。

**Example 2:**
The company is facing a difficult fiscal year due to rising operational costs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於營運成本上升，該公司正面臨艱難的財政年度。

## 4. **Artisanal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɑːtɪˈzænəl/
**Definition:** Made in a traditional way by skilled workers, often on a small scale.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 手工藝的、非工業化的

**Example 1:**
Many people were killed while working at an artisanal mining camp near the border.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多人在邊境附近的一個手工採礦營地工作時喪生。

**Example 2:**
She prefers buying artisanal bread from the local bakery instead of mass-produced loaves from the supermarket.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她喜歡從當地麵包店購買手工麵包，而不是從超市買大量生產的麵包。

## 5. **Credible** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkredəbl/
**Definition:** Able to be believed or trusted; convincing.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 可信的、可靠的

**Example 1:**
The police acted quickly after receiving credible information about a plot to harm the Prime Minister.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在收到關於傷害首相陰謀的可信情報後，警方迅速採取了行動。

**Example 2:**
He provided a credible explanation for why he was late to the meeting.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他對為什麼會議遲到提供了可信的解釋。

## 6. **Malicious** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /məˈlɪʃəs/
**Definition:** Intended to do harm or cause damage to someone or something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 惡意的

**Example 1:**
The King expressed concern about malicious online actors who are constantly evolving their cyber tactics.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國王對那些不斷演變其網絡戰術的惡意網絡行為者表示擔憂。

**Example 2:**
The software was designed to be malicious and secretly steal user data from the computer.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該軟件被設計成惡意的，並會秘密從電腦中竊取用戶數據。

## 7. **Prospector** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprɒspektə/
**Definition:** A person who searches for valuable minerals or resources in the ground.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 勘探者、探礦者

**Example 1:**
Gold prospectors often travel to remote areas in the hope of finding valuable deposits.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
黃金勘探者經常前往偏遠地區，希望能發現有價值的礦藏。

**Example 2:**
The local government is struggling to manage the safety of independent prospectors in the desert.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當地政府難以管理沙漠中獨立勘探者的安全問題。
