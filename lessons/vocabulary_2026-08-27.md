# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-27

## 1. **Reckoning** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrek.ən.ɪŋ/
**Definition:** A time when a person or system is held accountable for their past actions or mistakes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 結算；清算；報應

**Example 1:**
The company’s massive settlement may signal a day of reckoning for the entire social media industry.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家公司鉅額的和解金可能預示著整個社交媒體行業清算之日的到來。

**Example 2:**
After years of ignoring safety protocols, the hospital finally faced a reckoning following the tragic incident.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在多年無視安全準則後，這家醫院在發生這起悲劇後終於面臨了清算。

## 2. **Doomscrolling** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈduːmˌskroʊ.lɪŋ/
**Definition:** The act of continuously scrolling through bad or negative news on a smartphone or computer, even though it is saddening or depressing.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 沉浸式刷負面新聞；末日刷屏

**Example 1:**
I often find myself doomscrolling late at night, reading about global disasters until I feel anxious.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我常發現自己在深夜刷負面新聞，讀著全球各地的災難，直到我感到焦慮。

**Example 2:**
Technology editors warn that constant doomscrolling can have a detrimental effect on your mental health.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
科技編輯警告說，持續地刷負面新聞會對你的心理健康產生負面影響。

## 3. **Hailed** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /heɪld/
**Definition:** To praise or describe someone or something as very good or important.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 讚揚；稱頌；譽為

**Example 1:**
Scientists have hailed the new cancer drug as a major breakthrough in modern medicine.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
科學家們將這種新型抗癌藥物譽為現代醫學的一項重大突破。

**Example 2:**
The witness was hailed as a hero for alerting the staff to the fire.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該名目擊者因提醒員工注意火災而被譽為英雄。

## 4. **Band together** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /bænd təˈɡeð.ər/
**Definition:** To join together as a group in order to achieve something or to support each other.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 團結起來；聯合起來

**Example 1:**
The cyber agents decided to band together to conduct the security test effectively.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
網絡代理們決定團結起來，以有效地進行安全測試。

**Example 2:**
Local communities often band together to help those affected by sudden flash floods.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當地社區經常會團結起來，以幫助那些受突發洪水影響的人們。

## 5. **Game changer** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡeɪm ˌtʃeɪn.dʒər/
**Definition:** An event, idea, or procedure that effects a significant shift in the current way of doing or thinking about something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 改變遊戲規則的事物；突破性的進展

**Example 1:**
This new treatment is considered a game changer for patients suffering from pancreatic cancer.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這種新療法被認為是胰臟癌患者的一大福音，改變了治療的局面。

**Example 2:**
The introduction of artificial intelligence in the workplace is a real game changer for efficiency.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在工作場所引入人工智能，對提高效率來說是一個真正的轉折點。

## 6. **Demolition** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdem.əˈlɪʃ.ən/
**Definition:** The act of destroying or pulling down a building or structure completely.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 拆除；毀壞

**Example 1:**
The officials threatened the demolition of the building if the required renovations were not approved.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
官員們威脅稱，如果所需的翻新工程未獲批准，他們將拆除該建築。

**Example 2:**
The site was cleared for demolition after the old factory was found to be unsafe.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在發現這家舊工廠不安全後，該場地已被清理準備拆除。
