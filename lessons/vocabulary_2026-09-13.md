# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-13

## 1. **Reined in** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /reɪnd ɪn/
**Definition:** To control or limit something that is growing too fast or behaving in an uncontrolled way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 控制；限制

**Example 1:**
The researcher warned that AI development must be reined in before it causes harm.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
研究人員警告說，在人工智能造成傷害之前，必須對其發展加以限制。

**Example 2:**
The government is trying to rein in public spending to balance the budget.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府正試圖控制公共開支以平衡預算。

## 2. **Inquiry** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈkwaɪə.ri/
**Definition:** An official process of discovery or a formal investigation into a matter.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 調查；研訊

**Example 1:**
The Thirlwall inquiry will look into the failures at the hospital.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
瑟爾沃爾研訊將會調查該醫院內部的失職情況。

**Example 2:**
The police launched an inquiry into the cause of the fire.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
警方對火災原因展開了調查。

## 3. **Unified** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈjuː.nɪ.faɪd/
**Definition:** Joined together as a single unit or entity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 統一的

**Example 1:**
Trump expressed his opinion on the possibility of a unified Ireland.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
特朗普表達了他對愛爾蘭統一可能性的看法。

**Example 2:**
The team presented a unified front against the competition.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該團隊對抗競爭對手時展現了統一的立場。

## 4. **Donation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dəʊˈneɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** Money or goods given to help a person or organization, such as a charity or political party.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 捐款；捐贈

**Example 1:**
The political party received a significant donation from a crypto investor.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該政黨收到了一筆來自加密貨幣投資者的巨額捐款。

**Example 2:**
They are asking for a small donation to support the local animal shelter.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們正在請求小額捐款以支持當地的動物收容所。

## 5. **Level playing field** (Idiom)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlev.əl ˈpleɪ.ɪŋ fiːld/
**Definition:** A situation in which everyone has the same fair chance of succeeding.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公平的競爭環境

**Example 1:**
The party wants a level playing field for all candidates in the upcoming election.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該黨希望在即將到來的選舉中，所有候選人都能擁有公平的競爭環境。

**Example 2:**
New regulations were introduced to ensure a level playing field for small businesses.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當局引入了新規定，以確保小型企業擁有公平的競爭環境。

## 6. **Inflict** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈflɪkt/
**Definition:** To force someone to experience something unpleasant or harmful.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 使遭受（打擊、痛苦等）；造成

**Example 1:**
There are concerns that advanced AI models could inflict serious damage on society.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
人們擔心先進的人工智能模型可能會對社會造成嚴重損害。

**Example 2:**
The earthquake inflicted massive destruction on the coastal town.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
地震對沿海小鎮造成了巨大的破壞。

## 7. **Bordering** (Adjective/Participle)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbɔː.dər.ɪŋ/
**Definition:** Sharing a common boundary with another place or country.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 接壤的；鄰近的

**Example 1:**
The attack originated from an area bordering Iran.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這次襲擊源自一個與伊朗接壤的地區。

**Example 2:**
The house is in a quiet neighborhood bordering the national park.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這所房子位於鄰近國家公園的一個安靜社區內。
