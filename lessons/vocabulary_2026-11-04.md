# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-04

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary inspired by recent news stories. Let's get started.

## 1. **Sweep** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /swiːp/
**Definition:** To clear or remove something, often used metaphorically to mean hiding or ignoring something unpleasant.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 清掃；（引申為）掩蓋（醜聞或問題）

**Example 1:**
The government cannot simply sweep the historical scandal under the carpet.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府唔可以就咁將呢單歷史醜聞掃入地毯底（掩蓋）。

**Example 2:**
He tried to sweep his mistakes aside to focus on the future.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢試圖掩蓋佢嘅錯誤，集中精神喺未來。

## 2. **Battleground** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæt.əl.ɡraʊnd/
**Definition:** A place or situation where a conflict or intense competition takes place.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 戰場；競爭激烈嘅地方

**Example 1:**
The state has become a major battleground for the upcoming election.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢個州已經成為即將到嚟選舉嘅主要戰場。

**Example 2:**
The digital market is the new battleground for tech giants.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
數碼市場係科技巨頭嘅新戰場。

## 3. **Bloodbath** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈblʌd.bɑːθ/
**Definition:** A situation of great destruction, loss, or a very heavy defeat.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大屠殺；（引申為）慘敗

**Example 1:**
The stock market experienced a bloodbath after the negative report was released.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
負面報告出咗之後，股市經歷咗一場慘敗。

**Example 2:**
The political party suffered a bloodbath in the local elections.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰個政黨喺地方選舉中遭遇慘敗。

## 4. **Contender** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈten.dər/
**Definition:** A person or group that has a good chance of winning or achieving success.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 競爭者；有力爭奪者

**Example 1:**
She is considered a top contender for the leadership position.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢被認為係呢個領導職位嘅熱門人選。

**Example 2:**
There are several contenders for the championship this year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
今年有幾位冠軍嘅有力爭奪者。

## 5. **Collision** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈlɪʒ.ən/
**Definition:** An accident in which two or more things hit each other with force.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 碰撞；衝突

**Example 1:**
The high-speed collision caused significant damage to the train tracks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢次高速碰撞對火車軌道造成嚴重損壞。

**Example 2:**
Thankfully, no one was injured in the minor collision between the two cars.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
好彩兩架車嘅輕微碰撞中無人受傷。

## 6. **Speculation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌspek.jəˈleɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The forming of a theory or conjecture without firm evidence.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 推測；投機

**Example 1:**
There is a lot of speculation about who will be appointed as the new director.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
關於邊個會被委任為新總監，有好多猜測。

**Example 2:**
She refused to comment on the speculation surrounding her business deal.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢拒絕評論圍繞佢生意協議嘅推測。

## 7. **Legitimate** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ləˈdʒɪt.ɪ.mət/
**Definition:** Allowed by law or rules; reasonable and justifiable.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 合法嘅；合理嘅

**Example 1:**
He was invited to a legitimate job interview through an official platform.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢透過官方平台獲邀去進行一場合法嘅工作面試。

**Example 2:**
Her concerns about the new policy are completely legitimate.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢對新政策嘅擔憂係完全合理嘅。
