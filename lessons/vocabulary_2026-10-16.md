# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-16

## 1. **Metagenomics** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌmɛtəɡəˈnɒmɪks/
**Definition:** The study of genetic material recovered directly from environmental or clinical samples, often used to identify complex infections.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 宏基因組學

**Example 1:**
The hospital is using metagenomics to identify the exact cause of the patient's mystery infection.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
醫院正利用宏基因組學來確定病人神秘感染的確切原因。

**Example 2:**
Metagenomics has become a powerful tool for doctors working in intensive care units.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
對於深切治療部的醫生來說，宏基因組學已成為一種強大的工具。

## 2. **Choking** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtʃəʊkɪŋ/
**Definition:** A sexual activity involving the restriction of someone's breathing, which is being reviewed for new broadcasting regulations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 窒息（此處指性行為中的窒息動作）

**Example 1:**
The new regulations aim to ban adult content that depicts dangerous acts like choking.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新規定旨在禁止描繪如窒息等危險行為的成人內容。

**Example 2:**
Public concern regarding the normalisation of choking in pornography has led to this policy change.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公眾對色情作品中將窒息行為正常化的擔憂，導致了這次政策的改變。

## 3. **Tax raid** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /tæks reɪd/
**Definition:** A sudden or significant increase in taxes imposed by the government, often targeting a specific sector's profits.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 稅務突擊（指政府突然大幅加稅）

**Example 1:**
Banking executives are worried about a potential tax raid on their annual profits in the upcoming Budget.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
銀行高層擔心在即將公布的財政預算案中，他們的年度利潤可能會面臨稅務突擊。

**Example 2:**
The Chancellor is facing pressure to explain if a tax raid is necessary to balance the country's finances.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
財政大臣正面臨解釋壓力，說明是否需要進行稅務突擊來平衡國家財政。

## 4. **Incest** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪnsɛst/
**Definition:** Sexual relations between people who are closely related to each other.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 亂倫

**Example 1:**
The government has confirmed that content featuring incest will be prohibited from November.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府已證實，從十一月起將禁止包含亂倫內容的節目。

**Example 2:**
There is a strict legal and social consensus that incest is unacceptable.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
對於亂倫行為不可接受，法律及社會已達成嚴格共識。

## 5. **Rubbish dump** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrʌbɪʃ dʌmp/
**Definition:** A site for the disposal of waste materials, often associated with unpleasant smells or environmental issues.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 垃圾堆填區

**Example 1:**
Residents living near the rubbish dump have complained about the constant foul odour.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
住在垃圾堆填區附近的居民投訴那裡持續傳出惡臭。

**Example 2:**
New monitoring technology is being installed to track air quality around the local rubbish dump.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當局正安裝新的監測技術，以追蹤當地垃圾堆填區周圍的空氣質量。

## 6. **On the menu** (Idiom)
**Pronunciation:** /ɒn ðə ˈmɛnjuː/
**Definition:** Something that is being considered, planned, or is likely to happen to someone or something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 在考慮範圍內；即將被針對（口語）

**Example 1:**
With the new budget coming, many companies fear they are on the menu for higher taxes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
隨著新預算案即將公布，許多公司擔心自己會成為加稅的目標。

**Example 2:**
The manager suggested that budget cuts might be on the menu for all departments this year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經理暗示今年所有部門都可能面臨削減預算的風險。
