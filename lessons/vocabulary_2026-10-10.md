# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-10

## 1. **Ghoulish** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡuːlɪʃ/
**Definition:** Suggesting the horror of death and decay; morbid or macabre.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 令人毛骨悚然的、像鬼魅般的

**Example 1:**
The film featured a ghoulish scene set in a dark, abandoned graveyard.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
套戲有一幕係喺陰森荒廢嘅墓地拍攝，場面令人毛骨悚然。

**Example 2:**
He took a ghoulish interest in the details of the accident.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢對嗰場意外嘅細節表現出一種病態嘅好奇心。

## 2. **Midterm** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɪdtɜːm/
**Definition:** Refers to elections held in the middle of a president's term, or an exam in the middle of an academic term.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 期中（選舉或考試）

**Example 1:**
The president is concerned about his party’s prospects in the upcoming midterm elections.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統好擔心佢所屬政黨喺嚟緊期中選舉嘅選情。

**Example 2:**
We have our midterm assessments scheduled for next week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋下星期要進行期中評估。

## 3. **Tribunal** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /traɪˈbjuːnəl/
**Definition:** A group of people who are officially chosen to hear and decide a legal disagreement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 法庭、審判委員會

**Example 1:**
The international tribunal was established to investigate war crimes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際法庭成立嘅目的係調查戰爭罪行。

**Example 2:**
She appeared before the employment tribunal to discuss her case.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢出席咗勞資審裁處去處理佢嘅個案。

## 4. **Backlash** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæk.læʃ/
**Definition:** A strong and adverse reaction by a large number of people to a social or political development.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強烈反對、反彈

**Example 1:**
The company faced a massive backlash after releasing the controversial advertisement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
間公司喺發布咗嗰個具爭議性嘅廣告之後，面臨強烈反對。

**Example 2:**
Politicians often fear a public backlash when proposing new tax hikes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政客提出加稅時，往往會好驚公眾反彈。

## 5. **Sustained** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /səˈsteɪnd/
**Definition:** Continuing for a long period of time without interruption.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 持續嘅、長久嘅

**Example 1:**
The project requires sustained effort to ensure its long-term success.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢個計劃需要持續嘅努力，先可以確保長期成功。

**Example 2:**
After a sustained period of rain, the river finally overflowed its banks.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經過持續一段時間嘅落雨，河水終於氾濫。

## 6. **Landfall** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlænd.fɔːl/
**Definition:** The act of arriving on land after a journey by sea or air, often used when a storm hits land.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 登陸（指颱風或風暴）

**Example 1:**
The hurricane is expected to make landfall on the coast early tomorrow morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
預計颶風會喺聽日清晨登陸海岸。

**Example 2:**
Authorities issued warnings hours before the storm made landfall.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當局喺風暴登陸前幾個鐘就發出咗警告。
