# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-27

## 1. **Heinous** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈheɪ.nəs/
**Definition:** Extremely wicked, evil, or morally reprehensible.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 十惡不赦的；極其凶惡的

**Example 1:**
The Chancellor condemned the attack as a heinous act of violence against innocent civilians.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總理譴責這次襲擊是針對無辜平民的極其凶惡的暴力行為。

**Example 2:**
He was sentenced to life imprisonment for his heinous crimes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他因其十惡不赦的罪行被判終身監禁。

## 2. **Ramming** (Noun/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈræm.ɪŋ/
**Definition:** The act of driving a vehicle forcefully into something or someone.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 猛撞；衝撞

**Example 1:**
The police are investigating the motive behind the ramming attack in the city centre.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方正在調查市中心那宗衝撞襲擊背後的動機。

**Example 2:**
The suspect was arrested after ramming his car into a crowded pavement.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
疑犯在駕駛汽車衝撞擁擠的人行道後被捕。

## 3. **Foment** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /fəʊˈment/
**Definition:** To stir up or encourage the growth of something undesirable, such as violence or trouble.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 煽動；挑起（事端）

**Example 1:**
The politician was accused of fomenting social unrest with his inflammatory speeches.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該政治人物被指控利用煽動性的演講來挑起社會動盪。

**Example 2:**
Foreign powers were suspected of trying to foment discord between the two nations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
外界懷疑有外國勢力試圖煽動兩國之間的不和。

## 4. **Outage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈaʊt.ɪdʒ/
**Definition:** A period when a service, such as electricity or an internet network, is not working.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中斷；故障；停電

**Example 1:**
Millions of users were left frustrated following the unexpected network outage this morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
今早發生意外的網絡中斷後，數百萬用戶感到非常沮喪。

**Example 2:**
The company issued an apology for the service outage that affected banking transactions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司就影響銀行交易的服務中斷發出道歉聲明。

## 5. **Parched** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /pɑːtʃt/
**Definition:** Extremely dry, especially because of hot weather.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 極度乾旱的；乾涸的

**Example 1:**
Farmers are worried about their crops dying in the parched soil.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
農民很擔心農作物會在乾旱的土壤中枯死。

**Example 2:**
The heatwave has left the region parched and prone to wildfires.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
熱浪使該地區變得乾旱，並容易引發山火。

## 6. **Resolution** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌrez.əˈluː.ʃən/
**Definition:** A firm decision to do or not to do something, or the act of solving a problem.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 決心；解決方案；決議

**Example 1:**
The committee passed a resolution to improve the safety standards of public buildings.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
委員會通過了一項改善公共建築安全標準的決議。

**Example 2:**
We are working towards a peaceful resolution of the current dispute.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們正致力於和平解決目前的糾紛。
