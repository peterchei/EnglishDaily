# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-17

## 1. **Contempt** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈtɛmpt/
**Definition:** The state of being disobedient to or disrespectful of a court of law.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 藐視（法庭）

**Example 1:**
The billionaire was held in contempt after he refused to provide the requested documents.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位億萬富翁因為拒絕提供所需文件，被裁定藐視法庭。

**Example 2:**
She showed complete contempt for the rules of the inquiry.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢對調查委員會嘅規則表現出完全嘅藐視。

## 2. **Hike** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /haɪk/
**Definition:** A sharp increase in prices, costs, or interest rates.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （價格、利率等）大幅調升

**Example 1:**
The central bank decided to hike interest rates to control rising inflation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
中央銀行決定大幅調升利率以控制日益嚴重嘅通脹。

**Example 2:**
Commuters are angry about the sudden hike in train ticket prices.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
乘客對於火車票價突然大幅調升感到憤怒。

## 3. **Misalignment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌmɪsəˈlaɪnmənt/
**Definition:** The state of being incorrectly positioned or having goals that do not match the intended purpose.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 排列不當、偏差、目標不一致

**Example 1:**
The company is investigating a potential misalignment between its AI models and safety guidelines.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司正調查其人工智能模型與安全準則之間可能存在嘅偏差。

**Example 2:**
The political crisis stemmed from a clear misalignment of the two parties' long-term visions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢場政治危機源於兩黨長期願景嘅明顯不一致。

## 4. **Relentless** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈlɛntləs/
**Definition:** Continuing in an intense or extreme way without stopping.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 持續不斷嘅、無情嘅

**Example 1:**
The relentless rain has caused flooding in many parts of the country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
持續不斷嘅大雨導致全國多處地區水浸。

**Example 2:**
She worked at a relentless pace to meet the project deadline.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢以持續不斷嘅節奏工作，以趕上項目截止日期。

## 5. **Backlash** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæklæʃ/
**Definition:** A strong and adverse reaction by a large number of people to a decision or development.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強烈反對、負面反應

**Example 1:**
The singer faced a massive backlash after his controversial comments on social media.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位歌手因為喺社交媒體上嘅爭議性言論，面臨強烈反對。

**Example 2:**
The government is bracing for a public backlash if they cut funding for the health service.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果政府削減醫療服務資金，佢哋正準備應對公眾嘅強烈反對。

## 6. **Disclose** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈkləʊz/
**Definition:** To make secret or new information known to the public.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公開、披露

**Example 1:**
The company has promised to disclose all future safety incidents to the public.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰間公司承諾會向公眾披露未來所有嘅安全事故。

**Example 2:**
He refused to disclose the source of his funding during the interview.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺訪問期間，佢拒絕披露佢嘅資金來源。
