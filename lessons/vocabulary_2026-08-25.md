# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-25

## 1. **Blueprints** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbluː.prɪnts/
**Definition:** Detailed technical drawings or plans for a project or machine.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 藍圖 / 詳細計劃書

**Example 1:**
The engineers are currently finalizing the blueprints for the new housing estate.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
工程師們目前正在為新的住宅區敲定藍圖。

**Example 2:**
The government handed over the missile component blueprints to their allies.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府將導彈零件的藍圖移交給了他們的盟友。

## 2. **Reprehensible** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌrep.rɪˈhen.sə.bəl/
**Definition:** Morally unacceptable and deserving of strong criticism.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 應受譴責的 / 非常不道德的

**Example 1:**
Sharing videos of dangerous driving on public roads is truly reprehensible.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在公路上分享危險駕駛的影片是極其應受譴責的。

**Example 2:**
The company’s decision to ignore safety warnings was deemed reprehensible by the regulators.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司無視安全警告的決定被監管機構認為是應受譴責的。

## 3. **Marina** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /məˈriː.nə/
**Definition:** A specially designed harbour with moorings for pleasure yachts and small boats.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 遊艇碼頭

**Example 1:**
The body of the man was discovered near a marina in Auckland.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那名男子的遺體是在奧克蘭的一個遊艇碼頭附近被發現的。

**Example 2:**
We enjoyed a lovely lunch at a restaurant overlooking the local marina.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們在一家俯瞰當地遊艇碼頭的餐廳享用了美妙的午餐。

## 4. **Offensive** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈfen.sɪv/
**Definition:** A highly organized and active military or economic campaign.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 攻勢 / 進攻行動

**Example 1:**
Iran is bracing for what the US has described as its greatest financial offensive ever.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
伊朗正在為美國所描述的史上最強金融攻勢做準備。

**Example 2:**
The army launched a new offensive to regain control of the territory.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
軍隊發動了新一輪攻勢以重新奪回領土的控制權。

## 5. **Salt flats** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsɒlt ˌflæts/
**Definition:** A flat expanse of salt crust in a desert region, typically where a lake has evaporated.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 鹽灘 / 鹽沼

**Example 1:**
The tourist unfortunately died after his car became trapped in the salt flats during extreme heat.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那名遊客不幸在極端高溫下，因車輛困在鹽灘中而死亡。

**Example 2:**
The landscape of the salt flats is breathtakingly white and vast.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
鹽灘的景色呈現出一片令人驚嘆的白色與廣闊。

## 6. **Scaling up** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskeɪ.lɪŋ ʌp/
**Definition:** To increase the size, amount, or importance of an activity or process.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 擴大規模 / 提升產能

**Example 1:**
Factory owners are scaling up their use of robotics to improve efficiency.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
工廠老闆們正在擴大機器人的使用規模以提高效率。

**Example 2:**
We need to scale up our efforts if we want to finish the project by next week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果我們想在下週前完成項目，就必須擴大我們的努力。
