# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-11

## 1. **Pugnacious** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /pʌɡˈneɪ.ʃəs/
**Definition:** Having a strong desire to argue or fight.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 好鬥嘅，愛爭論嘅

**Example 1:**
The politician was known for her pugnacious style during parliamentary debates.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位政客喺國會辯論期間以佢好鬥嘅風格而聞名。

**Example 2:**
He took a pugnacious tone when the journalist asked him about his past mistakes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當記者問佢關於過往錯誤時，佢採取咗一種好鬥嘅語氣。

## 2. **Charismatic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌkær.ɪzˈmæt.ɪk/
**Definition:** Possessing a powerful personal quality that attracts, influences, and fascinates others.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 有領袖魅力嘅，有感召力嘅

**Example 1:**
She is a charismatic leader who can motivate an entire team with just a few words.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢係一位充滿魅力嘅領袖，只需幾句話就能夠激勵成個團隊。

**Example 2:**
The actor’s charismatic personality made him a favourite among the audience.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢位演員充滿魅力嘅個性令佢深得觀眾喜愛。

## 3. **Bumpy** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbʌm.pi/
**Definition:** Not smooth; having many problems or difficulties.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 崎嶇嘅，充滿波折嘅

**Example 1:**
The project had a bumpy start, but things are much more stable now.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢個計劃開頭充滿波折，但依家已經穩定好多。

**Example 2:**
It has been a bumpy week for the company due to unexpected market changes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於市場出現預料之外嘅變化，公司呢個禮拜過得幾崎嶇。

## 4. **Groomed** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɡruːmd/
**Definition:** To prepare someone for a particular job or activity by teaching them what they need to know.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 培養，培訓（以勝任某職位）

**Example 1:**
He was carefully groomed by the senior partners to take over the firm.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢由資深合夥人悉心培養，準備接手呢間公司。

**Example 2:**
She has been groomed for a high-level executive position for several years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢已經被培養去擔任高層行政職位好幾年喇。

## 5. **Backlash** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæk.læʃ/
**Definition:** A strong and negative reaction by a large number of people to a change or event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強烈反對，反彈

**Example 1:**
The government faced a massive backlash after announcing the new tax policy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府喺宣布新稅務政策後，面臨巨大嘅強烈反對。

**Example 2:**
There was a significant social media backlash against the company’s recent controversial ad.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢間公司最近嘅爭議性廣告引發咗顯著嘅社交媒體反彈。

## 6. **Landslide** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlænd.slaɪd/
**Definition:** A mass of rock and earth moving suddenly and quickly down a steep slope.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 山泥傾瀉，滑坡

**Example 1:**
Heavy rains in the region have increased the risk of dangerous landslides.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該地區嘅連場大雨增加咗發生危險山泥傾瀉嘅風險。

**Example 2:**
The road was blocked for hours following a landslide caused by the typhoon.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
颱風引發山泥傾瀉後，道路被封鎖咗幾個鐘。
