# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-17

## 1. **Mourn** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /mɔːn/
**Definition:** To feel or show great sadness because someone has died.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 哀悼

**Example 1:**
Fans across the world continue to mourn the sudden loss of the talented young actress.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
世界各地嘅粉絲繼續哀悼呢位天才年輕女演員嘅突然離世。

**Example 2:**
The community gathered in the town square to mourn the victims of the tragic motorway accident.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
社區居民喺市中心廣場聚集，哀悼嗰場悲慘高速公路事故嘅受害者。

## 2. **Devastating** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdevəsteɪtɪŋ/
**Definition:** Causing severe shock, distress, or damage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 毀滅性嘅；令人極度傷心嘅

**Example 1:**
The families received the devastating news that their loved ones had been killed in the collision.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
啲家屬收到嗰個令人極度傷心嘅消息，話佢哋嘅至親喺車禍中喪生。

**Example 2:**
The wildfire had a devastating impact on the local ecosystem and wildlife habitats.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
山火對當地生態系統同野生動物棲息地造成咗毀滅性嘅影響。

## 3. **Protracted** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˈtræktɪd/
**Definition:** Lasting for a long time, especially longer than expected or desirable.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 拖延嘅；時間過長嘅

**Example 1:**
Peace negotiators are worried that the talks will become a protracted process with no clear conclusion.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
和平談判代表擔心呢場談判會變成一場曠日持久且冇明確結論嘅過程。

**Example 2:**
After a protracted legal battle, the court finally ruled in favour of the small business owner.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經過一場拖延已久嘅法律訴訟，法庭最終判呢位小店主勝訴。

## 4. **Fatal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfeɪtəl/
**Definition:** Causing or ending in death.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 致命嘅

**Example 1:**
Police are investigating the circumstances that led to the fatal crash early Sunday morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方正喺度調查星期日凌晨導致嗰場致命車禍嘅情況。

**Example 2:**
The virus can be fatal if the patient does not receive medical treatment immediately.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果病人冇即時接受治療，呢種病毒可能會係致命嘅。

## 5. **Downsize** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdaʊnsaɪz/
**Definition:** To reduce the size or quantity of something, often used in military or business contexts.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 縮減規模；裁減

**Example 1:**
The government decided to downsize the military exercises to improve diplomatic relations with neighbouring countries.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府決定縮減軍事演習規模，以改善同鄰國嘅外交關係。

**Example 2:**
Many companies choose to downsize their office space as more employees opt to work from home.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於越來越多員工選擇在家工作，好多公司都選擇縮減辦公室規模。

## 6. **Mitigate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɪtɪɡeɪt/
**Definition:** To make something less severe, serious, or painful.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 緩解；減輕

**Example 1:**
The council is planning new drainage systems to mitigate the risk of flooding during the rainy season.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
市政廳正計劃建立新嘅排水系統，以緩解雨季期間嘅水浸風險。

**Example 2:**
Wearing sunscreen is an effective way to mitigate the damage caused by prolonged exposure to the sun.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
塗防曬霜係減輕長期曝曬所造成傷害嘅有效方法。
