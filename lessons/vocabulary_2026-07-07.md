# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-07

Hi there! Here are 6 useful words and phrases for today's lesson, curated from the latest news headlines.

## 1. **Interceptor** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪntəseptə/
**Definition:** A weapon, typically a missile, designed to detect and destroy an incoming enemy projectile or aircraft.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 攔截導彈 / 攔截機

**Example 1:**
The president requested more interceptor missiles to protect the city from air attacks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統要求更多攔截導彈以保護城市免受空襲。

**Example 2:**
Our air defence network relies on advanced interceptors to neutralise threats in the sky.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們的防空網絡依賴先進的攔截導彈來消除天空中的威脅。

## 2. **Verdict** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvɜːdɪkt/
**Definition:** A decision or judgment made by a jury or judge in a court of law.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 判決 / 裁決

**Example 1:**
The public is waiting to hear the court verdict on the high-profile legal battle today.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公眾正等待今天這場備受矚目的法律訴訟的裁決。

**Example 2:**
After weeks of testimony, the judge finally delivered the verdict.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經過幾週的作證，法官終於做出了裁決。

## 3. **Engagements** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈɡeɪdʒmənts/
**Definition:** Scheduled appointments or arrangements, especially official or professional ones.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公務行程 / 約會

**Example 1:**
The Duke has a busy week of charity engagements across the country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公爵這週在全國各地有繁忙的慈善公務行程。

**Example 2:**
I am afraid I cannot meet you then as I have prior professional engagements.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
恐怕我那時候沒辦法見你，因為我已經有了預定的公務安排。

## 4. **Sweeping** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈswiːpɪŋ/
**Definition:** Wide-ranging or having a broad effect; extensive.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 全面的 / 廣泛的 / 大規模的

**Example 1:**
Microsoft announced sweeping changes to its workforce, leading to thousands of redundancies.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
微軟宣佈了對其員工隊伍的全面調整，導致數千人被裁員。

**Example 2:**
The government plans to introduce sweeping reforms to the education system next year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府計劃在明年對教育系統進行全面改革。

## 5. **Spectacle** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈspektəkl/
**Definition:** A visually striking performance or public display, often on a large scale.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 場面 / 奇觀 / 公開展示

**Example 1:**
The state funeral was a grand political spectacle intended to showcase the nation's unity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國葬是一場宏大的政治場面，旨在展示國家的團結。

**Example 2:**
The fireworks display was a dazzling spectacle that attracted thousands of tourists.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
煙火表演是一場令人眼花繚亂的奇觀，吸引了成千上萬的遊客。

## 6. **Make light work of** (Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /meɪk laɪt wɜːk əv/
**Definition:** To accomplish a task very easily and quickly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 輕而易舉地完成 / 輕鬆解決

**Example 1:**
The team made light work of their opponents in the quarter-final match.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這支隊伍在半準決賽中輕而易舉地擊敗了對手。

**Example 2:**
With the right tools, you can make light work of these house repairs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
有了合適的工具，你可以輕鬆完成這些房屋維修工作。
