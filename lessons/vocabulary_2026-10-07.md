# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-07

Hello everyone! Let's look at some useful vocabulary from today's headlines.

## 1. **Fabricated** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfæb.rɪ.keɪ.tɪd/
**Definition:** Invented or made up; not true, especially in order to deceive someone.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 捏造的；虛構的

**Example 1:**
The gang was caught providing fabricated evidence to help migrants gain asylum.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該犯罪集團被發現提供捏造的證據，以幫助移民獲得庇護。

**Example 2:**
He was dismissed from his job after it was revealed he had submitted a fabricated report.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在被揭發提交了捏造的報告後，他被解僱了。

## 2. **Espionage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈes.pi.ə.nɑːʒ/
**Definition:** The activity of secretly collecting information about another country, organization, or person.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 間諜活動；情報工作

**Example 1:**
A former German spy chief has been arrested on charges of espionage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
一名德國前情報首長因間諜罪被捕。

**Example 2:**
Industrial espionage is a growing concern for companies dealing with sensitive technology.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
商業間諜活動是處理敏感技術的公司日益關注的問題。

## 3. **Treason** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtriː.zən/
**Definition:** The crime of betraying one's country, especially by attempting to kill the ruler or overthrow the government.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 叛國罪

**Example 1:**
The official was accused of treason for allegedly handing over classified documents to a foreign power.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該官員因涉嫌將機密文件交給外國勢力而被控叛國罪。

**Example 2:**
In many legal systems, treason is considered one of the most serious offences possible.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在許多法律體系中，叛國罪被認為是最嚴重的罪行之一。

## 4. **Repression** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈpreʃ.ən/
**Definition:** The use of force or violence to control a group of people and restrict their freedom.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 鎮壓；壓制

**Example 1:**
Protesters in France took to the streets to demonstrate against what they called police repression.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法國的抗議者走上街頭，抗議他們所稱的警察鎮壓。

**Example 2:**
The government’s brutal repression of dissent led to international condemnation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府對異議人士的殘酷鎮壓招致了國際社會的譴責。

## 5. **Cleric** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkler.ɪk/
**Definition:** A religious leader, such as a priest or bishop, especially in the Christian church.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 神職人員；牧師或主教

**Example 1:**
A senior Catholic cleric has been sentenced to prison for historical sex abuse charges.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
一位資深天主教神職人員因過去的性虐待指控被判入獄。

**Example 2:**
The local cleric played a major role in organizing the community's charity event.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這位當地神職人員在組織社區慈善活動中發揮了重要作用。

## 6. **Unauthorised** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ʌnˈɔː.θər.aɪzd/
**Definition:** Having no official permission or approval.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 未經授權的；未經許可的

**Example 1:**
Asos apologized after an unauthorised notification was sent to app users.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在向應用程式用戶發送了未經授權的通知後，Asos 作出了道歉。

**Example 2:**
Access to the restricted area is strictly forbidden without an unauthorised security pass.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
未經許可，嚴禁進入該限制區域。

## 7. **Reframing** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːˈfreɪ.mɪŋ/
**Definition:** Changing the way that something is presented or viewed by others.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重新構建；改變……的觀點

**Example 1:**
The Tory leader is attempting to reframe the public's perception of her party.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
保守黨領袖正試圖重新構建公眾對她所屬政黨的看法。

**Example 2:**
We need to start by reframing the problem so that we can find a better solution.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們需要先重新構建這個問題，以便找到更好的解決方案。
