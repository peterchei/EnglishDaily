# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-27

## 1. **Pivotal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpɪv.ə.təl/
**Definition:** Of crucial importance in relation to the development or success of something else.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 關鍵的，至關重要的

**Example 1:**
The election is at a pivotal moment for the country's future.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這場選舉對國家未來而言處於一個關鍵時刻。

**Example 2:**
Her support was pivotal to the success of our new business project.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她的支持對我們新商業項目的成功至關重要。

## 2. **Wrangling** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈræŋ.ɡlɪŋ/
**Definition:** Long, complicated, and often angry arguments or disputes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 爭吵，口角，複雜的爭論

**Example 1:**
After weeks of political wrangling, the committee finally reached a decision.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
經過數週的政治爭論，委員會最終達成了一項決定。

**Example 2:**
The contract was delayed by legal wrangling over the fine print.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該合同因關於細則的法律爭論而被延誤。

## 3. **Staple** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsteɪ.pəl/
**Definition:** A basic or important food or product that is used frequently.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 主食，必需品

**Example 1:**
Rice is a staple in many Asian households.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
米飯是許多亞洲家庭的主食。

**Example 2:**
The chancellor plans to reduce taxes on household staples to help families.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
財政大臣計劃降低家庭必需品的稅收以幫助各個家庭。

## 4. **Breathing space** (Noun Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbriː.ðɪŋ ˌspeɪs/
**Definition:** Time or a situation in which you can relax or catch your breath, especially after being very busy or under pressure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 喘息的空間，緩衝時間

**Example 1:**
The new tax cuts will give struggling families some much-needed breathing space.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新的減稅政策將為困難家庭提供急需的喘息空間。

**Example 2:**
We need a little breathing space before we start the next phase of the project.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在開始項目的下一階段前，我們需要一點喘息的空間。

## 5. **Incumbent** (Adjective/Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈkʌm.bənt/
**Definition:** Currently holding an office or official position.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 現任的，在職者

**Example 1:**
The incumbent president won the election by a narrow margin.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
現任總統以微弱優勢贏得了選舉。

**Example 2:**
It is often difficult to defeat an incumbent in local government elections.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在地方政府選舉中，擊敗現任官員往往很困難。

## 6. **Prospectus** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˈspek.təs/
**Definition:** A document describing a company or investment opportunity, usually issued by a business that is about to start trading shares publicly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 招股說明書，簡介

**Example 1:**
Investors carefully read the IPO prospectus before buying shares in the company.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
投資者在購買該公司股票前，認真閱讀了首次公開募股的招股說明書。

**Example 2:**
The college prospectus provides detailed information about all the available courses.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學院簡介提供了所有可用課程的詳細資訊。

## 7. **Foldable** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfəʊl.də.bəl/
**Definition:** Able to be bent or folded so that it becomes smaller or flatter.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 可摺疊的

**Example 1:**
The company is launching its first foldable smartphone next month.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司下個月將推出其首款可摺疊智慧型手機。

**Example 2:**
We bought a foldable table to save space in our small apartment.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們買了一張可摺疊桌子，以便在我們的小公寓裡節省空間。
