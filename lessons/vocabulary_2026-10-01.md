# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-01

## 1. **Syringe** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsɪr.ɪndʒ/
**Definition:** A medical instrument used for injecting liquid into or withdrawing liquid from the body.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 注射器，針筒

**Example 1:**
The nurse carefully prepared the syringe before the injection.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
護士喺打針之前小心翼翼咁準備好支針筒。

**Example 2:**
Medical staff used a syringe to administer the necessary medication.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醫護人員用注射器嚟幫病人用藥。

## 2. **Broadcaster** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbrɔːd.kɑː.stər/
**Definition:** A person whose job is presenting or talking on television or radio programmes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 廣播員，播音員

**Example 1:**
The veteran broadcaster was admired for her clear and engaging presenting style.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位資深廣播員因為佢清晰同吸引人嘅主持風格而備受讚賞。

**Example 2:**
Many people grew up listening to him as a household name broadcaster.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
好多人都係聽住呢位家喻戶曉嘅廣播員大。

## 3. **Motive** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈməʊ.tɪv/
**Definition:** A reason for doing something, especially one that is hidden or not obvious.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 動機，目的

**Example 1:**
The police are still trying to determine the motive behind the attack.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方仍然試緊去確認呢次襲擊背後嘅動機。

**Example 2:**
He had no clear motive for leaving his job so suddenly.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢冇咩明顯嘅動機要咁突然辭職。

## 4. **Careers** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈrɪəz/
**Definition:** The series of jobs that a person has in a particular area of work throughout their life.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 事業，職業生涯

**Example 1:**
Schools are increasingly focusing on preparing students for their future careers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
學校依家越嚟越注重幫學生為未來嘅職業生涯做準備。

**Example 2:**
She has had a successful career in the finance industry for over twenty years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢喺金融界有超過二十年嘅成功職業生涯。

## 5. **Ice loss** (Noun phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈaɪs ˌlɒs/
**Definition:** The reduction in the total volume or mass of ice, typically referring to glaciers or polar regions due to warming.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 冰層流失，冰量減少

**Example 1:**
The report highlights the alarming rate of ice loss in the region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
份報告強調咗該地區冰層流失嘅驚人速度。

**Example 2:**
Continued ice loss contributes significantly to rising sea levels.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
持續嘅冰層流失對海平面上升有重大影響。

## 6. **Backlash** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbæk.læʃ/
**Definition:** A strong, negative reaction by a large number of people to a change or event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強烈反對，抵制

**Example 1:**
The company faced a massive backlash after the controversial advertisement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
間公司喺嗰個具爭議性嘅廣告出街之後，面臨強烈嘅公眾反對。

**Example 2:**
The politician ignored the public backlash and stuck to his original plan.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰位政客無理會公眾嘅強烈反對，堅持佢原本嘅計劃。
