# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-29

## 1. **Impasse** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪm.pæs/
**Definition:** A situation in which no progress is possible, especially because of disagreement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 僵局

**Example 1:**
The negotiations have reached an impasse as neither side is willing to compromise.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
談判陷入了僵局，因為雙方都不願讓步。

**Example 2:**
The political leaders are trying to find a way to break the current impasse.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政治領袖正試圖尋找打破目前僵局的方法。

## 2. **Verification** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌver.ɪ.fɪˈkeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The process of establishing the truth, accuracy, or validity of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 核實 / 驗證

**Example 1:**
Social media platforms are introducing age verification to protect younger users.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
社交媒體平台正在引入年齡驗證，以保護年輕用戶。

**Example 2:**
Please provide your passport for identity verification purposes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請提供您的護照以進行身份核實。

## 3. **Persecution** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌpɜː.sɪˈkjuː.ʃən/
**Definition:** Hostility and ill-treatment, especially because of race, political, or religious beliefs.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 迫害

**Example 1:**
The candidate claimed he was fleeing his country to escape political persecution.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該候選人聲稱他逃離自己的國家是為了躲避政治迫害。

**Example 2:**
Many refugees have suffered severe persecution in their home countries.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多難民在他們的祖國遭受過嚴重的迫害。

## 4. **Dazed** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /deɪzd/
**Definition:** Unable to think clearly or react normally, usually because of a shock or a blow to the head.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 茫然的 / 頭昏眼花的

**Example 1:**
After the accident, the passengers climbed out of the train looking dazed and confused.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
事故發生後，乘客們爬出火車，看起來一臉茫然和困惑。

**Example 2:**
He sat in a dazed state for several minutes after hearing the bad news.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
聽到壞消息後，他茫然地坐了幾分鐘。

## 5. **Reform** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈfɔːm/
**Definition:** The improvement or amendment of what is wrong, corrupt, or unsatisfactory.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 改革

**Example 1:**
The government has promised significant reform to the national welfare system.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府已承諾對國家福利制度進行重大改革。

**Example 2:**
There is a growing public demand for tax reform to help lower earners.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公眾對稅制改革的要求日益增加，以幫助低收入者。

## 6. **Leak** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /liːk/
**Definition:** To intentionally disclose secret information.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 洩露

**Example 1:**
Personal details of thousands of staff were leaked online after a major security hack.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在一次嚴重的網絡入侵後，數千名員工的個人資料在網上被洩露。

**Example 2:**
It is illegal to leak confidential government documents to the press.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
向媒體洩露政府機密文件是違法的。
