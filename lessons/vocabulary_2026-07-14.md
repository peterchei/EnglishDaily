# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-14

## 1. **Brazen** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbreɪ.zən/
**Definition:** Bold and without shame.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 厚顏無恥的；明目張膽的

**Example 1:**
The attackers carried out a brazen raid on the tankers in broad daylight.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
襲擊者在光天化日之下對油輪進行了明目張膽的突襲。

**Example 2:**
He had the brazen audacity to lie to the police during the investigation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他竟然有膽量在調查過程中向警方撒謊，真是厚顏無恥。

## 2. **Jog** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dʒɒɡ/
**Definition:** To cause someone to remember something; to stimulate the memory.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 喚醒（記憶）；刺激（回憶）

**Example 1:**
The police released old photographs to see if they could jog the public's memory.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方發布了舊照片，看看能否喚醒公眾的記憶。

**Example 2:**
Looking at his childhood photos helped jog his memory about that trip.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
看著他童年的照片，有助於喚醒他對那次旅行的記憶。

## 3. **Lapses** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlæps.ɪz/
**Definition:** Small errors or failures in judgment, memory, or procedure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 失誤；疏忽；過失

**Example 1:**
The inquiry investigated safety lapses that led to the tragic fire at the bar.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
調查委員會審查了導致酒吧發生悲劇火災的安全疏忽。

**Example 2:**
Even small lapses in concentration can cause serious accidents at work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
即使是工作時注意力的小小疏忽，也可能導致嚴重的事故。

## 4. **Void** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /vɔɪd/
**Definition:** To declare that something is not legally binding or valid.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 使無效；廢止

**Example 1:**
The judge decided to void the agreement because it was signed under false pretenses.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法官決定廢止該協議，因為它是以欺騙手段簽署的。

**Example 2:**
If you make any changes to the document, you will void the contract.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果你對文件進行任何更改，就會使合約失效。

## 5. **Signage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsaɪ.nɪdʒ/
**Definition:** Signs collectively, especially those in a public place.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 指示牌；標誌系統

**Example 1:**
The building was heavily criticised for having poor signage near the emergency exits.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該建築物因緊急出口附近的指示牌不足而受到嚴厲批評。

**Example 2:**
Better signage would help tourists navigate the underground station more easily.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
更好的指示牌能幫助遊客更輕鬆地在地下鐵站找到方向。

## 6. **Undocumented** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnˈdɒk.jə.ment.ɪd/
**Definition:** Without the official documents needed to legally enter or stay in a country.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 無證件的；非法居留的

**Example 1:**
The government is debating new policies regarding undocumented migrants.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在討論有關無證移民的新政策。

**Example 2:**
Many undocumented workers struggle to access basic healthcare services.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多無證勞工在獲取基本醫療服務方面遇到困難。
