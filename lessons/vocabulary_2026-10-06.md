# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-06

## 1. **Incursion** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈkɜːr.ʒən/
**Definition:** An instance of entering a place or territory with hostile intent, often referring to military or physical entry.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 入侵；闖入

**Example 1:**
Nato members have expressed serious concern regarding the recent Russian drone incursion.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
北約成員國對最近的俄羅斯無人機入侵事件表示嚴重擔憂。

**Example 2:**
The company’s security team was alerted to an unauthorised incursion into the data centre.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司的保安團隊收到警報，指有人未經授權闖入了數據中心。

## 2. **Evacuation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˌvæk.juˈeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The process of removing people or things from a place of danger to a safer place.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 撤離；疏散

**Example 1:**
The mass evacuation of aircraft from the base was completed within twenty-four hours.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
從基地進行的大規模飛機撤離行動在二十四小時內完成。

**Example 2:**
The fire alarm triggered an immediate evacuation of the shopping centre.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
火警鐘響起，購物中心立即進行了疏散。

## 3. **Blasphemy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈblæs.fə.mi/
**Definition:** The act or offence of speaking sacrilegiously about God or sacred things.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 褻瀆；瀆神

**Example 1:**
He was sentenced to a severe penalty after being found guilty of blasphemy on social media.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他在社交媒體上被裁定犯有褻瀆罪後，被判處嚴厲的刑罰。

**Example 2:**
Many international organisations have campaigned against laws that criminalise blasphemy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多國際組織一直在開展運動，反對將褻瀆行為定為刑事犯罪的法律。

## 4. **Referendum** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌref.əˈren.dəm/
**Definition:** A general vote by the electorate on a single political question which has been referred to them for a direct decision.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 全民公投

**Example 1:**
The party promised to hold an independence referendum if they were elected to power.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該黨承諾，如果他們當選執政，將會舉行獨立公投。

**Example 2:**
The result of the referendum will decide the future direction of the country’s trade policy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公投結果將決定該國貿易政策的未來走向。

## 5. **Guardrail** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡɑːd.reɪl/
**Definition:** A system or policy designed to prevent something from going out of control or to provide protection; often used in the context of technology.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 防護欄；（比喻）安全防範機制/限制

**Example 1:**
The tech firm refused to remove the safety guardrails from their latest AI software.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家科技公司拒絕移除其最新人工智能軟件中的安全防範機制。

**Example 2:**
We need to establish clear guardrails to ensure the project stays within its budget.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們需要建立明確的制約機制，以確保項目保持在預算範圍內。

## 6. **Taskforce** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɑːsk.fɔːs/
**Definition:** A group of people organized for a specific purpose or to deal with a specific problem.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 專責小組；特別行動小組

**Example 1:**
The president has appointed a new AI taskforce to address growing concerns about technology.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統任命了一個新的人工智能專責小組，以應對日益增長的技術擔憂。

**Example 2:**
A government taskforce was set up to investigate the collapse of the local manufacturing industry.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府成立了一個專責小組，調查當地製造業的衰退情況。
