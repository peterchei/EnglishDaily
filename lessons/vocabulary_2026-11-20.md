# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-20

## 1. **Summit** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsʌmɪt/
**Definition:** A high-level meeting between leaders or heads of government to discuss important issues.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 峰會；高峰會議

**Example 1:**
The UK and EU leaders are meeting at the summit this Friday to discuss youth mobility.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英國同歐盟領袖今個星期五會喺峰會見面，討論青年流動計劃。

**Example 2:**
Diplomats prepared for the international summit to ensure the talks go smoothly.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
外交官為呢場國際峰會做好準備，以確保會談順利進行。

## 2. **Mobility** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /məʊˈbɪləti/
**Definition:** The ability to move between different places, jobs, or social classes, often used in the context of international travel or work permits.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 流動性；移動能力

**Example 1:**
The new youth mobility scheme will allow thousands of young people to work abroad.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新嘅青年流動計劃將會容許成千上萬嘅年輕人出國工作。

**Example 2:**
Improving social mobility is a key goal for the government’s new education policy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
提升社會流動性係政府新教育政策嘅一個主要目標。

## 3. **Tumour** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtjuːmə/
**Definition:** A mass of abnormal cells that grow in the body, which can be cancerous or non-cancerous.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 腫瘤

**Example 1:**
The clinical trial showed that the new pill could help shrink the patient's tumour.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
臨床試驗顯示，呢隻新藥丸可以幫助縮小病人嘅腫瘤。

**Example 2:**
Early detection is vital for the successful removal of a malignant tumour.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
及早發現對於成功切除惡性腫瘤至關重要。

## 4. **Besieged** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /bɪˈsiːdʒd/
**Definition:** Surrounded by armed forces aiming to capture a city or force its surrender.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被圍困嘅；受圍攻嘅

**Example 1:**
People living in the besieged city are struggling to find enough food and water.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
住喺被圍困城市嘅人，正努力搵足夠嘅食物同水。

**Example 2:**
Journalists found it incredibly difficult to report from the besieged area safely.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
記者覺得喺被圍困地區安全地進行採訪係極之困難。

## 5. **Entertainment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌentəˈteɪnmənt/
**Definition:** The action of providing or being provided with amusement or enjoyment.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 娛樂

**Example 1:**
The latest video game launch is being called the biggest event in entertainment history.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
最新嘅電子遊戲發布被稱為娛樂史上最大型嘅盛事。

**Example 2:**
We are looking for some light entertainment after a long week of work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經過忙碌嘅一週工作後，我哋想搵啲輕鬆嘅娛樂。

## 6. **Genocide** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdʒenəsaɪd/
**Definition:** The deliberate killing of a large group of people, especially those of a particular ethnic group or nation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 種族滅絕

**Example 1:**
The court ruled that the military actions constituted genocide against the minority group.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法庭裁定軍事行動構成對該少數族裔嘅種族滅絕。

**Example 2:**
International leaders have condemned the acts of genocide reported in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
國際領袖已經譴責該地區所報告嘅種族滅絕行為。

## 7. **Debut** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdeɪbjuː/
**Definition:** A person's or company's first appearance in a particular capacity, such as a company listing on the stock market.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 首度亮相；首次登場（常用於股市上市）

**Example 1:**
The AI company made an impressive debut on the stock market this morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢間人工智能公司今朝喺股市首度亮相，表現令人印象深刻。

**Example 2:**
Many fans waited years for the artist to make their debut performance in London.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
好多粉絲等咗好多年，就係為咗睇嗰位藝人喺倫敦嘅首次演出。
