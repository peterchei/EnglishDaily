# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-03

## 1. **Coordinated** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /kəʊˈɔːdɪneɪtɪd/
**Definition:** Organized so that different people or things work together effectively.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 協調的；整合的

**Example 1:**
The G7 nations launched a coordinated effort to stabilize global oil prices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
七大工業國組織發起了一項協調行動，以穩定全球油價。

**Example 2:**
Success in the project requires a coordinated approach from all departments.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
項目的成功需要各部門採取協調一致的方法。

## 2. **Evade** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈveɪd/
**Definition:** To escape or avoid something, especially by using trickery or cleverness.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 規避；逃避

**Example 1:**
The suspect tried to evade police questioning after the incident.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
事件發生後，疑犯試圖逃避警方的盤問。

**Example 2:**
Companies often look for loopholes to evade paying higher taxes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司經常尋找法律漏洞以逃避繳納更高的稅款。

## 3. **Disruption** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈrʌpʃn/
**Definition:** A situation in which something is prevented from continuing in its usual way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中斷；擾亂

**Example 1:**
The rail strike caused significant disruption to commuters across the city.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
鐵路罷工對全城的通勤者造成了嚴重的干擾。

**Example 2:**
We apologize for any disruption caused by the emergency building maintenance.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
對於緊急建築維修所造成的任何不便，我們深表歉意。

## 4. **Deception** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈsepʃn/
**Definition:** The act of hiding the truth, especially to get an advantage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 欺騙；詭計

**Example 1:**
The court found the woman guilty of long-term deception regarding her pregnancy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法庭裁定該女子在懷孕一事上長期欺騙他人的罪名成立。

**Example 2:**
Online financial deception is becoming a major concern for banking authorities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
網上金融詐騙正成為銀行監管機構的一個重大隱憂。

## 5. **Ventilator** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈventɪleɪtə(r)/
**Definition:** A machine that helps a patient breathe when they cannot do so on their own.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 呼吸機

**Example 1:**
The injured man is currently being kept alive on a ventilator in the intensive care unit.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
受傷的男子目前在深切治療部依靠呼吸機維持生命。

**Example 2:**
Doctors decided to take him off the ventilator once his lung function improved.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醫生在確認他的肺功能好轉後，決定讓他脫離呼吸機。

## 6. **Uncover** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ʌnˈkʌvə(r)/
**Definition:** To discover or make known something that was secret or hidden.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 揭露；發現

**Example 1:**
The investigation aims to uncover the truth behind the identity of the accuser.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
調查旨在揭露指控者身份背後的真相。

**Example 2:**
Archaeologists continue to uncover ancient artifacts in this historical site.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
考古學家繼續在這個歷史遺址中發掘古代文物。

## 7. **Pressure** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpreʃə(r)/
**Definition:** A strong demand or influence that makes someone feel they must do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 壓力；逼迫

**Example 1:**
The prime minister is under immense pressure to call a snap election following the vote defeat.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在投票失敗後，首相面臨著舉行提前大選的巨大壓力。

**Example 2:**
Peer pressure can often influence the decisions young people make at school.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
同輩壓力往往會影響年輕人在學校所作的決定。
