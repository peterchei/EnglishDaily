# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-23

## 1. **Deplorable** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈplɔːrəbəl/
**Definition:** Deserving strong condemnation; completely unacceptable or shockingly bad.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 應受譴責的；惡劣的

**Example 1:**
The widow described the decision to release the killers early as absolutely deplorable.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那位遺孀形容提早釋放兇手的決定是絕對令人震驚且不可接受的。

**Example 2:**
Conditions in the neglected building were deemed deplorable by the safety inspectors.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
安全檢查員認為這棟被忽視的建築物內的情況十分惡劣。

## 2. **Spiralling** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈspaɪərəlɪŋ/
**Definition:** Increasing or decreasing rapidly and in an uncontrolled way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 急劇上升（或下降）的；失控的

**Example 1:**
Google is facing concerns over its spiralling costs regarding artificial intelligence development.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
Google 在人工智能開發方面不斷飆升的成本正引發擔憂。

**Example 2:**
The company struggled to manage the spiralling debt that followed the market crash.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司在市場崩盤後，難以處理不斷惡化的債務問題。

## 3. **Raw** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rɔː/
**Definition:** Used to describe feelings that are very strong, painful, and fresh, especially after a traumatic event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （傷痛或情緒）未平復的；強烈的

**Example 1:**
Even months after the tragedy, the grief among the local community remains raw.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
即使悲劇發生數月後，當地社區心中的傷痛依然未平復。

**Example 2:**
He spoke about his experience with raw honesty, moving the entire audience.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他以赤裸裸的誠實談論自己的經歷，感動了全場觀眾。

## 4. **Boost** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /buːst/
**Definition:** To help or encourage something to increase, improve, or become more successful.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 促進；提高；增強

**Example 1:**
The new medication is designed to boost nerve signals in patients with multiple sclerosis.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這種新藥旨在增強多發性硬化症患者的神經信號。

**Example 2:**
A pay rise would certainly help boost the morale of the office staff.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
加薪肯定有助於提高辦公室員工的士氣。

## 5. **Eligible** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɛlɪdʒɪbəl/
**Definition:** Having the necessary qualities or satisfying the conditions to do or receive something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 有資格的；合適的

**Example 1:**
Up to five thousand patients are considered eligible for the new life-changing treatment.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
多達五千名患者被認為有資格接受這種能改變人生的新療法。

**Example 2:**
Check if you are eligible for the government grant before submitting your application.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在提交申請前，請檢查你是否有資格獲得政府補助金。

## 6. **Arson** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɑːsən/
**Definition:** The illegal act of deliberately setting fire to property.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 縱火（罪）

**Example 1:**
Police are treating the fire in Lima as a potential case of arson.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方正將利馬發生的火災視為潛在的縱火案處理。

**Example 2:**
The suspect was arrested after evidence of arson was discovered at the scene.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在現場發現縱火證據後，嫌疑人被逮捕了。

## 7. **Warehouse** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈweəhaʊs/
**Definition:** A large building where raw materials or manufactured goods are stored before they are sold or distributed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 倉庫；貨倉

**Example 1:**
Several retail warehouses belonging to the online giant were hit by drone attacks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家網購巨頭旗下的幾個零售倉庫遭到了無人機襲擊。

**Example 2:**
We need to move the stock from the small shop to the main warehouse today.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們今天需要把庫存從這家小店搬到主倉庫去。
