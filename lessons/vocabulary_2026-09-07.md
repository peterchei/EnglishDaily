# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-07

## 1. **Thuggish** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈθʌɡɪʃ/
**Definition:** Violent, aggressive, or behaving like a criminal.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 粗暴嘅、流氓式嘅

**Example 1:**
The local community was shocked by the thuggish behaviour of the individuals causing a disturbance.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當地社區對嗰班搞事份子嘅流氓行為感到好震驚。

**Example 2:**
The police force promised a zero-tolerance approach to anyone engaging in thuggish intimidation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方承諾會對任何參與流氓恐嚇行為嘅人採取零容忍態度。

## 2. **Margin** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɑːdʒɪn/
**Definition:** The difference between the winners and losers in a vote or competition.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 差距、差額

**Example 1:**
The party secured a victory with a significant margin of over ten percent.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰個政黨以超過百分之十嘅顯著差距贏得勝利。

**Example 2:**
We need to increase our profit margin to cover the rising operational costs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋需要提高利潤空間嚟抵銷上升嘅營運成本。

## 3. **Overrun** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌəʊvəˈrʌn/
**Definition:** To extend beyond a planned limit, boundary, or time frame.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 超出（限度、時間）、溢出

**Example 1:**
The cargo plane began to overrun the runway when it failed to stop in time.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰架貨機因為未能及時停低，開始衝出跑道。

**Example 2:**
The meeting is likely to overrun, so please inform your next appointment.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
會議可能會超時，請通知你下一個約會嘅對象。

## 4. **Envoys** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈenvɔɪz/

## 5. **Breakthrough** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbreɪkθruː/
**Definition:** A significant development or sudden success in a difficult situation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 突破、重大進展

**Example 1:**
Scientists are hoping for a breakthrough in the treatment of the disease.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
科學家正希望喺治療嗰種疾病上有重大突破。

**Example 2:**
Negotiators reported that there was no major breakthrough during the latest round of talks.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
談判代表表示，喺最新一輪會談中並無任何重大突破。

## 7. **Capped** (Adjective/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kæpt/
**Definition:** Placed a limit on the amount or cost of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 設有上限嘅、限制住

**Example 1:**
The government has capped the energy bills to protect households this winter.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府已經對能源賬單設定咗上限，以保護今個冬天嘅家庭用戶。

**Example 2:**
My mobile data is capped at fifty gigabytes per month.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我嘅流動數據每月限制喺五十GB。
