# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-13

## 1. **Burnout** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbɜːnaʊt/
**Definition:** A state of physical and emotional exhaustion caused by prolonged stress or overwork.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 過勞/職業倦怠

**Example 1:**
The report highlights how junior doctor burnout is negatively impacting patient care.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該報告強調了初級醫生過勞如何對病人護理產生負面影響。

**Example 2:**
Many employees in high-pressure industries are quitting due to extreme burnout.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多高壓行業的員工因極度過勞而辭職。

## 2. **Testify** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtestɪfaɪ/
**Definition:** To give evidence as a witness in a law court or before a formal body.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 作證/宣誓作證

**Example 1:**
The former executive was summoned to testify before Congress regarding the recent scandal.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該位前高管被傳喚到國會，就近期的醜聞作證。

**Example 2:**
She agreed to testify against her former employer in the fraud case.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她同意在那宗欺詐案中出庭指證其前僱主。

## 3. **Extramarital** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌekstrəˈmærɪtl/
**Definition:** Occurring outside of marriage, usually referring to a sexual relationship.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 婚外的

**Example 1:**
The CEO resigned following revelations of his alleged extramarital affairs.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在被揭發涉嫌婚外情後，該行政總裁辭職了。

**Example 2:**
The scandal involved several extramarital relationships that ruined his public reputation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這宗醜聞涉及多段婚外情，摧毀了他的公眾名聲。

## 4. **Outage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈaʊtɪdʒ/
**Definition:** A period when a service, such as electricity or internet, is not functioning.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中斷/故障/停電

**Example 1:**
A global Microsoft outage has left tens of thousands of users unable to access their systems.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
全球微軟系統中斷，導致數萬名用戶無法登入系統。

**Example 2:**
Please report any power outage to the local electricity provider immediately.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請立即向當地電力供應商報告任何停電情況。

## 5. **Defy** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfaɪ/
**Definition:** To openly resist or refuse to obey; to perform in a way that is contrary to expectations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 對抗/違抗/抵禦

**Example 1:**
The students at that school continue to defy the odds by achieving excellent grades.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該校學生繼續創造奇蹟，取得了優異的成績。

**Example 2:**
He chose to defy the new regulations despite the risk of a heavy fine.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
儘管面臨巨額罰款的風險，他還是選擇違抗新規定。

## 6. **Prey** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /preɪ/
**Definition:** A person or animal that is hunted or killed; in this context, someone easily deceived or harmed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 獵物/受害者

**Example 1:**
Cybercriminals often look for vulnerable people who might fall prey to their online schemes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
網絡罪犯經常尋找容易成為其網上騙局受害者的人。

**Example 2:**
The hackers targeted hundreds of victims who fell prey to the fake video call scam.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
黑客鎖定了數百名受害者，他們都中了偽造視像通話的圈套。

## 7. **Fresh** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /freʃ/
**Definition:** New or different; additional, especially after a previous similar event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 新的/額外的

**Example 1:**
The school received fresh threats today, forcing another round of evacuations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
學校今天收到了新的威脅，迫使校方再次進行疏散。

**Example 2:**
We need to come up with a fresh approach to solve this recurring problem.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們需要提出一個新的方法來解決這個反覆出現的問題。
