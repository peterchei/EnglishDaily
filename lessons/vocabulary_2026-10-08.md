# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-08

## 1. **Hybrid** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhaɪ.brɪd/
**Definition:** Relating to a combination of different types of threats, especially involving both physical and cyber-attacks.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 混合的（常用於形容現代戰爭或威脅形式）

**Example 1:**
The government is preparing to counter rising hybrid threats from hostile states.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正準備應對來自敵對國家日益增加的混合威脅。

**Example 2:**
Hybrid warfare often combines traditional military tactics with cyber espionage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
混合戰爭通常將傳統軍事戰術與網絡間諜活動結合起來。

## 2. **Shackled** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃæk.əld/
**Definition:** Having chains or metal fasteners around the wrists or ankles to prevent movement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被戴上鐐銬的；被束縛的

**Example 1:**
The prisoner remained shackled to the bed while recovering from the procedure.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
囚犯在手術後恢復期間，雙腳仍被鐐銬鎖在床上。

**Example 2:**
He felt shackled by the company's outdated and restrictive internal policies.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他覺得自己被公司過時且嚴苛的內部政策所束縛。

## 3. **Conscious** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒn.ʃəs/
**Definition:** Awake, thinking, and knowing what is happening around you.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 有意識的；清醒的

**Example 1:**
The patient is now conscious and able to communicate with the medical staff.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
病人現在已經清醒，能夠與醫護人員溝通。

**Example 2:**
She is very conscious of how her decisions might affect her colleagues.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她非常在意自己的決定可能會如何影響同事。

## 4. **Bloc** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /blɒk/
**Definition:** A group of countries or political parties that work together to achieve a shared goal.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 集團；聯盟（常指政治或經濟組織）

**Example 1:**
The finance minister argued that being part of the trade bloc is essential for the economy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
財政部長認為，作為貿易集團的一員對經濟至關重要。

**Example 2:**
The countries formed a regional bloc to strengthen their bargaining power.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這些國家組成了一個區域集團以加強他們的談判能力。

## 5. **Itinerary** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /aɪˈtɪn.ər.ər.i/
**Definition:** A detailed plan or list of places you will visit on a journey.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 行程；旅程計劃

**Example 1:**
Our travel itinerary was changed at the last minute due to bad weather.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於天氣惡劣，我們的行程在最後一刻被更改了。

**Example 2:**
Please check the itinerary carefully before you start packing your bags.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在開始收拾行李之前，請仔細檢查行程表。

## 6. **Accountability** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˌkaʊn.təˈbɪl.ə.ti/
**Definition:** The fact of being responsible for your decisions or actions and expected to explain them when you are asked.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 問責；承擔責任

**Example 1:**
The public is demanding greater accountability from those in positions of power.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公眾要求掌權者承擔更大的責任。

**Example 2:**
There is a lack of accountability within the department regarding the budget overspend.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該部門在預算超支問題上缺乏問責。

## 7. **Forecourt** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɔː.kɔːt/
**Definition:** The area of a petrol station where customers park to fill up their cars with fuel.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加油站的車道；前庭

**Example 1:**
Fuel prices are now clearly displayed on the digital signs at the petrol station forecourt.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
加油站車道的電子告示牌上，現在清楚地顯示著燃油價格。

**Example 2:**
The forecourt was extremely busy as drivers rushed to fill up before the price hike.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於司機們趕在漲價前加油，加油站前庭顯得極為擁擠。
