# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-06

## 1. **Interoperability** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪntəˌrɒpərəˈbɪləti/
**Definition:** The ability of computer systems or software to exchange and make use of information.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 互操作性（不同系統間交換資訊的能力）

**Example 1:**
The new software update improves the interoperability between our different office devices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新的軟件更新改善了我們不同辦公室設備之間的互操作性。

**Example 2:**
Ensuring data interoperability is vital for international vaccination programs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
確保數據的互操作性對於國際疫苗接種計劃至關重要。

## 2. **Plagiarism** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /'pleɪdʒəˌrɪzəm/
**Definition:** The practice of taking someone else's work or ideas and passing them off as one's own.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 抄襲

**Example 1:**
The professor resigned following a serious investigation into allegations of plagiarism.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在那位教授因抄襲指控受到嚴肅調查後，他辭職了。

**Example 2:**
Students are warned that plagiarism in their final thesis will result in immediate failure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學生被警告，畢業論文中的抄襲行為會導致立即不及格。

## 3. **Barrage** (Noun - *Note: context specific usage*)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbærɪdʒ/
**Definition:** A concentrated outpouring or overwhelming quantity of something, such as questions or missiles.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 連珠炮發；猛烈襲擊

**Example 1:**
The city is currently facing a relentless barrage of missile attacks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該城市目前正面臨猛烈的導彈襲擊。

**Example 2:**
The politician faced a barrage of criticism after the scandal was revealed.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醜聞曝光後，該政客面臨連珠炮發般的批評。

## 4. **Earnings** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɜːnɪŋz/
**Definition:** The money obtained in return for labor or services, or the profit of a company.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 收益；盈利

**Example 1:**
SpaceX shares dropped after their latest earnings report failed to meet investor expectations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
SpaceX 的股價在最新的收益報告未能達到投資者預期後下跌。

**Example 2:**
Many companies publish their quarterly earnings to keep shareholders informed.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多公司會公佈季度收益，讓股東知悉公司狀況。

## 5. **Extremism** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪkˈstriːmɪzəm/
**Definition:** The holding of extreme political or religious views, often associated with intolerance toward others.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 極端主義

**Example 1:**
The government has launched a new campaign to tackle online extremism.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府發起了一項新運動，旨在應對網絡極端主義。

**Example 2:**
We must educate young people to recognize the dangers of political extremism.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們必須教育年輕人去認識政治極端主義的危險。

## 6. **Vital** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvaɪtl/
**Definition:** Absolutely necessary or important; essential.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 至關重要的；必不可少的

**Example 1:**
The Strait of Hormuz is a vital waterway for global oil shipping.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
荷姆茲海峽是全球石油運輸至關重要的水道。

**Example 2:**
Clear communication is vital for any team to work effectively.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
清晰的溝通對於任何團隊有效運作都是至關重要的。
