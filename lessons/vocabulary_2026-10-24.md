# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-24

## 1. **Defy** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfaɪ/
**Definition:** To openly resist or refuse to obey.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 蔑視；違抗；公然反抗

**Example 1:**
The protesters decided to defy the police ban on demonstrations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
示威者決定公然反抗警方的示威禁令。

**Example 2:**
He chose to defy expectations and succeed in his own way.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他選擇違抗期望，並以自己的方式取得成功。

## 2. **Unveil** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnˈveɪl/
**Definition:** To show or announce something new for the first time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公佈；揭幕

**Example 1:**
The Chancellor will unveil the new budget plans later this week.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
財政大臣將於本週晚些時候公佈新的預算計劃。

**Example 2:**
The company is set to unveil its latest technology at the conference.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司準備在會議上發佈其最新技術。

## 3. **Scramble** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskræmbəl/
**Definition:** A hurried or disorganized attempt to achieve or find something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 爭奪；混亂的緊急行動

**Example 1:**
There was a global scramble to fix the security flaw in the software.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
全球正陷入一場修復軟件安全漏洞的混亂爭奪中。

**Example 2:**
After the news broke, there was a scramble for seats on the last train.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
新聞發佈後，眾人爭相搶搭最後一班火車。

## 4. **Defences** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfensɪz/
**Definition:** Measures or structures used to protect someone or something from attack.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 防禦；防衛手段

**Example 1:**
The government is looking to strengthen its digital defences against cyber-attacks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正尋求加強其針對網絡攻擊的數碼防禦。

**Example 2:**
The island’s natural defences protected it from the worst of the storm.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該島的天然防禦保護它免受風暴最嚴重的破壞。

## 5. **Flaw** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /flɔː/
**Definition:** A mistake or weakness in a system, plan, or object.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 漏洞；缺陷

**Example 1:**
A serious design flaw was discovered in the new engine.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新引擎被發現存在嚴重的設計缺陷。

**Example 2:**
There is a fundamental flaw in your argument.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你的論點存在一個根本性的漏洞。

## 6. **Deployment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈplɔɪmənt/
**Definition:** The action of moving troops or equipment into position for action.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 部署

**Example 1:**
The deployment of extra police officers helped to keep the streets calm.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
額外警力的部署有助於維持街道平靜。

**Example 2:**
The sudden deployment of resources addressed the urgent needs of the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
資源的突然部署解決了該地區的迫切需求。

## 7. **Residence** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrezɪdəns/
**Definition:** A person's home or the place where someone lives.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 住宅；居所

**Example 1:**
The fire caused significant damage to the historic Alpine residence.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這場大火對那座歷史悠久的阿爾卑斯山居所造成了嚴重損毀。

**Example 2:**
She has maintained her official residence in London for over ten years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她在倫敦維持正式居所已經超過十年了。
