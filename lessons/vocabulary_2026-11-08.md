# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-08

## 1. **Random** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrændəm/
**Definition:** Done, chosen, or happening without method or conscious decision.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 隨機的；胡亂的

**Example 1:**
The attacker fired at people in a random act of violence.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰名襲擊者進行咗一場隨機嘅暴力行為，向人群開槍。

**Example 2:**
I picked a random book from the shelf to read on the train.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我喺書架上隨機揀咗本書喺火車上面睇。

## 2. **Retreat** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈtriːt/
**Definition:** A place that is quiet or private where one can go to get away from everyday life or danger.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 靜修處；隱居地；撤退

**Example 1:**
The mountain cabin serves as a peaceful retreat for the family.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰間山中小屋係佢哋一家人平靜嘅靜修處。

**Example 2:**
The army was forced into a quick retreat after the heavy shelling.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺猛烈炮火之後，軍隊被迫迅速撤退。

## 3. **Armistice** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɑːmɪstɪs/
**Definition:** An agreement made by opposing sides in a war to stop fighting for a certain time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 停戰；休戰協議

**Example 1:**
Armistice Day is observed every year to remember those who died in the war.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
每年我哋都會慶祝停戰紀念日，以紀念喺戰爭中犧牲嘅人。

**Example 2:**
Both sides signed an armistice to allow aid into the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
雙方簽署咗停戰協議，容許救援物資進入該地區。

## 4. **Coalition** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌkəʊəˈlɪʃn/
**Definition:** A temporary alliance for combined action, especially of political parties forming a government.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 聯盟；聯合政府

**Example 1:**
The two parties are currently in talks to form a stable coalition.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰兩個政黨目前正商討組建一個穩定嘅聯合政府。

**Example 2:**
The international coalition aims to restore peace in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢個國際聯盟嘅目標係恢復該地區嘅和平。

## 5. **Verdict** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvɜːdɪkt/
**Definition:** A decision on an issue of fact in a civil or criminal case, or an opinion/judgment about something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 裁決；決定；評價

**Example 1:**
The jury reached a unanimous verdict after two days of deliberation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
陪審團喺討論兩日後達成咗一致嘅裁決。

**Example 2:**
What is your verdict on the new restaurant in town?
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你對市中心嗰間新餐廳有咩評價？

## 6. **Autonomous** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɔːˈtɒnəməs/
**Definition:** Having the freedom to govern itself or control its own affairs; acting independently.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 自治的；自主的；自動駕駛的

**Example 1:**
The company is testing autonomous taxis that do not require a driver.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰間公司正喺度測試唔需要司機嘅自動駕駛的士。

**Example 2:**
Students are encouraged to become more autonomous learners.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋鼓勵學生要成為更具自主性嘅學習者。
