# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-30

Hello everyone! Here are 6 useful words and phrases based on today's news for your English learning.

## 1. **Plummet** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈplʌmɪt/
**Definition:** To fall or drop straight down at high speed; to decrease rapidly in value or amount.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 暴跌；急劇下降

**Example 1:**
Google's share price began to plummet after the company announced lower advertising profits.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
Google 公佈廣告利潤下降後，股價開始暴跌。

**Example 2:**
Temperatures are expected to plummet overnight as the winter storm arrives.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
隨著冬季風暴來臨，預計氣溫將會在晚間急劇下降。

## 2. **Delegitimise** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdiːlɪˈdʒɪtɪmaɪz/
**Definition:** To attempt to make something or someone seem unofficial, illegal, or lacking authority.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 使……失去合法性；否定……的合法地位

**Example 1:**
The opposition party claims that the government is trying to delegitimise the election results.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
反對黨聲稱政府正試圖將選舉結果合法性抹煞。

**Example 2:**
Critics argue that spreading false information is a tactic used to delegitimise the media.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
批評者認為散佈虛假信息是抹黑媒體合法性的一種手段。

## 3. **Adjacent** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈdʒeɪsnt/
**Definition:** Next to or adjoining something else.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 鄰近的；毗連的

**Example 1:**
The helicopters crashed in a field adjacent to the main highway.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
直升機墜毀在鄰近主要公路的一片田野上。

**Example 2:**
We booked two adjacent rooms at the hotel so the family could stay together.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們在酒店訂了兩間相連的房間，好讓家人住在一起。

## 4. **Clearance** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈklɪərəns/
**Definition:** Official permission to do something or to go somewhere.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 批准；許可

**Example 1:**
The train driver had been given clearance to proceed along the track.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
火車司機已經獲得了沿軌道行駛的許可。

**Example 2:**
You need security clearance to enter the restricted area of the office.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
進入辦公室的禁區需要安全許可。

## 5. **Spooked** (Verb/Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /spuːkt/
**Definition:** Frightened, nervous, or panicked, often causing someone to react suddenly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 使受驚；嚇怕

**Example 1:**
Investors were spooked by the sudden news of the company's financial troubles.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
投資者被公司財務困境的突發新聞嚇到了。

**Example 2:**
The loud noise spooked the horses, causing them to bolt.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
巨大的噪音嚇壞了馬，令牠們受驚逃跑。

## 6. **Midterm** (Noun/Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɪdtɜːm/
**Definition:** Occurring at the middle of a political term or an academic period.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中期的；期中的

**Example 1:**
The results of the midterm elections will determine the balance of power in parliament.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
中期選舉的結果將決定議會中的權力平衡。

**Example 2:**
She is feeling stressed because she has to study for her midterm exams.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她感到壓力很大，因為她要為期中考試溫習。
