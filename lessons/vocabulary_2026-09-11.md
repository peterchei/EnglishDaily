# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-11

Hi there! Here are 7 useful words and phrases for today's lesson.

## 1. **Assisted dying** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈsɪstɪd ˈdaɪɪŋ/
**Definition:** A practice where a person with a terminal illness is helped to end their own life.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 協助死亡（安樂死的一種）

**Example 1:**
Parliament is debating whether to introduce new laws concerning assisted dying.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國會正在辯論是否引入有關協助死亡的新法律。

**Example 2:**
The issue of assisted dying remains a deeply sensitive topic for many voters.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
協助死亡的問題對許多選民來說仍然是一個非常敏感的話題。

## 2. **Intelligence** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈtelɪdʒəns/
**Definition:** Information gathered about the plans or activities of a rival group or foreign government.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 情報

**Example 1:**
The security services shared intelligence about potential threats to the region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
安全部門分享了關於該地區潛在威脅的情報。

**Example 2:**
He spent his career working in the field of military intelligence.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他職業生涯中一直從事軍事情報工作。

## 3. **Escalate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈeskəleɪt/
**Definition:** To become or make something become greater, worse, or more serious.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 升級、惡化

**Example 1:**
If the conflict continues to escalate, oil prices will likely remain high.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
如果衝突持續升級，油價很可能會保持在高位。

**Example 2:**
We must prevent the argument from escalating into a physical fight.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們必須防止爭論升級為肢體衝突。

## 4. **Fortress** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɔːtrəs/
**Definition:** A heavily defended city or building that is difficult to capture or penetrate.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 堡壘、防禦工事

**Example 1:**
The city has become a fortress, with soldiers guarding every main road.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這座城市已經變成了一座堡壘，每條主要道路都有士兵把守。

**Example 2:**
They turned their home into a fortress against the approaching storm.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們把家變成了一座堡壘，以抵禦即將到來的風暴。

## 5. **Workable** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɜːkəbl/
**Definition:** Capable of producing the desired effect; practical and able to be done.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 可行的

**Example 1:**
The finance minister is trying to find a workable solution to the budget deficit.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
財政大臣正試圖為預算赤字找到一個可行的解決方案。

**Example 2:**
Is this plan actually workable in the long term?
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個計劃從長遠來看真的可行嗎？

## 6. **Stampede** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /stæmˈpiːd/
**Definition:** A sudden panicked rush of a number of people or animals, often resulting in injuries.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 踩踏事件、人群驚慌奔逃

**Example 1:**
The crowd surged toward the exit, leading to a deadly stampede.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
人群湧向出口，導致了一場致命的踩踏事件。

**Example 2:**
Safety measures were put in place to prevent a stampede at the concert venue.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
現場採取了安全措施，以防止音樂會場地發生踩踏。

## 7. **Watchdog** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɒtʃdɒɡ/
**Definition:** An organization or government agency that monitors companies or sectors to ensure they follow rules and laws.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 監管機構

**Example 1:**
The healthcare watchdog is concerned about how AI will be used in hospitals.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
醫療監管機構對人工智慧在醫院的使用方式表示擔憂。

**Example 2:**
The industry watchdog has opened an inquiry into the company’s recent activities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
行業監管機構已對該公司的近期活動展開調查。
