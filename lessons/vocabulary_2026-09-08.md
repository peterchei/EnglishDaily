# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-08

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary derived from current events to help you navigate British news and professional settings.

## 1. **Dismantle** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈsmæn.təl/
**Definition:** To take something apart into separate pieces; or to gradually get rid of a system or organization.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 拆除；廢除（系統或組織）

**Example 1:**
The council plans to dismantle the temporary structures after the protest ends.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
抗議結束後，議會計劃拆除那些臨時構築物。

**Example 2:**
It will take years to fully dismantle the legacy computer systems at the hospital.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
要徹底廢除醫院內那些過時的電腦系統，需要花費多年時間。

## 2. **Negligence** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈneɡ.lɪ.dʒəns/
**Definition:** The failure to give enough care or attention to people or things that are your responsibility.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 疏忽；失職

**Example 1:**
The hospital faces a lawsuit regarding allegations of professional negligence.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該醫院正面臨有關專業失職指控的訴訟。

**Example 2:**
His injury was caused by the company’s blatant negligence regarding safety standards.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他的受傷是因為公司在安全標準上的嚴重疏忽所致。

## 3. **Discrepancy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈskrep.ən.si/
**Definition:** A difference between two things that should be the same.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 差異；不符

**Example 1:**
There is a significant discrepancy between the reported figures and the actual data.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
報告數字與實際數據之間存在顯著差異。

**Example 2:**
Staff noticed a discrepancy in the inventory records after the shipment arrived.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
貨物到達後，員工發現庫存記錄出現了不符。

## 4. **Retract** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈtrækt/
**Definition:** To say that something you said or wrote earlier is not true or correct; to withdraw a statement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 收回（言論）；撤回

**Example 1:**
The politician was forced to retract his controversial remarks after public backlash.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在公眾強烈反彈後，該政治人物被迫收回了他的爭議性言論。

**Example 2:**
The news outlet issued an apology and decided to retract the article.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
新聞媒體發表了道歉聲明，並決定撤回該篇文章。

## 5. **Oversight** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈəʊ.və.saɪt/
**Definition:** A mistake made because of a failure to notice something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 疏忽；漏掉

**Example 1:**
Due to a clerical oversight, the meeting invite was not sent to the director.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於文書上的疏忽，會議邀請沒有發送給總監。

**Example 2:**
The building design was safe, but the lack of an emergency exit was a serious oversight.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
建築設計是安全的，但缺乏緊急出口是一個嚴重的疏忽。

## 6. **Injunction** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈdʒʌŋk.ʃən/
**Definition:** An official order from a court that stops someone from doing something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 法庭禁制令

**Example 1:**
The company applied for an injunction to prevent the workers from striking.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司申請了一項禁制令，以阻止員工罷工。

**Example 2:**
The high court granted an injunction to protect the privacy of the royal family.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
高等法院頒布了一項禁制令，以保護皇室成員的隱私。

## 7. **Deteriorate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈtɪə.ri.ə.reɪt/
**Definition:** To become worse.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 惡化；變壞

**Example 1:**
Weather conditions in the region are expected to deteriorate rapidly this evening.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該地區的天氣狀況預計將於今晚迅速惡化。

**Example 2:**
Relations between the two countries have continued to deteriorate over the last few months.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
過去幾個月裡，兩國之間的關係持續惡化。
