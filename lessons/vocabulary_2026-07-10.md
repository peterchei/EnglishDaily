# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-10

## 1. **Tally** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtæli/
**Definition:** A current score or amount of something that is being counted.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 點算總數；記錄

**Example 1:**
The first nomination tally shows Burnham has significant support within the party.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首輪提名點算總數顯示，伯納姆在黨內獲得顯著支持。

**Example 2:**
Please keep a tally of how many people attend the meeting today.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請記錄一下今天有多少人參加會議。

## 2. **Blaze** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /bleɪz/
**Definition:** A very large and destructive fire.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 烈火；大火

**Example 1:**
Firefighters are struggling to contain the blaze spreading across the forest.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
消防員正努力控制在森林中蔓延的大火。

**Example 2:**
Six people were injured when the blaze broke out in the province of Almería.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
阿爾梅里亞省發生大火，導致六人受傷。

## 3. **Calendar year** (Noun Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈlɪndə jɪə/
**Definition:** The period from January 1st to December 31st.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 日曆年（即一月至十二月）

**Example 1:**
This July has been the hottest month in the current calendar year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
今年七月是目前日曆年中最熱的一個月。

**Example 2:**
We need to finalize our budget for the 2026 calendar year by next week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們需要在下週前完成 2026 日曆年的預算。

## 4. **Fossil fuel** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɒsɪl fjuːəl/
**Definition:** Natural fuels such as coal, oil, or gas formed from the remains of living organisms.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 化石燃料（如煤、石油、天然氣）

**Example 1:**
The port is under pressure to reduce its reliance on fossil fuel companies.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該港口正承受壓力，要求減少對化石燃料公司的依賴。

**Example 2:**
Many countries are trying to switch from fossil fuel to renewable energy sources.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多國家正嘗試從化石燃料轉向可再生能源。

## 5. **Debut** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdeɪbjuː/
**Definition:** A person's or firm's first appearance or performance in a particular capacity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 初次登場；首次亮相

**Example 1:**
The company is preparing for its mega share debut on the Nasdaq this Friday.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司正準備本週五在納斯達克進行大型股票首次公開發行。

**Example 2:**
The young athlete made an impressive debut in the international competition.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這位年輕運動員在國際比賽中初次登場，表現令人印象深刻。

## 6. **Humanitarian** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /hjuːˌmænɪˈteəriən/
**Definition:** Concerned with or seeking to promote human welfare.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 人道主義的

**Example 1:**
He was a prominent humanitarian figure who helped those affected by the war.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他是一位傑出的人道主義人物，曾幫助那些受戰爭影響的人。

**Example 2:**
The organization provides essential humanitarian aid to refugees in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該組織向該地區的難民提供必要的人道主義援助。

## 7. **Unopposed** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnəˈpəʊzd/
**Definition:** Without any competitor or person standing against someone.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 無對手的；未受阻撓的

**Example 1:**
Burnham is running unopposed in the race to replace the current leader.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
伯納姆在競選接替現任領袖的過程中沒有對手。

**Example 2:**
The motion was passed unopposed during the committee meeting.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該動議在委員會會議期間獲無異議通過。
