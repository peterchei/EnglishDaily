# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-09

## 1. **Candidacy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkændɪdəsi/
**Definition:** The state or fact of being a candidate for a position or office.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 候選資格 / 參選

**Example 1:**
His sudden withdrawal ended his candidacy for the local council.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他突然退出，結束了他對地方議會的參選資格。

**Example 2:**
She announced her candidacy for the leadership position yesterday.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她昨天宣布參加領導職位的競選。

## 2. **Tumult** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtjuːmʌlt/
**Definition:** A state of great confusion, disorder, or mental agitation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 騷動 / 混亂

**Example 1:**
The political party is in a state of tumult following the resignation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
辭職事件後，該政黨正處於一片混亂之中。

**Example 2:**
The sudden news caused a tumult among the waiting crowd.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這則突如其來的消息在等候的人群中引起了騷動。

## 3. **Opt out** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɒpt aʊt/
**Definition:** To choose not to participate in something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 選擇退出 / 不參與

**Example 1:**
Users can opt out of the data collection process in the settings menu.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
用戶可以在設定菜單中選擇退出數據收集程序。

**Example 2:**
We decided to opt out of the company pension scheme this year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們今年決定退出公司退休金計劃。

## 4. **Median** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmiːdiən/
**Definition:** Denoting or relating to a value or quantity lying at the midpoint of a frequency distribution.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中位數的

**Example 1:**
The median house price in London has increased significantly this year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
今年倫敦的房價中位數顯著上升。

**Example 2:**
The report shows the median income for households in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該報告顯示了該地區家庭收入的中位數。

## 5. **Retribution** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌretrɪˈbjuːʃn/
**Definition:** Punishment that is considered to be morally right and fully deserved.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 報應 / 懲罰

**Example 1:**
The government promised swift retribution for the attack on its ships.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府承諾對其船隻受襲事件進行迅速的報復。

**Example 2:**
They fear that any action might provoke further retribution from their enemies.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們擔心任何行動都可能引發敵人的進一步報復。

## 6. **Fit for purpose** (Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /fɪt fɔː ˈpɜːpəs/
**Definition:** Suitable for the requirements of a particular situation or task.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 適用的 / 切合實際用途的

**Example 1:**
The current disability support system is no longer fit for purpose.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
目前的殘疾支援制度已不再適用。

**Example 2:**
We need to ensure that the new equipment is fit for purpose before we buy it.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在購買之前，我們需要確保新設備是切合實際用途的。

## 7. **Alleged** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈledʒd/
**Definition:** Said, without proof, to have taken place or to have a specified illegal or undesirable quality.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被指控的 / 聲稱的

**Example 1:**
The police are investigating the alleged fraud at the bank.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方正在調查該銀行被指控的詐騙案。

**Example 2:**
He denied any involvement in the alleged activities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他否認參與了那些被指控的活動。
