# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-19

## 1. **Extradite** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈek.strə.daɪt/
**Definition:** To make someone return to a country where they have been accused of doing something illegal.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 引渡

**Example 1:**
The British authorities are working to extradite the suspect from the US to face trial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英國當局正致力於將該嫌疑人從美國引渡回國受審。

**Example 2:**
Extradite proceedings can often take several years to complete in the legal system.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在法律程序中，引渡程序往往需要幾年時間才能完成。

## 2. **Pledge** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /pledʒ/
**Definition:** A serious or formal promise, especially one made by a politician or organization.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 承諾 / 保證

**Example 1:**
The new prime minister made a major pledge to reduce the cost of living for families.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新任首相作出了一項重大承諾，旨在減輕家庭的生活成本。

**Example 2:**
He stood by his election pledge to improve public services across the country.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他堅持兌現他在選舉時作出的改善全國公共服務的承諾。

## 3. **Wardrobe** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈwɔːr.droʊb/
**Definition:** The collection of clothes that a person owns, often reflecting their personal style or brand.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 衣著風格 / 個人服飾庫

**Example 1:**
As a politician, he had to change his casual wardrobe to look more formal for his new role.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
作為一名政治家，他必須改變他那隨性的衣著風格，以適應新職位所需的莊重形象。

**Example 2:**
Building a professional wardrobe is essential for anyone starting a new corporate job.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
對於任何開始新企業工作的人來說，建立專業的衣著風格是必不可少的。

## 4. **Hostility** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /hɒsˈtɪl.ə.ti/
**Definition:** Unfriendly or threatening behavior, or an occasion of open conflict between groups or nations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 敵意 / 交戰狀態

**Example 1:**
The regional hostilities have caused great concern among the international community.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
地區間的交戰狀態引起了國際社會的極大關注。

**Example 2:**
There is a growing sense of hostility between the two rival factions in the parliament.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
議會中兩個對立派系之間的敵意日益加深。

## 5. **Logistics** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ləˈdʒɪs.tɪks/
**Definition:** The careful organization of a complicated activity or the movement of goods and supplies.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 物流 / 後勤

**Example 1:**
The military strike targeted key logistics facilities that were supplying the enemy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軍事打擊目標是那些為敵人提供補給的關鍵後勤設施。

**Example 2:**
Managing the logistics of a large international event requires a very experienced team.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
管理大型國際活動的後勤工作需要一個非常有經驗的團隊。

## 6. **Loyalist** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɔɪ.əl.ɪst/
**Definition:** A person who remains firm in their support for a particular leader, party, or cause.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 效忠者 / 忠實支持者

**Example 1:**
The president appointed a loyalist to the committee to ensure his policies would be supported.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統任命了一名忠實支持者進入委員會，以確保他的政策能得到支持。

**Example 2:**
Even when the party was failing, the loyalists refused to abandon their leader.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
即使政黨陷入困境，這些忠實支持者仍拒絕拋棄他們的領袖。

## 7. **Rival** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈraɪ.vəl/
**Definition:** To be as good, important, or valuable as someone or something else.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 與……競爭 / 媲美

**Example 1:**
The new artificial intelligence model is expected to rival the biggest tech companies in the world.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
預計這個新的人工智能模型將能與世界上大型科技公司媲美。

**Example 2:**
Few cities in the world can rival London for its cultural diversity.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
世界上很少有城市能在文化多元性方面與倫敦相媲美。
