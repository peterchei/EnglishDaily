# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-28

## 1. **Detainee** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdiː.teɪˈniː/
**Definition:** A person who is held in custody, especially for political reasons or suspected crimes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被拘留者

**Example 1:**
The police questioned the detainee for several hours regarding the suspicious vehicle.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方就該輛可疑車輛審問了那名被拘留者幾個小時。

**Example 2:**
Human rights groups have called for better conditions for every detainee at the facility.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
人權組織呼籲改善該設施內每一位被拘留者的待遇。

## 2. **Compromise** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒm.prə.maɪz/
**Definition:** An agreement reached by each side making concessions to settle a dispute.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 妥協

**Example 1:**
Both parties eventually reached a compromise after a long day of negotiations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
經過一整天的談判，雙方最終達成了一項妥協。

**Example 2:**
It is difficult to find a compromise that satisfies everyone involved in the parade dispute.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
要找到一個讓遊行糾紛中各方都滿意的妥協方案是很困難的。

## 3. **Allegation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌæl.əˈɡeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** A claim or assertion that someone has done something illegal or wrong, typically without proof.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 指控

**Example 1:**
The president denied every allegation of corruption made against his administration.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統否認了針對其政府的所有腐敗指控。

**Example 2:**
The company is investigating an allegation of misconduct within the office.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司正在調查一項關於辦公室不當行為的指控。

## 4. **Escalating** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈes.kə.leɪ.tɪŋ/
**Definition:** Becoming more intense, serious, or widespread.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 升級中的；惡化中的

**Example 1:**
The international community is worried about the escalating war in the region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際社會對該地區不斷升級的戰爭感到憂心。

**Example 2:**
Rising costs are an escalating problem for small businesses in the UK.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
成本上漲對於英國的小企業來說是一個日益嚴重的問題。

## 5. **Meddle** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmed.əl/
**Definition:** To interfere in or busy oneself unduly with something that is not one's concern.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 插手；干預

**Example 1:**
The report claims that foreign bots tried to meddle with the website's data.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
報告聲稱外國機器人試圖干預該網站的數據。

**Example 2:**
Please stop trying to meddle in my personal life; I can manage it myself.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請停止干預我的私生活；我自己可以處理。

## 6. **Resign** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzaɪn/
**Definition:** To voluntarily leave a job or position, especially one of high authority.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 辭職

**Example 1:**
The minister had to resign after the scandal was uncovered by the press.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
醜聞被媒體揭露後，部長不得不辭職。

**Example 2:**
It is rare for a president to resign before their term officially ends.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
總統在任期正式結束前辭職是罕見的。

## 7. **Iconic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /aɪˈkɒn.ɪk/
**Definition:** Widely recognized and well-established; representing a famous style or era.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 標誌性的；具代表性的

**Example 1:**
The game is based on an iconic horror series that started decades ago.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這款遊戲基於一個數十年前開始的標誌性恐怖系列。

**Example 2:**
Big Ben is one of the most iconic buildings in London.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
大笨鐘是倫敦最具代表性的建築之一。
