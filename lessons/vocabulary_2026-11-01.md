# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-01

## 1. **Intervention** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪntəˈvɛnʃn/
**Definition:** The act of becoming involved in a situation to influence or improve it.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 干預、介入

**Example 1:**
The government's intervention in the housing market aims to lower the cost of renting.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府對房屋市場的干預旨在降低租金成本。

**Example 2:**
Her timely intervention prevented the argument from becoming a physical fight.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她及時的介入阻止了爭吵演變成肢體衝突。

## 2. **Minefield** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmaɪnfiːld/
**Definition:** An area of land containing hidden explosive mines; metaphorically, a situation full of hidden dangers.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 地雷區；（喻）充滿危險的局面

**Example 1:**
The refugees had to walk through a dangerous minefield to reach safety.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
難民必須穿過一片危險的地雷區才能到達安全地帶。

**Example 2:**
Discussing politics at a family dinner can be a social minefield.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在家庭聚餐時討論政治可能會是一場社交陷阱。

## 3. **Rally** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈræli/
**Definition:** A large public meeting held to support a specific cause or protest.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 集會

**Example 1:**
Thousands of people gathered in the city centre for the political rally.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
成千上萬的人聚集在市中心參加政治集會。

**Example 2:**
The opposition party is organising a massive rally to demand higher wages.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
反對黨正在組織一場大型集會，要求提高工資。

## 4. **Resignation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌrɛzɪɡˈneɪʃn/
**Definition:** The act of officially giving up a job or a position.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 辭職

**Example 1:**
The election chief is facing pressure to submit his resignation following the voting scandal.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
選舉負責人因投票醜聞而面臨提交辭職信的壓力。

**Example 2:**
News of her sudden resignation shocked the entire office yesterday.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她昨日突然辭職的消息令整個辦公室感到震驚。

## 5. **Bubble** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbʌbl/
**Definition:** A situation where the price of something increases rapidly to an unrealistic level before suddenly crashing.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 泡沫（經濟現象）

**Example 1:**
Experts are worried that the recent growth in the AI sector is just an economic bubble.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
專家擔心最近人工智能領域的增長只是一個經濟泡沫。

**Example 2:**
When the housing bubble burst, many people lost a large amount of money.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當房地產泡沫破裂時，許多人損失了大量金錢。

## 6. **Revision** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈvɪʒn/
**Definition:** The process of changing or updating something to make it more accurate or current.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 修訂、修正

**Example 1:**
The voter roll revision exercise has caused significant concern among the public.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
選民名冊的修訂工作引起了公眾的極大擔憂。

**Example 2:**
We need to make a final revision to the project plan before we present it to the board.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在向董事會展示之前，我們需要對項目計劃進行最後的修訂。

## 7. **Trapped** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /træpt/
**Definition:** Unable to escape from a place or a difficult situation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 受困的

**Example 1:**
Many families remained trapped in the city for months without electricity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多家庭在沒有電的情況下，受困於那座城市長達數月。

**Example 2:**
He felt trapped in a job that he no longer enjoyed.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他感到自己被困在了一份他不再喜歡的工作中。
