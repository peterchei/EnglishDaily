# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-31

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary commonly found in British news and professional environments.

## 1. **Indefinite** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈdef.ɪ.nət/
**Definition:** Lasting for an unlimited or unspecified period of time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 無限期的，不明確的

**Example 1:**
The government is debating new regulations for those who have been granted indefinite leave to remain.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在討論針對那些已獲准無限期居留權人士的新規例。

**Example 2:**
He has been on indefinite leave following the company restructuring.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司重組後，他一直在放無限期假期。

## 2. **Reparations** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌrep.əˈreɪ.ʃənz/
**Definition:** Payments or other actions made to make amends for a past wrong or injury.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 賠償（通常指對歷史不公義的補償）

**Example 1:**
There is a heated political debate regarding whether the state should pay reparations for historical injustices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
關於國家是否應為歷史上的不公義行為支付賠償，目前存在激烈的政治辯論。

**Example 2:**
The prime minister insisted that reparations were not the appropriate solution for past colonial actions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
首相堅稱，對於過去的殖民行為，賠償並非合適的解決方案。

## 3. **Interceptors** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪn.təˈsep.təz/
**Definition:** Missiles or aircraft designed to stop or destroy an enemy's missiles or aircraft.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 攔截導彈，攔截機

**Example 1:**
The country requested more interceptors to help defend its cities against incoming ballistic missiles.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該國要求獲得更多攔截導彈，以幫助保護其城市免受來襲的彈道導彈攻擊。

**Example 2:**
Advanced radar systems are essential for directing interceptors toward their fast-moving targets.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
先進的雷達系統對於引導攔截導彈飛向高速移動的目標至關重要。

## 4. **Climax** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈklaɪ.mæks/
**Definition:** The most exciting or important point in a story, event, or series.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 高潮，頂點

**Example 1:**
The reality show reached its nail-biting climax after weeks of intense competition.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
經過數週的激烈競爭，這檔真人秀節目達到了扣人心弦的高潮。

**Example 2:**
The climax of the play was incredibly dramatic and left the audience in silence.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這部劇的高潮部分極具戲劇性，讓觀眾陷入了沈默。

## 5. **Justices** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdʒʌs.tɪ.sɪz/
**Definition:** Judges, especially those of a high-ranking court.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 法官（通常指高等法院法官）

**Example 1:**
The government's decision to suspend two senior justices has sparked a national controversy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府暫停兩名高級法官職務的決定引發了全國性的爭議。

**Example 2:**
The justices will review the case to ensure the law was applied correctly.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
法官們將審理此案，以確保法律得到正確的執行。

## 6. **Snap** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /snæp/
**Definition:** Done suddenly and without much prior preparation or warning.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 突然的，臨時的

**Example 1:**
The prime minister called a snap election to try and secure a stronger mandate from the public.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相宣佈舉行臨時大選，試圖從公眾那裡獲得更強的授權。

**Example 2:**
She had to make a snap decision because the deadline was only five minutes away.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
因為距離截止時間只剩下五分鐘，她不得不做出倉促的決定。
