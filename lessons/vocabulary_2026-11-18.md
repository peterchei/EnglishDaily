# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-18

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary appearing in recent news headlines.

## 1. **Eyes** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /aɪz/
**Definition:** To look at something or someone with interest, often because you want to obtain or achieve it.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 盯著；覬覦；打算獲取

**Example 1:**
The company eyes a significant expansion into the Asian market next year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司打算明年在亞洲市場進行大規模擴張。

**Example 2:**
He is eyeing a promotion after his successful project delivery.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他在成功交付項目後，正盯著一個升職機會。

## 2. **Raid** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /reɪd/
**Definition:** A sudden attack or incursion, typically involving a brief but intense physical intervention.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 突襲；搜查

**Example 1:**
The police conducted a dawn raid on the suspect's apartment.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方在黎明時分對嫌疑人的公寓進行了突襲搜查。

**Example 2:**
The military plane was destroyed during an hours-long raid by protesters.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這架軍用飛機在抗議者持續數小時的突襲中被毀。

## 3. **Divert** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /daɪˈvɜːt/
**Definition:** To cause something to change course or to use resources for a different purpose.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 轉移（資金或資源）；使繞道

**Example 1:**
The government plans to divert funds from benefits to local job training programs.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府計劃將資金從福利支出轉移到本地職業培訓項目上。

**Example 2:**
Heavy traffic was diverted through the side streets due to the accident.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於發生事故，繁忙的交通被分流到了側街。

## 4. **Bumper** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbʌmpə/
**Definition:** Exceptionally large or abundant, often used to describe profits, results, or harvests.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 豐收的；巨大的；非常好的（指業績或成果）

**Example 1:**
The tech giant reported bumper profits despite the current economic downturn.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管目前經濟下滑，這家科技巨頭仍報告了豐厚的利潤。

**Example 2:**
Farmers are celebrating a bumper crop of wheat this season.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
農民們正在慶祝本季度小麥的豐收。

## 5. **Sheltering** (Verb/Participle)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃeltərɪŋ/
**Definition:** Taking cover or protecting oneself from danger, bad weather, or attack.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 避難；躲藏；遮蔽

**Example 1:**
Families were sheltering in the basement while the sirens wailed outside.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當外面的警報聲響起時，家庭成員們都躲在地下室避難。

**Example 2:**
He spent the afternoon sheltering from the heavy rain under a shop awning.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他在商店的遮雨棚下躲了大半個下午，以避開大雨。

## 6. **Unfolded** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ʌnˈfəʊldɪd/
**Definition:** To reveal or develop in detail; to describe how an event happened step by step.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 展開；顯露；（事件）發生經過

**Example 1:**
Witnesses recounted exactly how the drama unfolded on the bridge.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
目擊者詳細講述了橋上事件是如何發生的。

**Example 2:**
The full story of the merger unfolded during the annual general meeting.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
合併事件的完整經過在年度股東大會上展現出來。
