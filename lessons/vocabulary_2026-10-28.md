# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-28

## 1. **Devolution** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdiːvəˈluːʃən/
**Definition:** The transfer or delegation of power from a central government to local or regional administrations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 權力下放

**Example 1:**
The government’s plan for devolution aims to give local councils more control over their own budgets.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府的權力下放計劃旨在讓地方議會對自己的預算有更多控制權。

**Example 2:**
Many voters believe that further devolution will help improve public services in the north of the country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多選民認為，進一步的權力下放將有助於改善該國北部的公共服務。

## 2. **Mauled** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /mɔːld/
**Definition:** To cause injury to a person or animal by scratching and biting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被（野獸）襲擊致傷

**Example 1:**
The zookeeper was unfortunately mauled by a leopard during the morning cleaning session.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那名動物園管理員在晨間清潔時不幸被一隻豹襲擊致傷。

**Example 2:**
Emergency services arrived quickly after reports that a child had been mauled by a stray dog.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在接到一名兒童被流浪狗咬傷的報告後，緊急服務人員迅速趕到現場。

## 3. **Stealthier** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈstelθiər/
**Definition:** More quiet, cautious, or secretive, often to avoid detection.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 更隱蔽的，更不易察覺的

**Example 1:**
Engineers are developing stealthier drones to reduce noise pollution in residential areas.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
工程師們正在開發更隱蔽的無人機，以減少住宅區的噪音污染。

**Example 2:**
The new military aircraft design is much stealthier than its predecessor, making it harder to track on radar.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這種新型軍用飛機的設計比其前身更隱蔽，使其在雷達上更難被追蹤。

## 4. **Thawed** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /θɔːd/
**Definition:** To become friendlier or less hostile after a period of tension.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （關係）緩和，解凍

**Example 1:**
After years of diplomatic silence, the relationship between the two nations has finally thawed.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
經過多年的外交沉默，兩國之間的關係終於緩和了。

**Example 2:**
Tensions in the office thawed once the manager decided to address the team's concerns openly.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
一旦經理決定公開處理團隊的擔憂，辦公室內的緊張氣氛就緩和了。

## 5. **Commonplace** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒmənpleɪs/
**Definition:** Done, occurring, or found very often; not unusual.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 常見的，司空見慣的

**Example 1:**
Electric cars are becoming increasingly commonplace on British roads.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
電動車在英國道路上正變得越來越常見。

**Example 2:**
Smartphones have become such a commonplace tool that it is hard to imagine life without one.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
智慧型手機已經成為了一種司空見慣的工具，很難想像沒有它的生活。

## 6. **Faults** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /fɔːlts/
**Definition:** Defects, imperfections, or weaknesses in something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 故障，缺陷

**Example 1:**
The tech company acknowledged several faults in the screen display of their new foldable device.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家科技公司承認其新款摺疊設備的螢幕顯示存在一些缺陷。

**Example 2:**
An inspector was sent to check for electrical faults in the old building.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
一名檢查員被派去檢查這棟舊建築是否有電氣故障。
