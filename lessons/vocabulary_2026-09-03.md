# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-03

## 1. **Underpinning** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌndəˈpɪnɪŋ/
**Definition:** A foundation or basis that supports an idea, system, or structure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 基礎，支撐點

**Example 1:**
The government’s new policy has no solid economic underpinning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府嘅新政策缺乏穩固嘅經濟基礎。

**Example 2:**
Strong research is the underpinning of his successful business model.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
強大嘅研究係佢成功商業模式嘅支撐點。

## 2. **Hailing** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /heɪlɪŋ/
**Definition:** Publicly describing or acclaiming someone or something as being very good or important.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 稱頌，讚揚

**Example 1:**
The mayor is hailing the new robotaxi trial as a major success.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
市長正稱頌呢個機械人出租車測試係一大成功。

**Example 2:**
Critics are hailing the film as a masterpiece of modern cinema.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
評論家正讚揚呢套戲係現代電影嘅傑作。

## 3. **Restructuring** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːˈstrʌktʃərɪŋ/
**Definition:** The act of organizing a company or system in a new way to make it more effective.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重組，改組

**Example 1:**
The company announced a major restructuring to cut costs and improve efficiency.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司宣佈進行大規模重組，以削減成本並提高效率。

**Example 2:**
After the merger, the restructuring of the departments took several months.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
合併之後，部門嘅重組花咗幾個月時間。

## 4. **Reckless** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrekləs/
**Definition:** Doing something without worrying about the danger or the bad results of your actions.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 魯莽，唔顧後果

**Example 1:**
Driving through the red light at that speed was incredibly reckless.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
喺嗰個速度衝紅燈係極之魯莽嘅行為。

**Example 2:**
The company’s reckless expansion led to its eventual bankruptcy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰間公司魯莽嘅擴張導致佢最終破產。

## 5. **Unanimous** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /juˈnænɪməs/
**Definition:** When everyone in a group agrees completely.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 一致（同意）

**Example 1:**
The jury reached a unanimous decision after hours of deliberation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
陪審團喺討論幾個鐘之後，達成咗一致嘅決定。

**Example 2:**
There was unanimous support for the proposal among the staff members.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
員工之間對呢個提案有一致嘅支持。

## 6. **Veiled** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /veɪld/
**Definition:** Expressed in a way that is not direct or clear, often used for threats or criticisms.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 含蓄嘅，隱晦嘅

**Example 1:**
He made a veiled threat to quit if his demands were not met.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢發出咗一個隱晦嘅威脅，話如果唔滿足佢嘅要求，佢就會辭職。

**Example 2:**
The article contained veiled criticism of the government's recent actions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰篇文章包含咗對政府近期行為含蓄嘅批評。

## 7. **Specialist** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈspeʃəlɪst/
**Definition:** Having a deep knowledge or skill in a specific area or subject.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 專門嘅，專業嘅

**Example 1:**
The children were referred to a specialist clinic for treatment.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
啲細路被轉介去一間專門診所接受治療。

**Example 2:**
We need to hire a specialist consultant to fix this technical problem.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋需要聘請一位專業顧問去解決呢個技術問題。
