# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-30

## 1. **Referendum** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌref.ərˈen.dəm/
**Definition:** A general vote by the electorate on a single political question that has been referred to them for a direct decision.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公投

**Example 1:**
The country is holding a referendum to decide whether to resume EU membership talks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該國正在舉行公投，以決定是否恢復歐盟成員資格談判。

**Example 2:**
The government promised to respect the result of the national referendum.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府承諾會尊重這次全國公投的結果。

## 2. **Depot** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdep.əʊ/
**Definition:** A place where large amounts of food, goods, or equipment are stored.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 倉庫、貯存庫

**Example 1:**
The military depot was destroyed in an overnight strike.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該軍事倉庫在一次夜間襲擊中被摧毀了。

**Example 2:**
Emergency supplies are being moved to a central depot for distribution.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
緊急物資正被轉移至中央倉庫進行分發。

## 3. **Shadow cabinet** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃæd.əʊ ˌkæb.ɪ.nət/
**Definition:** A group of senior members of the opposition party who act as spokespeople for the specific policy areas of the government.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 影子內閣

**Example 1:**
He decided to quit the shadow cabinet to focus on his campaign for London mayor.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他決定辭去影子內閣職務，專注於競選倫敦市長。

**Example 2:**
The shadow cabinet challenged the Prime Minister's new economic policy today.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
影子內閣今天對首相的新經濟政策提出了質疑。

## 4. **Distress** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈstres/
**Definition:** A state of extreme danger or difficulty, especially when a ship or aircraft requires urgent help.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 危難、急難

**Example 1:**
The Coastguard received a signal from a vessel in distress off the coast.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
海岸防衛隊收到了一艘在海岸附近陷入危難的船隻發出的信號。

**Example 2:**
Many fishing boats rushed to help the sailors who were in distress.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多漁船趕往協助那些陷入危難的水手。

## 5. **Provocateur** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˌvɒk.əˈtɜːr/
**Definition:** A person who provokes others, especially into committing an illegal act or causing a public disturbance.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 煽動者、挑釁者

**Example 1:**
The online provocateur was deported after being detained by immigration officials.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位網絡挑釁者在被移民官員拘留後遭到了驅逐出境。

**Example 2:**
He has built a career as a political provocateur by making controversial statements.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他通過發表具爭議性的言論，建立了作為政治煽動者的事業。

## 6. **Billboard** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbɪl.bɔːd/
**Definition:** A very large board on which advertisements or messages are shown, usually at the side of a road.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 廣告牌、告示牌

**Example 1:**
The province installed a massive billboard along the shoreline to send a message to their neighbours.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該省在海岸線安裝了一塊巨大的告示牌，向鄰居傳遞訊息。

**Example 2:**
Drivers can see the new billboard from several miles away.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
司機在幾英里外就能看到那塊新的告示牌。

## 7. **Cloning** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkləʊ.nɪŋ/
**Definition:** The process of creating an exact copy, often used in technology to describe replicating human voices using AI.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 克隆、複製（技術）

**Example 1:**
Actors are calling for new laws to protect their rights against AI voice cloning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
演員們正呼籲制定新法律，以保護他們的權利免受人工智能語音克隆的侵害。

**Example 2:**
Voice cloning technology is becoming so advanced that it is hard to tell if a recording is real.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
語音克隆技術正變得如此先進，以至於很難分辨錄音是否真實。
