# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-19

## 1. **Suspended** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /səˈspɛndɪd/
**Definition:** Temporarily prevented from continuing or being in effect.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 暫停的；懸而未決的

**Example 1:**
Elections have been suspended in the region due to the ongoing state of emergency.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於持續的緊急狀態，該地區的選舉已被暫停。

**Example 2:**
The train service was suspended for two hours following a signal failure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
列車服務在訊號故障後暫停了兩小時。

## 2. **Difficulty** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdɪfɪkəlti/
**Definition:** A situation that causes problems or requires effort to deal with.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 困難；困境

**Example 1:**
The hikers got into difficulty when the weather turned suddenly stormy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當天氣突然變壞時，遠足者陷入了困境。

**Example 2:**
Many families are experiencing financial difficulty during the current economic climate.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在當前的經濟環境下，許多家庭正經歷著經濟困難。

## 3. **Initiative** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈnɪʃətɪv/
**Definition:** A new plan or process to achieve something or solve a problem.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 倡議；新計劃

**Example 1:**
The council launched a new initiative to provide shelter for those sleeping on the streets.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
議會發起了一項新倡議，為露宿者提供庇護所。

**Example 2:**
She took the initiative to organise the office charity event.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她主動提議組織了辦公室的慈善活動。

## 4. **Capacity** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈpæsɪti/
**Definition:** The maximum amount that something can contain or produce; the ability to do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 容量；能力

**Example 1:**
The stadium reached its full capacity for the final match of the tournament.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
體育場在錦標賽決賽時達到了滿座狀態。

**Example 2:**
Regular exercise can significantly improve your lung capacity.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
定期運動可以顯著提高你的肺活量。

## 5. **Diagnosis** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdaɪəɡˈnəʊsɪs/
**Definition:** The identification of the nature of an illness or other problem by examination of the symptoms.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 診斷（結果）

**Example 1:**
The doctor's diagnosis was that he needed rest and a change of diet.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
醫生的診斷是他需要休息並調整飲食。

**Example 2:**
Receiving a cancer diagnosis is an incredibly stressful experience for any family.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
收到癌症診斷對任何家庭來說都是極具壓力的經歷。

## 6. **Curtail** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kɜːˈteɪl/
**Definition:** To reduce in extent or quantity; to impose a restriction on.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 縮減；限制

**Example 1:**
The government decided to curtail spending to address the national budget deficit.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府決定縮減開支，以解決國家預算赤字。

**Example 2:**
They had to curtail their holiday plans because of an urgent work matter.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於緊急工作事務，他們不得不縮短假期計劃。

## 7. **Lawsuit** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɔːsuːt/
**Definition:** A claim or dispute brought to a court of law for adjudication.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 訴訟；官司

**Example 1:**
Several states have filed a major lawsuit against the technology giant.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
幾個州已對這家科技巨頭提起了重大訴訟。

**Example 2:**
The company settled the lawsuit out of court to avoid further bad publicity.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司在庭外和解了這場訴訟，以避免進一步的負面宣傳。
