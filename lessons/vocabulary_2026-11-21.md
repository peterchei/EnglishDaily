# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-21

Hello everyone, here are your 6 key words for today based on the latest news.

## 1. **Correction** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈrek.ʃən/
**Definition:** A market situation where stock prices fall by at least 10% from their recent peak.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （股市）調整；回調

**Example 1:**
The tech index entered correction territory after the sudden sell-off.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
科技指數喺拋售潮之後進入咗調整區。

**Example 2:**
Investors are nervous that this market correction could last for several weeks.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
投資者擔心呢次市場回調可能會持續幾個禮拜。

## 2. **Groom** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɡruːm/
**Definition:** To build a relationship with someone, often a child or vulnerable person, for the purpose of sexual exploitation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誘拐（通常指通過建立信任關係來進行性剝削）

**Example 1:**
The criminal used the internet to groom his victims before meeting them in person.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢名罪犯利用互聯網去誘拐受害人，之後先見面。

**Example 2:**
Laws are being strengthened to better protect minors from those who groom them online.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
法律正喺度加強，以便更好咁保護未成年人免受網上誘拐者嘅傷害。

## 3. **Aggravated** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈæɡ.rə.veɪ.tɪd/
**Definition:** Describing a crime that is made more serious by specific factors, such as racism or extreme violence.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加重（罪行）；惡劣（情節）

**Example 1:**
The attack was classified as an aggravated assault due to the victim's ethnicity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於受害人嘅種族背景，呢次襲擊被定性為加重罪行嘅毆打。

**Example 2:**
He received a longer sentence because the judge considered the offence an aggravated circumstance.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢被判處更長刑期，因為法官認為呢次罪行屬於情節惡劣。

## 4. **Hypothesis** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /haɪˈpɒθ.ə.sɪs/
**Definition:** An idea or theory that is not yet proven but is used as a starting point for further investigation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 假設；假說

**Example 1:**
The mathematician claims to have finally proved the famous Riemann hypothesis.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位數學家聲稱終於證明咗著名嘅黎曼假說。

**Example 2:**
We need more data to confirm whether our initial hypothesis is correct.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋需要更多數據去確認我哋最初嘅假設係咪正確。

## 5. **Verification** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌver.ɪ.fɪˈkeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The process of checking or proving that something is true, accurate, or genuine.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 核實；驗證

**Example 1:**
The scientific community requires independent verification of the new discovery.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
科學界要求對呢項新發現進行獨立驗證。

**Example 2:**
Please wait for email verification before you can access your account.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請等待電郵核實，然後你先可以登入你嘅帳戶。

## 6. **Emissions** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /iˈmɪʃ.ənz/
**Definition:** The production and discharge of something, especially gas or radiation, into the atmosphere.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 排放（物）

**Example 1:**
The new trade deal includes strict regulations on carbon emissions for food exports.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢份新貿易協議包括咗對食品出口碳排放嘅嚴格規定。

**Example 2:**
Many countries are working together to reduce harmful industrial emissions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
好多國家正喺度合作去減少有害嘅工業排放。
