# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-21

Hi there! Here is your English lesson for today, focusing on vocabulary relevant to current global events and professional news.

## 1. **Raft** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɑːft/
**Definition:** A large number or range of something, often policies or measures.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大量；一批（通常指政策或措施）

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister announced a new raft of policies to help families with the cost of living.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相宣布了一批新的政策，以幫助家庭應對生活成本問題。

**Example 2:**
There is a raft of evidence suggesting that the new regulations will improve efficiency.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
有大量證據顯示新規定將會提高效率。

## 2. **In-depth** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈdepθ/
**Definition:** Done in great detail; thorough and comprehensive.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 深入的；詳盡的

**Example 1:**
The newspaper published an in-depth report on the new cabinet appointments.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
報紙刊登了一篇關於新內閣任命的深入報導。

**Example 2:**
We need an in-depth analysis of the market before making any major investment decisions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在做出任何重大投資決定之前，我們需要對市場進行深入分析。

## 3. **Duties** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdjuːtiz/
**Definition:** Taxes imposed by a government on imported goods.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 關稅

**Example 1:**
The government is imposing higher import duties on electronics from abroad.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在對外國進口的電子產品徵收更高的關稅。

**Example 2:**
These trade duties are expected to cause prices to rise for local consumers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這些貿易關稅預計會導致本地消費者的物價上漲。

## 4. **Embargo** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪmˈbɑːɡəʊ/
**Definition:** An official ban on trade or other commercial activity with a particular country.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 禁運；貿易限制

**Example 1:**
The group announced a maritime embargo to block supplies from reaching the ports.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該組織宣布了一項海上禁運，以阻止物資進入港口。

**Example 2:**
International pressure led to the lifting of the oil embargo last year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
國際壓力導致去年解除了石油禁運。

## 5. **Extradition** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌekstrəˈdɪʃn/
**Definition:** The act of sending a person accused or convicted of a crime to the country where they committed the crime.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 引渡

**Example 1:**
The legal team is fighting against the extradition request to ensure the brothers stay in the country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法律團隊正在反對引渡請求，以確保這對兄弟能留在國內。

**Example 2:**
Extradition proceedings can take several months or even years to complete.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
引渡程序可能需要幾個月甚至幾年的時間才能完成。

## 6. **Cartel** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kɑːˈtel/
**Definition:** An association of manufacturers or suppliers with the purpose of maintaining prices at a high level and restricting competition, often used for illegal drug organizations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 卡特爾（壟斷組織）；販毒集團

**Example 1:**
The police have spent years trying to dismantle the powerful drug cartel.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方多年來一直試圖瓦解那個強大的販毒集團。

**Example 2:**
The cartel controlled the distribution of illegal substances across the border.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該集團控制了邊境兩側非法物品的流通。

## 7. **Tournament** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtʊənəmənt/
**Definition:** A series of contests between a number of competitors, often in a sport.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 錦標賽；聯賽

**Example 1:**
Many illegal websites were shut down during the international football tournament.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在國際足球錦標賽期間，許多非法網站被關閉。

**Example 2:**
She has been training hard for the upcoming tennis tournament.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她一直為即將到來的網球錦標賽努力訓練。
