# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-01

## 1. **Honeymoon** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhʌn.i.muːn/
**Definition:** A short initial period of time when a new leader or person in a new job is treated with special kindness and given time to settle in before being criticized.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 蜜月期（指新官上任初期受公眾或媒體優待的時期）

**Example 1:**
The new Prime Minister hopes his honeymoon period will last long enough to pass the budget.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新任首相希望他的蜜月期能足夠長，讓他順利通過預算案。

**Example 2:**
Public support often drops once the honeymoon is over and the reality of governing sets in.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
一旦蜜月期結束，執政的現實顯現出來，公眾支持率往往會下降。

## 2. **Fast-track** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌfɑːstˈtræk/
**Definition:** To make a process happen or finish more quickly than usual.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加快（流程或計劃的進度）

**Example 1:**
The government decided to fast-track the funding to address the severe overcrowding in prisons.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府決定加快撥款，以解決監獄嚴重擠擁的問題。

**Example 2:**
We need to fast-track this application to meet the deadline next week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們需要加快這份申請的流程，以趕上下週的截止日期。

## 3. **Anxiously** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈæŋk.ʃəs.li/
**Definition:** In a way that shows you are worried or nervous about something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 焦急地；憂慮地

**Example 1:**
The families are waiting anxiously for news about the trapped workers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
家屬們正焦急地等待受困工人的消息。

**Example 2:**
He looked around anxiously as the train failed to arrive on time.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於火車沒有準時到達，他焦急地四處張望。

## 4. **Resorted to** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzɔːrt tuː/
**Definition:** To do something that you do not want to do because you cannot find any other way of achieving something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 採取（某種手段，通常是不理想的）；訴諸

**Example 1:**
Rescue teams have resorted to using explosives to clear the blocked hillside.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援隊已採取使用炸藥的方式來清理受阻的山坡。

**Example 2:**
Faced with financial ruin, he eventually resorted to selling his family home.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
面對經濟破產，他最終不得不訴諸於賣掉祖屋。

## 5. **Alleged** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈledʒd/
**Definition:** Stated or described as something, especially a crime, but not yet proved to be true.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 涉嫌的；聲稱的

**Example 1:**
The public defender is representing the singer in the alleged murder case.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該名公設辯護律師正在為涉嫌謀殺案的歌手進行辯護。

**Example 2:**
The police are investigating the alleged theft of confidential documents from the firm.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
警方正在調查該公司涉嫌被竊的機密文件。

## 6. **Listing** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɪs.tɪŋ/
**Definition:** The act of a company’s shares being officially accepted for trading on a stock exchange.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （股票）上市

**Example 1:**
Shein’s long-awaited Hong Kong listing finally took place this Tuesday.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
希音（Shein）期待已久的香港上市終於在本週二完成。

**Example 2:**
Many tech startups are currently planning their stock market listing for the next year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多科技初創公司目前正計劃明年在證券交易所上市。

## 7. **Volatility** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌvɒl.əˈtɪl.ə.ti/
**Definition:** The quality of being likely to change suddenly and unexpectedly, especially in a way that is bad.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 波動性；易變性

**Example 1:**
The Bank of England governor warned that AI could lead to increased market volatility.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英格蘭銀行行長警告說，人工智能可能會導致市場波動加劇。

**Example 2:**
Investors are concerned about the volatility of the global economy due to energy prices.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
投資者對能源價格帶來的全球經濟波動感到擔憂。
