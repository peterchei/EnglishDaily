# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-06

## 1. **Reprisal** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈpraɪ.zəl/
**Definition:** An act of retaliation or a counter-attack against an enemy or opponent.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 報復；報復行動

**Example 1:**
The country launched missile strikes as a reprisal for the attack on its ships.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該國發動導彈襲擊，作為對其船隻遇襲的報復。

**Example 2:**
He was afraid that speaking out would lead to a professional reprisal from his boss.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他擔心說出真相會引來老闆在工作上的報復。

## 2. **Capped** (Adjective/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kæpt/
**Definition:** Having a limit or maximum amount set on something, such as prices or usage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 有上限的；被限制的

**Example 1:**
The energy company has capped the monthly subscription cost for its digital services.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家能源公司已為其數字服務的月費訂閱設下上限。

**Example 2:**
With the new policy, overtime hours are now capped at ten hours per week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
根據新政策，現在每週加班時間上限為十小時。

## 3. **Airlifted** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈeə.lɪf.tɪd/
**Definition:** Transported to a place by aircraft, usually in an emergency.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 空運；用飛機運送（通常指緊急情況）

**Example 1:**
The injured hiker was airlifted to the nearest hospital by a rescue helicopter.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
受傷的遠足者由救援直升機空運到最近的醫院。

**Example 2:**
Thousands of refugees were airlifted out of the region before the conflict intensified.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在衝突加劇之前，數千名難民被空運離開該地區。

## 4. **Undercover** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌn.dəˈkʌv.ər/
**Definition:** Working or done in secret to investigate or gather information.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 臥底的；秘密進行的

**Example 1:**
The journalist conducted an undercover investigation into the company’s hiring practices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位記者對該公司的招聘行為進行了臥底調查。

**Example 2:**
The police officer went undercover to gather evidence against the criminal gang.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這名警官進行臥底，以收集針對犯罪集團的證據。

## 5. **Subscribers** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /səbˈskraɪ.bəz/
**Definition:** People who pay money to regularly receive a service, product, or access to content.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 訂閱用戶

**Example 1:**
The streaming platform now has over ten million active subscribers worldwide.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該串流平台目前在全球擁有超過一千萬名活躍訂閱用戶。

**Example 2:**
Subscribers will get early access to the new features starting next month.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
訂閱用戶將從下個月開始提前使用新功能。

## 6. **Intensify** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈten.sɪ.faɪ/
**Definition:** To become more extreme, stronger, or more serious.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加劇；強化；變得強烈

**Example 1:**
Climate change is expected to intensify the frequency of extreme weather events.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
氣候變化預計會加劇極端天氣事件的頻率。

**Example 2:**
The pressure to find a peaceful solution began to intensify after the latest incident.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
事件發生後，尋求和平解決方案的壓力開始加大。
