# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-25

## 1. **Legacy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈleɡəsi/
**Definition:** Something that is a result of events in the past or that someone has left behind after their death.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 遺產；後遺症；深遠影響

**Example 1:**
The independent panel will examine the health legacy of the atomic programme.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
獨立小組將會審查該核計劃所留下的健康後遺症。

**Example 2:**
He wants to leave a positive legacy for future generations of students.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他希望為未來的學生留下一份正面的遺產。

## 2. **Intercession** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪntəˈseʃn/
**Definition:** The action of intervening on behalf of another person, often to resolve a conflict.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 調停；代求；介入

**Example 1:**
The president’s intercession helped resolve the dispute between the two companies.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統的介入幫助解決了這兩間公司之間的糾紛。

**Example 2:**
She sought the intercession of her manager to settle the argument in the office.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她尋求經理的調停，以解決辦公室裡的爭執。

## 3. **Inextricably** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪnɪkˈstrɪkəbli/
**Definition:** In a way that is impossible to disentangle or separate.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 密不可分地；無法分開地

**Example 1:**
The CEO's political fortunes are now inextricably linked to the president's decisions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位行政總裁的政治命運現在與總統的決定密不可分。

**Example 2:**
Economic growth and environmental health are inextricably bound together.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經濟增長與環境健康是密不可分的。

## 4. **Sexting** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsekstɪŋ/
**Definition:** The act of sending sexually explicit messages or photos via mobile phone.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 發送色情訊息（文字或圖片）

**Example 1:**
Schools are concerned about the rise in sexting among teenagers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
學校對於青少年間發送色情訊息的增加感到憂慮。

**Example 2:**
Parents should talk to their children about the risks involved in sexting.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
家長應與子女討論發送色情訊息所涉及的風險。

## 5. **Mortality** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /mɔːˈtæləti/
**Definition:** The state of being subject to death; the number of deaths in a given population.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 死亡率；必死性

**Example 1:**
Leaked data show that the war has fundamentally changed mortality patterns in the region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
洩露的數據顯示，戰爭已從根本上改變了該地區的死亡率模式。

**Example 2:**
Advancements in medicine have significantly improved life expectancy and reduced infant mortality.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醫學的進步顯著提高了預期壽命並降低了嬰兒死亡率。

## 6. **Racketeering** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrækɪtərɪŋ/
**Definition:** Dishonest and fraudulent business dealings or illegal activities, often involving organized crime.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 敲詐勒索；非法勾當

**Example 1:**
The company was acquitted of racketeering charges after a long trial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司在經過長時間的審訊後，被判無罪，免受敲詐勒索的指控。

**Example 2:**
He was arrested for his involvement in a large-scale racketeering operation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他因參與一項大型的非法敲詐活動而被捕。

## 7. **Verdict** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvɜːdɪkt/
**Definition:** A decision on a disputed issue in a civil or criminal case or an inquest.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 裁決；判決；定論

**Example 1:**
The jury reached a unanimous verdict after three days of deliberation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
陪審團在審議三天後達成了一致的裁決。

**Example 2:**
We are still waiting for the final verdict on the new energy policy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們仍在等待關於新能源政策的最終定論。
