# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-13

## 1. **Tranche** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /trɑːntʃ/
**Definition:** A portion or slice of something, especially money or a group of items, released in stages.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 一部分，分期（款項或批次）

**Example 1:**
The government is releasing the second tranche of prisoners this week.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府將於本週釋放第二批囚犯。

**Example 2:**
We will receive the final tranche of funding in December.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們將在十二月收到最後一筆資金。

## 2. **Unflinchingly** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /ʌnˈflɪn.tʃɪŋ.li/
**Definition:** In a way that shows no fear or hesitation when dealing with something difficult or unpleasant.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 堅定地，不畏縮地

**Example 1:**
The minister spoke unflinchingly about the harsh realities of the new policy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
部長堅定地談到了新政策的嚴酷現實。

**Example 2:**
She stared unflinchingly at her opponent during the debate.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
辯論期間，她毫無畏縮地盯著對手。

## 3. **Soaring** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsɔː.rɪŋ/
**Definition:** Rising very quickly to a high level.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 急升的，飛漲的

**Example 1:**
Households are struggling to pay for soaring heating bills this winter.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
今個冬天，家庭正為飛漲的暖氣費而苦苦掙扎。

**Example 2:**
The company reported soaring profits after the successful product launch.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
產品成功發布後，該公司報告稱利潤急劇增長。

## 4. **Tight-lipped** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌtaɪtˈlɪpt/
**Definition:** Refusing to speak about something or keeping information secret.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 守口如瓶的，不願透露詳情的

**Example 1:**
The official remained tight-lipped about the details of the secret meeting.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
官員對於秘密會議的細節守口如瓶。

**Example 2:**
Moscow has been tight-lipped regarding the recent ceasefire proposals.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
莫斯科對於近期的停火建議保持沉默。

## 5. **Falsified** (Verb - Past Participle/Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɒl.sɪ.faɪd/
**Definition:** Altered information or documents dishonestly in order to mislead.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 偽造的，篡改的

**Example 1:**
He was caught using falsified documents to avoid paying his taxes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他因使用偽造文件以逃避繳稅而被抓獲。

**Example 2:**
The accountant falsified the records to cover up the missing money.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
會計師篡改了記錄以掩蓋失蹤的資金。

## 6. **Escalation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌes.kəˈleɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** A situation in which something becomes more intense, serious, or violent.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 惡化，升級（指衝突或事態）

**Example 1:**
The latest airport attack marks a significant escalation in the regional conflict.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
最近對機場的襲擊標誌著區域衝突的重大升級。

**Example 2:**
We must prevent any further escalation of the situation at the border.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們必須防止邊境局勢進一步惡化。

## 7. **Breached** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /briːtʃt/
**Definition:** Broken or failed to follow a law, agreement, or code of conduct; also refers to breaking through a defense.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 違反（法律/協議），突破（防線）

**Example 1:**
The minister warned that cyber defences could be easily breached by hackers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
部長警告稱網絡防禦可能會被黑客輕易突破。

**Example 2:**
The company breached the contract by failing to deliver the goods on time.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司因未能按時交貨而違反了合約。
