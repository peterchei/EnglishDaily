# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-18

## 1. **Giddily** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡɪd.əl.i/
**Definition:** In a manner showing high spirits or light-headed excitement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 輕浮地；興奮地

**Example 1:**
She laughed giddily when she heard the good news about her promotion.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
聽到升職的好消息時，她興奮得笑了起來。

**Example 2:**
The children ran giddily through the park after school ended.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
放學後，孩子們興高采烈地跑過公園。

## 2. **Elated** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈleɪ.tɪd/
**Definition:** Extremely happy and excited, often because of success.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 興高采烈的；得意洋洋的

**Example 1:**
The team was elated after winning the championship trophy in the final minute.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
球隊在最後一分鐘贏得冠軍獎盃後感到非常興奮。

**Example 2:**
He felt elated at the prospect of travelling abroad for the first time.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
想到第一次出國旅行，他感到非常雀躍。

## 3. **Beacon** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbiː.kən/
**Definition:** A light or fire used as a signal, or a person/place that provides inspiration.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 燈塔；指路明燈；標誌

**Example 1:**
The country is seen as a beacon of democracy in a region filled with conflict.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在這個充滿衝突的地區，該國被視為民主的燈塔。

**Example 2:**
The lighthouse serves as a vital beacon for ships navigating the rocky coast.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
那座燈塔是船隻在多岩海岸航行時重要的導航標誌。

## 4. **Torn** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /tɔːn/
**Definition:** Feeling uncertain about what to do because of choosing between two options.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 糾結的；猶豫不決的

**Example 1:**
I am torn between taking the new job offer or staying with my current company.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我對於接受新工作邀請還是留在現職公司感到十分糾結。

**Example 2:**
She was torn about whether to attend the wedding or go to her sister's graduation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她對於要參加婚禮還是去參加妹妹的畢業典禮感到猶豫不決。

## 5. **Existential** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌeɡ.zɪˈsten.ʃəl/
**Definition:** Relating to existence; often used to describe a threat that could destroy everything.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 存在性的；涉及生存的

**Example 1:**
Climate change is viewed by many scientists as an existential threat to humanity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多科學家認為氣候變化是對人類生存的威脅。

**Example 2:**
The company is facing an existential crisis after losing its biggest client.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在失去最大客戶後，這家公司正面臨生存危機。

## 6. **Delegation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdel.ɪˈɡeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** A group of people who represent a country or an organization at a meeting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 代表團

**Example 1:**
The trade delegation arrived in London to discuss new business opportunities.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
貿易代表團抵達倫敦，商討新的商業機會。

**Example 2:**
Our delegation will present the proposal to the board of directors tomorrow.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們的代表團明天將向董事會提交提案。

## 7. **String** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /strɪŋ/
**Definition:** A series of similar things, such as events or crimes, happening one after another.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 一連串；一串

**Example 1:**
Police are investigating a string of burglaries that occurred in the neighbourhood.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方正在調查鄰近地區發生的一連串入室盜竊案。

**Example 2:**
He had a string of bad luck throughout the entire year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他這一整年都運氣不佳，接連倒霉。
