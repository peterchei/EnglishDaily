# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-14

## 1. **Tribute** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtrɪbjuːt/
**Definition:** An act, statement, or gift that is intended to show gratitude, respect, or admiration.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 致敬、頌詞

**Example 1:**
The family paid a moving tribute to their loved one during the memorial service.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
家人在追思會上向摯愛作出了感人的致敬。

**Example 2:**
Many fans left flowers outside the stadium as a tribute to the legendary player.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多球迷在球場外獻花，以向這位傳奇球員致敬。

## 2. **Lobe** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ləʊb/
**Definition:** A rounded part of an organ, such as the brain, lung, or liver, that is partially separated from the rest of the organ.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 肺葉、耳垂

**Example 1:**
The surgeon successfully removed the upper lobe of his lung to treat the infection.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
外科醫生成功切除了他肺部的上葉以治療感染。

**Example 2:**
The brain's frontal lobe is responsible for decision-making and personality.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
大腦的額葉負責決策和性格。

## 3. **Insert** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈsɜːt/
**Definition:** To put something into something else.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 插入、放入

**Example 1:**
You need to insert your memory stick into the USB port to transfer the files.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
你需要將記憶棒插入USB接口來傳輸文件。

**Example 2:**
Please insert the coin into the slot before pressing the button.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請在按下按鈕前將硬幣插入投幣口。

## 4. **Criticism** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkrɪtɪsɪzəm/
**Definition:** The expression of disapproval of someone or something on the basis of perceived faults or mistakes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 批評、指責

**Example 1:**
The government is facing harsh criticism regarding the new delivery system.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正面臨關於新派送系統的嚴厲批評。

**Example 2:**
She accepted the constructive criticism and improved her work performance.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她接受了建設性的批評，並改善了工作表現。

## 5. **Misrepresent** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌmɪsˈreprɪzent/
**Definition:** To give a false or misleading account of the nature of someone or something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 歪曲、不實陳述

**Example 1:**
Some candidates misrepresent their qualifications on their CVs to get more interviews.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
有些求職者會在簡歷上歪曲自己的資歷，以獲得更多面試機會。

**Example 2:**
The advertisement was accused of misrepresenting the product's actual capabilities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該廣告被指責歪曲了產品的實際功能。

## 6. **Peer group** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpɪə ˌɡruːp/
**Definition:** A group of people of approximately the same age, status, or interests.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 同輩、同儕群體

**Example 1:**
Teenagers are often heavily influenced by the opinions of their peer group.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
青少年經常受到同輩群體意見的強烈影響。

**Example 2:**
It is important for graduates to stay connected with their peer group while looking for work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
畢業生在找工作時，與同儕保持聯繫非常重要。
