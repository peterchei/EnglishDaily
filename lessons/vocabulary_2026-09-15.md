# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-15

## 1. **Hoax** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /həʊks/
**Definition:** A humorous or malicious deception; a falsehood passed off as truth.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 騙局、惡作劇

**Example 1:**
The president dismissed the concerns about artificial intelligence as a dangerous hoax.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統將對人工智能的擔憂斥為一場危險的騙局。

**Example 2:**
Police confirmed that the bomb threat was a hoax intended to cause panic.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
警方證實該炸彈威脅是一場旨在引起恐慌的騙局。

## 2. **Coffers** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒf.əz/
**Definition:** Large chests or strongboxes used for holding money or valuables; government or organizational funds.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 金庫、國庫、資金儲備

**Example 1:**
The party’s coffers were significantly boosted by recent multi-million pound donations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該黨的金庫因最近數百萬英鎊的捐款而顯著增加。

**Example 2:**
Heavy taxes have helped to replenish the national coffers after the economic downturn.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
沉重的稅收有助於在經濟低迷後補充國庫。

## 3. **Airspace** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈeə.speɪs/
**Definition:** The section of the atmosphere above a particular country that is considered to be under its control.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 領空

**Example 1:**
Nato jets were scrambled after an unidentified drone entered Lithuanian airspace.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在一架身份不明的無人機進入立陶宛領空後，北約戰機緊急升空。

**Example 2:**
Commercial flights are often diverted if there is a risk to safety in restricted airspace.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果限制領空存在安全風險，商業航班通常會改道。

## 4. **Prestige** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /presˈtiːʒ/
**Definition:** The respect and admiration that someone or something gets for being successful or important.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 聲望、威望

**Example 1:**
Winning an Emmy is a mark of great prestige in the television industry.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
贏得艾美獎是電視行業中極具聲望的標誌。

**Example 2:**
The university has long enjoyed international prestige for its medical research.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這所大學因其醫學研究長期享有國際聲望。

## 5. **Trek** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /trek/
**Definition:** A long, difficult journey, typically made on foot.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 長途跋涉、艱苦的旅程

**Example 1:**
The wild dogs completed an exhausting 2,500-mile trek across the wilderness.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那些野狗完成了穿越荒野、長達2,500英里的艱苦跋涉。

**Example 2:**
It was a long trek to the summit, but the view was absolutely worth the effort.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
那是一段前往頂峰的長途跋涉，但景色絕對值得這份努力。

## 6. **Establishment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈstæb.lɪʃ.mənt/
**Definition:** A business organization, public institution, or household; a place of business.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 機構、場所、店鋪

**Example 1:**
Under the new rules, every licensed establishment must check the age of its customers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
根據新規定，每家持牌場所都必須核實顧客的年齡。

**Example 2:**
This is a reputable establishment that has been serving the local community for decades.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這是一家信譽良好的機構，數十年來一直為當地社區服務。
