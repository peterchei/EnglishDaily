# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-22

## 1. **Dismiss** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈsmɪs/
**Definition:** To officially remove someone from their job.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 解僱；免職

**Example 1:**
The CEO decided to dismiss the manager after the project failed.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於項目失敗，行政總裁決定解僱該經理。

**Example 2:**
He was dismissed from his post for failing to follow safety protocols.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他因未能遵守安全守則而被免職。

## 2. **Allowance** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈlaʊ.əns/
**Definition:** An amount of money that is allowed or given to someone for a specific purpose, often tax-free.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 津貼；免稅額

**Example 1:**
The government is debating whether to increase the personal tax allowance.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在討論是否增加個人免稅額。

**Example 2:**
She receives a monthly housing allowance from her company.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她每月從公司領取房屋津貼。

## 3. **Garnish** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡɑː.nɪʃ/
**Definition:** A small amount of food used to decorate a dish.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 裝飾配菜（通常指食物）

**Example 1:**
The chef used fresh herbs as a simple garnish for the soup.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
廚師用新鮮香草作為湯的簡單裝飾配菜。

**Example 2:**
The salad was served with a garnish of edible flowers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
那份沙律上碟時，配有可食用花卉作為裝飾。

## 4. **Shake-up** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃeɪk.ʌp/
**Definition:** A large-scale reorganization or change in an organization or government.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重組；大變動

**Example 1:**
There has been a major shake-up in the leadership team this morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
今天早上領導層進行了一次重大重組。

**Example 2:**
The department is undergoing a shake-up to improve its efficiency.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該部門正在進行重組以提高效率。

## 5. **Ditch** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪtʃ/
**Definition:** To get rid of something or someone that is no longer wanted.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 拋棄；丟掉

**Example 1:**
It is time to ditch the old office dress code and wear something more comfortable.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
現在是時候拋棄舊有的辦公室著裝要求，穿些更舒適的衣服了。

**Example 2:**
He decided to ditch his car and start taking the bus to work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他決定賣掉汽車，改為乘巴士上班。

## 6. **Axed** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ækst/
**Definition:** To be removed, cancelled, or terminated, especially regarding jobs or programs.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被裁撤；被取消

**Example 1:**
Several long-running television shows were axed by the network this year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
電視台今年取消了多個長壽電視節目。

**Example 2:**
Many administrative roles were axed during the company's budget cuts.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在公司的預算削減期間，許多行政職位被裁撤了。
