# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-22

## 1. **Levy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈliːvi/
**Definition:** A tax, fee, or fine imposed by an authority.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 徵稅、稅款

**Example 1:**
The government introduced a new levy on imported goods to protect local industries.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府對進口商品徵收新稅，以保護本地產業。

**Example 2:**
There is a small levy included in the price of your flight for carbon offsetting.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你的機票價格中包含了一筆用於碳補償的小額徵費。

## 2. **Stems from** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /stɛmz frɒm/
**Definition:** To arise or originate from a specific cause or source.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 源於、起因於

**Example 1:**
The current dispute stems from a disagreement over trade boundaries.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
目前的糾紛源於對貿易邊界的意見分歧。

**Example 2:**
Her success stems from years of hard work and dedication.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她的成功源於多年的努力與奉獻。

## 3. **Defying** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfaɪɪŋ/
**Definition:** Openly resisting or refusing to obey something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公然反抗、蔑視

**Example 1:**
The company continued to operate, defying the new local regulations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司繼續營運，公然反抗當地的新法規。

**Example 2:**
Defying all expectations, the student passed the exam with top marks.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這名學生出人意料地以最高分通過了考試。

## 4. **Double-tap** (Adjective/Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdʌbəl tæp/
**Definition:** A military or tactical strike conducted in quick succession at the same location to hit rescue workers or those arriving after the first strike.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 二次打擊（戰術）

**Example 1:**
International observers condemned the double-tap attack on the civilian area.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際觀察員譴責了對該平民區進行的二次打擊。

**Example 2:**
The site of the first explosion was hit again in a brutal double-tap.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
第一次爆炸的地點在殘酷的二次打擊中再次遭到攻擊。

## 5. **Electrified** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈlɛktrɪfaɪd/
**Definition:** Converted to use electric power instead of other fuel sources.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 電動化的、電氣化的

**Example 1:**
The council is planning to replace all diesel buses with electrified models.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
市議會計劃將所有柴油巴士更換為電動車型。

**Example 2:**
More gardeners are switching to electrified tools to reduce noise pollution.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
越來越多園丁改用電動工具以減少噪音污染。

## 6. **Pioneer** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌpaɪəˈnɪər/
**Definition:** A person who is among the first to explore or settle in a new area.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 先驅、開拓者

**Example 1:**
She was a pioneer in the field of renewable energy research.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
她是可再生能源研究領域的先驅。

**Example 2:**
The pioneer families arrived at the site early this morning to build their new homes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
開拓者家庭今天清晨抵達現場，準備建造他們的新家。
