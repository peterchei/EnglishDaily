# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-20

## 1. **Credential** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /krɪˈden.ʃəl/
**Definition:** A qualification, achievement, or aspect of a person's background that indicates their suitability for something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 資格；證書；憑證

**Example 1:**
The hackers were able to guess the website credentials to gain unauthorized access to the servers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
黑客能夠猜出網站憑證，從而未經授權進入伺服器。

**Example 2:**
She has all the professional credentials required for the high-level management role.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她擁有擔任該高層管理職位所需的所有專業資格。

## 2. **Premises** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprem.ɪ.sɪz/
**Definition:** The land and buildings owned by someone, especially by a company or organization.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 處所；場所；廠房

**Example 1:**
Following the fire at the hotel, all guests were safely cleared from the premises.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
酒店發生火災後，所有住客已安全撤離該處所。

**Example 2:**
Smoking is strictly prohibited within the office premises.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
辦公場所內嚴禁吸煙。

## 3. **Annex** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈneks/
**Definition:** To take possession of an area of land or a country, usually by force or without permission from the people living there.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 兼併；吞併

**Example 1:**
There were international concerns when the leader threatened to annex the neighboring territory.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當該領導人威脅要吞併鄰近領土時，國際社會感到擔憂。

**Example 2:**
The small kingdom was eventually annexed by its more powerful neighbor.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個小王國最終被其更強大的鄰國吞併了。

## 4. **Trio** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtriː.əʊ/
**Definition:** A group of three people or things.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 三人組；三個一組

**Example 1:**
The new hosting trio will bring a fresh dynamic to the popular television show.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這組新的三人主持團隊將為這個受歡迎的電視節目帶來新的活力。

**Example 2:**
A musical trio performed classical pieces at the wedding ceremony.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
一個音樂三人組在婚禮上演奏了古典樂曲。

## 5. **Sceptical** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskep.tɪ.kəl/
**Definition:** Doubting that something is true or useful.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 懷疑的；持懷疑態度的

**Example 1:**
Many industry experts remain sceptical about whether AI can truly replace human creativity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多行業專家對人工智能是否能真正取代人類創造力仍持懷疑態度。

**Example 2:**
He was sceptical of the new marketing plan until he saw the positive results.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在看到正面結果之前，他對這個新的營銷計劃一直持懷疑態度。

## 6. **Depot** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdep.əʊ/
**Definition:** A building or place where supplies or vehicles are stored.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 倉庫；存儲場；站

**Example 1:**
The missile strike reportedly hit a fuel depot near the international airport.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
據報導，導彈襲擊擊中了國際機場附近的一個燃料庫。

**Example 2:**
The buses are repaired and cleaned at the central maintenance depot every night.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
巴士每晚都會在中央維修站進行修理和清潔。
