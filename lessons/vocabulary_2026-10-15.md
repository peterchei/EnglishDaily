# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-15

## 1. **Haze** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /heɪz/
**Definition:** A slight obscuration of the lower atmosphere, typically caused by fine suspended particles like smoke or dust.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 煙霾，霧霾

**Example 1:**
The city was covered in a thick orange haze due to the nearby wildfires.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於附近的山火，成個城市都被一層厚厚嘅橙色煙霾籠罩。

**Example 2:**
I could barely see the buildings through the morning haze.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
透過清晨嘅煙霾，我幾乎睇唔到啲建築物。

## 2. **Shore up** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ʃɔːr ʌp/
**Definition:** To support or strengthen something that is weak or likely to fail.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 鞏固，支持

**Example 1:**
The President visited various capitals to shore up support for his country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統訪問咗多個首都，以鞏固對佢國家嘅支持。

**Example 2:**
We need to shore up our finances before the end of the year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺年底之前，我哋需要鞏固我哋嘅財務狀況。

## 3. **Credentials** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /krɪˈdenʃəlz/
**Definition:** Qualifications, achievements, or aspects of a person's background that indicate their suitability for a job.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 資歷，證明文件

**Example 1:**
She was asked to present her academic credentials during the interview.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
面試嗰陣，佢被要求出示學歷證明。

**Example 2:**
Critics are questioning his professional credentials for such a high-level position.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
批評者質疑佢擔當呢個高層職位嘅專業資歷。

## 4. **Barbaric** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /bɑːˈbærɪk/
**Definition:** Extremely cruel, brutal, or uncivilized.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 野蠻嘅，殘忍嘅

**Example 1:**
Many people described the public execution as a barbaric act.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
好多人形容公開處決係一種野蠻嘅行為。

**Example 2:**
The treatment of the prisoners was considered barbaric by international observers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
國際觀察員認為對囚犯嘅對待方式好野蠻。

## 5. **Surge** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /sɜːdʒ/
**Definition:** A sudden, powerful forward or upward movement; a significant increase.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 急升，激增

**Example 1:**
The company reported a massive surge in revenue this quarter.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢間公司報告話今季收入有大幅度嘅激增。

**Example 2:**
There has been a sudden surge of interest in artificial intelligence.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
近期大家對人工智能嘅興趣突然激增。

## 6. **Taint** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /teɪnt/
**Definition:** To spoil or contaminate something, or to damage the reputation of someone or something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 玷污，損害

**Example 1:**
The scandal will surely taint the reputation of the entire department.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢單醜聞肯定會玷污成個部門嘅聲譽。

**Example 2:**
His career was tainted by allegations of corruption.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢嘅職業生涯因為貪污指控而蒙上污點。

## 7. **Prospects** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprɒspekts/
**Definition:** The possibility or likelihood of some future event occurring, especially success or career advancement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 前景，前途

**Example 1:**
The students are protesting because they are worried about their job prospects.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
學生們抗議係因為佢哋擔心自己嘅就業前景。

**Example 2:**
The company has excellent prospects for growth in the Asian market.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢間公司喺亞洲市場嘅增長前景非常之好。
