# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-01

## 1. **Scrap** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /skræp/
**Definition:** To abolish, discard, or cancel a plan or system.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 廢除、取消

**Example 1:**

**Cantonese Example 1:**

**Example 2:**

**Cantonese Example 2:**
