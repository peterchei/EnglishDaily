# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-04

## 1. **Candid** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkændɪd/
**Definition:** Truthful and straightforward; frank.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 坦誠的；直率的

**Example 1:**
The outgoing Prime Minister gave a candid interview about his reasons for stepping down.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
即將卸任嘅首相就佢辭職嘅原因，接受咗一個坦誠嘅訪問。

**Example 2:**
To be candid, I don't think the current plan will solve the problem effectively.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
坦白講，我唔認為目前嘅計劃可以有效解決到個問題。

## 2. **Bash** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /bæʃ/
**Definition:** A large or lively party or celebration.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 派對；盛會

**Example 1:**
The wedding at Madison Square Garden was described as the bash of the century.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
喺麥迪遜廣場花園舉行嗰場婚禮，被形容為世紀盛會。

**Example 2:**
Are you going to Sarah’s birthday bash this weekend?
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你呢個週末會唔會去 Sarah 嘅生日派對？

## 3. **Officiate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈfɪʃieɪt/
**Definition:** To perform the official duties of a ceremony, such as a wedding.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 主持（儀式等）

**Example 1:**
It was surprising to see a comedian officiate the celebrity wedding.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
見到一個喜劇演員去主持呢場名人婚禮，真係令人意外。

**Example 2:**
A local priest will officiate at the service tomorrow morning.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
聽日朝早個儀式會由一位本地牧師主持。

## 4. **Scorching** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskɔːtʃɪŋ/
**Definition:** Very hot; burning.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 酷熱的；極熱的

**Example 1:**
The capital is bracing for scorching temperatures during the 250th-anniversary celebrations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首都正為 250 週年慶典期間嘅酷熱天氣做好準備。

**Example 2:**
Make sure you stay in the shade because the sun is absolutely scorching today.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
記住留喺陰涼地方，因為今日個太陽真係好曬。

## 5. **Case out** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /keɪs aʊt/
**Definition:** To secretly observe a place or area to plan a crime or activity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 踩線；視察環境（尤指犯罪前）

**Example 1:**
The suspect spent several days casing out the scene of the attack before taking action.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
疑犯喺採取行動前，用咗幾日時間去視察襲擊現場嘅環境。

**Example 2:**
The gang was seen casing out the bank for weeks before the robbery.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰班賊喺打劫之前，被人見到佢哋連續幾個星期喺間銀行門外踩線。

## 6. **Makeshift** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmeɪkʃɪft/
**Definition:** Used as a temporary substitute for something else, usually in an emergency.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 臨時湊合的；權宜之計的

**Example 1:**
The hospital had to set up a makeshift morgue to accommodate the victims of the earthquake.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
醫院被迫設立一個臨時停屍間，以安置地震嘅遇難者。

**Example 2:**
We used a pile of boxes as a makeshift desk until our new furniture arrived.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺新傢俬送到之前，我哋用咗一疊箱做臨時書枱。

## 7. **Lie in state** (Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /laɪ ɪn steɪt/
**Definition:** To have a coffin placed in a public building so that people can pay their respects before a funeral.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 停靈（供公眾瞻仰）

**Example 1:**
The Ayatollah’s body will lie in state at the Grand Mosalla for the public to pay their respects.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
阿亞圖拉嘅遺體將會喺大清真寺停靈，畀公眾瞻仰。

**Example 2:**
Thousands of people queued for hours to see the former leader lying in state.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
成千上萬嘅人排咗幾個鐘隊，就係為咗瞻仰嗰位前領袖嘅遺容。
