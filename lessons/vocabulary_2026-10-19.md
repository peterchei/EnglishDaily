# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-19

Hello everyone! Here are 6 useful words for today’s lesson, selected from current events.

## 1. **Inequality** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪn.ɪˈkwɒl.ə.ti/
**Definition:** The unfair situation in society when some people have more opportunities, money, or rights than others.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 不平等

**Example 1:**
The report highlights the growing inequality in access to quality education across different regions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢份報告強調咗唔同地區喺獲取優質教育方面日益嚴重嘅不平等。

**Example 2:**
Addressing social inequality is a major priority for the new government.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
解決社會不平等係新政府嘅主要工作重點。

## 2. **Saboteur** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌsæb.əˈtɜːr/
**Definition:** A person who secretly damages or destroys something, especially for political or military reasons.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 破壞者、搞破壞嘅人

**Example 1:**
The authorities are hunting for the saboteur who damaged the power grid.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當局正喺度追捕嗰位破壞電力網嘅搞手。

**Example 2:**
He claimed to be a saboteur working to undermine the opposing forces from within.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢聲稱自己係一個由內部瓦解敵方勢力嘅破壞者。

## 3. **Reconnaissance** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈkɒn.ɪ.səns/
**Definition:** The activity of getting information about an area, especially by a military force.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 偵察

**Example 1:**
The drone was sent out on a reconnaissance mission to map the enemy's position.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
無人機被派去執行偵察任務，以繪製敵軍位置。

**Example 2:**
They gathered vital reconnaissance before launching their operation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢哋喺發動行動之前，搜集咗重要嘅偵察情報。

## 4. **Blockbuster** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈblɒkˌbʌs.tər/
**Definition:** Something that is very successful, large, or important, often used for films or business deals.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 非常成功嘅、轟動嘅（形容生意或作品）

**Example 1:**
The tech company is preparing for a blockbuster stock market listing next month.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢間科技公司正準備下個月進行一場轟動嘅上市活動。

**Example 2:**
Everyone is talking about this blockbuster deal between the two global firms.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
所有人都在談論呢兩間跨國公司之間呢單轟動嘅交易。

## 5. **Acceleration** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əkˌsel.əˈreɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The process of happening or developing more quickly than before.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加速

**Example 1:**
We have seen an acceleration in economic growth over the last two quarters.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
過去兩季，我哋見到經濟增長有所加速。

**Example 2:**
The acceleration of AI technology is changing the way we work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
人工智能技術嘅加速發展正改變我哋嘅工作方式。

## 6. **Domestic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dəˈmes.tɪk/
**Definition:** Relating to a person's own country, or relating to the home/family.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 國內嘅、家庭嘅

**Example 1:**
Domestic demand remains weak despite improvements in the export sector.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管出口業有所改善，但國內需求依然疲弱。

**Example 2:**
The minister returned home to deal with pressing domestic issues.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
部長返咗國去處理緊迫嘅國內事務。
