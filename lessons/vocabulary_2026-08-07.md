# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-07

## 1. **Classified** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈklæs.ɪ.faɪd/
**Definition:** Officially secret and accessible only to authorized people.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 機密的；保密的

**Example 1:**
The government refused to release the classified documents regarding the weapons supply.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府拒絕公開有關武器供應的機密文件。

**Example 2:**
He was dismissed for leaking classified information to the press.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他因為向媒體洩露機密資訊而被解僱。

## 2. **Disorder** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈsɔː.dər/
**Definition:** A state of confusion or a situation where people behave violently in public.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 混亂；騷亂

**Example 1:**
Police were called to the city centre to deal with the public disorder.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方被召喚到市中心處理公眾騷亂。

**Example 2:**
The ongoing disorder has forced many local shops to close early.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
持續的騷亂迫使許多當地商店提早關門。

## 3. **Genetic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dʒəˈnet.ɪk/
**Definition:** Relating to genes or the origin and development of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 基因的；遺傳的

**Example 1:**
Scientists are using AI to modify the genetic code of synthetic viruses.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
科學家正利用人工智能來修改合成病毒的基因密碼。

**Example 2:**
There is growing concern about the ethical implications of genetic engineering.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
人們對基因工程的倫理影響越來越感到擔憂。

## 4. **Consecutive** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈsek.jə.tɪv/
**Definition:** Following one after another without an interruption.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 連續的

**Example 1:**
The village has suffered through fifty consecutive days of dry weather.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該村莊已經歷了連續五十天的乾燥天氣。

**Example 2:**
She has won the championship for three consecutive years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她已經連續三年贏得冠軍。

## 5. **Daring** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdeə.rɪŋ/
**Definition:** Brave and taking risks.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大膽的；勇敢的

**Example 1:**
The aviation crew performed a daring rescue in the freezing darkness of Antarctica.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
航空機組人員在南極洲冰冷的黑暗中進行了一次大膽的救援。

**Example 2:**
It was a daring plan, but it ultimately succeeded.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這是一個大膽的計劃，但最終成功了。

## 6. **Boycott** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbɔɪ.kɒt/
**Definition:** A refusal to buy something or take part in something as a way of protesting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 抵制；杯葛

**Example 1:**
Uefa has warned that a boycott of the event remains a possibility.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
歐洲足協警告稱，抵制該活動仍然是一種可能。

**Example 2:**
The workers organized a boycott to demand better working conditions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
工人們發起了一場抵制行動，以要求改善工作條件。
