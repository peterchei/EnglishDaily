# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-20

## 1. **Buck** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /bʌk/
**Definition:** To resist, oppose, or go against something that is expected or established.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 對抗；抵制（趨勢或規則）

**Example 1:**
He hopes his new policies will buck the trend of declining economic growth.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他希望他的新政策能扭轉經濟增長放緩的趨勢。

**Example 2:**
It is difficult for a small startup to buck the market dominance of large tech firms.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
小型初創企業很難抵制大型科技公司的市場壟斷地位。

## 2. **Trite** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /traɪt/
**Definition:** Lacking originality or freshness; overused to the point of being boring.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 陳腔濫調的；老套的

**Example 1:**
Although the victory speech felt a bit trite, the fans were still moved by his sincerity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
雖然勝利演說聽起來有點老套，但球迷們仍被他的真誠所感動。

**Example 2:**
Avoid using trite phrases in your professional emails if you want to sound more authoritative.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果你想聽起來更有權威感，在專業郵件中應避免使用陳腔濫調。

## 3. **Blue-collar** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbluːˌkɒl.ə/
**Definition:** Relating to manual work or workers, typically involving physical labour.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 藍領的；體力勞動的

**Example 1:**
The book offers a fascinating insight into the daily lives of blue-collar workers in the city.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這本書提供了深入了解城市藍領工人日常生活的視角。

**Example 2:**
Many blue-collar jobs are currently facing a shortage of skilled personnel.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多藍領工作目前面臨技術人員短缺的問題。

## 4. **Unveiled** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnˈveɪld/
**Definition:** To show or announce something new or secret for the first time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公佈；推出；揭幕

**Example 1:**
The tech giant unveiled its latest artificial intelligence model during the conference yesterday.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家科技巨頭在昨天的會議上推出了其最新的人工智能模型。

**Example 2:**
A new plaque was unveiled in the park to honour the local heroes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公園裡揭幕了一塊新牌匾，以紀念當地的英雄。

## 5. **Neutral** (Noun/Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈnjuː.trəl/
**Definition:** Not supporting either side in a disagreement, competition, or war.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中立的；不偏不倚的

**Example 1:**
As a neutral observer, she provided a very balanced report on the political situation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
作為一名中立的觀察者，她對政治局勢提供了非常平衡的報導。

**Example 2:**
The referee must remain neutral throughout the entire football match.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
裁判必須在整場足球比賽中保持中立。

## 6. **Market-moving** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmɑː.kɪtˌmuː.vɪŋ/
**Definition:** Having a significant enough impact to cause changes in the price or value of stocks and financial assets.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 能影響市場走向的；足以撼動市場的

**Example 1:**
Investors are eagerly waiting for the market-moving announcement from the central bank.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
投資者正熱切等待中央銀行發布足以影響市場的消息。

**Example 2:**
He developed a platform that provides instant access to market-moving news for traders.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他開發了一個平台，為交易員提供即時的市場影響力新聞。
