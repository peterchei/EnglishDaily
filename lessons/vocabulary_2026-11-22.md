# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-22

Hello everyone! Here are 6 useful words and phrases based on today's current events for your daily practice.

## 1. **Overtime** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈəʊvə.taɪm/
**Definition:** Time spent working in addition to one's normal working hours, or an extra period of time added to a game or meeting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加時；加班

**Example 1:**
The climate summit entered its second day of overtime as negotiators struggled to reach a final deal.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於談判代表難以達成最終協議，氣候峰會進入了第二天的加時。

**Example 2:**
I had to work a lot of overtime this week to finish the project before the deadline.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
為了在截止日期前完成項目，我這週不得不加了很多班。

## 2. **Documentary** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdɒj.jəˈmen.tər.i/
**Definition:** A film or television or radio programme that provides a factual report on a particular subject.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 紀錄片

**Example 1:**
Catherine spoke openly about her health journey in a new documentary screened on the BBC.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
凱瑟琳在英國廣播公司播放的一部新紀錄片中，公開談論了她的健康歷程。

**Example 2:**
We watched a fascinating documentary about marine life last night.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們昨晚看了一部關於海洋生物的精彩紀錄片。

## 3. **Emulate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈem.jə.leɪt/
**Definition:** To copy something achieved by someone else and try to do it as well as they did.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 效仿；媲美

**Example 1:**
The Welsh rugby team could not emulate their previous success against New Zealand.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
威爾斯橄欖球隊未能複製他們之前對陣紐西蘭時的成功。

**Example 2:**
He hopes to emulate the achievements of his father in the family business.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他希望在家族生意中能效仿他父親的成就。

## 4. **Spoils** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /spɔɪlz/
**Definition:** The prizes or benefits that someone gains from winning a competition or conflict.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 戰利品；勝利果實

**Example 1:**
Australia claimed the Nations Championship spoils after their narrow victory in Cardiff.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
澳洲隊在卡迪夫取得險勝後，奪得了國家錦標賽的勝利果實。

**Example 2:**
After a long war, the victorious army divided the spoils among themselves.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
戰爭結束後，勝利的軍隊瓜分了戰利品。

## 5. **Database** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdeɪ.tə.beɪs/
**Definition:** A structured set of data held in a computer, especially one that is accessible in various ways.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 資料庫

**Example 1:**
The FBI is investigating an apparent hack of its central criminal database.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
聯邦調查局正在調查其中央犯罪資料庫疑似被駭客入侵的事件。

**Example 2:**
Our company uses a secure database to manage customer information.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們公司使用一個安全的資料庫來管理客戶資訊。

## 6. **Verification** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌver.ɪ.fɪˈkeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The process of establishing the truth, accuracy, or validity of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 核實；驗證

**Example 1:**
The scientific claim is exciting, but full verification will take some time.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這項科學宣稱令人振奮，但全面的驗證還需要一些時間。

**Example 2:**
Please provide proof of identity for verification purposes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請提供身分證明以便進行核實。
