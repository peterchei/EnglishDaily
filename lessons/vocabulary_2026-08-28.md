# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-28

## 1. **Capitalise** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkapɪtəˌlʌɪz/
**Definition:** To take the chance to gain an advantage from a situation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 利用（機會），從中獲利

**Example 1:**
Private care providers are looking to capitalise on the growing demand for autism assessments.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
私人護理機構正尋求利用對自閉症評估日益增長的需求來獲利。

**Example 2:**
The team failed to capitalise on their early lead and eventually lost the match.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該隊未能把握早期的領先優勢，最終輸掉了比賽。

## 2. **Blockbuster** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈblɒkˌbʌstə/
**Definition:** A thing of great power or size, especially a film or game that is very successful.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 轟動一時的產品（如大片或大作）

**Example 1:**
Grand Theft Auto 6 is expected to be the biggest blockbuster in gaming history.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
《俠盜獵車手 6》預計將成為遊戲史上最轟動的作品。

**Example 2:**
The studio spent millions on the blockbuster, hoping it would dominate the summer box office.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
製片廠在這部大片上投入了數百萬美元，希望它能稱霸暑期票房。

## 3. **Drafted** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /drɑːftɪd/
**Definition:** To select a person or group for a special task or duty.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 調派，徵召

**Example 1:**
The government drafted in extra police officers to manage the public disorder.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府調派了額外的警員來處理公共騷亂。

**Example 2:**
He was drafted into the committee because of his expertise in cybersecurity.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於他在網絡安全方面的專業知識，他被徵召進入委員會。

## 4. **Preliminary** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /prɪˈlɪmɪn(ə)ri/
**Definition:** Denoting an action or event preceding or preparing for something more important.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 初步的，預備的

**Example 1:**
Scientists provided a preliminary report on the cause of the glacier collapse.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
科學家就冰川崩塌的原因提供了初步報告。

**Example 2:**
We need to conduct a preliminary check before we start the main experiment.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在開始主要實驗之前，我們需要進行初步檢查。

## 5. **Jury** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdʒʊəri/
**Definition:** A body of people sworn to give a verdict in a legal case on the basis of evidence submitted to them.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 陪審團

**Example 1:**
The jury is currently deliberating on the verdict for the murder trial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
陪審團目前正在就這宗謀殺案的裁決進行商議。

**Example 2:**
The jury reached a unanimous decision after three days of discussion.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
陪審團在討論三天後達成了一致的裁決。

## 6. **Sap** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /sap/
**Definition:** To gradually weaken or destroy someone's strength, courage, or confidence.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 削弱，使（信心、精力）流失

**Example 1:**
A string of technical failures has sapped the public's confidence in robotic technology.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
一系列技術故障削弱了公眾對機器人技術的信心。

**Example 2:**
Long working hours can slowly sap your energy and motivation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
長時間工作會慢慢耗盡你的精力和動力。
