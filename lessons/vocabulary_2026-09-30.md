# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-30

## 1. **Balaclava** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌbæləˈklɑːvə/
**Definition:** A close-fitting garment covering the whole head and neck except for parts of the face, typically made of wool.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 頭套（只露出眼鼻口的滑雪面罩）

**Example 1:**
The police were alerted after witnesses spotted several men wearing balaclavas near the site.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
有目擊者見到幾個戴住頭套嘅男人喺嗰個地點附近出現後，警方隨即接獲通報。

**Example 2:**
He pulled his black balaclava down to hide his face before entering the shop.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢入舖頭之前拉低咗黑色頭套嚟遮住塊面。

## 2. **Bail** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /beɪl/
**Definition:** The temporary release of an accused person awaiting trial, sometimes on condition that a sum of money be lodged to guarantee their appearance in court.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 保釋

**Example 1:**
The detectives released the suspects on bail while the investigation continues.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
探員喺調查期間將疑犯保釋外出。

**Example 2:**
She was granted bail after appearing before the magistrate this morning.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢今朝上庭之後獲准保釋。

## 3. **Triple lock** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtrɪpəl lɒk/
**Definition:** A UK government policy ensuring that the state pension rises by the highest of three measures: earnings growth, inflation, or 2.5%.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （英國退休金）三重保障機制

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister announced plans to end the existing pension triple lock by 2030.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相宣佈計劃喺2030年之前終止現行嘅退休金三重保障機制。

**Example 2:**
Many pensioners are concerned about the future of the triple lock system.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
好多退休人士都好擔心三重保障機制嘅未來。

## 4. **Grieving** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡriːvɪŋ/
**Definition:** Feeling or expressing intense sorrow, especially caused by someone's death.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 悲痛的；哀傷的

**Example 1:**
The speech was delivered by a prime minister who is also a grieving son.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢番講話係由一位同時亦係痛失至親嘅首相所發表。

**Example 2:**
The community offered support to the grieving family after the tragic accident.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰單慘劇發生後，社區向嗰個悲痛嘅家庭提供咗支援。

## 5. **Bioweapon** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbaɪəʊˌwepən/
**Definition:** A bacterium, virus, or other microorganism that can be used in war to kill or injure people, animals, or plants.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 生物武器

**Example 1:**
Researchers were shocked when the AI tool explained how to create a bioweapon.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當嗰個人工智能工具講解點樣製造生物武器時，研究人員都感到好震驚。

**Example 2:**
International treaties strictly prohibit the development and use of bioweapons.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
國際條約嚴格禁止研發同埋使用生物武器。

## 6. **Eviction** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈvɪkʃn/
**Definition:** The action of expelling someone, especially a tenant, from a property.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 驅逐（租客）；收樓

**Example 1:**
The government announced a temporary ban on evictions following widespread protests.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府喺大規模示威之後宣佈暫時禁止驅逐租客。

**Example 2:**
She faced eviction from her flat after falling behind on her rent payments.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢因為遲交租而面臨被趕出住所。

## 7. **Personnel** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌpɜːsəˈnel/
**Definition:** People employed in an organization or engaged in an organized undertaking, such as military service.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 人員；員工（通常指軍隊或機構）

**Example 1:**
Three South Korean military personnel were injured in the explosion.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
三名南韓軍事人員喺爆炸中受傷。

**Example 2:**
The company is hiring more security personnel to monitor the facility.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
間公司正聘請更多保安人員去監察嗰個設施。
