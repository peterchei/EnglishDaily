# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-04

## 1. **Mishandling** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌmɪsˈhændlɪŋ/
**Definition:** The act of managing or dealing with something in an inefficient or dishonest way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 處理不當、不妥善處理

**Example 1:**
The company fired several staff members for the mishandling of sensitive client data.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司因員工處理敏感客戶資料不當而解僱了他們。

**Example 2:**
The public inquiry will focus on the government's mishandling of the housing crisis.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公開調查將集中於政府對房屋危機的處理不當。

## 2. **Memorial** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /məˈmɔːriəl/
**Definition:** A structure or event established to remind people of a person or an event that has passed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 紀念碑、追悼會

**Example 1:**
Families gathered at the local park for a memorial to honour those who were lost.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
各個家庭聚集在公園舉行追悼會，以紀念逝者。

**Example 2:**
They are planning to build a stone memorial in the city centre.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們正計劃在市中心興建一座石製紀念碑。

## 3. **Execution** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌeksɪˈkjuːʃn/
**Definition:** The act of carrying out a sentence of death, or the process of performing a task.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 處決、執行（任務）

**Example 1:**
The prison governor resigned following the failure of the scheduled execution.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
監獄典獄長在預定的處決失敗後辭職了。

**Example 2:**
The project failed not because of the idea, but because of poor execution.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個項目失敗並非因為構思不好，而是因為執行過程很差。

## 4. **Frat** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /fræt/
**Definition:** Short for 'fraternity', a social organization for male university students in North America.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大學兄弟會

**Example 1:**
The university has launched an investigation into allegations regarding a popular frat house.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
大學已針對涉及一間熱門兄弟會宿舍的指控展開調查。

**Example 2:**
Students often choose to live in a frat house to build social connections.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學生通常會選擇住在兄弟會宿舍來建立社交人脈。

## 5. **Disturbing** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈstɜːbɪŋ/
**Definition:** Causing anxiety; worrying or upsetting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 令人不安的、煩擾的

**Example 1:**
The president described the reports of violence at the campus as deeply disturbing.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
校長形容有關校園暴力的報告令人極度不安。

**Example 2:**
It is a disturbing trend that more young people are struggling with their mental health.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
越來越多年輕人的心理健康出現問題，這是一個令人不安的趨勢。

## 6. **Cliff edge** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌklɪf ˈedʒ/
**Definition:** A point at which a sudden and significant change happens, often causing a sharp loss of benefits or support.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 懸崖邊緣（比喻突然失去福利或支援的臨界點）

**Example 1:**
The government wants to remove the childcare cliff edge to encourage more parents to return to work.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府希望消除育兒津貼的「懸崖邊緣」，以鼓勵更多父母重返職場。

**Example 2:**
Many families find themselves at a financial cliff edge when their temporary support ends.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多家庭在臨時補助結束時，會發現自己陷入了經濟危機的臨界點。

## 7. **Drugged** (Adjective/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /drʌɡd/
**Definition:** Made unconscious or less aware by the administration of a drug.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被下藥的

**Example 1:**
The victim claimed she had been drugged before the assault took place.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
受害者聲稱她在襲擊發生前被下了藥。

**Example 2:**
The police are investigating whether the suspect drugged the victim to commit the robbery.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
警方正在調查嫌疑人是否為了搶劫而向受害者下藥。
