# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-21

## 1. **Lingers** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɪŋɡəz/
**Definition:** To stay in a place or to continue to exist for longer than expected.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 徘徊；逗留；（感覺、氣味等）揮之不去

**Example 1:**
The scent of her perfume lingers in the room long after she has left.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
佢走咗之後，房入面仲殘留住佢陣香水味。

**Example 2:**
The hurt from their argument still lingers in his mind.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢哋嗌完交之後，嗰種傷害依然留喺佢心入面。

## 2. **Milestone** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmaɪlstəʊn/
**Definition:** An important event or stage in the development of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 里程碑；重大轉折點

**Example 1:**
Reaching a trillion dollars in debt is a significant economic milestone for the country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
債務達到一萬億美元，係呢個國家經濟上嘅一個重大里程碑。

**Example 2:**
Learning to drive was a major milestone in my teenage years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
學識揸車係我青少年時期一個重要嘅里程碑。

## 3. **Adept** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈædɛpt/
**Definition:** Very skilled or proficient at something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 熟練的；擅長的

**Example 1:**
Iran has proven adept at navigating international trade sanctions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
伊朗已經證明咗佢哋好擅長應付國際貿易制裁。

**Example 2:**
She is very adept at fixing computers when they break down.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢好擅長喺電腦壞咗嗰陣進行維修。

## 4. **Looping** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈluːpɪŋ/
**Definition:** Describing a sequence of images or sound that repeats continuously.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 循環播放的

**Example 1:**
The new app features six-second looping videos that play over and over again.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢個新應用程式主打六秒循環播放嘅影片，會不斷重複咁播。

**Example 2:**
He created a looping animation to use as his computer background.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢整咗一個循環動畫做佢嘅電腦背景。

## 5. **Subvert** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /səbˈvɜːt/
**Definition:** To try to destroy or damage the power or authority of a government or political system.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 顛覆；破壞（政治體制）

**Example 1:**
The activists were charged with plotting to subvert state power.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢啲活躍分子被控密謀顛覆國家政權。

**Example 2:**
The group tried to subvert the established rules of the club.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰班人試圖去破壞個會原本訂立嘅規矩。

## 6. **Settlement** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsɛtəlmənt/
**Definition:** A place where people establish a community; in this context, specific housing areas built in occupied territories.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 定居點；殖民區

**Example 1:**
The international community continues to criticize the expansion of the West Bank settlement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際社會持續批評西岸定居點嘅擴張。

**Example 2:**
Archaeologists discovered the remains of an ancient settlement near the river.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
考古學家喺河邊發現咗一個古代定居點嘅遺蹟。
