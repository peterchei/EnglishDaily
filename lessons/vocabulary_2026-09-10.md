# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-10

## 1. **Levy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈliːvi/
**Definition:** A tax, fee, or fine imposed and collected by a government or authority.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 徵稅；稅款

**Example 1:**
The local council decided to introduce a new tourist levy on overnight hotel stays.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
地方議會決定對過夜酒店住宿徵收新的旅客稅。

**Example 2:**
The government is considering a levy on sugary drinks to improve public health.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府正考慮對含糖飲料徵稅，以改善公眾健康。

## 2. **Benchmark** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbentʃmɑːk/
**Definition:** A standard or point of reference against which things may be compared or assessed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 基準；參照點

**Example 1:**
Brent crude serves as the global benchmark for oil prices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
布倫特原油是全球石油價格的基準。

**Example 2:**
This test score will act as a benchmark for measuring student progress throughout the year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個考試分數將作為衡量學生全年進步的基準。

## 3. **Earmarked** (Adjective/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪəmɑːkt/
**Definition:** Designated or set aside for a particular purpose or destination.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 指定用途的；撥出的

**Example 1:**
The land was earmarked by the authorities for future housing developments.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這塊土地已被當局指定用於未來的住房開發。

**Example 2:**
A large portion of the budget has been earmarked for healthcare improvements.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
預算中很大一部分已被指定用於醫療保健方面的改善。

## 4. **Vessel** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvesl/
**Definition:** A ship or large boat, typically used for transporting goods or people.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 船隻；艦艇

**Example 1:**
The fire broke out while the vessel was nearing its final destination.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
火災發生時，船隻正接近其最終目的地。

**Example 2:**
Several rescue vessels were deployed to help the stranded passengers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
幾艘救援船隻被派去幫助受困的乘客。

## 5. **Surge** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /sɜːdʒ/
**Definition:** A sudden, powerful forward or upward movement, or a significant increase in something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 激增；急升

**Example 1:**
The police were warned about a potential surge of migrants at the border.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方接獲警告，邊境可能會出現移民激增的情況。

**Example 2:**
There has been a sudden surge in demand for electricity during the heatwave.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
熱浪期間，電力需求突然激增。

## 6. **Data centre** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdeɪtə ˌsentə/
**Definition:** A large group of networked computer servers used by organizations for the remote storage, processing, or distribution of large amounts of data.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 數據中心

**Example 1:**
Google has chosen Finland as the location for its massive new data centre.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
谷歌選擇芬蘭作為其大型新數據中心的所在地。

**Example 2:**
Modern businesses rely heavily on the efficiency of their data centre infrastructure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
現代企業非常依賴其數據中心基礎設施的效率。

## 7. **Healthcare** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhelθkeə/
**Definition:** The organized provision of medical care to individuals or a community.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 醫療保健

**Example 1:**
The government is introducing new laws to regulate the use of AI in healthcare.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在引入新法律，以規範人工智能在醫療保健中的應用。

**Example 2:**
Providing high-quality healthcare for an aging population is a major challenge.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
為高齡人口提供高質素的醫療保健是一項重大挑戰。
