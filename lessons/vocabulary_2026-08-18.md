# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-18

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary appearing in recent news headlines.

## 1. **Expiration** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌek.spɪˈreɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The date or time at which something comes to an end.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 到期、終止

**Example 1:**
The expiration of the peace deal could lead to further conflict in the region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
和平協議嘅到期可能會導致該地區進一步嘅衝突。

**Example 2:**
Please check the expiration date on your passport before booking a flight.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
請喺訂機票前檢查你護照嘅到期日。

## 2. **Trauma** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtrɔː.mə/
**Definition:** Severe emotional shock or physical injury.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 創傷、精神受創

**Example 1:**
The coroner found no signs of physical trauma in the actress's passing.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
驗屍官喺呢位女演員離世時並冇發現任何身體創傷嘅跡象。

**Example 2:**
It takes a long time to recover from the trauma of such a difficult experience.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
要從呢種艱難經歷嘅創傷中恢復過嚟需要很長時間。

## 3. **Substantially** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /səbˈstæn.ʃəl.i/
**Definition:** To a large or significant degree.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大幅度地、相當大地

**Example 1:**
The government plans to substantially reduce the number of military exercises this year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府計劃喺今年大幅度減少軍事演習嘅數量。

**Example 2:**
Our company’s profits have grown substantially since the new strategy was implemented.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
自從執行新策略以來，我哋公司嘅盈利已經大幅增長。

## 4. **Cooperate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kəʊˈɒp.ər.eɪt/
**Definition:** To work together toward a common end or to help someone by doing what they ask.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 合作、配合

**Example 1:**
The suspect refused to cooperate with the police during the investigation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嫌疑人喺調查期間拒絕配合警方。

**Example 2:**
If we cooperate, we can finish this project by the end of the day.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果我哋合作，我哋就可以喺今日結束前完成呢個項目。

## 5. **Deserted** (Verb - Past Tense)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈzɜː.tɪd/
**Definition:** To leave someone or something behind, especially in a disloyal or unfair way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 拋棄、離棄

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister felt betrayed after several members of his cabinet deserted him.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相喺幾位內閣成員離棄佢之後感到被背叛。

**Example 2:**
He deserted his post during the crisis and fled the city.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢喺危機期間拋棄咗佢嘅職位並逃離咗嗰個城市。

## 6. **Overhaul** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈəʊ.və.hɔːl/
**Definition:** A thorough examination and repair, or a complete change to improve a system.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 全面檢查、徹底改革

**Example 1:**
The social media platform faces a massive overhaul to improve child safety.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢個社交媒體平台面臨一次大規模嘅徹底改革，以提升兒童安全。

**Example 2:**
The car needs an engine overhaul before it can be driven safely again.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
架車需要進行一次引擎全面檢查先可以再次安全行駛。
