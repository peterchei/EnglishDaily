# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-16

## 1. **Ascend** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈsend/
**Definition:** To move upward or to rise to a higher position, rank, or status.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 上升；登位；攀登

**Example 1:**
The crown prince will officially ascend the throne next year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
王儲將於明年正式登位。

**Example 2:**
We watched the hot air balloon ascend slowly into the clear blue sky.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我們看着熱氣球緩緩升向湛藍的天空。

## 2. **Pulp** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /pʌlp/
**Definition:** To crush something into a soft, wet, shapeless mass, often used in the context of recycling paper or books.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 把……打成紙漿；搗爛

**Example 1:**
The unsold copies of the magazine were sent to be pulped.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
未售出的雜誌被送去打成紙漿。

**Example 2:**
The heavy rain pulped the cardboard boxes left outside.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
大雨把留在外面的紙箱浸爛成漿。

## 3. **Succession** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /səkˈseʃ.ən/
**Definition:** The process or right of inheriting a title, office, or property.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 繼承；接班

**Example 1:**
The royal family updated the laws of succession to ensure equality.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
皇室更新了繼承法以確保平等。

**Example 2:**
There was a peaceful succession when the CEO retired last month.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
上個月行政總裁退休時，進行了平穩的接班過渡。

## 4. **Infrastructure** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪn.frəˌstrʌk.tʃər/
**Definition:** The basic physical and organizational structures (e.g., buildings, power grids, transport) needed for a society to operate.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 基礎設施

**Example 1:**
The government is investing heavily in transport infrastructure to reduce traffic jams.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正投放大量資金於交通基礎設施，以減少塞車。

**Example 2:**
Military strikes targeted the region's energy infrastructure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
軍事襲擊針對了該地區的能源基礎設施。

## 5. **Assessment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈses.mənt/
**Definition:** The act of judging or deciding the amount, value, quality, or importance of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 評估；評價

**Example 1:**
The team conducted a rapid assessment of the earthquake damage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
團隊對地震造成的破壞進行了快速評估。

**Example 2:**
Your final grade is based on a written exam and a practical assessment.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你的最終成績是根據筆試和實務評估來計算的。

## 6. **Welfare** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈwel.feər/
**Definition:** Financial support or services provided by the government to help people who are in need, unemployed, or ill.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 福利；社會保障

**Example 1:**
The new policy aims to reform the welfare system to encourage employment.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新政策旨在改革福利制度，以鼓勵就業。

**Example 2:**
Many families rely on welfare payments to cover their basic living costs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多家庭依賴福利金來支付基本生活開支。

## 7. **Blaze** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /bleɪz/
**Definition:** A large, strong, and dangerous fire.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大火；烈火

**Example 1:**
Firefighters worked through the night to bring the massive blaze under control.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
消防員通宵工作，才將那場大火控制住。

**Example 2:**
Several buildings were destroyed in the blaze that started yesterday evening.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
昨晚發生的大火摧毀了幾座建築物。
