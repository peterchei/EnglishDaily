# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-02

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary derived from current news headlines, focusing on terms that will help you sound more like a native speaker in a British context.

## 1. **Hitman** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhɪt.mæn/
**Definition:** A person who is paid to kill someone, usually for a criminal organization.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 職業殺手

**Example 1:**
The police are hunting for a notorious hitman suspected of several murders across Europe.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方正在追捕一名據信在歐洲犯下多宗謀殺案的著名職業殺手。

**Example 2:**
The gang recruited a teenage hitman to carry out their violent operations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該犯罪集團招募了一名青少年殺手來執行他們的暴力行動。

## 2. **Influx** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪn.flʌks/
**Definition:** The arrival of a large number of people or things at the same time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 湧入；匯入

**Example 1:**
The city is struggling to manage the sudden influx of migrants at the border.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這座城市正努力應對邊境移民突然湧入的問題。

**Example 2:**
There has been a massive influx of tourists to London since the restrictions were lifted.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
自從限制措施取消以來，倫敦遊客數量大幅激增。

## 3. **Normalise** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈnɔː.məl.aɪz/
**Definition:** To make something become normal or standard, especially if it was previously considered unusual or unacceptable.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 使正常化

**Example 1:**
We must ensure that cyber attacks do not become normalised in the corporate world.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我們必須確保網絡攻擊不會在企業界變得習以為常。

**Example 2:**
The government is working to normalise relations between the two countries.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府正致力於推動兩國關係正常化。

## 4. **Proliferation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˌlɪf.ərˈeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The rapid increase in the number or amount of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 擴散；激增

**Example 1:**
The social media platform is trying to combat the proliferation of fake content created by AI.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該社交媒體平台正試圖打擊人工智能生成的虛假內容的氾濫。

**Example 2:**
Experts are concerned about the proliferation of nuclear weapons in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
專家們對該地區核武器的擴散感到擔憂。

## 5. **Detonate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdet.ən.eɪt/
**Definition:** To make something explode or to explode.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 引爆；爆炸

**Example 1:**
The bomb was detonated inside the restaurant, causing significant damage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
炸彈在餐廳內被引爆，造成嚴重破壞。

**Example 2:**
The army had to detonate the old explosive device safely in an open field.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
軍隊必須在空曠地帶安全地引爆該枚舊爆炸裝置。

## 6. **Under-pressure** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌn.dəˈpreʃ.ə/
**Definition:** Experiencing stress or difficulty, often due to demands from other people or situations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 處於壓力下的

**Example 1:**
The Fifa president is currently under-pressure to explain the recent financial scandals.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際足協主席目前面臨巨大壓力，需要解釋近期的財務醜聞。

**Example 2:**
Managers often feel under-pressure when deadlines are approaching quickly.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當截止日期臨近時，經理們經常會感到壓力重重。
