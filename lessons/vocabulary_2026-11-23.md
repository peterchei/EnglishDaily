# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-23

Hi there! Today we are looking at some useful vocabulary appearing in recent news headlines.

## 1. **Geopolitical** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌdʒiː.əʊ.pəˈlɪt.ɪ.kəl/
**Definition:** Relating to the study of the geographical factors in world politics and international relations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 地緣政治的

**Example 1:**
The prime minister noted that the talks were hindered by difficult geopolitical circumstances.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相指出，談判受到困難的地緣政治環境所阻礙。

**Example 2:**
Geopolitical tensions often influence the global price of oil and gas.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
地緣政治緊張局勢往往會影響全球石油和天然氣的價格。

## 2. **Substance** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsʌb.stəns/
**Definition:** A particular type of matter with uniform properties, often used when referring to chemicals or drugs.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 物質

**Example 1:**
The venue was evacuated after an unknown substance was found in the crowd.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於在人群中發現了一種不明物質，場館進行了疏散。

**Example 2:**
The cleaning crew wore protective gear to handle the hazardous substance.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
清潔人員穿著防護裝備來處理這種危險物質。

## 3. **Screening** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskriː.nɪŋ/
**Definition:** The testing or examining of people to see if they have a particular disease or condition.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 篩查（尤指醫療檢測）

**Example 1:**
The NHS has launched a new trial for AI-assisted breast cancer screening.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英國國家醫療服務體系（NHS）啟動了一項關於人工智慧輔助乳腺癌篩查的新試驗。

**Example 2:**
Early screening is vital for detecting health issues before they become serious.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
早期篩查對於在健康問題變得嚴重之前發現它們至關重要。

## 4. **Rubble** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrʌb.əl/
**Definition:** Piles of broken stone, bricks, and debris from buildings that have been destroyed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 瓦礫

**Example 1:**
Rescue workers spent hours digging through the rubble to find survivors.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援人員花費數小時在瓦礫中挖掘，以尋找倖存者。

**Example 2:**
After the earthquake, many houses were reduced to piles of rubble.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
地震過後，許多房屋變成了一堆瓦礫。

## 5. **Epicentre** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈep.i.sen.tər/
**Definition:** The central point or the area where the most important or intense activity of a specific type occurs.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中心；震央（引申為活動中心）

**Example 1:**
San Francisco is considered the epicentre of the current artificial intelligence boom.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
舊金山被認為是當前人工智慧熱潮的中心。

**Example 2:**
London remains the epicentre of the UK's financial services industry.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
倫敦仍然是英國金融服務業的中心。

## 6. **Overheated** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌəʊ.vəˈhiː.tɪd/
**Definition:** Used in economics to describe an economy or industry that is growing too fast and is likely to experience a sudden decline.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 過熱的

**Example 1:**
Some experts worry that the tech sector has become overheated after years of rapid growth.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
一些專家擔心科技行業在經歷了多年的快速增長後已經過熱。

**Example 2:**
The government is trying to cool down the overheated property market.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府正試圖為過熱的房地產市場降溫。
