# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-26

## 1. **Reimburse** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːɪmˈbɜːrs/
**Definition:** To pay back money to someone who has spent it for a specific purpose.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 報銷、償還

**Example 1:**
The company will reimburse you for any travel expenses you incur during the business trip.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司會為你喺公幹期間產生嘅任何差旅費進行報銷。

**Example 2:**
The government agreed to reimburse the costs of rebuilding the border infrastructure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府同意償還重建邊境基礎設施嘅費用。

## 2. **Allegedly** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈledʒɪdli/
**Definition:** Used to convey that something is claimed to be the case, but there is no proof yet.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 據稱、涉嫌

**Example 1:**
The suspect allegedly stole thousands of pounds from the charity fund.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位疑犯據稱由慈善基金度偷咗幾千英鎊。

**Example 2:**
Nearly a third of the boat crossings allegedly involved engines from his firm.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
據稱有近三分之一嘅船隻偷渡都係用咗佢公司嘅引擎。

## 3. **Shift** (Noun/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ʃɪft/
**Definition:** A slight change in position, direction, or tendency.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 轉變、變動

**Example 1:**
The Met Office reports a significant shift in UK weather patterns due to climate change.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英國氣象局報告指，由於氣候變化，英國嘅天氣模式出現咗明顯嘅轉變。

**Example 2:**
There has been a cultural shift in how people approach remote working in the UK.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺英國，人們對遙距辦公嘅態度出現咗文化上嘅轉變。

## 4. **Levy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlevi/
**Definition:** An extra amount of money that has to be paid, especially as a tax to a government.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 徵稅、稅款

**Example 1:**
The US government is planning to impose a new levy on imported goods from various countries.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
美國政府計劃對多國嘅進口貨品徵收新稅。

**Example 2:**
The money raised from this environmental levy will be used to clean up local rivers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由呢項環境稅籌集得嚟嘅錢，將會用喺清潔本地河流。

## 5. **Conviction** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈvɪkʃn/
**Definition:** A strong belief or opinion, or the formal declaration that someone is guilty of a criminal offense.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 定罪、信念

**Example 1:**
The judges stated there was little chance of a conviction in the current case.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法官指出，呢單案好難會有定罪嘅可能。

**Example 2:**
She speaks about her political views with great conviction.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢以好強烈嘅信念去講述佢嘅政治觀點。

## 6. **Acquit** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈkwɪt/
**Definition:** To decide officially in a law court that someone is not guilty of a particular crime.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 宣判無罪、開釋

**Example 1:**
The jury took only two hours to acquit the defendant of all charges.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
陪審團只係用咗兩個鐘就宣判被告所有罪名不成立。

**Example 2:**
Although the company was found guilty of one charge, it was acquitted of racketeering.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
雖然間公司被判其中一項罪名成立，但佢哋被判敲詐勒索罪不成立。

## 7. **Consent** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈsent/
**Definition:** Permission for something to happen or agreement to do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 同意、准許

**Example 1:**
The game developer used the AI voice with the full consent of the family.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
遊戲開發商喺得到佢家人嘅全力同意下，使用咗嗰個人工智能聲音。

**Example 2:**
You cannot use his photograph for advertising without his written consent.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
未經佢書面同意，你唔可以用佢張相嚟做廣告。
