# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-05

Hello everyone! Let's learn some useful vocabulary from today's headlines.

## 1. **Scuffle** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskʌf.əl/
**Definition:** A short, confused, and often non-serious fight or struggle.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 小規模衝突、打鬥

**Example 1:**
Protesters had a minor scuffle with police near the processing centre.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
抗議者在處理中心附近與警察發生了小規模衝突。

**Example 2:**
The two fans got into a scuffle over the last remaining seat in the stand.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
那兩名球迷為了看台最後一個空位而起了爭執。

## 2. **Run-off** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrʌn.ɒf/
**Definition:** A further contest or election held to settle a tie or to determine a winner after an initial vote.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 決選、第二輪投票

**Example 1:**
Brazil's presidential election will head to a run-off later this month.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
巴西總統選舉將於本月晚些時候進入第二輪投票。

**Example 2:**
Neither candidate secured the majority, so there must be a run-off vote.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
兩名候選人都沒有獲得過半數選票，因此必須進行決選。

## 3. **Unveil** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnˈveɪl/
**Definition:** To show or announce something new to the public for the first time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公布、揭幕

**Example 1:**
The shadow chancellor is expected to unveil new tax policies tomorrow.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
影子財政大臣預計將於明天公布新的稅收政策。

**Example 2:**
The company will unveil its latest AI technology at the conference.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司將在會議上公布其最新的人工智能技術。

## 4. **Ashore** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈʃɔːr/
**Definition:** Towards or onto the shore from the water.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 上岸、向岸邊

**Example 1:**
The migrants were brought safely ashore by the rescue team.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
移民們被救援隊安全地帶上了岸。

**Example 2:**
After weeks at sea, the sailors were relieved to finally step ashore.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在海上航行數週後，水手們終於能上岸，感到如釋重負。

## 5. **Taskforce** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɑːsk.fɔːs/
**Definition:** A group of people organized for a specific, temporary objective.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 專責小組、特遣部隊

**Example 1:**
The president appointed a new taskforce to oversee AI safety regulations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統任命了一個新的專責小組來監督人工智能安全法規。

**Example 2:**
A special taskforce has been set up to investigate the recent data leak.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
一個特別專責小組已經成立，旨在調查最近的數據洩露事件。

## 6. **Clashes** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈklæʃ.ɪz/
**Definition:** Violent confrontations or disagreements between groups of people.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 衝突、對抗

**Example 1:**
Violent clashes broke out between students and riot police in Paris.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
巴黎發生了學生與防暴警察之間的激烈衝突。

**Example 2:**
There were several clashes between the opposing political parties during the debate.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在辯論過程中，對立政黨之間發生了多次衝突。

## 7. **Sensitive** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsen.sə.tɪv/
**Definition:** Needing to be treated with care or secrecy; relating to private or confidential information.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 敏感的、機密的

**Example 1:**
The employees were fired for mishandling highly sensitive information.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這些員工因處理高度機密的信息不當而被解僱。

**Example 2:**
Diplomatic relations remain a very sensitive topic in the current climate.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在目前的形勢下，外交關係仍然是一個非常敏感的話題。
