# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-29

## 1. **Common ground** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒm.ən ɡraʊnd/
**Definition:** Opinions or interests shared by two or more parties who otherwise disagree.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 共同立場 / 共通點

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister is hoping to find common ground with opposition parties to reform social care.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相希望與反對黨找到共同立場，以改革社會護理服務。

**Example 2:**
It is often difficult to find common ground in such a politically divided climate.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在如此政治分裂的氣氛下，要找到共同立場往往很困難。

## 2. **Outsource** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈaʊt.sɔːs/
**Definition:** To obtain goods or a service from an outside or foreign supplier.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 外判 / 外包

**Example 1:**
Drug cartels have started to outsource the production of illicit substances to labs in Nigeria.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
販毒集團已開始將非法物質的生產外判給尼日利亞的實驗室。

**Example 2:**
Many tech companies outsource their customer service departments to reduce operating costs.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多科技公司將客戶服務部外判，以降低營運成本。

## 3. **Humanoid** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈhjuː.mə.nɔɪd/
**Definition:** Having an appearance or character resembling that of a human.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 人形（機器人）

**Example 1:**
The US government has implemented a ban on the import of new Chinese humanoid robots.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
美國政府已實施禁令，禁止進口新型中國人形機器人。

**Example 2:**
Engineers are designing humanoid machines to assist with dangerous tasks in factories.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
工程師正在設計人形機器，以協助工廠內危險的工作。

## 4. **Containment** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈteɪn.mənt/
**Definition:** The action of keeping something harmful under control or within limits.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 遏制 / 控制（火勢或疫情）

**Example 1:**
The extreme heat in France is making the containment of wildfires much more difficult for firefighters.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法國的極端高溫使得消防員對山火的控制變得更加困難。

**Example 2:**
The containment of the virus is the primary goal of the local health authorities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
遏制病毒是當地衛生部門的首要目標。

## 5. **Diaspora** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /daɪˈæspərə/
**Definition:** People who have spread or been dispersed from their original homeland.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 離散群體 / 僑民

**Example 1:**
A new report highlights a significant increase in antisemitism affecting the Jewish diaspora in the UK.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
一份新報告強調了影響英國猶太僑民的反猶太主義有顯著增加。

**Example 2:**
The city has a large diaspora community that contributes to its diverse culture.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這座城市擁有龐大的離散群體，為其多元文化作出了貢獻。

## 6. **Clumsy** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈklʌm.zi/
**Definition:** Awkward in movement or in handling things; lacking skill or grace.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 笨拙的 / 粗手粗腳的

**Example 1:**
The hackers were described as clumsy, yet their attack still caused widespread disruption.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
黑客被形容為笨拙，但他們的攻擊仍然造成了廣泛的混亂。

**Example 2:**
I felt very clumsy when I tripped over my own feet during the presentation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在演講時被自己的腳絆倒，我感到非常笨拙。

## 7. **Lull** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /lʌl/
**Definition:** A temporary interval of quiet or lack of activity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 暫時的平靜 / 間歇

**Example 1:**
The brief lull in fighting between the US and Iran was shattered by new missile strikes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
美國與伊朗之間短暫的交火平靜被新的導彈襲擊打破了。

**Example 2:**
There was a short lull in the storm before the wind picked up again.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
風暴中出現了短暫的平靜，隨後風力再次增強。
