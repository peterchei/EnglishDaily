# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-24

## 1. **Contamination** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˌtæmɪˈneɪʃn/
**Definition:** The action or state of making or being made impure by polluting or poisoning.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 污染

**Example 1:**
The chemical factory faces lawsuits regarding the contamination of local water supplies.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢間化工廠面臨關於當地供水污染嘅訴訟。

**Example 2:**
Strict safety protocols are in place to prevent the contamination of laboratory samples.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
為咗防止實驗室樣本受污染，已實行嚴格嘅安全守則。

## 2. **Inquiry** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈkwaɪəri/
**Definition:** An act of asking for information or an official investigation into a matter.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 調查、查詢

**Example 1:**
The government has launched a formal inquiry into the cause of the jet crash.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府已經針對嗰單噴射機墜毀事故展開正式調查。

**Example 2:**
I made an inquiry about the availability of tickets for the football match.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我查詢過場波飛仲有冇位。

## 3. **Supremacy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /suːˈpreməsi/
**Definition:** The state or condition of being superior to all others in authority, power, or status.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 至高無上、霸權

**Example 1:**
The two nations are engaged in a fierce competition for AI supremacy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國正處於爭奪人工智能霸權嘅激烈競爭中。

**Example 2:**
The team fought hard to maintain their supremacy in the league.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
嗰支球隊奮力爭取以維持佢哋喺聯賽中嘅統治地位。

## 4. **Discrimination** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˌskrɪmɪˈneɪʃn/
**Definition:** The unjust or prejudicial treatment of different categories of people.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 歧視

**Example 1:**
The politician argued that the ban on drinking in stands amounts to discrimination.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位政客認為禁止喺看台飲酒係一種歧視。

**Example 2:**
Laws are designed to protect citizens from discrimination in the workplace.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
法律旨在保障市民喺職場免受歧視。

## 5. **Breach** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /briːtʃ/
**Definition:** An act of breaking or failing to observe a law, agreement, or code of conduct.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 違反、缺口

**Example 1:**
The company apologized for the data breach that exposed user information.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司就洩露用戶資料嘅數據違規事件道歉。

**Example 2:**
Walking out of the meeting was a clear breach of protocol.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺會議中途離場係明顯違反禮儀。

## 6. **Defiant** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfaɪənt/
**Definition:** Showing defiance; resisting authority or an opposing force.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 挑釁嘅、蔑視嘅

**Example 1:**
The leader gave a defiant speech despite the threats from international powers.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管受到國際勢力威脅，嗰位領導人依然發表咗一篇挑釁性嘅演講。

**Example 2:**
He maintained a defiant attitude even when he was clearly in the wrong.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
即使明知自己有錯，佢仍然保持一種蔑視嘅態度。

## 7. **Reconstruction** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːkənˈstrʌkʃn/
**Definition:** The action or process of rebuilding something that has been damaged or destroyed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重建

**Example 1:**
International donors have pledged billions to assist in the reconstruction of Gaza.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際捐助者已承諾撥出數十億元協助加沙嘅重建工作。

**Example 2:**
The reconstruction of the city centre will take at least five years.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
市中心嘅重建工程至少需要五年時間。
