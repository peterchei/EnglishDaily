# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-19

## 1. **Pitfall** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpɪt.fɔːl/
**Definition:** A hidden or unsuspected danger or difficulty.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 陷阱、隱患

**Example 1:**
The new policy sounds promising, but it is full of potential pitfalls.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢項新政策聽落好有前景，但其實充滿咗潛在嘅隱患。

**Example 2:**

**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺合約談判中避開常見嘅陷阱，可以為你間公司慳返好多錢。

**Pronunciation:** /ˈkæp.ɪŋ/

**Cantonese Meaning:** 設置上限、封頂

**Example 1:**
