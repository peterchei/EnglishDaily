# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-06

## 1. **Thriller** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈθrɪl.ər/
**Definition:** A book, play, or film, or in this context, a sports match, that is very exciting and involves suspense.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 驚悚片；（體育賽事中）緊張刺激的比賽

**Example 1:**
The football match was a real thriller, with the score changing in the final minutes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰場足球賽真係好緊張刺激，比分喺最後幾分鐘仲有變動。

**Example 2:**
I love reading a good psychological thriller before I go to sleep.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我鍾意喺瞓覺前睇一本好嘅心理驚悚小說。

## 2. **Convert** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈvɜːt/
**Definition:** To successfully score a penalty kick or a point in a game; also used generally to mean changing something into a different form.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （體育）射入罰球；轉變、轉換

**Example 1:**
Harry Kane managed to convert the penalty under immense pressure.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
哈利·簡尼喺巨大壓力下成功射入嗰個十二碼。

**Example 2:**
The office was converted into a temporary classroom for the training session.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢間辦公室被改裝成臨時課室嚟進行培訓。

## 3. **Private** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpraɪ.vət/
**Definition:** Intended for the use of a single person or group, often implying payment rather than state-funded (e.g., healthcare).
**Cantonese Meaning:** 私人的；私營的

**Example 1:**
You can now buy the medication through private pharmacies in the UK.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
你而家可以透過英國嘅私營藥房買到呢隻藥。

**Example 2:**
The meeting was held behind closed doors to ensure it remained private.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢個會議喺閉門狀態下進行，以確保內容係私人性質。

## 4. **Boom** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /buːm/
**Definition:** A period of sudden rapid growth, development, or increase in popularity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 繁榮；興起；熱潮

**Example 1:**
There has been a huge boom in the aesthetics industry over the last few years.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
過去幾年，醫學美容行業出現咗巨大嘅熱潮。

**Example 2:**
The city experienced an economic boom following the discovery of new trade routes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺發現新貿易路線後，呢個城市經歷咗經濟繁榮。

## 5. **Outset** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈaʊt.set/
**Definition:** The beginning or start of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 開端；開始

**Example 1:**
The conflict was incredibly difficult from the very outset.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢場衝突由一開始就非常困難。

**Example 2:**

**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺項目開始嘅時候，我已經向我嘅團隊講清楚我嘅意圖。

## 6. **Counterfeit** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkaʊn.tə.fɪt/
**Definition:** Made to look like an original product, usually for illegal purposes; fake.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 偽造的；冒牌的

**Example 1:**
The authorities are working hard to stop the trade of counterfeit luxury goods.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當局正努力阻止冒牌奢侈品嘅交易。

**Example 2:**
He was arrested for attempting to use a counterfeit banknote at the store.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢因為試圖喺商店使用偽鈔而被捕。

## 7. **Abuse** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈbjuːs/
**Definition:** Cruel, violent, or unfair treatment of someone, or the improper usage of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 虐待；濫用

**Example 1:**
The police have launched a campaign to raise awareness about online child abuse.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方已經發起一項運動，以提高公眾對網上兒童虐待問題嘅認識。

**Example 2:**
The government is looking at stricter regulations to prevent the abuse of public funds.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府正考慮採取更嚴格嘅規管，以防止公款被濫用。
