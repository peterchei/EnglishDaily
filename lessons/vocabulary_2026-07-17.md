# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-17

## 1. **Nationalisation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌnæʃ.ən.əl.aɪˈzeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The action of a government taking control of a private industry or company.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 國有化

**Example 1:**
The government is considering the nationalisation of the steel plant to protect local jobs.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在考慮將鋼鐵廠國有化，以保障當地職位。

**Example 2:**
Public support for the nationalisation of the rail network has grown significantly this year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公眾對鐵路網絡國有化的支持在今年顯著增加。

## 2. **Safeguard** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈseɪf.ɡɑːd/
**Definition:** To protect something from harm or damage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 保護；保衛

**Example 1:**
The new laws were introduced to safeguard the rights of all military personnel.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新法例的引入旨在保障所有軍事人員的權利。

**Example 2:**
We must take immediate action to safeguard our country's critical infrastructure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們必須立即採取行動來保護我們國家的關鍵基礎設施。

## 3. **Mandatory** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmæn.də.tər.i/
**Definition:** Required by law or rules; compulsory.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 強制的；必須履行的

**Example 1:**
Wearing a seatbelt in a moving car is mandatory in the UK.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在英國，行駛中的車輛內必須佩戴安全帶。

**Example 2:**
The company has made it mandatory for all staff to attend the safety training session.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司已規定所有員工必須參加安全培訓課程。

## 4. **Incite** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈsaɪt/
**Definition:** To encourage or stir up violent or unlawful behaviour.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 煽動；鼓動

**Example 1:**
He was arrested for attempting to incite hatred between different ethnic groups.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他因企圖煽動不同種族群體之間的仇恨而被捕。

**Example 2:**
The speech was criticised for inciting unrest among the protesters.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該演講因煽動示威者之間的不安情緒而受到批評。

## 5. **Regulator** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈreɡ.jə.leɪ.tər/
**Definition:** An official body that controls a particular public service or industry.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 監管機構

**Example 1:**
The communication regulator is investigating whether TikTok has sufficient age checks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
通訊監管機構正在調查 TikTok 是否有足夠的年齡審查。

**Example 2:**
The financial regulator fined the bank for failing to follow the new rules.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
金融監管機構對該銀行進行了罰款，原因是其未能遵守新規定。

## 6. **Vanished** (Verb - Past Participle/Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvæn.ɪʃt/
**Definition:** Disappeared suddenly and completely.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 失蹤的；消失的

**Example 1:**
More than 500 people have vanished at sea after their boats went missing.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在船隻失蹤後，已有超過 500 人在海上失蹤。

**Example 2:**
The suspect vanished into thin air before the police could arrive.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在警察到達之前，嫌疑人就消失得無影無蹤。
