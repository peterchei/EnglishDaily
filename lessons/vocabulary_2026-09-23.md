# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-23

## 1. **Broker** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbrəʊkə/
**Definition:** A person or entity that acts as an intermediary to negotiate contracts or mediate between parties.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中介人、經紀人

**Example 1:**
The UK aims to act as an honest broker in international AI negotiations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英國旨在國際人工智能談判中充當誠實的中介人。

**Example 2:**
He works as a property broker helping people find affordable housing in London.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他是一位物業經紀，幫助人們在倫敦尋找可負擔的住所。

## 2. **Lease** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /liːs/
**Definition:** To grant or hold property or equipment by a contract for a specified period in return for rent.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 租賃、出租

**Example 1:**
The government plans to lease back the military base after transferring the islands.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府計劃在移交這些島嶼後，租回該軍事基地。

**Example 2:**
We decided to lease a new printer for the office instead of buying one.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們決定為辦公室租賃一台新打印機，而不是購買。

## 3. **Staircase** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsteəkeɪs/
**Definition:** A set of stairs and the structure that supports them, often used metaphorically for a series of steps leading to a result.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 樓梯、階梯（比喻）

**Example 1:**
The IMF chief described the rising debt levels as a staircase leading to economic trouble.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
國際貨幣基金組織總裁形容債務水平的上升，就像一條通往經濟困境的階梯。

**Example 2:**
Please use the staircase if the lift is out of service.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果電梯暫停服務，請使用樓梯。

## 4. **Annihilate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈnaɪɪleɪt/
**Definition:** To destroy completely; to wipe out.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 殲滅、徹底摧毀

**Example 1:**
The president threatened to annihilate the enemy if peace talks failed.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統威脅說，如果和平談判失敗，他將徹底摧毀敵人。

**Example 2:**
The forest fire threatened to annihilate the entire village.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
森林大火威脅要將整個村莊夷為平地。

## 5. **Sidelines** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsaɪdlaɪnz/
**Definition:** The area just outside the boundary of a playing field, often used to refer to informal or secondary discussions held during a major event.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 場邊、會議期間的非正式場合

**Example 1:**
Officials met on the sidelines of the UN General Assembly to discuss the conflict.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
官員們在聯合國大會期間會面，討論這場衝突。

**Example 2:**
She has been on the sidelines of the project for too long and wants to get involved.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她參與這個項目已經太久沒發揮作用，現在想積極投入其中。

## 6. **Convict** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈvɪkt/
**Definition:** To declare someone to be guilty of a criminal offence by the verdict of a jury or the decision of a judge.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 判決有罪、定罪

**Example 1:**
The court will convict the suspects if there is enough evidence.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
如果有足夠的證據，法院將會判決疑犯有罪。

**Example 2:**
He was convicted of fraud after a long trial.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經過漫長的審訊後，他被判詐騙罪成。

## 7. **Capacity** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈpæsəti/
**Definition:** The maximum amount that something can contain or produce; the ability to do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 容量、能力

**Example 1:**
Switching to light technology will increase the capacity of data centres.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
改用光技術將會增加數據中心的容量。

**Example 2:**
The stadium is operating at full capacity for the final match.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這座體育場正以滿載的狀態舉辦決賽。
