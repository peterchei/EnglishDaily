# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-31

## 1. **Consultation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌkɒn.sʌlˈteɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The act of discussing something with someone or a group to get their advice or opinion before making a decision.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 諮詢；磋商

**Example 1:**
The local council is holding a public consultation regarding the new housing development.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
地方議會正就新的房屋發展項目舉行公眾諮詢。

**Example 2:**
After a long period of consultation, the union finally agreed to the new contract terms.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經過長時間的磋商，工會最終同意了新的合約條款。

## 2. **Postcode** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /pəʊst.kəʊd/
**Definition:** A group of letters and numbers added to a postal address to assist in the sorting of mail.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 郵遞區號（英國常用）

**Example 1:**
Could you please provide your postcode so I can check if we deliver to your area?
**Cantonese Example 1:**
可以請你提供郵遞區號嗎？這樣我可以確認我們是否送貨到你那個地區。

**Example 2:**
Every postcode in the city should have equal access to high-quality public services.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
市內的每一個郵遞區號區域都應該平等地獲得優質的公共服務。

## 3. **Stabilising** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsteɪ.bəl.aɪ.zɪŋ/
**Definition:** Becoming firm, steady, or unlikely to change or fail; preventing further deterioration.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 穩定下來；趨於穩定

**Example 1:**
The patient’s condition is stabilising after the emergency surgery.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
病人的情況在緊急手術後正在穩定下來。

**Example 2:**
Firefighters say the wildfire is stabilising, but they remain on high alert.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
消防員表示山火正趨於穩定，但他們仍保持高度警戒。

## 4. **Disarm** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪsˈɑːm/
**Definition:** To take weapons away from someone or to stop being hostile.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 解除武裝；放下武器

**Example 1:**
The peace treaty requires both sides to disarm immediately.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
和平條約要求雙方立即解除武裝。

**Example 2:**
He managed to disarm the attacker before anyone was hurt.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他在任何人受傷之前成功解除了襲擊者的武裝。

## 5. **Avalanche** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈæv.əl.ɑːnʃ/
**Definition:** A mass of snow, ice, and rock falling rapidly down a mountainside.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 雪崩

**Example 1:**
The search team is struggling to reach the climbers after the massive avalanche.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
搜救隊在大型雪崩過後，正努力接觸那些登山者。

**Example 2:**
Warnings have been issued to hikers about the high risk of an avalanche in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當局已向徒步旅行者發出該地區雪崩風險高的警告。

## 6. **Enclave** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈen.kleɪv/
**Definition:** A small territory or area that is surrounded by a larger territory whose inhabitants are culturally or ethnically distinct.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 飛地；被包圍的領土

**Example 1:**
The city serves as an enclave surrounded by desert on all sides.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這座城市是一塊四面都被沙漠包圍的飛地。

**Example 2:**
Thousands of migrants gathered at the border of the Spanish enclave.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
數以千計的移民聚集在這個西班牙飛地的邊境。

## 7. **Predominantly** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /prɪˈdɒm.ɪ.nənt.li/
**Definition:** Mostly or mainly; for the most part.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 主要地；佔大多數地

**Example 1:**
The audience was predominantly made up of young students.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
觀眾主要由年輕學生組成。

**Example 2:**
The weather in this country is predominantly rainy during the winter months.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個國家的天氣在冬季月份主要是多雨的。
