# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-25

## 1. **Panned out** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /pænd aʊt/
**Definition:** To develop or result in a particular way, often referring to how a situation concludes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 發展結果如何；情況如何演變

**Example 1:**
The journalist analyzed how the diplomatic trip panned out for the two nations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
記者分析了這次外交訪問對兩國而言結果如何。

**Example 2:**
I am waiting to see how the project pans out before I commit more money.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我正等著看這個項目會如何發展，才決定是否投入更多資金。

## 2. **Unpack** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌnˈpæk/
**Definition:** To explain or analyze something complex in order to make it easier to understand.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 剖析；詳細解釋（複雜的事物）

**Example 1:**
We need time to unpack the implications of the new trade agreement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我們需要時間來剖析這項新貿易協定的含義。

**Example 2:**
She did a great job unpacking the complicated political situation for the audience.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她很出色地為觀眾剖析了複雜的政治局勢。

## 3. **Dinghy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdɪŋɡi/
**Definition:** A small, often inflatable, open boat.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 小艇；橡皮艇

**Example 1:**
The migrants were rescued after their dinghy began to take on water.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這些移民在小艇開始進水後獲救。

**Example 2:**
They spent the afternoon sailing a small dinghy around the bay.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們花了一下午在海灣裡駕駛一艘小艇。

## 4. **Cowards** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkaʊədz/
**Definition:** People who lack the courage to do what is right or face danger.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 懦夫；膽小鬼

**Example 1:**
The leader called his critics moral cowards for walking out of his speech.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
那位領導人稱那些在他演講中途離場的批評者為道德上的懦夫。

**Example 2:**
Don't be a coward; just tell him the truth about how you feel.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
別當個懦夫，直接告訴他你的真實感受吧。

## 5. **Sidelines** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsaɪdlaɪnz/
**Definition:** A position of not being directly involved in an activity; the edge of a sports field.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 局外；場邊（指旁觀位置）

**Example 1:**
He has finally moved from the sidelines to take a leading role in the government.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他終於從局外人轉變為政府的核心領導角色。

**Example 2:**
The manager watched the game from the sidelines, shouting instructions to the players.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經理在場邊觀看比賽，並向球員高聲喊話指揮。

## 6. **Compelling** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /kəmˈpelɪŋ/
**Definition:** Evoking interest, attention, or admiration in a powerful or irresistible way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 引人入勝的；令人信服的

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister must present a compelling vision to win the public's trust.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相必須提出一個令人信服的願景，才能贏得公眾的信任。

**Example 2:**
There is a compelling argument for investing more in renewable energy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在投資再生能源方面，有一個令人信服的論點。

## 7. **Breach** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /briːtʃ/
**Definition:** A break in a relationship, security, or a failure to observe a law or code of conduct.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 違規；漏洞；破壞（規則或安全）

**Example 1:**
The company announced a significant data breach that affected thousands of users.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司宣布了一起嚴重的數據洩漏事故，影響了數千名用戶。

**Example 2:**
His actions were considered a serious breach of the professional code of conduct.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他的行為被認為是嚴重違反了職業行為準則。
