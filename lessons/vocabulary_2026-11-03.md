# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-03

## 1. **Ballot** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbælət/
**Definition:** A process of voting, or a piece of paper used to record a vote.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 選票；投票

**Example 1:**
Voters cast their ballot early to avoid long queues on election day.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
選民提前投下選票，以避免在選舉日排長龍。

**Example 2:**
The commission is currently counting every single ballot from the state.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
委員會目前正在點算該州每一張選票。

## 2. **Defensive** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfensɪv/
**Definition:** Used or intended to protect someone or something against attack.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 防禦性的；防衛的

**Example 1:**
The military officials described the operation as a strictly defensive measure.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軍方官員形容這次行動純粹是一項防禦措施。

**Example 2:**
He took a defensive posture when the reporter asked about the scandal.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當記者問及醜聞時，他採取了防衛的姿態。

## 3. **Summit** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsʌmɪt/
**Definition:** A meeting between heads of government or high-ranking officials to discuss important issues.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 高峰會；首腦會議

**Example 1:**
The UK and EU leaders will meet at the upcoming summit to finalize the agreement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英歐領導人將在即將舉行的高峰會上會面，以敲定協議。

**Example 2:**
Climate change is expected to dominate the agenda of the international summit.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
氣候變化預計將成為這次國際高峰會的主要議題。

## 4. **Red tape** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌred ˈteɪp/
**Definition:** Excessive bureaucracy or adherence to official rules and formalities, especially in public business.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 行政繁文縟節；官僚作風

**Example 1:**
The new deal aims to cut red tape for food exports between the UK and the EU.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這項新協議旨在減少英歐之間食品出口的繁文縟節。

**Example 2:**
Small businesses often struggle with the red tape involved in applying for government grants.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
小型企業往往難以應對申請政府撥款時所涉及的官僚手續。

## 5. **Formal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfɔːml/
**Definition:** Done in accordance with rules of convention or etiquette; official.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 正式的

**Example 1:**
The prime minister is preparing to issue a formal apology for historical grievances.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相正準備就歷史遺留的問題發表正式道歉。

**Example 2:**
Please ensure you wear formal attire for the awards ceremony this evening.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請確保今晚頒獎典禮穿著正式服裝。

## 6. **Rehearse** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈhɜːs/
**Definition:** To practice a play, piece of music, or other performance, or in this context, a military operation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 排練；預演

**Example 1:**
European troops will rehearse their deployment plans to ensure they are ready for peace initiatives.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
歐洲軍隊將預演他們的部署計劃，以確保為和平行動做好準備。

**Example 2:**
The actors need to rehearse the final scene several more times before the premiere.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
演員們在首映前還需要對最後一幕進行幾次排練。

## 7. **Factor** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfæktə(r)/
**Definition:** A circumstance, fact, or influence that contributes to a result or outcome.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 因素

**Example 1:**
Investigators believe that severe weather was a key factor in the recent jet crash.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
調查人員認為惡劣天氣是導致近期噴射機墜毀的一個關鍵因素。

**Example 2:**
Price is a major factor when consumers decide which new mobile phone to buy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
價格是消費者決定購買哪款新手機時的一個主要因素。
