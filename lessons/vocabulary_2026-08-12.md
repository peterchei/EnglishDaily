# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-12

## 1. **Decoy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdiːkɔɪ/
**Definition:** A person or thing used to deceive or lure someone into a trap.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誘餌、幌子

**Example 1:**
The president used a decoy jet to ensure his safety while traveling after the summit.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總統使用了一架幌子噴射機，以確保他在峰會後出行時的安全。

**Example 2:**
Police placed a decoy vehicle on the road to catch speeding drivers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
警方在路上放置了一輛誘餌車，以捕捉超速駕駛的司機。

## 2. **Rubble** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrʌbl/
**Definition:** Waste or rough fragments of stone, brick, or concrete, especially from a building that has been destroyed.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
瓦礫、碎石

**Example 1:**
Rescuers are carefully searching through the rubble to find survivors of the earthquake.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援人員正在瓦礫中仔細搜查，以尋找地震的倖存者。

**Example 2:**
After the explosion, the old house was reduced to a pile of rubble.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
爆炸過後，那座老房子變成了一堆瓦礫。

## 3. **Spike** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /spaɪk/
**Definition:** To increase sharply and suddenly in value or amount.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 急升、猛增

**Example 1:**
Temperatures are expected to spike again as the heatwave continues across the country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
隨著熱浪席捲全國，氣溫預計將會再次急升。

**Example 2:**
Energy prices often spike during the winter months due to high demand.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於需求高企，能源價格在冬季月份經常會出現猛增。

## 4. **Tokenomics** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌtəʊkəˈnɒmɪks/
**Definition:** The system of incentives and economic rules governing a cryptocurrency or digital asset.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 代幣經濟學

**Example 1:**
Investors should carefully study the tokenomics of a project before putting their money into it.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
投資者在投入資金前，應仔細研究項目的代幣經濟學。

**Example 2:**
The company is struggling to design sustainable tokenomics for its new AI service.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該公司正努力為其新的人工智能服務設計可持續的代幣經濟學。

## 5. **Turbulent** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtɜːbjələnt/
**Definition:** Characterized by conflict, disorder, or confusion; not controlled or calm.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 動盪的、混亂的

**Example 1:**
The two nations have a long and turbulent history of diplomatic relations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這兩個國家在外交關係上有著悠久而動盪的歷史。

**Example 2:**
The pilot warned passengers about the turbulent weather during the flight.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
機師在飛行期間就動盪的天氣向乘客發出警告。

## 6. **Intervention** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɪntəˈvenʃn/
**Definition:** The action of becoming involved in a difficult situation in order to improve it or prevent it from getting worse.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 干預、介入

**Example 1:**
The police chief made an urgent intervention to address the safety concerns of the public.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警察局長進行了緊急干預，以解決公眾的安全疑慮。

**Example 2:**
Early intervention by medical staff saved the patient's life.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醫護人員的及時介入挽救了病人的生命。
