# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-28

## 1. **Precaution** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prɪˈkɔː.ʃən/
**Definition:** An action taken in advance to protect against possible danger or failure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 預防措施

**Example 1:**
The department took the system offline as a precaution against further cyber attacks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該部門將系統離線，作為預防進一步網絡攻擊的措施。

**Example 2:**
Always take the precaution of backing up your files before updating your computer.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
更新電腦前，請務必採取備份檔案的預防措施。

## 2. **Literacy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlɪt.ər.ə.si/
**Definition:** The ability to read and write.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 讀寫能力

**Example 1:**
The new government curriculum aims to improve literacy rates among primary school children.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府的新課程旨在提高小學生的讀寫能力。

**Example 2:**
Adult literacy programs help those who did not have the chance to finish school.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
成人識字計劃幫助那些沒有機會完成學業的人。

## 3. **Rally** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈræl.i/
**Definition:** To come together or bring people together to support a common cause, especially in politics.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 集結、召集支持者

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister plans to rally his supporters in a final push before the election.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相計劃在選舉前最後衝刺階段召集他的支持者。

**Example 2:**
The community rallied to help the family after their house was damaged by the storm.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
風暴過後，社區民眾集結起來幫助那個家庭。

## 4. **Remorseful** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈmɔːs.fəl/
**Definition:** Feeling or expressing deep regret or guilt for a wrong committed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 懊悔的、懺悔的

**Example 1:**
The defendant appeared truly remorseful when he apologized to the victims' families in court.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
被告在法庭上向受害者家屬道歉時，顯得非常懊悔。

**Example 2:**
He felt remorseful for his harsh words and decided to apologize the next day.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他對自己說過的刻薄話感到懊悔，並決定第二天道歉。

## 5. **Clone** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kləʊn/
**Definition:** To create an exact copy of something, often using digital technology or biological processes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 複製

**Example 1:**
Gamers are using AI to clone elements of the unreleased video game.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
遊戲玩家正在使用人工智能來複製未發布遊戲中的元素。

**Example 2:**
Scientists managed to clone the plant to preserve its unique characteristics.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
科學家成功複製了這種植物，以保留其獨特的特徵。

## 6. **Shortage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈʃɔː.tɪdʒ/
**Definition:** A state in which there is not enough of something that is needed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 短缺、不足

**Example 1:**
There is a significant shortage of skilled elevator mechanics in the city.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
城中出現了嚴重缺乏熟練電梯技工的情況。

**Example 2:**
The supply chain disruption led to a temporary shortage of fresh produce in shops.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
供應鏈中斷導致商店內新鮮農產品出現暫時性短缺。
