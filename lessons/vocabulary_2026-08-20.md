# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-20

## 1. **Enrolled** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈrəʊld/
**Definition:** Officially registered as a member of an institution or a student on a course.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 已註冊；已入學

**Example 1:**
Prince Archie is enrolled to start at his new school in September.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
Archie 王子已註冊入學，將於九月開始上課。

**Example 2:**
I have enrolled in a professional course to improve my accounting skills.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我已經報讀了一個專業課程，以提升我的會計技能。

## 2. **Surprise** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /səˈpraɪz/
**Definition:** An unexpected event, fact, or thing.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 驚喜；意外之事

**Example 1:**
Harry and Meghan are experts in surprise, often catching the public off guard.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
哈里與梅根是製造意外的專家，經常令公眾感到措手不及。

**Example 2:**
To my surprise, the bank resolved the payment issue within an hour.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
令我感到意外的是，銀行在一小時內就解決了付款問題。

## 3. **Massive** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmæsɪv/
**Definition:** Exceptionally large, heavy, or extensive.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 大規模的；巨大的

**Example 1:**
The city was shaken by a massive attack yesterday morning.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這座城市昨天早上遭受了一次大規模襲擊。

**Example 2:**
There is a massive amount of data to process for this research project.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這個研究項目有大量的數據需要處理。

## 4. **Frenzy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfrenzi/
**Definition:** A state or period of uncontrolled excitement or wild behavior.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 瘋狂；狂熱狀態

**Example 1:**
Giant whales are entering Greenland waters to engage in feeding frenzies.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
巨鯨正進入格陵蘭海域進行瘋狂進食。

**Example 2:**
The announcement caused a shopping frenzy among local customers.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這則公告在當地顧客中引發了一場搶購狂潮。

## 5. **Prosecute** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprɒsɪkjuːt/
**Definition:** To institute legal proceedings against a person or organization.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 起訴；檢控

**Example 1:**
Australia expressed anger over the decision not to prosecute the soldiers involved.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
澳洲對當局決定不起訴涉案士兵表示憤怒。

**Example 2:**
The company could be prosecuted if it fails to follow safety regulations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果該公司未能遵守安全法規，可能會面臨起訴。

## 6. **Crackdown** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkrækdaʊn/
**Definition:** A severe movement to restrict or discourage undesirable or illegal people or behavior.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 嚴厲打擊；掃蕩

**Example 1:**
HMRC has launched a crackdown on crypto holders who failed to pay their taxes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
英國稅務海關總署已針對未繳稅的加密貨幣持有者展開嚴厲打擊。

**Example 2:**
The government’s crackdown on illegal parking has reduced traffic congestion.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府對違例泊車的嚴厲打擊減少了交通擠塞。

## 7. **Resolved** (Adjective/Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzɒlvd/
**Definition:** Settled or found a solution to a problem or dispute.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 已解決的；已消除的

**Example 1:**
Monzo confirmed that the issue with card payments is now resolved.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
Monzo 確認銀行卡支付的問題現已解決。

**Example 2:**
We are determined to get this technical error resolved by the end of the day.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們決心在今天結束前解決這個技術錯誤。
