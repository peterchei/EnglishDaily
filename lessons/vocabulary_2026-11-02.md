# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-02

## 1. **Gained entry** (Verb Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ɡeɪnd ˈɛntri/
**Definition:** To successfully enter a place, often by force or without permission.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 進入；闖入

**Example 1:**
The intruders gained entry to the building through a broken window.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
闖入者通過一扇破碎的窗戶進入了這棟建築。

**Example 2:**
Security footage showed the suspect as he gained entry to the warehouse.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
閉路電視片段顯示了嫌疑人進入倉庫的過程。

## 2. **Rocked** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɒkt/
**Definition:** To shock or disturb someone or something significantly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 震驚；震撼

**Example 1:**
The sudden resignation of the leader rocked the entire political party.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
領導人的突然辭職震撼了整個政黨。

**Example 2:**
Recent scandals have rocked the foundation of the local government.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
近期的醜聞動搖了地方政府的根基。

## 3. **Surge** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /sɜːdʒ/
**Definition:** A sudden, powerful forward or upward movement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 急升；湧現

**Example 1:**
There has been a surge in interest regarding electric vehicles this year.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
今年人們對電動車的興趣急劇上升。

**Example 2:**
The unexpected surge of voters caused long queues at the polling stations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
意想不到的選民湧入導致票站出現長長的人龍。

## 4. **In the wake of** (Phrase)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪn ðə weɪk ɒv/
**Definition:** Coming after or following something, often as a result of it.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 緊隨……之後；作為……的結果

**Example 1:**
New security measures were introduced in the wake of the cyber attack.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在網絡攻擊事件發生後，當局推出了新的保安措施。

**Example 2:**
Public opinion shifted in the wake of the tragic news.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在這一悲劇新聞傳出後，公眾輿論發生了轉變。

## 5. **Endorse** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈdɔːs/
**Definition:** To publicly support or declare one's approval of a person or idea.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 公開支持；背書

**Example 1:**
The local newspaper chose to endorse the independent candidate for mayor.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當地報章選擇公開支持這位市長獨立候選人。

**Example 2:**
Many celebrities endorse products on social media to increase brand awareness.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多名人在社交媒體上為產品做宣傳以提高品牌知名度。

## 6. **Betrayal** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /bɪˈtreɪəl/
**Definition:** The action of being disloyal to someone or something one is supposed to support.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 背叛；出賣

**Example 1:**
His decision to join the rival team was seen as a betrayal by the fans.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
他加入競爭對手球隊的決定被球迷視為背叛。

**Example 2:**
The discovery of the secret meeting felt like a deep betrayal of trust.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
發現那場秘密會議讓人感到是對信任的嚴重背叛。

## 7. **Burst** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /bɜːst/
**Definition:** To break open or apart suddenly, especially due to internal pressure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 爆破；突然破裂

**Example 1:**
Economists are warning that the housing bubble could burst soon.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
經濟學家警告說，樓市泡沫可能會很快破裂。

**Example 2:**
The water pipe burst, causing flooding in the basement.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
水管爆裂，導致地下室水浸。
