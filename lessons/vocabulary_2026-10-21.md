# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-21

## 1. **At loggerheads** (Idiom)
**Pronunciation:** /æt ˈlɒɡəhedz/
**Definition:** In a state of strong disagreement or conflict with someone.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 僵持不下，意見嚴重分歧

**Example 1:**
The two parties are currently at loggerheads over the proposed trade agreement.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
雙方目前就擬議的貿易協定僵持不下。

**Example 2:**
Even after hours of negotiation, the committee remained at loggerheads.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
即使經過數小時的談判，委員會仍然意見不合。

## 2. **Glittering** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɡlɪtərɪŋ/
**Definition:** Impressive, successful, or richly decorated.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 輝煌的，閃閃發光的

**Example 1:**
The royal banquet was a glittering event attended by world leaders.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這場皇家宴會是一場各國領導人出席的輝煌盛事。

**Example 2:**
She has had a glittering career in the film industry.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她在電影界擁有輝煌的事業。

## 3. **Unrest** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ʌnˈrest/
**Definition:** A state of dissatisfaction or disturbance in a country or group of people.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 動盪，騷亂

**Example 1:**
The government is trying to contain the growing social unrest in the capital.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正試圖遏制首都日益嚴重的社會動盪。

**Example 2:**
Economic hardship has led to widespread civil unrest across the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經濟困境導致該地區出現廣泛的社會騷亂。

## 4. **Mega-merger** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈmeɡə ˌmɜːdʒə/
**Definition:** A very large joining together of two or more companies.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 超大型企業合併

**Example 1:**
Analysts are debating the potential impact of the proposed mega-merger.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
分析師們正在討論這場擬議中的超大型合併可能帶來的影響。

**Example 2:**
If the mega-merger proceeds, it will reshape the global tech market.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果這場超大型合併成行，將會重塑全球科技市場。

## 5. **Misgivings** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /mɪsˈɡɪvɪŋz/
**Definition:** Feelings of doubt or apprehension about the outcome or consequences of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 疑慮，擔憂

**Example 1:**
Many residents have serious misgivings about the new construction project.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多居民對這項新建築工程有嚴重的疑慮。

**Example 2:**
Despite his initial misgivings, he decided to support the decision.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
儘管他最初有所疑慮，他還是決定支持這個決定。

## 6. **Capacity** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈpæsəti/
**Definition:** A specified position or role; the ability to perform a particular task.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 身份，職位，能力

**Example 1:**
She is attending the meeting in an advisory capacity.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
她是以顧問的身份出席會議。

**Example 2:**
The stadium is filled to its maximum capacity for the final match.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
決賽時體育場已坐滿，達到了最大容納量。
