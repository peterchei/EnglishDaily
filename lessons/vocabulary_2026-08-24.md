# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-24

## 1. **Mound** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /maʊnd/
**Definition:** A large pile or heap of earth, stones, or other materials.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 土墩、堆積物

**Example 1:**
The landslide left a massive mound of debris blocking the main road.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
山泥傾瀉留下一大堆瓦礫，堵塞了主幹道。

**Example 2:**
Workers cleared the mound of rubbish that had accumulated near the site.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
工人清理了在工地附近堆積如山的垃圾。

## 2. **Valuation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌvæljuˈeɪʃn/
**Definition:** An estimation of the worth or price of something, especially a company or property.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 估值

**Example 1:**
The tech startup achieved a high valuation after its latest round of funding.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家科技初創公司在最新一輪融資後，獲得了很高的估值。

**Example 2:**
Investors are closely watching the firm's valuation before the stock market launch.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
投資者在該公司上市前正密切關注其估值。

## 3. **Retaliatory** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈtæliətəri/
**Definition:** Done in order to harm someone because they have harmed you.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 報復性的

**Example 1:**
The government announced retaliatory measures following the breakdown of trade talks.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府在貿易談判破裂後，宣佈了報復性措施。

**Example 2:**
He warned that any further aggression would result in a retaliatory strike.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他警告說，任何進一步的侵略行為都會導致報復性打擊。

## 4. **Comply** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kəmˈplaɪ/
**Definition:** To act in accordance with a wish, command, or set of rules.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 遵守、順從

**Example 1:**
All companies must comply with the new safety regulations by next month.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
所有公司必須在下個月前遵守新的安全條例。

**Example 2:**
The organisation refused to comply with the court’s request for internal documents.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
該組織拒絕遵守法庭索取內部文件的要求。

## 5. **Practices** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpræktɪsɪz/
**Definition:** Professional businesses, especially those of doctors, dentists, or lawyers.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 診所、律師事務所（專業執業處所）

**Example 1:**
Many local dental practices are struggling to keep up with the demand for appointments.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多本地牙科診所正努力應付預約需求。

**Example 2:**
The audit examined the administrative procedures of several medical practices.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
審計檢視了幾間醫療診所的行政程序。

## 6. **Reaffirm** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːəˈfɜːm/
**Definition:** To state a fact or belief again, especially to strengthen it.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 重申、再次肯定

**Example 1:**
The leader used the visit to reaffirm the strong ties between the two nations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該領導人利用這次訪問重申兩國之間的牢固關係。

**Example 2:**
They issued a statement to reaffirm their commitment to the peace process.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們發表聲明，重申對和平進程的承諾。

## 7. **Drones** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /drəʊnz/
**Definition:** Remotely piloted aircraft or flying robots often used for surveillance or delivery.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 無人機

**Example 1:**
The military is testing new drones to improve their reconnaissance capabilities.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
軍方正在測試新型無人機，以提高偵察能力。

**Example 2:**
Some companies are using drones to deliver small parcels directly to customers' homes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
有些公司正使用無人機將小型包裹直接運送到顧客家中。
