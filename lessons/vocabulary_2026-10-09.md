# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-09

## 1. **Under-diagnosis** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌʌndəˌdaɪəɡˈnəʊsɪs/
**Definition:** The failure to correctly identify or recognize a medical condition or disease in a patient.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 診斷不足

**Example 1:**
The report suggests that many children are currently suffering due to the under-diagnosis of their learning conditions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該報告指出，許多兒童目前因學習障礙未被及時診斷而受苦。

**Example 2:**
Experts fear that under-diagnosis leads to a lack of support for those who really need it.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
專家擔心診斷不足會導致真正需要幫助的人得不到支援。

## 2. **Firing squad** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfaɪərɪŋ ˌskwɒd/
**Definition:** A group of soldiers or officials ordered to execute a prisoner by shooting.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 行刑隊

**Example 1:**
The use of a firing squad for capital punishment remains a highly controversial topic in the United States.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在美國，使用行刑隊執行死刑仍然是一個極具爭議的話題。

**Example 2:**
Public debate has intensified after it was announced that the prisoner would face a firing squad.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在宣佈囚犯將面對行刑隊後，公眾辯論變得更加激烈。

## 3. **Livestreamed** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlaɪvstriːmd/
**Definition:** To broadcast an event live over the internet as it happens.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 直播（網上直播）

**Example 1:**
The conference will be livestreamed so that international participants can watch it from home.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
會議將會進行直播，讓國際參與者可以在家收看。

**Example 2:**
Many people were shocked that such a sensitive event was being livestreamed to the public.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多人對於如此敏感的活動竟向公眾進行直播感到震驚。

## 4. **Assassination** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /əˌsæsɪˈneɪʃn/
**Definition:** The act of murdering an important person, usually for political or ideological reasons.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 暗殺

**Example 1:**
The police are investigating an attempted assassination of a high-profile local businessman.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
警方正在調查一宗針對知名當地商人的未遂暗殺事件。

**Example 2:**
Security has been tightened following reports of an assassination plot targeting the official.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在收到針對該官員的暗殺陰謀報告後，保安工作已經加強。

## 5. **Volatility** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌvɒləˈtɪləti/
**Definition:** The quality of being subject to frequent, rapid, and significant changes, especially in market prices.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 波動性（不穩定性）

**Example 1:**
Investors are worried about the recent volatility in the global stock markets.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
投資者對全球股市近期的波動感到擔憂。

**Example 2:**
The company decided to delay its expansion due to the economic volatility in the region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於該地區的經濟波動，公司決定推遲擴展計劃。

## 6. **Solar tiles** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈsəʊlə taɪlz/
**Definition:** Roofing materials that function as solar panels to generate electricity while looking like regular roof tiles.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 太陽能瓦片

**Example 1:**
Homeowners are increasingly interested in solar tiles as a more aesthetic way to harness renewable energy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
業主對太陽能瓦片越來越感興趣，認為這是一種更美觀的利用再生能源方式。

**Example 2:**
Although solar tiles are efficient, the high installation cost has prevented them from becoming widespread.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
雖然太陽能瓦片效率很高，但昂貴的安裝成本阻止了它們的普及。
