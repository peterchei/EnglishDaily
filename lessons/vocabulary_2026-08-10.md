# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-10

## 1. **Bulletproof** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbʊl.ɪt.pruːf/
**Definition:** Designed to be unable to be damaged or penetrated by bullets.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 防彈的

**Example 1:**
The prison service used a bulletproof vehicle to transport the high-profile suspect to court.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
懲教署用咗一架防彈車將嗰位備受矚目嘅疑犯送去法庭。

**Example 2:**
The bodyguard wore a bulletproof vest under his suit for protection.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
保鏢喺佢件西裝入面着咗件防彈背心嚟保護自己。

## 2. **Extradite** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈek.strə.daɪt/
**Definition:** To make someone return to the country where they have been accused of a crime.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 引渡（罪犯）

**Example 1:**
Authorities worked for years to extradite the crime boss from Dubai to face trial.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當局花咗多年時間，先至能夠將嗰位犯罪集團首腦從杜拜引渡返嚟受審。

**Example 2:**
It is often a complex legal process to extradite a suspect from a foreign country.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
從外國引渡一名疑犯通常係一個好複雜嘅法律程序。

## 3. **Prosecution** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌprɒs.ɪˈkjuː.ʃən/
**Definition:** The lawyers who conduct the case against a person who is accused of a crime in a court of law.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 檢控方；控方

**Example 1:**
The prosecution presented new evidence to the court regarding the murder case.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
檢控方就呢單謀殺案向法庭呈交咗新證據。

**Example 2:**
The judge listened carefully to the arguments put forward by the prosecution.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
法官好仔細咁聆聽控方所提出嘅論點。

## 4. **Landfall** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlænd.fɔːl/
**Definition:** The point at which a storm or typhoon arrives on land from the sea.
**Cantonese Meaning:** （風暴等）登陸

**Example 1:**
Residents were warned to prepare as the typhoon made landfall late last night.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
颱風喺昨晚深夜登陸，居民收到通知要做好準備。

**Example 2:**
The weather station tracked the eye of the storm until it finally made landfall.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
氣象站一直追蹤風眼，直至佢最終登陸為止。

## 5. **Drought** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /draʊt/
**Definition:** A long period when there is little or no rain.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 乾旱

**Example 1:**
The government has introduced water restrictions because the region is suffering from a severe drought.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於呢個地區正經歷嚴重乾旱，政府已經實施咗制水措施。

**Example 2:**
Farmers are worried that the prolonged drought will destroy this year's harvest.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
農夫好擔心持續嘅乾旱會毀咗今年嘅收成。

## 6. **Archaeological** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌɑː.ki.əˈlɒdʒ.ɪ.kəl/
**Definition:** Relating to the study of buildings, objects, and human remains from the past.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 考古學的

**Example 1:**
The ancient shipwreck is considered one of the most significant archaeological discoveries of the decade.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢艘古代沉船被視為呢十年嚟最重大嘅考古發現之一。

**Example 2:**
University students are participating in an archaeological dig in Sicily this summer.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
大學生今年夏天會去西西里島參加考古挖掘工作。

## 7. **Ruling** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈruː.lɪŋ/
**Definition:** An official decision made by a judge or a court.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 裁決；裁定

**Example 1:**
The tech giant faces a massive fine following the court's latest ruling on child safety.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
喺法院就兒童安全做出最新裁決後，呢間科技巨頭面臨巨額罰款。

**Example 2:**
Everyone is waiting to hear the judge's final ruling on the matter.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
大家正等待法官對呢件事作出最終裁決。
