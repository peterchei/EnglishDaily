# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-30

## 1. **Drought** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /draʊt/
**Definition:** A prolonged period of abnormally low rainfall, leading to a shortage of water.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 旱災 / 乾旱

**Example 1:**
The prolonged drought has caused significant damage to local crops and farmers' livelihoods.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
長期嘅旱災對當地農作物同農民嘅生計造成咗重大破壞。

**Example 2:**
Many regions are introducing water restrictions to cope with the severe summer drought.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
好多地區正喺度實施限水措施，以應對嚴重嘅夏季旱災。

## 2. **Dilemma** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈlem.ə/
**Definition:** A situation in which a difficult choice has to be made between two or more alternatives.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 困境 / 進退兩難

**Example 1:**
The government faces a dilemma over whether to prioritize economic growth or environmental protection.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府面對住一個困境，就係要優先考慮經濟增長定係環境保護。

**Example 2:**
Choosing between his job and his family was the biggest dilemma of his life.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
喺工作同屋企之間做出選擇，係佢人生最大嘅難題。

## 3. **Flare-up** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfleər.ʌp/
**Definition:** A sudden outburst or intensification of something, such as fire, violence, or a medical condition.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 爆發 / 復燃

**Example 1:**
Firefighters are struggling to contain the flare-ups caused by the extreme heat and strong winds.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
消防員正努力控制由極端高溫同強風引起嘅火災復燃。

**Example 2:**
There has been a sudden flare-up of tension between the two neighboring countries.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
兩國之間嘅緊張關係突然升溫。

## 4. **Hands-off** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌhændzˈɒf/
**Definition:** Not involving direct interference or active control; letting things happen without much intervention.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 不干預嘅 / 非干預性嘅

**Example 1:**
The manager prefers a hands-off approach, allowing his team to work independently.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
經理傾向採取不干預嘅管理方式，容許佢嘅團隊獨立工作。

**Example 2:**
Critics argue that a hands-off policy on AI regulation could lead to security risks.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
批評者認為，對人工智能監管採取放任政策可能會導致安全風險。

## 5. **Premiere** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprem.i.eər/
**Definition:** The first public performance or showing of a film, play, or other entertainment.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 首映禮

**Example 1:**
The cast members attended the red-carpet premiere in London last night.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
演員陣容噚晚出席咗倫敦嘅紅地氈首映禮。

**Example 2:**
The film received positive reviews immediately after its world premiere.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
套戲喺全球首映之後，隨即獲得咗正面評價。

## 6. **Adapt** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈdæpt/
**Definition:** To change or adjust something to suit different conditions or a new purpose.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 適應 / 改編

**Example 1:**
We need to adapt our homes to withstand higher temperatures in the future.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我哋需要改造屋企，以抵禦未來更高嘅氣溫。

**Example 2:**
It takes time for new employees to adapt to the company's working culture.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
新員工需要時間去適應公司嘅工作文化。
