# Daily English Lesson - 2026-11-17

## 1. **Reforms** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈfɔːmz/
**Definition:** Changes or improvements made to a system, law, or organization.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 改革

**Example 1:**
The government is planning significant reforms to the education system to help young people find work.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正計劃對教育制度進行重大改革，以幫助年輕人尋找工作。

**Example 2:**
Public support for the proposed tax reforms remains high across the country.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
全國對擬議稅務改革的民意支持仍然很高。

## 2. **Draft** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /drɑːft/
**Definition:** A preliminary version of a document or piece of legislation that may still be changed.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 草擬的；草案

**Example 1:**
The committee is currently reviewing the draft bill regarding gender care.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
委員會目前正在審查有關性別護理的草案。

**Example 2:**
Please send me the draft copy of your report before you submit the final version.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請在提交最終版本前，將你的報告草稿發給我。

## 3. **Consequences** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈkɒnsɪkwənsɪz/
**Definition:** Results or effects, typically ones that are unwelcome or unpleasant.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 後果

**Example 1:**
Experts warn that the new policy could have serious unintended consequences for families.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
專家警告稱，這項新政策可能會對家庭產生嚴重的非預期後果。

**Example 2:**
You must be prepared to face the consequences of your actions.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你必須準備好承擔你行為的後果。

## 4. **Pre-orders** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpriːɔːdəz/
**Definition:** Orders placed for an item before it is available for purchase or release.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 預購；預訂

**Example 1:**
The game has already generated over a billion dollars in pre-orders.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該遊戲已經產生了超過十億美元的預購收入。

**Example 2:**
Customers who place pre-orders will receive an exclusive digital reward.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
進行預購的顧客將會獲得獨家數位獎勵。

## 5. **Endangered** (Verb/Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˈdeɪndʒəd/
**Definition:** To put someone or something at risk or in danger of harm.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 危及；處於危險中

**Example 1:**
The lawsuit claims that the company’s actions have endangered public safety.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該訴訟指控該公司的行為已危及公眾安全。

**Example 2:**
Poor maintenance has endangered the lives of the workers in the factory.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
維護不善已經危及了工廠工人的生命安全。

## 6. **Misled** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /mɪsˈled/
**Definition:** To give someone the wrong idea or impression, often intentionally.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 誤導

**Example 1:**
The Attorney General alleges that the firm intentionally misled the public about its data security.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
總檢察長指控該公司在數據安全方面故意誤導公眾。

**Example 2:**
I feel that the brochure misled me about the quality of the hotel.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我覺得那本小冊子在酒店質素方面誤導了我。

## 7. **Reinstate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌriːɪnˈsteɪt/
**Definition:** To restore someone or something to their former position or state.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 恢復（職位或原狀）

**Example 1:**
There is a growing campaign to reinstate the former CEO of the chip giant.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
目前有一項日益高漲的運動，要求讓這位晶片巨頭的前執行長官復職。

**Example 2:**
The council decided to reinstate the bus service that was cancelled last year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
議會決定恢復去年取消的巴士服務。
