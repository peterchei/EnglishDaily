# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-29

## 1. **Excavator** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈeks.kə.veɪ.tər/
**Definition:** A large machine used for digging earth and moving heavy objects, often used in construction or rescue operations.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 挖掘機 / 泥頭車

**Example 1:**
The rescue team used an excavator to clear the debris and reach the trapped woman.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
救援隊利用挖掘機清理瓦礫，以救出受困的婦人。

**Example 2:**
Roadworks in the city centre often require a heavy-duty excavator to break up the concrete.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
市中心的道路工程經常需要重型挖掘機來破碎混凝土。

## 2. **Fragile** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈfrædʒ.aɪl/
**Definition:** Easily broken, damaged, or destroyed; physically weak or delicate.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 脆弱的 / 易碎的

**Example 1:**
The peace agreement remains fragile despite the recent efforts of both nations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管兩國最近作出了努力，和平協議依然脆弱。

**Example 2:**
Please handle the antique vase with care as it is extremely fragile.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請小心處理那隻古董花瓶，因為它非常易碎。

## 3. **Ascend** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /əˈsend/
**Definition:** To move upward, climb, or rise to a higher position or rank.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 上升 / 登基 / 攀登

**Example 1:**
The crown prince will officially ascend the throne during the ceremony tomorrow.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
王儲將於明天的儀式上正式登基。

**Example 2:**
We watched the hot air balloon slowly ascend into the clear morning sky.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們看著熱氣球緩緩升入清澈的晨空。

## 4. **Revive** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈvaɪv/
**Definition:** To bring something back to life, activity, or popularity.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 復甦 / 恢復 / 重新流行

**Example 1:**
The new government policy aims to revive the country's struggling economy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新政府政策旨在令該國陷入困境的經濟復甦。

**Example 2:**
The band plans to revive some of their classic songs for the upcoming tour.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
樂隊計劃在即將到來的巡演中重新演繹他們的一些經典歌曲。

## 5. **Sophisticated** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /səˈfɪs.tɪ.keɪ.tɪd/
**Definition:** Having a high degree of complexity, developed to a high level, or involving advanced technology.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 複雜的 / 高階的 / 老練的

**Example 1:**
Cyber-attacks are becoming increasingly sophisticated as hackers use artificial intelligence.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
隨著黑客使用人工智能，網絡攻擊正變得日益複雜。

**Example 2:**
She has a very sophisticated understanding of international trade laws.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她對國際貿易法有著非常高深和老練的理解。

## 6. **Performer** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /pəˈfɔː.mər/
**Definition:** A person who entertains an audience, such as an actor, musician, or dancer.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 表演者 / 藝人

**Example 1:**
Many well-known performers have signed the petition to protect their digital voices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多知名表演者已簽署請願書，以保護他們的數字聲音權益。

**Example 2:**
The theatre is looking for a versatile performer who can both sing and dance.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
這家劇院正在尋找一位既能唱歌又能跳舞的多才多藝的表演者。
