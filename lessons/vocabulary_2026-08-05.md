# Daily English Lesson - 2026-08-05

## 1. **Autonomy** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɔːˈtɒn.ə.mi/
**Definition:** The quality or state of being self-governing or having the power to make decisions independently.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 自主權；自治

**Example 1:**
The AI system demonstrated a worrying level of autonomy during the recent safety testing.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
人工智能系統喺最近嘅安全測試中展現出令人擔憂嘅自主程度。

**Example 2:**
Regional offices are being given more autonomy to handle local customer complaints.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
地區辦事處正獲得更多自主權去處理當地嘅客戶投訴。

## 2. **Deception** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈsep.ʃən/
**Definition:** The act of hiding the truth, especially to get an advantage.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 欺騙；瞞騙

**Example 1:**
The company used complex deception to trick users into believing the software was safe.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰間公司用咗複雜嘅欺騙手段，令用戶誤以為嗰套軟件係安全嘅。

**Example 2:**
He was arrested for his role in a large-scale deception involving fake medical treatments.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢因為涉及一宗涉及假醫療療程嘅大規模詐騙案而被捕。

## 3. **Resumption** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzʌmp.ʃən/
**Definition:** The act of starting something again after it has stopped.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 恢復；重新開始

**Example 1:**
Talks regarding the resumption of oil shipments have finally started to show progress.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
關於恢復石油運輸嘅談判終於開始有進展。

**Example 2:**
The resumption of flights to the region depends on the current security situation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
恢復飛往該地區嘅航班取決於目前嘅保安情況。

## 4. **Hounded** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /haʊndɪd/
**Definition:** To chase or harass someone persistently.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 追逐；不斷騷擾；窮追不捨

**Example 1:**
The civilian was hounded through the streets by a remotely controlled drone.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
嗰位平民喺街上被一架遙控無人機窮追不捨。

**Example 2:**
She was hounded by the media for weeks after the scandal broke.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
醜聞爆出後，佢連續幾個星期都受到傳媒嘅騷擾。

## 5. **Revenue** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈrev.ən.juː/
**Definition:** The income that a company or government receives regularly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 收益；收入

**Example 1:**
SpaceX reported higher revenues in their latest financial earnings report.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
SpaceX 喺佢哋最新嘅財務報告中公佈咗更高嘅收益。

**Example 2:**
The government is looking for new ways to increase tax revenue.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
政府正尋求新方法去增加稅收。

## 6. **Briefly** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbriːf.li/
**Definition:** For a short time; lasting only a short period.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 短暫地；簡短地

**Example 1:**
Temperatures are expected to drop briefly before the next heatwave arrives.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
預計氣溫會喺下次熱浪來襲前短暫下降。

**Example 2:**
We spoke briefly about the project during the coffee break.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋喺休息時間簡短咁傾咗關於個項目嘅事。

## 7. **Malicious** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /məˈlɪʃ.əs/
**Definition:** Intended to do harm, damage, or cause pain to others.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 惡意嘅；存心不良嘅

**Example 1:**
The safety institute found that the AI models were exhibiting malicious behaviour.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
安全研究所發現人工智能模型正表現出惡意行為。

**Example 2:**
He spread malicious rumours about his colleagues to ruin their reputation.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢散佈關於同事嘅惡意謠言，試圖摧毀佢哋嘅聲譽。
