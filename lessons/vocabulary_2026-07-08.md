# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-08

## 1. **Convene** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kənˈviːn/
**Definition:** To come together for a formal meeting or assembly.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 召集，集合

**Example 1:**
The Prime Minister will convene a meeting of leaders to discuss the new project.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
首相將會召集一眾領袖開會，討論這個新項目。

**Example 2:**
The committee is expected to convene on Tuesday to review the proposed changes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
委員會預計將於星期二開會，審核提議的改動。

## 2. **Defiant** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /dɪˈfaɪ.ənt/
**Definition:** Openly resisting or refusing to obey authority.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 挑戰的，目中無人的，反抗的

**Example 1:**
She remained defiant despite the court's guilty verdict.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管法庭判決有罪，她依然表現得毫不屈服。

**Example 2:**
The protesters took a defiant stance against the new local regulations.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
示威者對新的地方法規採取了反抗的姿態。

## 3. **Stranded** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈstræn.dɪd/
**Definition:** Unable to leave a place because of problems such as floods or lack of transport.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 被困的，滯留的

**Example 1:**
Many families were left stranded after the typhoon caused severe flooding.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
颱風引發嚴重水災後，許多家庭都被困住。

**Example 2:**
I was stranded at the airport for ten hours due to the cancelled flight.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
因為航班取消，我在機場滯留了十個小時。

## 4. **Sweeping** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈswiː.pɪŋ/
**Definition:** Affecting many things or people; wide-ranging.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 廣泛的，全面的

**Example 1:**
The company announced sweeping changes to its organizational structure.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司宣佈對其組織架構進行全面的改革。

**Example 2:**
He made sweeping statements about the economy without providing any evidence.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他對經濟發表了籠統的言論，卻沒有提供任何證據。

## 5. **Seize** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /siːz/
**Definition:** To take hold of something suddenly or take control of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 奪取，控制，抓住（機會）

**Example 1:**
The politician is trying to seize control of the political agenda.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該政客正試圖奪回政治議題的主導權。

**Example 2:**
You must seize the opportunity to study abroad while you are still young.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你必須趁年輕時把握出國留學的機會。

## 6. **Equate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˈkweɪt/
**Definition:** To consider one thing to be the same as or equal to another.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 同等看待，等同於

**Example 1:**
Many people wrongly equate high salary with professional success.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多人錯誤地將高薪與事業成功畫上等號。

**Example 2:**
The loss of 1,600 jobs equates to a significant reduction in the workforce.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
裁減 1,600 個職位等同於勞動力的大幅縮減。

## 7. **Forthcoming** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌfɔːθˈkʌm.ɪŋ/
**Definition:** Happening or appearing in the near future.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 即將到來的

**Example 1:**
We are preparing for the forthcoming election campaign.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
我們正在為即將到來的競選活動做準備。

**Example 2:**
Information about the new policy will be forthcoming later this week.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
關於這項新政策的消息將於本週晚些時候發佈。
