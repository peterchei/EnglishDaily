# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-20

## 1. **Equalising** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈiːkwəlaɪzɪŋ/
**Definition:** To make things the same in size, amount, or value.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 使均等化

**Example 1:**
The government is debating the possibility of equalising taxes across different types of income.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在辯論將不同類型的收入稅項均等化的可能性。

**Example 2:**
Equalising the opportunities for all students remains a key priority for the education department.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
為所有學生均等化學習機會仍然是教育部門的一個重點優先事項。

## 2. **Looted** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈluːtɪd/
**Definition:** Stolen goods from shops or homes, typically during a war or riot.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 搶掠

**Example 1:**
Local business owners were devastated to find their shops had been looted during the night.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當地的店主發現他們的店舖在夜間遭到搶掠，感到非常痛心。

**Example 2:**
The police are investigating who looted the electronics store after the security system failed.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
警方正在調查保安系統失靈後，是誰搶掠了那間電器店。

## 3. **Motorists** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈməʊtərɪsts/
**Definition:** People who drive cars.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 駕車人士

**Example 1:**
Motorists are advised to avoid the city centre due to the ongoing road closures.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
由於道路持續封閉，建議駕車人士避開市中心。

**Example 2:**
The new parking regulations aim to protect motorists from unfair and excessive fines.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
新的泊車規則旨在保護駕車人士免受不公平及過高的罰款。

## 4. **Incoming** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪnkʌmɪŋ/
**Definition:** Arriving or about to arrive.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 即將到來的

**Example 1:**
The air raid siren warned citizens of an incoming missile attack.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
空襲警報向市民發出即將有導彈襲擊的警告。

**Example 2:**
The manager is busy preparing a handover report for the incoming CEO.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
經理正忙於為即將上任的行政總裁準備交接報告。

## 5. **Starve** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /stɑːv/
**Definition:** To suffer or die from extreme lack of food.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 捱餓 / 餓死

**Example 1:**
The cult leader was convicted for telling parents to starve their children.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該邪教領袖因教唆父母讓孩子捱餓而被判罪。

**Example 2:**
Without humanitarian aid, thousands of refugees risk starving in the conflict zone.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果沒有人道主義援助，成千上萬的難民在衝突地區有餓死的風險。

## 6. **Separation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌsepəˈreɪʃn/
**Definition:** The act of ending a relationship or living apart from a partner.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 分居 / 分手

**Example 1:**
The tennis star announced her separation from her husband after years of marriage.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位網球明星在結婚多年後宣布與丈夫分居。

**Example 2:**
They decided that a trial separation would be the best way to resolve their personal issues.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們決定以嘗試分居的方式來解決彼此的個人問題。

## 7. **Tension** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtenʃn/
**Definition:** A feeling of fear or anger between two groups that do not trust each other.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 緊張局勢

**Example 1:**
Economic tension between the two countries has led to new restrictions on technology exports.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國之間的經濟緊張局勢導致了對科技出口的新限制。

**Example 2:**
There is a lot of tension in the office following the announcement of the redundancies.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在裁員消息公布後，辦公室內的氣氛非常緊張。
