# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-02

## 1. **Commute** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kəˈmjuːt/
**Definition:** To change a punishment to one that is less severe, often used in legal contexts regarding death sentences.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 減刑

**Example 1:**
The lawyer filed a petition to commute the prisoner's death sentence to life imprisonment.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
律師提交了一份請願書，要求將該囚犯的死刑減為終身監禁。

**Example 2:**
The judge decided to commute his sentence after new evidence was presented to the court.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
在新的證據提交給法庭後，法官決定減輕他的刑罰。

## 2. **Botched** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /bɒtʃt/
**Definition:** (Of a task or job) carried out badly or without the necessary skill, resulting in failure.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 搞砸的；拙劣的

**Example 1:**
The authorities launched an investigation into the botched execution that left the victim in critical condition.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
當局對那次導致受害者情況危急的拙劣處決展開了調查。

**Example 2:**
He blamed the botched repair job for the ongoing leaks in the kitchen.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他將廚房持續漏水歸咎於那次搞砸了的維修工作。

## 3. **Indoctrination** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪnˌdɒk.trɪˈneɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The process of teaching a person or group to accept a set of beliefs uncritically.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 洗腦；灌輸思想

**Example 1:**
Security experts are concerned about the rise of extremist indoctrination on social media platforms.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
安全專家對社交媒體平台上極端主義思想灌輸的增加感到擔憂。

**Example 2:**
The suspect was reportedly influenced by years of radical indoctrination before carrying out the attack.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
據報導，嫌疑人在發動襲擊前受到了多年激進思想的洗腦。

## 4. **Mishandle** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌmɪsˈhæn.dəl/
**Definition:** To deal with or manage something in an incompetent or dishonest way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 處理不當；亂搞

**Example 1:**
The tech company fired several staff members for mishandling private user data.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這家科技公司解僱了幾名員工，因為他們對私人用戶數據處理不當。

**Example 2:**
If you mishandle the situation, you risk losing the trust of your clients.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果你處理這件事的方法不當，你就有失去客戶信任的風險。

## 5. **Frat** (Noun - Informal)
**Pronunciation:** /fræt/
**Definition:** A social organization for male students at a US college or university (short for fraternity).
**Cantonese Meaning:** 男大學生聯誼會

**Example 1:**
The university has suspended all activities at the frat house pending a police investigation.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在警方調查期間，大學已暫停了該聯誼會的所有活動。

**Example 2:**
Many students choose to join a frat to build social connections and networking opportunities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
許多學生選擇加入聯誼會，以建立社交關係和聯絡機會。

## 6. **Provoke** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˈvəʊk/
**Definition:** To deliberately annoy or make someone angry, especially in order to cause a reaction or conflict.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 挑釁；煽動

**Example 1:**
The leader warned that their military presence was intended to provoke a response from the neighbouring country.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該領導人警告說，他們的軍事存在是為了挑釁鄰國做出反應。

**Example 2:**
Please do not provoke the dog while it is eating its food.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
當狗在吃東西時，請不要去挑釁牠。

## 7. **Row** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /raʊ/
**Definition:** A noisy argument or disagreement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 爭吵；糾紛

**Example 1:**
The two countries are currently involved in a diplomatic row over prisoner rights.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國目前因囚犯權利問題陷入了一場外交糾紛。

**Example 2:**
There was a huge row in the office today regarding the new project deadlines.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
辦公室今天因為新項目的截止日期發生了一場激烈的爭吵。
