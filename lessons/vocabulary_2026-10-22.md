# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-22

## 1. **Defect** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdiːfɛkt/
**Definition:** A fault, flaw, or imperfection in a system, product, or machine.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 缺陷、瑕疵

**Example 1:**
The software contains a critical defect that allows hackers to bypass security.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢套軟件有一個嚴重嘅缺陷，容許黑客繞過保安系統。

**Example 2:**
Engineers are working to fix the structural defect found in the new bridge.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
工程師正努力修復喺新橋發現嘅結構性缺陷。

## 2. **Imminent** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪmɪnənt/
**Definition:** About to happen or occur very soon.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 即將發生嘅、迫在眉睫嘅

**Example 1:**
The company warned that a total system shutdown was imminent.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
公司警告話全面系統停運即將發生。

**Example 2:**
We should seek shelter because a heavy storm appears imminent.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋應該搵地方避一避，因為睇嚟好快就會有大風暴。

## 3. **Exposed** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪkˈspəʊzd/
**Definition:** Left unprotected or vulnerable to harm or danger.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 暴露嘅、無保護嘅

**Example 1:**
The report highlighted that many users were exposed to data theft.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
報告強調好多用戶都處於數據被盜嘅風險之中。

**Example 2:**
Sensitive information was left exposed on the server for several days.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
敏感資料喺伺服器上面無防護咁放咗幾日。

## 4. **Resumption** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzʌmpʃən/
**Definition:** The act of beginning something again after it has been paused or stopped.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 恢復、重新開始

**Example 1:**
There is hope for the resumption of peace talks between the two nations.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
兩國之間嘅和平談判有望恢復。

**Example 2:**
The resumption of train services will begin at five o'clock this morning.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
火車服務將會喺今朝五點鐘恢復。

## 5. **Capability** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌkeɪpəˈbɪlɪti/
**Definition:** The power or ability to do something effectively.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 能力、才能、軍事或技術實力

**Example 1:**
The committee is worried about the lack of naval capability in the region.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
委員會擔心該地區缺乏海軍實力。

**Example 2:**
Our new AI tool has the capability to process data in milliseconds.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋新嘅人工智能工具具備喺毫秒內處理數據嘅能力。

## 6. **Simultaneously** (Adverb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌsɪməlˈteɪniəsli/
**Definition:** Happening at the same time.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 同時地

**Example 1:**
The researchers are testing two different drugs simultaneously to save time.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
研究人員同時測試兩種唔同嘅藥物，以節省時間。

**Example 2:**
The news of the attack broke globally and appeared on all major websites simultaneously.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
襲擊嘅新聞喺全球傳出，並且同時出現喺所有大型網站上面。
