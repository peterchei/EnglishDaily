# Daily English Lesson - 2026-09-21

## 1. **Proceedings** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /prəˈsiːdɪŋz/
**Definition:** A sequence of events or the formal process of a court case.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 法律訴訟程序；會議過程

**Example 1:**
The court allowed the witness to view the proceedings from a separate, secure room.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
法庭准許證人從另一間安全房間觀看審訊程序。

**Example 2:**
Please remain quiet while the formal proceedings are taking place in the chamber.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
請在議會進行正式程序時保持安靜。

## 2. **Vows** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /vaʊz/
**Definition:** To make a serious promise or firm commitment to do something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 發誓；鄭重承諾

**Example 1:**
The politician vows to continue his work despite the poor election results.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管選舉結果不理想，這位政客仍發誓會繼續其工作。

**Example 2:**
She vows to improve the company's safety standards by the end of the year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她承諾在年底前改善公司的安全標準。

## 3. **Barrage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbær.ɑːʒ/
**Definition:** A concentrated outpouring or overwhelming number of things, such as attacks or questions.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 猛烈攻擊；連珠炮發（問題或攻擊）

**Example 1:**
The city faced a nightly barrage of drone strikes throughout the conflict.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這座城市在衝突期間面臨徹夜的無人機猛烈襲擊。

**Example 2:**
The minister faced a barrage of criticism after the announcement was made.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
聲明發出後，該部長面臨了一連串的猛烈批評。

## 4. **Chaotic** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /keɪˈɒt.ɪk/
**Definition:** In a state of complete confusion and disorder.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 混亂的

**Example 1:**
The beach scene became chaotic after the argument turned into a physical fight.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
爭執演變成肢體衝突後，海灘場面變得一片混亂。

**Example 2:**
Traffic was chaotic during the morning rush hour due to the sudden road closure.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
由於道路突然封閉，早晨繁忙時間的交通非常混亂。

## 5. **Sceptical** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈskep.tɪ.kəl/
**Definition:** Not easily convinced; having doubts about a claim or statement.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 懷疑的；持懷疑態度的

**Example 1:**
Many employees are sceptical about whether the new AI tools will actually save time.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
許多員工對於新的 AI 工具是否真的能節省時間持懷疑態度。

**Example 2:**
I am sceptical of his claim that he finished the report in only ten minutes.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我對他聲稱只用了十分鐘就完成報告表示懷疑。

## 6. **Credentials** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /krɪˈden.ʃəlz/
**Definition:** Qualifications, achievements, or pieces of information that prove someone is who they claim to be or is suitable for a task.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 資格證明；憑證；身分認證資料

**Example 1:**
The AI model was able to guess the login credentials for several company websites.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該 AI 模型能夠猜測到幾家公司網站的登入憑證。

**Example 2:**
She has all the right academic credentials for the research position.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她具備擔任該研究職位所需的所有學術資格證明。
