# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-03

## 1. **Barrage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈbær.ɑːʒ/
**Definition:** A concentrated outpouring or stream of something, especially weapons or projectiles.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 猛烈連珠炮發；連環攻擊

**Example 1:**
The city was hit by a massive barrage of rockets overnight.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢個城市昨晚受到大量火箭彈嘅猛烈連環攻擊。

**Example 2:**
She faced a barrage of questions from the reporters after the announcement.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
宣佈消息後，佢面對記者連珠炮發咁提問。

## 2. **Excommunicate** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌek.skəˈkjuː.nɪ.keɪt/
**Definition:** To officially exclude someone from participation in the sacraments and services of the Christian Church.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 逐出教會

**Example 1:**
The church decided to excommunicate members who refused to follow the new rules.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
教會決定將拒絕遵守新規則嘅成員逐出教會。

**Example 2:**
He was excommunicated for speaking out against the leadership of the sect.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢因為公開反對教派嘅領導層而被逐出教會。

## 3. **Star-studded** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈstɑːˌstʌd.ɪd/
**Definition:** Featuring or attended by a large number of famous people.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 星光熠熠嘅；由多位明星出席嘅

**Example 1:**
It was a star-studded event held at the centre of New York.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
呢場喺紐約市中心舉行嘅活動真係星光熠熠。

**Example 2:**
The film premiere was a star-studded affair with many A-list celebrities.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢場電影首映禮星光熠熠，有好多頂級明星出席。

## 4. **Paywall** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpeɪ.wɔːl/
**Definition:** A system that prevents internet users from accessing webpage content without a paid subscription.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 付費牆；付費機制

**Example 1:**
The newspaper put most of its articles behind a paywall last month.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
間報社上個月將大部分文章設為付費閱讀。

**Example 2:**
I refuse to pay a monthly fee just to get past the paywall.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我拒絕為咗睇呢啲內容而支付月費去突破付費牆。

## 5. **Persist** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /pəˈsɪst/
**Definition:** To continue to exist or endure despite interference or opposition.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 堅持；持續存在

**Example 1:**
Despite the economic challenges, the company’s vision continues to persist.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
儘管經濟困難，公司嘅願景依然持續存在。

**Example 2:**
If the symptoms persist, you should see a doctor immediately.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
如果症狀持續，你應該立即去睇醫生。

## 6. **Licensing** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlaɪ.sən.sɪŋ/
**Definition:** The granting or regulation of official permission to do something, such as selling alcohol.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 發牌；牌照管理

**Example 1:**
The government is reviewing the licensing laws for pubs and clubs.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正在檢討酒吧同夜店嘅牌照法律。

**Example 2:**
You need to apply for a special licensing permit to extend your business hours.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
你需要申請特別牌照許可先可以延長營業時間。
