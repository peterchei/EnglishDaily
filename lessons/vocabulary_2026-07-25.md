# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-25

## 1. **Deterrent** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdet.ər.ənt/
**Definition:** Something that discourages or prevents someone from doing something by making them afraid of the consequences.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 阻嚇物 / 阻嚇因素

**Example 1:**
The heavy police presence acts as a strong deterrent to potential vandals.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
大量警力喺場嘅存在，對潛在嘅破壞者係一種強大嘅阻嚇。

**Example 2:**
Many believe that strict laws are the best deterrent against cybercrime.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
好多人認為嚴厲嘅法律係打擊網絡罪行最有效嘅阻嚇手段。

## 2. **Evacuation** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ɪˌvæk.juˈeɪ.ʃən/
**Definition:** The process of moving people from a place of danger to a safer place.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 撤離 / 疏散

**Example 1:**
The evacuation of the village was completed just hours before the fire reached the houses.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
喺火勢蔓延到村屋之前幾個鐘，村民撤離行動已經完成。

**Example 2:**
Emergency services have prepared a detailed evacuation plan for coastal residents.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
緊急服務部門已經為沿海居民準備咗一份詳細嘅疏散計劃。

## 3. **Publicity** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpʌb.lə.sə.ti/
**Definition:** Information or activities that are intended to attract the public's attention to someone or something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 宣傳 / 知名度

**Example 1:**
The company is seeking positive publicity to restore its reputation after the incident.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
間公司正尋求正面嘅宣傳，以喺事件後挽回聲譽。

**Example 2:**
The protest was clearly designed to gain as much publicity as possible.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢場抗議明顯係為咗爭取最大嘅宣傳效果。

## 4. **Resign** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈzaɪn/
**Definition:** To voluntarily leave a job or position.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 辭職

**Example 1:**
The minister decided to resign after the public backlash regarding his recent policy decisions.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
部長喺大眾對佢近期政策決定表示反對後，決定辭職。

**Example 2:**
He chose to resign from his post rather than compromise his professional ethics.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
佢選擇辭去職位，亦唔願意喺職業操守上妥協。

## 5. **Outage** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈaʊ.tɪdʒ/
**Definition:** A period when a service, such as electricity or an internet connection, is not working.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 中斷 / 故障 (指服務)

**Example 1:**
The sudden power outage left thousands of homes in darkness during the storm.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
風暴期間嘅突然停電令數千戶家庭陷入黑暗。

**Example 2:**
The company issued an apology for the network outage that lasted for over six hours.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公司就持續咗六個幾鐘嘅網絡中斷事件發出道歉聲明。

## 6. **Prominent** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈprɒm.ɪ.nənt/
**Definition:** Very noticeable, important, or well-known.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 顯著的 / 傑出的 / 重要的

**Example 1:**
Several prominent figures in the industry attended the defense exhibition.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
幾位業界嘅傑出人士出席咗嗰個國防展覽。

**Example 2:**
The new building has a prominent position in the city centre.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
呢座新建築喺市中心處於一個非常顯眼嘅位置。

## 7. **Tackle** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈtæk.əl/
**Definition:** To try to deal with a difficult problem or situation.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 處理 / 對付 / 解決

**Example 1:**
The government is under pressure to tackle the rising cost of living.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府正面臨壓力，需要處理生活成本上升嘅問題。

**Example 2:**
We need new strategies to tackle the environmental challenges facing our region.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我哋需要新策略去應對我哋地區面臨嘅環境挑戰。
