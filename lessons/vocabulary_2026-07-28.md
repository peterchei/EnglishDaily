# Daily English Lesson - 2026-07-28

## 1. **Inundating** (Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈɪn.ʌn.deɪ.tɪŋ/
**Definition:** To overwhelm someone or something with a very large number of things.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 湧入；應接不暇；氾濫

**Example 1:**
The company was inundated with complaints after the faulty product launch.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該公司在缺陷產品發布後，收到了鋪天蓋地的投訴。

**Example 2:**
Fake AI videos are currently inundating social media platforms.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
偽造的人工智能影片目前正充斥著社交媒體平台。

## 2. **Jitters** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈdʒɪt.əz/
**Definition:** Feelings of extreme nervousness or anxiety.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 緊張；不安；（股市的）恐慌

**Example 1:**
The market jitters caused by the new policy led to a drop in share prices.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
新政策引發的市場不安情緒導致股價下跌。

**Example 2:**
I always get the jitters before giving a presentation at work.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我在公司做簡報前總會感到很緊張。

## 3. **Adhesives** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ədˈhiː.sɪvz/
**Definition:** Substances used for sticking objects or materials together, such as glue.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 黏合劑；膠水

**Example 1:**
Strong industrial adhesives are used to keep the aircraft components together.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
強力的工業黏合劑被用於將飛機組件固定在一起。

**Example 2:**
We are looking for eco-friendly adhesives that don't hinder the recycling process.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
我們正在尋找不會妨礙回收過程的環保黏合劑。

## 4. **Talc** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /tælk/
**Definition:** A soft mineral used in powder form for cosmetic or medicinal purposes.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 滑石粉

**Example 1:**
The lawsuit claims that the company's talc products were contaminated.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
該訴訟聲稱該公司的滑石粉產品受到污染。

**Example 2:**
Some people use talc to keep their skin dry during the summer months.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
有些人會使用滑石粉在夏季保持皮膚乾爽。

## 5. **Remains** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /rɪˈmeɪnz/
**Definition:** The body of a person or animal that has died.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 遺體；殘骸

**Example 1:**
The tragic discovery of the victim's remains shocked the local community.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
受害者遺體的悲慘發現震驚了當地社區。

**Example 2:**
Archaeologists have unearthed the remains of an ancient settlement nearby.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
考古學家在附近挖掘出了古代定居點的遺址。

## 6. **Closes in on** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /kləʊzɪnɒn/
**Definition:** To move steadily nearer to someone or something, often in a threatening way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 逐漸逼近；圍攏

**Example 1:**
The wildfire closes in on the town, forcing residents to evacuate immediately.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
野火正逼近市鎮，迫使居民立即撤離。

**Example 2:**
As the deadline closes in on us, we need to pick up the pace.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
隨著截止日期臨近，我們需要加快步伐。
