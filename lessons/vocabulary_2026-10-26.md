# Daily English Lesson - 2026-10-26

## 1. **Knife-edge** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈnaɪf.edʒ/
**Definition:** A situation where there is a high degree of uncertainty or tension, and the outcome could easily go either way.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 緊張時刻；勝負難料的

**Example 1:**
The election is on a knife-edge, with neither candidate holding a clear lead in the polls.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這場選舉正處於勝負難料的緊張關頭，兩位候選人在民調中都沒有明顯領先。

**Example 2:**
The final match was a knife-edge contest that kept the audience on the edge of their seats.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這場決賽是一場勝負難料的較量，讓觀眾全程都屏息以待。

## 2. **Veteran** (Adjective/Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈvet.ər.ən/
**Definition:** Someone who has had long experience in a particular activity, profession, or role.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 資深人士；老手

**Example 1:**
The veteran politician announced his retirement after thirty years in parliament.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這位資深政治家在議會工作三十年後宣布退休。

**Example 2:**
As a veteran teacher, she knew exactly how to handle a restless classroom.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
作為一名資深教師，她非常清楚如何處理躁動不安的課堂。

## 3. **Referendum** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˌref.əˈren.dəm/
**Definition:** A vote in which all the people in a country or an area are asked to give their opinion about or decide on an important political or social issue.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 全民公投

**Example 1:**
The government has decided to hold a referendum on the proposed constitutional changes.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
政府已決定就擬議的憲法修改舉行全民公投。

**Example 2:**
The results of the referendum will determine the future direction of the country's trade policy.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
公投結果將決定該國貿易政策的未來走向。

## 4. **Exile** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈek.saɪl/
**Definition:** The state of being sent or kept away from your own country, often for political reasons.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 流亡

**Example 1:**
After years in exile, the opposition leader was finally allowed to return home.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
在流亡多年後，這位反對派領袖終於獲准回國。

**Example 2:**
He lived in political exile for a decade while his party was banned.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他在政黨被禁期間，度過了十年的流亡生活。

## 5. **Pivotal** (Adjective)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈpɪv.ə.təl/
**Definition:** Extremely important; central to the success or development of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 關鍵的；舉足輕重的

**Example 1:**
This election is a pivotal moment for the country's economic recovery.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
這次選舉對該國的經濟復甦來說是一個關鍵時刻。

**Example 2:**
She played a pivotal role in negotiating the new international climate agreement.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
她在談判新的國際氣候協議中扮演了關鍵角色。

## 6. **Lifeblood** (Noun)
**Pronunciation:** /ˈlaɪf.blʌd/
**Definition:** The thing that is most important to the continued success and existence of something.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 生命線；命脈

**Example 1:**
Small businesses are the lifeblood of the local economy.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
小型企業是當地經濟的命脈。

**Example 2:**
Water is the lifeblood of the desert ecosystem, sustaining all forms of life.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
水是沙漠生態系統的生命線，維持著各種生命的存在。

## 7. **Shoring up** (Phrasal Verb)
**Pronunciation:** /ʃɔːr ʌp/
**Definition:** To support or improve something that is weak or likely to fail.
**Cantonese Meaning:** 加強；鞏固（以防倒塌或失敗）

**Example 1:**
The Chancellor is focusing on shoring up the country's defences against cyber threats.
**Cantonese Example 1:**
財政大臣正專注於加強該國針對網絡威脅的防禦能力。

**Example 2:**
They are working hard at shoring up the company's finances before the end of the year.
**Cantonese Example 2:**
他們正努力在年底前鞏固公司的財務狀況。
